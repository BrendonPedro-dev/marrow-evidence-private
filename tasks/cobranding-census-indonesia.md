Extend the co-branding census to Indonesia - market ten, the final market of this campaign. Same job as the nine completed markets: the verified campaign table and the verdict, on the IDENTICAL definition, row-for-row comparable. Full keyword net (brand-side AND artist-side) from birth.

## The definition (identical - do not drift)

A row is a brand x character IP collaboration campaign running in Indonesia with any activity in 2024, 2025, or 2026 year-to-date. F&B-led but not F&B-only: retail, convenience, telco, banking, e-commerce, mall, and loyalty campaigns count when a character IP fronts a consumer campaign.

**Ownership test per IP:** portfolio = corporation/estate/platform/franchise-owned (or majority stake); independent = creator-owned (agency-managed but creator-owned = independent, the 咖波 precedent); unclear = genuinely unresolvable after digging, used sparingly with the search trail recorded.

**Pinned edge cases (carried forward):**
- Miffy = portfolio; Snoopy = portfolio; government-owned mascots = EXCLUDED (Kumamon rule)
- Platform-owned characters = portfolio; label-captive indies = REP no (Toyzeroplus precedent)
- OWN-MASCOT exclusion (a brand's proprietary mascot on its own campaign is not a row; third-party characters licensed IN are)
- Per-IP ownership test even for one creator on both sides (Molly Factory precedent)
- Self-presentation is not ownership

**REPRESENTABLE sub-tag** on independent rows: yes / no / unclear.
