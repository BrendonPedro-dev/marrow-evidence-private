# China platforms (阿里魚/Alifish, 泡泡瑪特 ecosystem, 京東IP授權) - verdict with documented trail

**Seed question 3:** does any China platform quantify brand-IP fit for physical products, or is it marketplace/listing only?

**Researched:** 2026-07-15. zh-CN queries primary, English secondary, per task rules.

---

## Verdict

**No China platform was found selling a quantified brand-IP fit score for physical products.**
The ecosystem is richer than "marketplace/listing only" - China's big players have real e-commerce data assets and make data-assisted-matching *claims* - but in every traced case the artifact behind the claim is either (a) internal data assist inside a human agency/brokerage business, (b) published IP trend reports, or (c) an IP-side analytics database that brands browse and filter, with humans (顾问, BD teams) making the pairing call.

The single strongest piece of evidence is an on-record admission: **京东's own head of IP licensing (高雷, 2022) describes big-data-supported matching in the future tense**, calling licensing "相对感性的行业" (a relatively gut-feel industry) that big data *would* give theoretical grounding to (§2).

The nearest thing to a competitor is **原仓IP数据 (Yuancang, yuancangip.com)** - an IP licensing big-data platform with an "AI推荐 数据引擎" tagline - but its product is IP-side popularity/sales/audience analytics plus a digitized tender marketplace and human consultants, not a score of fit between a specific brand/product and an IP (§4).

**Confidence: HIGH** that none of the named platforms *claims* a productized brand-IP fit score on its own current surfaces (Alifish and 原仓 homepages read directly - evidence of absence; JD position stated by its own business head).
**MEDIUM** for the ecosystem-wide negative: China's platform landscape is large and fast-moving, so this is "not found under the queries in §6", not an exhaustive audit.
Single-source items are flagged inline.

## 1. 阿里鱼 / Alifish (Alibaba) - a data-rich giant that is still an agency

**Who they are.**
Alibaba's IP operations and commercialization platform (est. 2016), self-described "IP2B2C全链路服务模式", 200+ licensor partnerships (Sanrio, Pokemon, Universal, Crayon Shin-chan, Chiikawa).
License Global's "Top Global Licensing Agents 2025" (Apr 2025) puts Alifish at **$4.1B retail sales, #6 globally, #1 APAC** - i.e. it wins as a licensing *agent*, not as a scoring product.
Sources: https://ip.alibaba.com/about (read directly), https://baike.baidu.com/item/%E9%98%BF%E9%87%8C%E9%B1%BC/19692326, License Global figure via zh search summary + https://www.licenseglobal.com/licensing-resources/ubm-lima-and-alifish-join-forces-china (2016 background).

**The data-matching claims, traced.**

- 2016 PR (UBM/LIMA/Alifish): platform offers "online licensing match-making ... powered by big data" and "sophisticated identification of potential licensees".
  Marketing language at platform launch; no mechanism described.
  Source: https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/ubm-lima-and-alifish-join-forces-to-strengthen-chinese-licensing-market-300344190.html
- 2019-10-30, 吴倩 (Alibaba 大文娱 VP / Alifish president), China Licensing Expo interview: "通过数据产品来辅助我们的双端（IP端和商家端）进行IP指数评估、IP商家匹配、品类开发规划" - data products *assisting* both sides with IP index evaluation, IP-merchant matching, and category planning.
  This is the strongest Alifish quant claim found; note the verb 辅助 (assist) - it frames data as decision support inside their brokerage, and the interview's actual advice to brands is to start from their own positioning, not to consume a score.
  Source: https://www.chinalicensingexpo.com/cn/media_center/detail?id=11382
- What "IP指数" became in practice: **published trend reports**, not a live tool - 阿里鱼IP研究中心 × 阿里数据 released 《2020中国市场IP电商指数报告：母婴亲子篇》, 《2020各圈层人群IP电商指数报告》, 《2020天猫"双11"IP电商指数报告》.
  Source trail: zh search results incl. https://www.geekpark.net/news/342406 and https://www.licensing.org.cn/10762.html (report titles corroborated across both).
- 2021-08-25, 江旭恒 (Alifish VP GM / IP授权业务负责人), LEC interview: hopes to use "阿里的整体生态能力及阿里的大数据" to link IP owners to B-side merchants "非常准确地" - but the piece throughout describes 精细化运营 and human service teams; data is infrastructure, not an exposed matching product.
  Source: https://www.digitaling.com/articles/521898.html

**What the live platform actually exposes (evidence of absence).**
ip.alibaba.com homepage read directly: IP catalog (旗下IP), collaboration case gallery (合作案例), and five services - 旗舰店运营, 内容活动, 市场营销, 图库开发, IP授权 - with the sole merchant entry point being a 授权合作咨询 contact form promising sales follow-up "3-5天".
**No data tool, index, score, or matching feature is exposed to users.**
The /about page likewise contains no 大数据/指数/匹配/AI/评估 claims.
Sources: https://ip.alibaba.com/ and https://ip.alibaba.com/about (both read directly).

**Classification:** data-assisted *agency brokering* at giant scale. The quant vocabulary exists in executive interviews (2016-2021) but never surfaced as a purchasable fit-scoring product, and the current site has dropped even the vocabulary.

## 2. 京东 / JD - 京纪圈 is a bridge platform, and JD's own lead says data-driven matching is the future, not the present

**Who/what.**
京纪圈 (launched end of 2017) is JD's 版权交易服务平台 - explicitly "为版权方与京东平台的商家建立桥梁，促成授权合作" (a bridge between copyright holders and JD merchants to facilitate licensing deals), evolved from a pure transaction platform to a transaction *services* platform.
JD's IP文娱业务 = sales channel (POP storefronts for licensors) + supply-chain output (co-developed goods sold via JD自营) + 京纪圈 matchmaking.
Scale per its own head: "大大小小几千个授权项目" over 5 years, "千余个活跃的IP".

**The money quote (2022-07-21, 高雷, 京东零售 IP文娱业务负责人, Licensing International China column):**

> "未来大数据驱动版权合作才是真正能让这个行业更上一层楼。大数据给合作双方合作匹配提供支撑才能让授权从相对感性的行业有理论的数据支撑。"

("In the *future*, big-data-driven copyright cooperation is what can really take this industry to the next level. Big data supporting the matching of the two cooperating sides is what can give licensing - a relatively gut-feel industry - theoretical data support.")

Future tense, from the person running JD's licensing platform: quantified matching support is an aspiration, and the industry as practiced is 相对感性.
His own list of open questions ("你的IP是什么，用户覆盖是什么，年龄分层如何，与什么类型的商家合作覆盖及带来转化会更好？") is precisely the fit question - posed as unanswered.
Source: https://www.licensing.org.cn/9410.html (full text read via curl; WebFetch 403s).
Note this is a 2022 statement - treated as the platform's philosophy of record absent anything newer found (nothing newer surfaced under §6 queries).

**Classification:** marketplace/bridge + services. The fit gap is acknowledged in-house.

## 3. 泡泡玛特 / Pop Mart ecosystem - an IP owner licensing out on qualitative judgment

Pop Mart sits on the *licensor* side: it licenses MOLLY, SKULLPANDA, THE MONSTERS etc. out to brands (瑞幸, 安踏, 优衣库, vivo, 必胜客, 丝芙兰, Fresh/Make Up For Ever, 哈根达斯...).
Its outbound licensing revenue grew 10.36亿 → 16.01亿 RMB from 2023 to 2024 (+54.5%) - **single-source** (雷报 via licensingexpochina.com archive; search-level, not annual-report-verified).

How partners are chosen, per 杨健澎 (泡泡玛特市场授权总监), 2021-09-14 deep-dive interview: three qualitative dimensions - partner tier/relevance (奢侈品牌/轻奢品牌 or 衣食住行 lifestyle brands with trend-forward positioning), design sincerity (calling out lazy co-brands: "有些品牌...拿一个普通款的产品...放到礼盒里...被粉丝吐槽，认为这是没有诚意的联名"), and marketing resource commitment.
Three deal forms by IP maturity (existing IP ~2-3 months; pre-launch IP; custom IP 9+ months).
**No mention of scoring, data mechanisms, or AI in partner selection** - brand judgment end to end.
Sources: https://news.qq.com/rain/a/20210914A06XQ700 (read directly), case roundups https://www.digitaling.com/articles/666917.html, https://www.digitaling.com/articles/668639.html, revenue figure https://www.licensingexpochina.com/archives/3934

**Classification:** relationship/judgment licensing by a hot licensor; no fit-quantification offering to anyone.

## 4. 原仓IP数据 (Yuancang) - the closest China analogue, and it is IP-side analytics + tender marketplace, not pairing fit

**This is the one to watch and the one a sharp VC associate might raise.**

What it is: a dedicated IP licensing big-data platform (www.yuancangip.com, console at console.yuancangip.com, tagline "专注品牌IP授权行业的大数据分析工具"), claiming 20,000+ aggregated IPs with data across 用户画像 (user profiles), 衍生品市场表现力 (derivative market performance), 媒体影响力 (media influence), 舆情动向 (sentiment).
Homepage carries an "**AI推荐 数据引擎**" tagline and the promise "帮你找到最适配的IP" (help you find the most suitable IP).
Product surface read directly: IP动态 feed, **IP热搜榜, IP带货榜, IP衍生品榜** (rankings), IP catalog with browse/filter, IP直供, 入驻/寻求合作 funnels.
Around it: a first-of-kind **IP授权数字化招投标** mode (brand posts requirements online, 20-30 day cycle vs 3-6 months traditional), an 授权SaaS management system, and 专业顾问对接 (professional consultant matchmaking).
The 2025 汕头AI电商生态大会 writeup describes it as "通过数据化工具和专业服务，帮助企业精准匹配与自身品牌调性相符的IP" - data tools *plus professional services*.

**What it is not:** nowhere on the read surfaces does 原仓 claim to compute a fit/适配度/契合度 *score* between a specific brand or product and an IP.
The quantification is all IP-side (heat, sales pull, audience, sentiment) - a Chinese IP-analytics database brands filter through, with consultants and a tender process closing the loop.
"AI推荐" is a homepage tagline with no described mechanism (**single-source**, vendor's own site; no independent coverage of the AI feature found).

Sources: https://www.yuancangip.com/ (read directly), https://console.yuancangip.com/home (header only; app is JS-walled), https://zhuanlan.zhihu.com/p/1974829911442220049 (403 to fetch; content via search summary - **flagged**), 四-platform structure per https://news.sina.cn/sx/2022-08-03/detail-imizirav6564684.d.html (search-level, **single-source**), related property https://www.ipyimatong.com/ (原仓·IP授权数字化应用, not fetched).

**Classification:** type-C analytics + marketplace infrastructure (in the landscape doc's taxonomy) - closest China competitor on vocabulary, still no brand×IP pairing score.

## 7. Flags

- **Single-source:** Pop Mart licensing revenue split (雷报); 原仓 "AI推荐 数据引擎" (vendor homepage only); 原仓 four-platform corporate structure (sina.cn 2022); 吴倩 2019 quote (one interview transcript, though consistent with 2016 PR language).
- **Staleness:** the JD 高雷 statement is 2022; the Alifish executive quotes are 2019-2021. Their *current* site surfaces (read 2026-07-15) show no newer quant product, which is the load-bearing evidence.
- **Watch item for the pitch:** 原仓 is the entity most likely to move from IP-side analytics toward pairing scores; its 招投标 mode already digitizes the transaction. If asked "does China have this?", the honest answer is: 原仓 quantifies the IP, nobody quantifies the *pairing*.

## Pitch implication

China strengthens rather than threatens the "our segment is open" claim, with a better story than "nothing exists": the largest data owner (Alibaba/Alifish, $4.1B retail sales as an agent) talks about data-assisted matching but ships an agency with a contact form; JD's licensing head says on the record the industry is 相对感性 and big-data matching is the future; the specialist data platform (原仓) quantifies IP heat, not brand-IP fit.
