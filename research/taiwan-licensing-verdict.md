# Taiwan licensing agencies and platforms (授權代理 / IP授權平台) - verdict with documented trail

**Seed question 2:** who are the Taiwan incumbents, and does ANY offer quantified matching or fit scoring vs relationship brokering?
Name names, cite what they actually claim.

**Researched:** 2026-07-15. zh-TW queries primary, English secondary, per task rules.

---

## Verdict

**NO quantified matching or fit scoring found in Taiwan's licensing ecosystem - it is relationship brokering end to end.**
The incumbents split into three layers, and every one of them describes its matching mechanism as human curation, agent judgment, or event-based introductions:

1. **Commercial IP agencies** (udn 聯合數位文創, Hu Creates 胡創, 卡洛特 Character Co., 毛毛蟲文創; anime-merch side: 木棉花 Muse, 群英社 Top-Insight, 曼迪 Mighty Media) - classic agency brokering.
2. **Matchmaking platforms/programs** (IPCP 育成媒合平台 by 成真文創; TAICCA 文策院 matchmaking and expo delegations) - curated rosters and themed events, explicitly human.
3. **Associations** (TCBLA 台灣角色品牌授權協會) - delegation organizing and industry advocacy.

The single "AI + big data" artifact found anywhere in the Taiwan trail is a **2019 one-off expo booth installation** (Fresh Taiwan pavilion, Shanghai licensing expo): an AI smart signage that guessed visitor gender/age to display a recommended character, plus foot-traffic analytics for exhibitors.
That is booth marketing tech for B2C passersby, not a pre-deal IP × product fit score, and no recurrence after 2019 was found (§1d).

**Confidence: HIGH** that no named Taiwan incumbent *claims* quantified fit scoring.
For IPCP, Hu Creates, and 卡洛特 this is **evidence of absence** (their own pages were read directly and contain no 評分/數據/AI/演算法 claims).
For the remaining players it is "no evidence found" at search level under the queries in §3, not an exhaustive audit.
Single-source items are flagged inline.

## 1. The incumbents - who they are and what they actually claim

### a. Image/character IP agencies (原創圖像 IP 經紀)

- **udn 聯合數位文創 (圖像IP授權發展中心)** - the heavyweight.
  Its own homepage lists services as: 圖像經紀, 圖像授權, 異業結盟, 資源媒合, 國際展會 (image agency, image licensing, cross-industry alliances, resource matchmaking, international expos) - agency vocabulary, no scoring or data product.
  Credentials shown: LIMA (Licensing International) member; executing unit of the Fresh Taiwan Taiwan pavilion at the Japan, Hong Kong, and Shanghai licensing expos; LIMA Taiwan representative office co-announcement (2018-07-30).
  Represents 貓小姐 Ms.Cat, 二搞創意, 黃色書刊, 麻吉貓 MajiMeow, 肥貓吻吻 and others (news archive of co-branding deals: luggage, sake, EasyCard, banks, cosmetics).
  Source: https://ulicensing.udnfunlife.com/ - **live site refuses connections (ECONNREFUSED)**; read via Wayback snapshot 2025-05-13: http://web.archive.org/web/20250513151757/http://ulicensing.udnfunlife.com/
  Corroboration that udn acts as agent (represents 貓小姐): 貿易雜誌 no.347, https://www.ieatpe.org.tw/magazine/ebook347/storypage03.html

- **Hu Creates 胡創** (Taipei) - self-described "台灣專業IP授權代理商" representing 17+ character/art IPs (Bu2ma 我是白爛貓, Mr. Shark, Sharkitty, Cabypara and others).
  Direct page read: services are agency suite + social-media marketing + OEM/ODM production, philosophy of "growing together with our artists and building bridges."
  **No mention of quantified matching, fit scoring, algorithms, or AI** (evidence of absence).
  Source: https://www.hucreates.com/

- **卡洛特 Character Co., Ltd.** (Taipei, est. 2015) - IP agent for Fruit Cats 果果喵, Poncorgi 椪椪, Mew's Good.
  Direct page read (via curl; WebFetch got 403): positions itself as "IP 經紀人"; for companies it claims "卡洛特將協將企業團體，與適合的旗下 IP 進行合作" (Character Co. will help enterprises partner with *suitable* IPs from its roster) - suitability judged by the agent, no mechanism claimed.
  Its "粉絲經營" pillar includes 資料庫建立 (fan database building), which is CRM/fan-ops, not pairing analytics.
  **No AI/scoring/data-matching claims** (evidence of absence).
  Source: https://www.crter.com.tw/about.html

- **毛毛蟲文創 (Caterpillar Creative)** (est. 2015) - character management and licensing agent, 16 contracted character brands per a 2020 Mirror Media industry feature; positioning quote from founder: "角色圖像本身就有價值，流量只是附加" (the character image itself has value; traffic is only additive).
  No matching tech claims in the article.
  Source (single-source for the company detail): https://www.mirrormedia.mg/story/20200925insight013

### b. Anime/content licensing agents (動漫商品授權窗口)

These are the merchandise-licensing gatekeepers for imported IP in Taiwan; all are brokering businesses:

- **木棉花國際 (Muse Communication)** - ~30 years of anime IP agency/distribution incl. merchandise licensing; industry-association profile: https://www.ccpa.org.tw/twip/data.php?id=168
- **群英社國際 (Top-Insight)** - est. 1995, anime licensing expanded into merchandise, new media, and brand planning/integrated marketing; category strategy described purely by age-demographic judgment of each title (children's dailygoods → collector items for teen titles) in a Bahamut GNN industry interview: https://gnn.gamer.com.tw/detail.php?sn=56337
- **曼迪傳播 (Mighty Media)** - anime distribution and licensing agent; named alongside the two above as Taiwan's main agents: https://gnn.gamer.com.tw/detail.php?sn=56337
- No fit-scoring or data-matching claims found for any of the three (search level).

### c. Matchmaking platforms and programs (the closest thing to "平台")

- **IPCP 育成媒合平台** (ipcp.io) - run by **成真文創 (ChengJen Creative)**; self-described as Taiwan's IP licensing incubation + matchmaking platform since 2019.
  Direct page read of the matchmaking page: monthly themed **online matchmaking events** (last Friday of each month), curated IP and vendor rosters, consultations, corporate training.
  Mechanism in their own words: "嚴選IP名單、廠商名單...依據每月不同主題，去尋找有相關需求的廠商及創作者" (strictly curate IP and vendor lists... find vendors and creators with relevant needs based on each month's theme).
  **Zero mentions of 評分 (scoring), 數據 (data), AI, or 演算法 (algorithms)** on the matchmaking page (evidence of absence).
  Sources: https://ipcp.io/ ; https://ipcp.io/matchmaking/ ; media profile https://www.techbang.com/posts/94654-ipcp

- **TAICCA 文策院 (Taiwan Creative Content Agency)** - the government layer.
  Its matchmaking offerings are IP Meetup, screenwriter matchmaking, comic-base consultations, and expo delegations (cooperation/matchmaking page: https://taicca.tw/resources/cooperation_matchmak/introduction - page is behind a Cloudflare JS wall to non-browser fetchers; offering names confirmed from the site's own navigation and search snippets, flagged accordingly).
  How its expo matchmaking actually works, from its own 2019 Shanghai wrap-up: TAICCA staff "主動與通路商、玩具或嬰童用品商、零售業、教育業、公關廣告業等業內人士，介紹及媒合其與參展品牌洽商" (proactively introduced and matched exhibitors with channel, toy/baby-goods, retail, education, and PR/ad industry contacts), arranged VIP visits (incl. Sanrio, Japan's CBLA, and Character Databank), and subsidized brand agents (品牌經紀人) to attend - human introductions throughout.
  Source: https://taicca.tw/article/4fa5d025 (retrieved via Wayback Machine; live page Cloudflare-blocked).
  Its 2026 Taiwan IP Expo in Malaysia is described as "品牌推廣與授權洽商雙軌策略" and a "一站式平台" with AR interaction and mascots - still no AI/data matching: https://www.cna.com.tw/news/acul/202605200388.aspx

- **TCBLA 台灣角色品牌授權協會 (Taiwan Character Brand Licensing Association)** - association collecting Taiwan original character brands, organizing overseas expo delegations with TAICCA (e.g. Korea character licensing fair).
  Site https://www.tcbla.org.tw/ was unreachable in this session (curl exit 6, DNS); mission claims taken from search snippets and CNA coverage - **flagged single-source**: https://www.cna.com.tw/postwrite/chi/407472

### d. The one "AI + 大數據" artifact - a 2019 expo booth, not a product

The only quantified-sounding thing in the entire Taiwan trail, verbatim from udn's news item of 2019-10-16 ("上海授權展Fresh Taiwan臺灣館主視覺亮相 文策院首創導入AI科技應用"):

> 文策院首度與臺灣科技大廠合作，將在展會現場架設AI智慧看板。藉由智慧科技，判斷來賓性別、年齡，主動導入圖像角色推薦技術；並透過人流追蹤及大數據系統，提供參展品牌更精準的目標客群統計分析。

(TAICCA partnered with an unnamed Taiwan tech company to install an AI smart signage at the expo that judges a visitor's gender and age to actively recommend image characters, plus foot-traffic tracking and a big-data system giving exhibitors more precise target-audience statistics.)

Classification: **demographic-triggered character display for booth passersby + visitor analytics for exhibitors.**
It scores nothing about an IP × product pairing and involves no licensee.
In this file's parent taxonomy (quantified-licensing-landscape.md §0) it is at best type (C) audience analytics, deployed as event marketing.
TAICCA's own post-event wrap-up (https://taicca.tw/article/4fa5d025) does not mention the signage at all, and no recurrence at later expos was found under the queries in §3.
The tech partner is unnamed in the source ("臺灣科技大廠") - **single-source, vendor/organizer-stated**.
Source: http://web.archive.org/web/20250513151757/http://ulicensing.udnfunlife.com/ (news item dated 2019-10-16 in the archived news list).

## 2. How fit is actually evaluated in Taiwan, per industry sources

The trade-association magazine 貿易雜誌 (IEAT, no.347) describes the standard evaluation path explicitly, and it is conversational:

> 被授權方只要與經紀代理商聊聊，即可了解一個IP與粉絲族群的樣貌，此時再與自家產品交叉分析

(A licensee just needs to *chat with the agent* to understand an IP and its fanbase, then cross-analyze against its own products.)

The same article advises budget-limited firms to "尋求經紀代理商進行推薦" (seek agent recommendations) to reduce risk.
No tool, score, or dataset is named anywhere in the piece.
Source: https://www.ieatpe.org.tw/magazine/ebook347/storypage03.html

Supporting ecosystem references (same picture, no quantification):
- The News Lens practitioner guide to IP licensing for small brands (risk checklist, negotiation advice): https://www.thenewslens.com/article/127274
- 益思科技法律事務所 on licensing-deal participants (licensor/licensee/agent roles): https://is-law.com/post/4/1491
- Mirror Media 2020 industry survey (agencies + government support, expo-driven): https://www.mirrormedia.mg/story/20200925insight013
