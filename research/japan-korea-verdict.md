# Japan / Korea adjacent - verdict with documented trail

**Seed question 4:** any quantified-fit offering beyond Yodo1's in-game IPverse?

**Researched:** 2026-07-15. ja and ko queries primary for this question (with a zh cross-check per task rules), English secondary.

---

## Verdict

**No Japan or Korea offering was found that quantifies IP x product / IP x brand fit for licensing, beyond Yodo1's IPverse (which is games-vertical, Beijing-run, and already classified as recommendation-from-history, not validated fit prediction - see `quantified-licensing-landscape.md`).**

What exists instead falls into three buckets:

1. **Marketplace / listing platforms** - Japan's CHARATARO (Sumitomo Corporation, PoC 2022, now apparently abandoned: the domain serves Chinese SEO spam as of this check) and Aeon Fantasy's キャラis; Korea's WitzShop (WITZ) and Innerboos. All connect parties and digitize transactions; none scores the pairing (§1, §3).
2. **IP-side popularity data sellers** - Japan's Character Databank (CharaBiz DATA annual, Monthly Ranking) and Video Research (Kids/ex character penetration survey). Survey-based awareness/popularity/ownership numbers that a brand can consult, with the pairing judgment left to humans (§2).
3. **Relationship brokering, including state-run** - KOCCA's Character Licensing Fair "비즈니스 커넥팅" is pre-scheduled 1:1 buyer meetings; KOCCA IP 사업화 상담회 is pitching support plus pre-matched meetings (§4).

The most telling artifact is Korean: **WITZ's own co-CEO 김정민, writing in I Love Character (2023-05-22), listed "IP 가치를 측정하는 서비스" (IP value measurement) and customized IP recommendation to licensees as services "출시를 앞두고 있다" (awaiting launch).**
Three years later, WITZ's live homepage sells transaction digitization ("창작자와 생산자, 디자이너와 유통사를 연결하는 라이선스 거래 플랫폼") and a voice-IP/publicity-rights pivot with ElevenLabs; its shipped AI is counterfeit detection (WitzGuard/BrandGuard), not fit scoring (§3).
This is the Japan/Korea parallel to JD's 高雷 future-tense admission in `china-platforms-verdict.md`.

**Confidence: HIGH** that the named platforms do not claim a productized fit score on their current surfaces (CHARATARO beta site read in full via Wayback; witz.ai homepage read directly; Innerboos and キャラis launch PRs read directly).
**MEDIUM** for the ecosystem-wide negative: Japan's licensing agency layer (ShoPro, Sony Creative Products, TMS, etc.) and Korea's in-house IP operators (IPX/Line Friends, Kakao) were not audited page-by-page this pass, so the claim is "not found under the queries in §6", not an exhaustive audit.
Single-source items flagged inline.

## 1. Japan - CHARATARO: the one real matching-platform attempt, and it stalled

**Who/what.**
CHARATARO (キャラタロウ) was a licensing-business matching platform from Sumitomo Corporation's internal venture program (0→1 Challenge 2021), beta developed by group DX company Insight Edge, PoC announced 2022-11-07.
Sources: https://www.sumitomocorp.com/ja/jp/news/topics/2022/group/20221107_2 (read directly), https://prtimes.jp/main/html/rd/p/000000815.000000726.html, https://insightedge.jp/news/media/221107_sc-news/.

**What it actually was (beta site read in full via Wayback, snapshot 2023-02-07).**
A listing/search marketplace: "WEB上で掲載ライセンサー・ライセンシーを一覧出来", "条件や希望商材／時期が可視化されており、自社の希望と合う相手を見つけ商談を申し込むことが出来ます".
Users filter a partner list by target, merchandise, and timing, then request meetings.
The beta page itself states "β版ではマッチング機能の一部のみが実装されています" and that the beta is a free, limited-period PoC.
No AI, index, score, or algorithmic matching is claimed anywhere on the page.
Source: http://web.archive.org/web/20230207060241id_/https://charataro.com/ (full text recovered).

**Current status (evidence of abandonment).**
Wayback CDX for charataro.com: 200s in 2022-11 and 2023-02, then only 301 redirects through 2024-2025, then a 200 in 2025-07.
The live charataro.com today serves a Chinese-language SEO-spam page (title "贵港市涵如科技有限公司" with movie keywords and obfuscated redirect JS) - the domain has lapsed to a squatter.
No Sumitomo follow-up PR about commercialization was found under the queries tried (§6 q4).
Classification: **marketplace PoC, apparently dead**; the strongest Japanese platform attempt never got past listing/filtering.
Single-source caveat: "no commercialization" is an absence claim at search level.

**Note the problem framing in Sumitomo's own PR:** licensing matching "依然として展示会や紹介などアナログな方法に頼っている" (still relies on analog methods - trade shows and introductions).
A major trading company diagnosed the same gap in 2022 and answered it with a directory, not a score.

## 2. Japan - the data layer is IP-side popularity, sold as reports and surveys

- **Character Databank / CharaBiz** (charabiz.com): Monthly Ranking of character-goods purchases (overall and by gender), quarterly CharaBiz+ magazine, and the annual CharaBiz DATA yearbook (market size, rankings by product category / sex-age / channel).
  This is the reference data vendor for the industry, and everything it sells is IP-side popularity and market structure, not pairing fit.
  Sources: https://www.charabiz.com/service_list.php?tab=news, https://www.charabiz.com/book_detail.php?tab=mem&article_id=13431, https://www.ssk21.co.jp/R0000103.php?gpage=05V0025 (read at search+page level).
- **Video Research Kids/ex キャラクター版**: survey of ~150 major characters measuring 浸透度 (penetration: awareness, popularity, ownership) among children 3-12 and their parents.
  The character analogue of their famous タレントイメージ調査 used for casting decisions - survey data consumed by humans choosing a character, not a fit score.
  Sources: https://vr-datacity.jp/?pid=169251672, https://www.videor.co.jp/press/2018/180123.html, https://www.videor.co.jp/digestplus/tv/2017/05/8512.html.
- **キャラis** (Aeon Fantasy, PR Times): "「キャラクターを描きたいクリエイター」と「キャラクターが欲しい人」をつなぐwebサービス" - a creator-to-company matching marketplace for commissioning original characters, adjacent to licensing but not it; no data/AI matching claim in the PR.
  Source: https://prtimes.jp/main/html/rd/p/000000845.000060567.html (read directly).
- Search-level flag (single-source, unverified): an AMBI recruiting feature mentions a company using "社内のAIツールやデータ" to scout "ネクストヒットIP" - internal tooling at an unnamed employer, not a sellable fit product.
  Source: https://en-ambi.com/featured/1625/ (not fetched; search summary only).

No ja query tried (§6) surfaced any 相性/適合診断 (fit diagnosis) service for character licensing.

## 3. Korea - platforms digitize the transaction, not the decision

- **WITZ (위츠, witz.ai / witzshop.com)** - founded 2022, POSTECH Holdings TIPS-backed, CES 2025 exhibitor.
  WitzShop is marketed as Korea's "국내 유일 One-Stop 라이선싱 계약 시스템" - digitized contracts through post-deal management.
  Its shipped AI is counterfeit detection (WitzGuard / BrandGuard) and, since 2025-2026, an ElevenLabs PoC to license voice actor 배한성's voice as AI TTS (a publicity-rights pivot confirmed by shareholder-meeting agenda items on the homepage adding 대중문화예술기획업 to the corporate purpose).
  The homepage tagline is connection language: "창작자와 생산자, 디자이너와 유통사를 연결하는 라이선스 거래 플랫폼".
  **The fit-scoring gap in their own words:** co-CEO 김정민's I Love Character column (2023-05-22) describes IP-value measurement and customized IP recommendation to licensees as services awaiting launch - future tense, and still not on the product surface today.
  Sources: https://witz.ai/ (read directly via curl), https://kr.aving.net/news/articleView.html?idxno=1796354, https://www.venturesquare.net/1040381, https://ilovecharacter.com/news/newsview.php?ncode=1065574542202454&dt=m (read directly).
- **Innerboos (이너부스)** - platform launched 2022-04-30: SaaS one-stop for "캐릭터IP와 이용기업의 매칭 뿐 아니라 계약 체결, IP 상품화 단계, 로열티 관리" - matching named as a feature, methodology unstated, with e-contracts, dashboards, and hologram authenticity certificates as the described substance.
  Marketplace/brokerage ops, no scoring claim.
  Single-source: one Money Today article.
  Source: https://www.mt.co.kr/future/2022/05/03/2022050311250916932 (read directly).
- **Story (PIP Labs)** - Layer-1 blockchain for programmable IP licensing (register, license, auto-distribute royalties via smart contracts; IP Graph provenance).
  Korean-founded and often raised in Korean coverage; it automates licensing *terms and execution*, and the coverage contains no evidence of IP-product fit quantification.
  Adjacent, not a competitor on the fit question.
  Source: https://byline.network/2025/10/1002-4/ (read directly).
- **IPVERSE (Kowloon)** - blockchain IP tokenization; already documented in `quantified-licensing-landscape.md` as a name collision with Yodo1's IPverse, not a fit-scoring player.

## 4. Korea - the state-run layer is explicit relationship brokering

- **KOCCA Character Licensing Fair (characterfair.kr)** - the flagship annual event (25th edition, 2026-07-16~19, COEX; MCST hosts, KOCCA co-organizes).
  Its "비즈니스 커넥팅" is described as 1:1 meetings between exhibitors and distribution-channel buyers, "온라인 사전 매칭을 통해 확정된 파트너 및 스케줄에 따라 현장 상담이 진행" - pre-scheduled introductions, i.e. the trade-show model Sumitomo's PR called analog.
  Sources: https://www.characterfair.kr/fairContents.do?FAIRMENU_IDX=17147&hl=KOR (search-level), https://www.etnews.com/20250717000207, https://www.etoday.co.kr/news/view/2602396.
- **KOCCA 콘텐츠 IP 사업화 상담회 / storyum.kr programs** - selected IPs get pitching-prep support (intro videos, directory books) and pre-matched business meetings with producers.
  Human matchmaking with subsidies, no quantification.
  Sources: https://www.storyum.kr/story/progrm/master/view.do?prgSn=563&listTp=StartN&menuNo=500004&siteId=5&inoutSe=01&pageIndex=5, https://jpn.kocca.kr/kocca/bbs/view/B0158919/1948161.do.

## 5. What this means for the pitch

- Japan and Korea confirm the pattern from Taiwan and China: where digitization exists, it targets **discovery (listings) and transaction plumbing (contracts, royalties, authenticity)**, never the fit decision.
- The two best pre-answered objections from this pass: (a) "Sumitomo built this" - no, CHARATARO was a filterable directory and the domain is now squatted; (b) "WITZ is an AI licensing platform" - its AI is counterfeit detection and voice synthesis, and its own co-CEO framed value-measurement/recommendation as future services in 2023.
- Yodo1's IPverse remains the only quantified-sounding offering in the region and it is (i) games-vertical, (ii) recommendation-from-history; the consumer-product fit segment stays open on this evidence.
