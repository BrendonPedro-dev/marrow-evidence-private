# Negosh's APAC Footprint

*Research file — factual findings only, no pitch interpretation. Interpretation lives in `/pitch/`.*

**Question addressed:** How real is Negosh's Japan operation, and does Negosh have any
presence elsewhere in APAC (Taiwan, Korea, China, Southeast Asia)? What best explains the
shape of the APAC play?

**One-line answer:** Negosh's only substantiated APAC presence is a Japan-only,
partner-dependent channel operated by InfoLens Inc. (Tokyo). Beyond Japan, no evidence of
any Negosh presence was found in TW, KR, CN, or SEA. No Japan deal volume is disclosed
anywhere; traction is **unknown**.

## 2. The InfoLens relationship — what it actually is

- **InfoLens Inc.** (株式会社InfoLens): Tokyo, Chuo-ku. Founded **October 2018**. CEO
  **Yasukawa Hiroshi (安川 洋)**. Core business: sanctioned import, manufacture and sale of
  gaming/character merchandise for the Japanese market; manages ~**500 product types**;
  runs **INFOLENS GEEK SHOP** (online + Ikebukuro Parco store). (Third News, July 2025;
  InfoLens site.) **Verified.**
- **Role for Negosh:** InfoLens is the *local operator/reseller* of Negosh in Japan — it
  provides the Japanese-language version, brings "unique Japanese IP and licensees into the
  network," and represents Negosh at Japanese trade shows. Negosh CEO **Ari Zebersky**:
  "With our partnership with Infolens, we are so excited to expand Japanese IP
  opportunities both inbound and outbound." InfoLens CEO Yasukawa: "We're excited to team
  up with Negosh to open doors for licensors and licensees in Japan." (BusinessWire /
  Licensing Letter, Mar 2024.) **Verified.**
- **Relationship status (as of this research):** The Japanese Negosh page on infolens.com
  appears actively maintained (free-to-register model described, sign-up flow present).
  But InfoLens's largest *disclosed* 2025 licensing action (Fallout™/Bethesda) makes **no
  reference to Negosh**, suggesting the Negosh channel is not InfoLens's primary licensing
  engine. This is an **inference from a documented absence**, not a stated fact.

## 6. Best-supported explanation for the shape of the APAC play

Stated as a ranked set of explanations grounded in the evidence above (this is
*explanation of observed facts*, not pitch framing):

1. **Partner-dependent go-to-market.** Negosh enters an APAC market only when a local
   partner operates it (InfoLens in Japan). It has no disclosed in-region entity or staff.
   Absent a partner for TW/KR/CN/SEA, there is no presence there. *(Best supported —
   directly matches the Japan pattern.)*
2. **Japan chosen for scale + a ready partner, not as step one of a broader APAC rollout.**
   All Japan activity clusters 2023–2024; no subsequent "next APAC market" announcement
   exists. The 2024–2025 corporate direction visibly shifts toward digital/AI (Spaceport,
   Made AI, negosh.ai) rather than APAC geographic expansion.
3. **Rights/language/relationship complexity of non-Japan APAC** (character IP licensing in
   CN/KR/TW involves different rights holders, languages, and often local platform
   incumbents) is a plausible *contributing* barrier but is **not evidenced** in any Negosh
   statement → offered as hypothesis only.

**What is NOT claimed:** that Negosh has decided to avoid APAC-beyond-Japan, or that its
Japan operation has failed. Neither is supported; both are unknown.
