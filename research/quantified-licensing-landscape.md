# The Quantified-Licensing Landscape

*Research file — factual findings only, no pitch interpretation. Interpretation lives in `/pitch/`.*

**Question addressed:** Who, anywhere, scores or predicts IP × partner/product *fit* with
data? For each player found, what is actually quantified, in which vertical, in which
region, at what price, since when — and how good is the evidence?

**One-line answer:** Across licensing-ops SaaS, games platforms, IP-valuation firms, East
Asian character-data holders, and the influencer-marketing adjacency, the market quantifies
three *different* things that are easy to conflate: **(A) pre-deal FIT PREDICTION**, **(B)
post-deal ROYALTY/RIGHTS ADMIN**, and **(C) POPULARITY / brand-equity RANKING**. Almost
everything found is (B) or (C). The nearest things to (A) — Yodo1's IPverse (games) and
Sprout Social's "Brand Fit Score" (influencers) — are **recommendation-from-history** and
**alignment scoring**, not validated outcome prediction, and neither covers character ×
physical consumer-product licensing. No player was found that predicts fit for that segment
(see `consumer-product-gap.md` for the explicit gap check).

---

## 0. The three claim-types (read this first)

These are **different claims** and must never be merged:

- **(A) FIT PREDICTION / matching** — scores or predicts how well a *specific* IP and a
  *specific* partner/product will perform *together*, **before** a deal is signed.
- **(B) ROYALTY / RIGHTS / APPROVAL ADMIN** — contract, rights, royalty tracking, product
  approvals, DAM. All **post-deal** operations.
- **(C) POPULARITY / brand-equity RANKING** — measures an IP's *standalone* recognition,
  favorability, social buzz, or demand. An **input** a buyer might use, not a pairing score.

A tool can quantify (C) or run (B) and still do **zero** (A). Marketing copy routinely
blurs (A) into (C) ("eliminate guesswork," "find the right partner"); this file grades on
mechanism, not slogan.

## 2. Fit prediction (A) — the honest read on the closest candidates

Nobody found ships a *validated* pre-deal fit-prediction model. The closest are:

- **Yodo1 IPverse (games).** Verified (2-src): unveiled at **ChinaJoy 2025** (Shanghai, late
  Jul 2025), **free** public launch **~late Jan 2026**, run by Yodo1 (**Beijing** HQ — not
  Shanghai; the Shanghai dateline is the ChinaJoy venue). Scale claims match the **Jan 2026**
  vintage (~80k in-game events / ~20k brands / ~15k games); the brand count grew from
  ~15k at ChinaJoy to ~20k at launch in six months, so treat the round numbers as
  marketing-rounded and dated. Features verified: **Dynamic IP Rankings** (weekly social
  deltas), **quadrant analysis** (brand-activation vs social-engagement), **AI matchmaking**,
  and **DeepMatch** (structured proposal in ~30 min). *Mechanism finding:* what is actually
  quantified is **popularity + historical-collaboration patterns + recommendation**, not an
  empirically validated "this game × this IP will succeed" forecast. No public methodology or
  accuracy validation exists → the "fit prediction" framing is an **unverified marketing
  claim over a recommendation engine**. ("Agent Y," announced at ChinaJoy as coming-soon,
  may or may not be the same feature as DeepMatch — **UNVERIFIED / ambiguous**.) Vertical is
  **in-game / digital collabs**, not physical consumer products.
- **Sprout Social "Brand Fit Score" (influencers).** Verified (2-src): a distinct, named,
  AI feature announced **Apr 14 2025** (a rebuild of **Tagger Media**, which Sprout acquired
  **Aug 2 2023 for $140M**). Sprout's own press release defines it as an *"instant assessment
  of how well a creator's content aligns with a brand's social presence,"* scoring a creator's
  content-theme ↔ audience-interest **alignment** plus audience location, with creator
  selection framed as driven by **data rather than guesswork**. (The catchier phrasing
  *"based on data, not gut feelings"* could **not** be verified as verbatim Sprout copy — a
  competitor, **Modash**, publicly notes *"it's unclear how they measure this number (and
  whether it's reliable)"* — so do not quote the slogan.) *Mechanism finding:* it is a genuine
  **configurable alignment score**, but a rules/alignment score,
  **not** a validated predictive model of campaign outcome; no accuracy validation is
  published. Vertical is **influencer marketing**, not IP licensing.
- **DoLicense** claims ML matching of brands↔licensees (true type-A positioning) but its only
  sources are a **mutually-affiliated vendor cluster** (the product site, its build agency
  **DATAC**, and its CMS vendor **Directus**) — all commercially tied, so not independent
  corroboration. Its self-reported metrics (60% faster partner discovery, 40% fewer disputes)
  and lone named customer ("U.S. Polo Assn.") are unaudited; no press, funding, or third-party
  validation found → **UNVERIFIED**; do not treat as an established product.

**Everything else labeled "A-adjacent"** (Klear/Meltwater Brand Score, Traackr, HypeAuditor,
GRIN, Alifish) is either a **user-weighted match calculator**, an **observed-behavior
affinity attribute**, or **attribute-based partner-finding** — useful inputs, none a
validated pairing-outcome predictor.

## 3. Royalty / rights / approval admin (B) — the incumbent core

The established consumer-product **licensing-ops** platforms are, without exception found,
type **B**: Flowhaven, Octane5 BrandComply, Brainbase Assist, Dependable Solutions,
Xelacore/Fabacus, MyMediabox, RoyaltyZone. Their marketed scope is agreements, rights,
royalty tracking/forecasting, product approvals, DAM, and (Xelacore) product-data
governance / Digital Product Passport. **None markets a *validated pre-deal fit-score*.** For
**Flowhaven** this is **evidence of absence** (the vendor's own platform page was read and no
fit/matchmaking feature is present). **Brainbase is a precise partial exception:** its
**Marketplace** product does offer partner **discovery, curated/AI recommendations, and
similarity-based matchmaking** (marketing describes recommending *"manufacturers who have
worked with similar partners"* and ~300 manually-paired sellers/buyers) — but **no validated
pre-deal fit *score*** predicting how a specific IP × specific partner will perform, the same
recommendation-vs-prediction line that separates Yodo1 IPverse and Alifish from true type-A.
For the remaining type-B tools it is **"no evidence found"** at search level, not an
exhaustive page-by-page audit.

Two structural notes: **Brainbase was acquired by Jonas Software (Sep 16 2022)**, which also
owns **MyMediabox (since 2012)** — two list entries now share a corporate parent. **Pricing
is not public** for any incumbent (all enterprise-quote).

**Adjacent B not in scope:** patent/tech-licensing AI tools (ArcPrime, XLScout) "score
licensees," but that is patent monetization, a different domain from consumer-product brand
licensing — noted for completeness only.

## 4. Popularity / brand-equity ranking (C) — the other thing that gets called "data"

- **CharaBiz / Character Databank** (Tokyo, co. founded **Jul 2000**): the **Character
  Power survey** measures a character's standalone **recognition / favorability / image**
  (ages 3–65, online; founder Kazuo Rikukawa) at **¥50,000/character** for members,
  **¥100,000** non-members; the annual **CharaBiz DATA** book (non-member: electronic ¥50k /
  print ¥38k; members ~10% off) reports category/gender/age rankings and market size (e.g.,
  FY2019 character-merch retail **¥1.55tn**, 101.1% YoY). This is **popularity ranking**, not
  pairing fit — a due-diligence input at most.
- **Parrot Analytics** DEMAND360 / "Brand Affinity": global content-**demand** ranking, with
  a "demand-based brand matching" use case (e.g., fantasy-series fans over-index for premium
  coffee). On direct read it functions as **category selection + launch timing**, not a
  formal per-partner fit score.
- **Sanrio** runs its famous annual fan-vote character ranking (internal, type C). Chinese
  潮玩/market-research reports (LeadLeo, CBNData, KPMG) supply IP-category performance data —
  market research, not per-pairing prediction.

## 5. IP-valuation firms — value in isolation, not fit

**Brand Finance** (Royalty Relief method + ISO-10668 Brand Strength Index, MASB-certified),
**Intangible Business**, **RoyaltyRange / RoyaltyStat (Exactera) / RoyaltySource**, and
**Kroll** all quantify **brand/IP value or an arm's-length royalty rate in isolation**
(income / royalty-relief / market-comparable methods). **None predicts the fit of a specific
IP against a specific partner.** That is a genuinely different category from type-A fit
prediction and should not be cited as a fit competitor.

## 8. What is quantified where — the one-paragraph synthesis

Pre-deal **fit prediction** exists only as (i) a *recommendation-from-history* engine in the
**games/in-game** vertical (IPverse) and (ii) an *alignment score* in the **influencer**
vertical (Sprout Brand Fit Score) — both unvalidated as outcome predictors, neither in
physical consumer-product character licensing. The **consumer-product licensing-ops** stack
is uniformly **post-deal admin (B)**. The **character-data** and **valuation** firms sell
**popularity/brand-equity or isolated value (C / valuation)**. **APAC** adds attribute-based
matchmaking (Alifish) and directories, but no fit predictor. The recurring pattern — clearest
in influencer marketing, with **academic congruence-scoring frameworks** (e.g. Semerádová &
Weinlich 2024, Jaccard-similarity congruence) behind it — is that fit gets quantified once
the underlying interaction data is **digital**. That pattern's application to physical
character licensing is what remains unoccupied.
