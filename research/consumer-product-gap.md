# The Consumer-Product Gap Check

*Research file — factual findings only, no pitch interpretation. Interpretation lives in `/pitch/`.*

**Question addressed (research question 3):** Does *anyone*, anywhere, quantify **fit** for
**character × physical consumer-product** licensing specifically — i.e. score or predict, with
data, how well a given character IP and a given physical product/licensee will perform
*together*, *before* the deal is signed? Every candidate found is listed below with the reason
it does or does not count, followed by the search paths tried so the absence claim is
defensible.

**One-line answer:** **No player was found that quantifies pre-deal fit for character ×
physical consumer-product licensing.** This is a **strong "no evidence found," approaching
evidence of absence** for the incumbent categories whose own product surfaces were read
directly (licensing-ops SaaS), and a plain **"no evidence found"** for the categories covered
only at search level (APAC directories, agency in-house tools). The two nearest analogues in
*other* verticals — Yodo1 IPverse (in-game) and Sprout's Brand Fit Score (influencers) — are
covered in `quantified-licensing-landscape.md`; both fail this test on **vertical** (neither is
physical consumer product) *and* on **mechanism** (recommendation-from-history / alignment
score, not a validated pairing-outcome predictor).

---

## 1. The strict test (what "counts")

A candidate counts as quantified fit for this segment only if it meets **all four**:

1. **FIT** — scores a *pairing* (IP × partner/product), not either side in isolation. A
   standalone popularity/awareness score (type C) or an isolated brand valuation does **not**
   count.
2. **PRE-DEAL** — usable *before* signing, to inform the choice. Post-deal royalty/approval
   admin (type B) and post-launch sell-through reporting do **not** count.
3. **PHYSICAL CONSUMER PRODUCT** — the licensed output is a physical good (toy, apparel, F&B,
   stationery, homeware). In-game skins, influencer campaigns, and content-demand rankings are
   different verticals.
4. **CHARACTER / ENTERTAINMENT IP** — the licensor side is character/entertainment IP, the
   PBC target segment (not patents, not corporate trademarks in the abstract).

Types (A) fit-prediction / (B) royalty-admin / (C) popularity-ranking are defined in
`quantified-licensing-landscape.md §0`. Only **type A, in this vertical** clears the bar.

## 2. Candidates considered — and why each falls short

Grouped by the reason they fail. "Fails on N" refers to the four test criteria above.

### 2a. Right vertical, wrong function — the licensing-ops SaaS (fails on 1 + 2)
**Flowhaven, Octane5 BrandComply, Brainbase Assist, Dependable Solutions, Xelacore/Fabacus,
MyMediabox, RoyaltyZone.** These *are* character × physical consumer-product tools (criteria 3
+ 4 met), and they are the incumbent core of the segment. But every one is **type B**:
agreements, rights, royalty tracking/forecasting, product approvals, DAM, product-data
governance — all **post-deal administration**. None markets a pre-deal fit-scoring or
matchmaking engine.
- **Evidence of absence** (vendor's own page/announcement read directly, no fit feature
  present): **Flowhaven** (platform page) and **Brainbase** (acquisition scope).
- **"No evidence found"** (search-level, not exhaustive page audit): Octane5, Dependable,
  Xelacore, MyMediabox, RoyaltyZone.
- Note the consolidation: Brainbase (Jonas Software, acq. 2022) and MyMediabox (Jonas since
  2012) share a parent — two nominal entries, one owner — so the "field" is narrower than the
  list length implies.

### 2b. Right function, wrong vertical — fit prediction that exists elsewhere (fails on 3)
- **Yodo1 IPverse** — real matchmaking/recommendation + quadrant analysis, but **in-game IP
  collabs**, digital. Also mechanism-weak (recommendation-from-history + popularity, no
  validated outcome model). Fails 3, and arguably 1.
- **Sprout Social "Brand Fit Score," Klear/Meltwater, Traackr, HypeAuditor, GRIN "Gia,"
  LTK Match.AI, Skeepers** — creator/audience alignment or affinity scoring in **influencer
  marketing**. The closest *cultural* precedent for "price on fit, not fame," but the licensed
  output is a **social campaign, not a physical product**. Fails 3. (LTK Match.AI and Skeepers
  are additional creator↔brand matchers surfaced this iteration; same disposition as the
  others.)

### 2c. Right vertical, wrong object — popularity/awareness ranking (fails on 1)
- **CharaBiz / Character Databank Character Power survey** — measures a character's *standalone*
  recognition / favorability / image (¥50k member / ¥100k non-member per character, per
  `quantified-licensing-landscape.md`). A due-diligence **input**, not a pairing score. Fails 1.
- **Sanrio annual fan-vote ranking; Parrot Analytics DEMAND360 "Brand Affinity"; Chinese
  潮玩/market-research reports (LeadLeo, CBNData, KPMG).** All measure IP or category demand in
  isolation. Fail 1.

### 2d. Right region, wrong function — APAC matchmaking & directories (fails on 1 or 2)
- **Alibaba Alifish (阿里鱼)** — the strongest APAC candidate: "big-data matchmaking" of
  licensors ↔ licensees on attributes (positioning / fan tier / price) plus post-hoc sales
  tracking and auto-royalty. But it is **attribute-based licensee *discovery* + post-deal
  analytics**, not a pre-deal success *forecast* for a specific pairing. Fails 1/2 on the
  strict test (it's discovery + type-B admin, marketed with matchmaking language).
- **Tencent T-JOY, KOCCA WelCon / Licensing Con, Licensing Japan / JETRO matchmaking, Tao IP
  Library (淘IP库, UNVERIFIED), IP365.cc (UNVERIFIED), Taiwan IP Showcase / TAICCA / TCBLA.**
  Human 1:1 buyer-matching, program support, and searchable directories — **directory /
  matchmaking-by-attribute (type d)**, no quantified pairing prediction. Fail 1.
- **Taiwan specifically:** ecosystem is showcase/directory + government support (TAICCA, TCBLA,
  CCPA "Taiwan IP Showcase," UDN, Taiwan Bar). **No Taiwan platform found that quantifies IP ×
  product fit** — "no evidence found," multiple org pages checked.

### 2e. Value / royalty benchmarking — not fit at all (fails on 1)
- **Brand Finance (Royalty Relief), Intangible Business, RoyaltyRange / RoyaltyStat (Exactera)
  / RoyaltySource, Kroll.** Quantify **brand/IP value or an arm's-length royalty rate in
  isolation**. Genuinely different category; must **not** be cited as fit competitors. Fail 1.

### 2f. Retail demand/trend forecasting — right data, wrong decision (fails on 1 + 2 + 4)
This is the most important *counter* to check, because it looks quantitative and touches
physical products (detailed further in `data-availability.md`):
- **Target "Trend Brain"** (gen-AI trend tool, previewed Oct 2025; by Dec 2025 Target reported
  it in active use in apparel, e.g. reading swimwear silhouettes/prints ahead of the season) —
  predicts colors / materials / silhouettes for Target's **own owned-brands** (Wild Fable,
  Universal Thread), a design/assortment decision, not licensing pairings.
- **AI merchandise-planning / demand-forecasting vendors** (Increff, Toolio, Blue Yonder-class
  tools) — forecast SKU/store-level **sell-through and assortment** from historical sales.
- **Why none counts:** these predict *demand for a product a retailer already decided to
  carry*, using a retailer's own POS history — a **buying/replenishment** decision, **inside**
  one company, **after** the property is chosen. They do **not** score which *IP × licensee
  pairing* to sign. They fail criterion 1 (not a pairing score), 2 (post-selection), and
  typically 4 (private-label, not character-IP). They prove the *data and modeling capability
  exist*; they do **not** occupy the pre-deal fit decision.

### 2g. Claimed-but-unverified type-A players (cannot be counted)
- **DoLicense (dolicense.com)** — claims ML brand↔licensee recommendations (true type-A
  *positioning*), but is **single-source vendor marketing** with no independent coverage,
  named customers, methodology, or validation → **UNVERIFIED**. Even at face value it targets
  brand/creator licensing generically, not character × physical product; cannot be counted as
  a filled gap.

### 2h. Pre-deal AI *scoring* in licensing — but of applicant quality, not pairing fit (fails on 1)
- **Sidechat IP (ip.side.host, "Sidechat for IP Licensing," at Licensing Expo 2026; a
  Spaceport Technologies product — corporate identity since CONFIRMED, see
  `sidechat-ip-verdict.md`; product claims still vendor-described)** — the
  single closest *surface-level* counter to this file's whole thesis, because it markets an
  **AI "deal intake scoring"** feature that runs **before** signing: inbound license requests
  are captured, AI-assessed, and **ranked by "deal quality"** in ~60 seconds. Crucially, the
  page says it scores **revenue history, IP compliance, and completeness** of the *applicant* —
  i.e. it triages which inbound licensee leads are strongest/most legitimate, **not** whether a
  given character IP and a given product will *perform well together*. The rest of the product
  (deal/royalty monitoring across 20+ licensees, marketplace infringement scanning, contract
  drafting, pipeline CRM) is standard **type-B** admin. **Disposition:** it clears criterion 2
  (pre-deal) but **fails criterion 1** — it scores *applicant deal-quality*, not an *IP × product
  pairing outcome*. It is the best evidence that "AI scoring at the pre-deal moment in
  licensing" is now being marketed (2026), and simultaneously that even this newest entrant
  scores the **wrong object** for the PBC wedge. Claims are vendor-stated only (no independent
  coverage, named customers, or traction) → **UNVERIFIED**; note also the name collision with
  the unrelated "Sidechat" anonymous-campus app (sidechat.lol only — correction 2026-07-15:
  side.chat is Spaceport's own Sidechat "AI employee" product, not the campus app; see
  `sidechat-ip-verdict.md §4`).

## 3. Distinguishing "no evidence found" from "evidence of absence"

| Category | Coverage depth | Claim strength |
|---|---|---|
| Licensing-ops SaaS (Flowhaven, Brainbase) | Vendor product page / acquisition scope **read directly** | **Evidence of absence** — the feature is affirmatively not on the surface |
| Pre-deal AI scoring in licensing (Sidechat IP) | Vendor page **read directly** (§2h) | **Evidence of absence** for *pairing-fit* prediction — the AI scores applicant deal-quality/compliance, not IP×product fit |
| Other licensing-ops SaaS (Octane5, Dependable, Xelacore, MyMediabox, RoyaltyZone) | Search-level + marketing scan, no exhaustive page audit | **No evidence found** |
| APAC directories / agency matchmaking / Taiwan | Multiple org pages + search sweeps | **No evidence found** (strong) |
| Agency *in-house / unreleased* predictive tools | Not publicly documented by definition | **Absence of public evidence** — a private tool could exist and be undisclosed; cannot be ruled out |
| Outcome-validation of the near-analogues' scores | Searched for published accuracy studies | **No evidence found** — no vendor discloses predictive validation; not the same as the feature being absent |

The honest composite: **for the exact segment (pre-deal fit prediction, character × physical
consumer product), no product was found in any category.** The strongest single sentence is
therefore *"no player was found that does this,"* backed by direct evidence of absence in the
incumbent SaaS category and broad no-evidence-found elsewhere — **not** an absolute "no such
tool exists anywhere," which desk research cannot prove.

## 5. What the gap actually is (one paragraph, factual)

The segment **character × physical consumer-product licensing** is served by a mature **type-B
administrative** software stack and a set of **type-C popularity** and **valuation** data
products, plus **directory/attribute matchmaking** in APAC. Pre-deal **fit prediction (type A)**
appears only in **adjacent digital-native verticals** — in-game collabs (IPverse) and
influencer marketing (Sprout et al.) — and even there as unvalidated recommendation/alignment
rather than proven outcome forecasting. Retail demand-forecasting AI demonstrates the *modeling
capability* on physical-product data but is aimed at a *different, post-selection buying
decision inside a single retailer*. Across every category checked, **no product was found that
scores an IP × physical-product/licensee pairing before the deal.** Whether that space is empty
because it is *hard* (physical performance data is fragmented and post-hoc, per
`data-availability.md`) or merely *not yet built* is a question this file does not resolve — it
only documents that, as of the date accessed, the space is unoccupied by any player found.
