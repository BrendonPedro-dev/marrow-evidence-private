# Academic / consulting licensing-fit analytics - verdict with documented trail

**Seed question 5:** does anyone sell licensing-fit analytics to brands (reports, scoring services) that a VC could name as "this exists"?

**Researched:** 2026-07-15. zh-CN and zh-TW queries primary per task rules, English secondary (this question is inherently English-heavy on the consulting side).

---

## Verdict

**Yes, a VC can name things - but every nameable offering quantifies something other than validated IP x product licensing fit, and the pitch should pre-answer them by name rather than claim a void.**

What is actually sold falls into four buckets:

1. **Qualitative consulting "brand fit assessment"** - US licensing consultancies (Nevium, Brandar, Valen Group) list fit assessment as a service, delivered as expert analysis and reports, with no algorithm, score product, or validation claimed (§1).
2. **IP-side popularity data reports** - Q Scores' Cartoon Q (600+ characters, familiarity/appeal, semiannual) is the canonical US example; buyers use it to pick a popular character, and the pairing judgment stays human (§2).
3. **Generic quantified concept testing** - Kantar (Concept eValuate) and Ipsos (Innotest) will survey-test any product concept, including a licensed one, per-case; it is innovation tooling, not a licensing-fit service, and nothing is licensing-specific (§3).
4. **China marketing-measurement vendors** - the significant find of this pass: **秒针系统 (Miaozhen Systems) sells an "IPPI" IP-selection scoring model that is explicitly brand-relative** (品类和品牌相关度以及对品牌助力程度), inside an IP联名 (marketing collab) solution.
   This is the closest thing to a sold pairing-fit score found in the entire five-question sweep - but it targets 联名 marketing campaigns (co-branding buzz), is delivered as measurement-vendor consulting plus dashboards, and publishes no outcome validation (§4).

**No offering was found - academic or commercial, zh or en - that sells a validated, productized prediction of whether a specific IP on a specific physical product will succeed commercially.**
Academic literature has fit/congruence constructs (brand extension, sponsorship) measured per-study via consumer surveys, and its own 2023 systematic review calls licensing research fragmented with "sounder empirical insights" still on the agenda (§5).

**Confidence: HIGH** on the characterization of every named vendor (Nevium, Brandar, Miaozhen pages read directly; Q Scores homepage read directly with the Cartoon Q detail page 500-erroring, so Cartoon Q specifics rest on its own 2014 PR plus trade press).
**MEDIUM** on the ecosystem-wide negative: consulting is a long-tail industry and this is "not found under the queries in §6", not an exhaustive audit of every boutique.
Single-source items flagged inline.

## 1. US consulting: "brand fit assessment" is sold, as judgment

- **Nevium Intellectual Property Consultants** (nevium.com) is the strongest-sounding candidate on paper: it offers to "Analyze your corporate brand and related brand assets and identify best-fit product categories" and has a named **"Brand Score Tool"** that "identifies the strengths and weaknesses of your brand which allows comparisons between different brands used with similar products".
  Read directly: the twelve listed services are consulting analysis (comparable case studies, demographic analysis, price-premium scoring of comparables, financial forecasts, counter-party risk assessment).
  No algorithm, no productized score, no validation claim; the Brand Score Tool is a comparison framework inside an engagement.
  Focus skews celebrity/endorsement licensing.
  Source: https://nevium.com/ip-monetization/licensing-partner-analysis/ (read directly).
- **Brandar Consulting** (brandar.com) lists "Licensing Category Target Analysis and Brand Fit Assessment" and "Brand Extension Ideation" among its services, alongside royalty revenue projection modeling and licensee due diligence.
  Read directly: purely advisory; no data platform, scoring methodology, or analytics claims anywhere on the page.
  Sources: https://brandar.com/brand-licensing, https://brandar.com/ (read directly).
- **Valen Group** (valengroup.com) is a Top-25 licensing agency/consultancy selling strategy via a trademarked "Agile Methodology" (iterative sprints, market insights); its public copy claims data-driven methods but names no fit-scoring product.
  Sources: https://www.valengroup.com/brand-licensing/, https://www.valengroup.com/brand-licensing/brand-licensing-consulting/ (search-level). **1-src (own site) flag.**
- The Brand Liaison and Falkenstein Brand Licensing surfaced in the same search as advisory services (licensee qualification, negotiation prep); nothing quantified claimed. **Search-level only.**

Classification: this bucket is the consulting analogue of relationship brokering - fit is assessed by experts and delivered as a report, which is precisely the manual practice the pitch proposes to quantify.

## 2. IP-side popularity data reports: Q Scores' Cartoon Q

- **Marketing Evaluations / Q Scores** (qscores.com, since 1963) sells **Cartoon Q**: recognition and appeal ("Q Score") of **600+ characters and licensed properties**, surveyed among ~1,800 consumers (kids 6-11, teens 12-17, parents, adults 18+), released **every six months**.
  Trade usage is explicit: "If a company is looking to license a cartoon character on a lunch box... buying the latest character Q Score data could help it select a winner" - i.e. the brand buys IP-side appeal data and makes the pairing call itself.
  The homepage (read directly) confirms Character Q as a product for "characters, intellectual properties and brands" and offers no character-to-product-category fit or matching service.
  Sources: https://www.qscores.com/ (read directly), https://prweb.com/releases/q-scores/cartoonq/prweb11941996.htm (Cartoon Q Spring 2014 PR), https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Q_Score, https://kidscreen.com/1996/10/01/17324-19961001/, http://sales.garfield.com/q_study.html (a licensor citing its own Q study to sell licenses).
  Note: https://qscores.com/home/CartoonQ.aspx returned HTTP 500 this session; Cartoon Q specifics therefore rest on the vendor's PR and trade press, not a live product page. **Flagged.**
- This is the US counterpart of Japan's CharaBiz DATA and Video Research Kids/ex (see `japan-korea-verdict.md` §2): survey-based, IP-side, humans do the matching.

## 3. Generic concept testing: quantified, per-case, not licensing-specific

- **Kantar Concept eValuate** (kantar.com/marketplace) and **Ipsos Innotest / product testing** sell survey-based concept screening with success-potential metrics (Kantar's Meaningfully-Different framework; Ipsos' Relevance/Expensiveness/Differentiation "Trial Index").
  A brand could run a specific licensed-product concept ("character X toaster") through these and get numbers - so the honest framing is "per-case survey testing exists; a licensing-fit analytics layer does not".
  Neither vendor markets a licensing- or IP-specific fit product under the queries tried.
  Sources: https://www.kantar.com/marketplace/solutions/innovation-and-product-development/concept-testing, https://www.ipsos.com/en-us/solutions/innovation/product-testing (search-level).
- Brand/IP valuation and royalty-rate firms (Brand Finance, Interbrand, GYbrand, World Brand Lab, RoyaltyRange/RoyaltyStat/RoyaltySource, Kroll) price the IP in isolation and are already classified in `quantified-licensing-landscape.md` §5 - value, not fit.
  zh-CN searches confirm the same firms dominate the 品牌价值评估 conversation with 成本法/市场法/收益法 methods.
  Sources: https://www.woshipm.com/operate/4014029.html, https://www.ipzhihu.com/NewsDetail/3472389.html (search-level).

## 4. China marketing-measurement vendors: the Miaozhen IPPI find

- **秒针系统 (Miaozhen Systems)** - China's major ad/marketing measurement firm - sells an **IP marketing solution** covering 选 (selection), 评 (evaluation), content, and placement, built on a proprietary IP database (**8,000+ IPs, 130 subcategories, 12 major categories**).
  Its selection step uses the **IPPI 打分模型 (IP Potential Index scoring model)** across three verbatim dimensions: **社交影响 (social influence), 品牌助力 (brand assistance), 潜力预估 (potential forecast)**.
  Critically, the evaluation is **brand-relative, not generic**: it measures "IP的潜力与适配度" (the IP's potential and fit) considering "品类和品牌相关度以及对品牌助力程度" (category relevance, brand relevance, degree of助力 to the brand).
  Post-campaign, it sells 联名 effect evaluation (brand heat, image, conversion, audience penetration).
  Delivery is consulting plus dashboards (魔方Pro social monitoring, 小明助理 LLM assistant); **no outcome-prediction validation, accuracy rate, or named methodology beyond "实战验证过" (battle-tested) is published**.
  Sources: https://www.miaozhen.com/marticles/5577.html (solution + IPPI detail, 2024-04-22, read directly), https://www.miaozhen.com/marticles/11137.html (Jellycat/LABUBU "IP力" article, read directly), https://www.miaozhen.com/tag/ip%E8%81%94%E5%90%8D.
  **Classification: the closest sold analogue to pairing-fit scoring found anywhere in this research - but its object is 联名 marketing campaigns (short-cycle co-branding buzz), not licensing programs putting an IP on physical product lines, and it is an input-scoring model (social data), not a validated success predictor. 1-src (vendor) on all capability claims; no independent review of IPPI found under the queries tried.**
- **数说故事 (DataStory)** (datastory.com.cn) - social big-data vendor (500+ enterprise clients) whose 数说雷达 platform identifies trending IPs/topics in real time and ranks them, with IP联名 case-analysis content marketing on top.
  Heat-ranking and audience overlap for IP shortlisting; no claimed pairing-fit score.
  Sources: https://datastory.com.cn/, https://www.datastory.com.cn/product (search-level), https://blog.csdn.net/datastory1/article/details/140874564.
- **艺恩 (Endata)** - film/TV data firm selling content-IP evaluation for adaptation/investment decisions (script, IP改编, concept, trailer testing) and celebrity/content value assessment for brand marketing.
  IP-side and campaign-side; nothing on brand x IP product pairing.
  Sources: https://www.endata.com.cn/solution/digitalEnt.html?tags=3, https://www.endata.com.cn/ (search-level).
- CBNData / LeadLeo / 21财经-cited rankings publish IP联名 market reports (e.g. 115 food-and-beverage collabs in Q1 2025) - market research, already classified as type C in the landscape file.
  Sources: https://www.cbndata.com/information/293807, https://m.21jingji.com/article/20240823/herald/fc7a5045e95f1aee0869f8290b9877e9.html.

## 5. Academic: constructs exist, no commercial service

- The 2023 systematic review of licensing research (Journal of Business Research, "From formation to performance outcomes: a review and agenda for licensing research", 105 papers 2000-2018) describes the field as "more limited and fragmented" than franchising research and lists refined performance criteria and "sounder empirical insights" as *future* agenda items - a citable academic admission that validated licensing-outcome prediction does not exist even in the literature.
  Source: https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0148296323005404.
- Fit/congruence is heavily theorized in adjacent fields - brand extension ("Dimensions of brand-extension fit", IJRM 2021: https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0167811621000811), sponsorship congruence (https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/full/10.1177/14413582241240077, https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S016781161300089X), licensed brand extensions (LSU dissertation: https://repository.lsu.edu/cgi/viewcontent.cgi?article=5349&context=gradschool_dissertations).
  All measure perceived fit via per-study consumer surveys; none is commercialized as a scoring service.
  The influencer-vertical academic congruence-scoring precedent (Semerádová & Weinlich 2024) is already in `quantified-licensing-landscape.md` §8.
- **Not found under any query tried: a professor-founded or university-spun licensing-fit analytics vendor.**
- 北京大学文化产业研究院 hosts film-IP valuation commentary (邱章红, https://www.ici.pku.edu.cn/xzyj/xzgc/1365077.htm) - valuation again, not fit. zh-side academic search surfaced nothing closer.

## 8. What this means for the pitch

- If an associate says "consultants already sell fit assessment": true, and it is judgment-in-a-report (Nevium's own copy is analysis language; Brandar lists no data capability) - the existence of a paid manual version is demand validation, not a competing product.
- If an associate says "Q Scores exists": it prices the IP's appeal, not the pairing; the lunch-box example in its own trade coverage shows the buyer still makes the fit call.
- If an associate names Miaozhen IPPI (the sharpest possible objection from the China side): concede it cheerfully and differentiate - marketing-collab buzz scoring from social data, vendor-claimed, unvalidated, and aimed at campaign selection, not physical-product licensing programs with sales outcomes.
  It also proves a measurement giant sees brand-relative IP scoring as sellable - demand validation from the data side.
