## Honest confidence line per finding

**Finding 3 (gut-feel selection): corroborated, MEDIUM-HIGH.**
Six instances across four languages agree that fame, decision-maker preference (眼缘/喜愛), relationships, and herd behavior are the operative selection criteria, and two incumbents (JD, WITZ) confirm no data-supported alternative exists.
Cap: every voice is an incumbent, agency, or industry researcher describing brands; no named brand manager describing their own process first-person was found.

**Finding 2 (attribution failure): corroborated, MEDIUM.**
An industry-association conference agenda lists collab-effect evaluation as an unsolved licensee pain point, two media analyses state no standard beyond exposure exists, and the 2025 tea-sector pullback (HEYTEA 37-39 collabs to zero, top-10 brand collab counts down 41.2%) is documented with named brands.
Caps: no first-person "we could not attribute it" quote, zero zh-TW instances despite zh-TW being the primary target language, and the documented pullback is multi-causal (fatigue, repositioning, diminishing returns), not cleanly a measurement-failure story.

**Finding 1 (brand erasure): partially corroborated, MEDIUM.**
The mechanism is publicly documented in behavioral and financial form: trade media warn that collab budgets become 为IP打工 (working for the IP), reported consumer behavior treats the brand's own product as the disposable part of its collab (separate packaging, proxy-drinking, merch resold at near-set price), and one IP fronting four brands simultaneously makes the merch, not the brand, the purchase decider.
Caps: the literal recall claim from our interviews ("consumers remembered the IP, not us") has zero direct public instances and rests on inference from behavior; the interview idioms 反客為主/為人作嫁 appear in no public post-mortem found; zh-TW support is one weak columnist piece on dilution.

## Cross-cutting honest caveats

- Across all three findings, ZERO first-person, named, brand-side self-admissions were found in roughly 35 queries over zh-TW, zh-CN, ja, ko, and en.
  The public evidence base is incumbents, trade associations, industry researchers, and business media describing brand behavior, plus reported consumer behavior.
  This is consistent with the findings being real but publicly unspeakable by brands, and it means our two interviews remain the only brand-side first-person sources; the public record corroborates the pattern, not the testimony.
- zh-TW, the primary target language, is the weakest layer everywhere: one agency-side voice for finding 3, zero instances for finding 2, one weak columnist for finding 1.
  Taiwanese licensing coverage found is uniformly success-framed.
- Best single upgrade path if this is ever revisited: a post-event transcript of the CLC 2024 licensee roundtable (蔡建淳/蒋海涛/朱焕涛), and verbatim re-verification of the Tmall 漫笛 selection quote (36kr.com/p/2571700074079622), both recorded as leads in the finding files.
