# Finding 2: ATTRIBUTION FAILURE - public corroboration hunt

**Claim from our two brand interviews:** brands cannot isolate a collab's effect from concurrent promos, so the ROI of licensing is effectively unmeasurable, and some brands cut back collabs on cost-vs-unclear-return.

**Compiled:** 2026-07-16 (iteration 2 of the corroboration run).
Every quote below was verified verbatim against the page source (curl of raw HTML, including JSON-embedded article bodies), not just an AI summary of the page.

**Verdict so far: CORROBORATED (medium confidence)** - five independent public instances across zh-CN and en.
The two halves of the claim are unevenly supported: "evaluation beyond exposure metrics does not exist" is stated by an industry-association conference agenda and two media analyses; "brands cut back collabs" is documented at scale as 2025 behavior (named brands, counted collab declines), though the press attributes the pullback to a mix of consumer fatigue, diminishing marginal returns, and strategy shifts rather than purely to measurement failure.
No first-person brand-side "we could not attribute it" quote has been found yet.

## Instances

### 1. China Licensing Conference 2024 roundtable agenda: "how to effectively evaluate collab results" listed as a headline licensee pain point

- **URL:** https://www.licensing.org.cn/11959.html
- **Publication:** 国际授权业协会 (Licensing International China), 2024-07-08, preview of the CLC 2024 roundtable 《IP联名时代的实战策略与痛点》 ("Practical strategies and pain points of the IP collab era").
- **Who:** conference organizers (Licensing International + LEC) framing the questions for a panel of named LICENSEE executives: 蔡建淳 (chairman, 金添动漫, licensee of Ultraman/Peppa Pig/Crayon Shin-chan and a dozen more), 蒋海涛 (GM, FIPO, apparel licensee of SMILEY/Care Bears), 朱焕涛 (founder, VIPO, licensee of Le Petit Prince/Miffy/Moomin).
- **Quote (one of four "关键问题" the roundtable will debate):** 「联名产品推出后，如何有效评估联名效果，并基于反馈进行快速市场策略调整；」
  (Gloss: "after a collab product launches, how to effectively evaluate the collab's results, and quickly adjust market strategy based on the feedback.")
- **Verdict line:** **corroborates** - the industry's own trade association puts effectiveness evaluation on the shortlist of unsolved pain points for a licensee panel.
  Flag: organizer-framed agenda, not a first-person quote; the panelists named are licensees whose actual on-stage words were not found published (post-event transcript searched, not located).

### 2. 深响 (Feb 2025): evaluating a collab's value still lacks any standard beyond exposure counts

- **URL:** https://www.cbndata.com/information/293610 (CBNData authorized repost of 深响; also on sina: https://finance.sina.com.cn/tech/csj/2025-02-07/doc-ineisaue7581702.shtml). Title: 《春节档联名启示：爆款难押，品牌玄学仍然待解》.
- **Who:** 深响 (DeepEcho), marketing/business media - MEDIA voice.
- **Quote:** 「而对于汽车品牌之类高单价品类，联名很难在短时间内转化为销量，如何评估联名的价值，更有待给出"曝光量"之外的标准。」
  (Gloss: "for high-ticket categories like cars, a collab hardly converts into sales in the short term; how to evaluate a collab's value still awaits a standard beyond 'exposure volume'.")
- **Verdict line:** **corroborates** - a direct statement that the measurement toolkit stops at impressions and that no value-attribution standard exists.
  Flag: media voice; same article already cited for finding 3 (different passage), so it counts once per finding but is a shared source across findings.

### 3. 雷报 via 36kr (Jul 2025): mid-tier brands see no visible sales return, and some brands reduced or paused collabs

- **URL:** https://36kr.com/p/3361190115624962 (36kr republication, 2025-07-02; original: 公众号 雷报, author 段轻, editor 努尔哈哈赤). Title: 《72起IP联名，半年烧钱上亿，新茶饮营销为何"高投入低热度"？》.
- **Who:** 雷报, an IP/entertainment-industry trade media outlet - MEDIA/industry-report voice describing brand behavior.
- **Quote 1:** 「这折射出行业的普遍困境，对大部分腰部品牌而言，IP联名活动只是被迫跟进头部品牌的"军备竞赛"，但有限的资源和声量，导致其未必有头部品牌那样直观的销售额回报。」
  (Gloss: "this reflects the industry's common dilemma: for most mid-tier brands, IP collabs are a forced arms race following the head brands, but with limited resources and reach they do not necessarily get the visible sales return the head brands get.")
- **Quote 2:** 「认识到这一点，也有某些品牌由于营销战略调整，减少或暂停了IP联名。」 followed by the example: 「例如曾经的"联名狂魔"喜茶，曾在2024年以37起联名的数量在新茶饮品牌中登顶，2025年上半年却没有推出联名产品」
  (Gloss: "recognizing this, some brands have reduced or paused IP collabs as part of marketing-strategy adjustments" - e.g. HEYTEA, the former "collab maniac" that topped the sector with 37 collabs in 2024, launched zero collab products in H1 2025.)
- **Quote 3 (section heading posing the cost-vs-return question):** 「上亿成本挡不住品牌指数骤降40%，联名是不是"赔钱赚吆喝"？」
  (Gloss: "hundred-million-yuan costs could not stop a 40% plunge in brand index - are collabs 'losing money to buy noise'?")
- **Verdict line:** **corroborates** - both halves: unclear sales return stated as the industry's common dilemma, and named brands cutting back.
  Flag: media voice; the HEYTEA pullback is attributed by the article partly to a deliberate brand repositioning (去联名化), so the cutback is not purely a measurement-failure story.

### 4. 九派新闻 (Oct 2025): collab marginal returns decline and premium brands shrink collab programs

- **URL:** https://news.qq.com/rain/a/20251028A026GV00 (Tencent News mirror of 九派新闻/九派财经, 2025-10-28; reporters 蓝婉齐, 林婉娜). Title: 《2025年，为什么看不到茶饮联名了？》.
- **Who:** 九派财经 news reporting, citing 多位茶饮行业人士 (multiple unnamed tea-beverage industry insiders) - NEWS voice with anonymous BRAND-SIDE sourcing.
- **Quote 1:** 「联名效益与新茶饮品牌发展阶段相关。在品牌扩张上升期，联名活动可以起到刺激销量、扩大品牌知名度的作用；到达饱和阶段，联名活动的边际效益呈现递减，营销费用的增长无法刺激同比甚至更高的营收增长。」
  (Gloss: "collab benefits depend on the brand's growth stage: in expansion they stimulate sales and awareness; at saturation the marginal benefit declines, and marketing-spend growth can no longer produce matching revenue growth.")
- **Quote 2:** 「以"品牌溢价"为核心的高端茶饮品牌，则开始收缩联名规模，考虑联名活动对品牌沉淀的转化。2024年联名39次、被称为奶茶联名鼻祖的喜茶在2025一季度暂停了所有联名动作，调整品牌营销风格；霸王茶姬也开始降低外部联名转向维护品牌调性」
  (Gloss: premium tea brands are shrinking collab scale while weighing whether collabs convert into durable brand equity; HEYTEA (39 collabs in 2024) paused ALL collabs in Q1 2025, and CHAGEE also cut external collabs to protect brand tone.)
- **Context stat (same article's framing, reported across the cooldown coverage):** top-10 tea brands' collab count fell 41.2% year-on-year in Jan-Jul 2025; sector collab events halved (149 in H1 2024 to 72 in H1 2025 per 雷报's count in instance 3).
- **Verdict line:** **corroborates** - the pullback half is documented with named brands and counted declines, and the stated reason is spend that no longer visibly moves revenue.
  Flag: the operative explanation is "diminishing returns," which presumes some measurement; it corroborates cost-vs-unclear-return but does not by itself prove attribution is impossible.

### 5. IMC Licensing (Stephen Reily, en): no one has a way to isolate and measure licensing's intangible brand impact

- **URL:** https://imclicensing.com/beyond-royalty-revenue-measuring-roi-from-licensing/
- **Who:** Stephen Reily, founder of IMC Licensing (US licensing AGENCY) - AGENCY voice; the piece is undated on-page but cites 2009-era survey data, so treat as an older essay.
- **Quote 1:** "Yet no one has yet come up with a way to isolate and measure the impact of these steps in consumers' lives."
  (Context: the brand-building benefits a licensed product delivers - experiencing the brand in new places - cannot be isolated or measured.)
- **Quote 2 (survey stats the essay cites on marketing ROI generally):** "One survey showed that 77% are dissatisfied with their ability to measure marketing accountability. Only 31% believe that ROI can be calculated for more than half of their marketing budgets, and 22% admit that they cannot calculate ROI for any of their marketing budget."
- **Verdict line:** **corroborates (weak)** - an incumbent agency concedes in writing that isolating licensing's brand impact is an unsolved problem.
  Flag: agency thought piece (the objective says these count only as weak corroboration); its overall argument is pro-licensing (royalties make licensing MORE measurable than other marketing), so it corroborates only the intangible-benefit attribution gap; older source.

## Confidence line

**Corroborated with MEDIUM confidence.**
Five instances agree that collab-effect evaluation has no standard beyond exposure metrics (instances 1, 2, 5) and that in 2025 named brands visibly cut back collabs amid spend that no longer produced matching returns (instances 3, 4, with counted declines of 41-52%).
The gaps keeping this at MEDIUM rather than MEDIUM-HIGH: zero first-person brand-side attribution complaints found, zero zh-TW instances, and the documented pullback is multi-causal (fatigue, repositioning, diminishing returns) rather than cleanly "we cut because we could not measure."
