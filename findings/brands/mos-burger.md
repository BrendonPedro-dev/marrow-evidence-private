# 摩斯漢堡 MOS Burger (安心食品服務股份有限公司) - Partner-Brand Brief

MOS Taiwan is already a proven character-meal operator that runs its OWN in-house picture-book IP (MOS Friends 雞塊兄弟) as a recurring kids-and-family engine plus at least one external Sanrio tie-in, which makes it an unusually warm QSR target for Cheesy Duck but also means any outside duck has to out-perform a house character the chain owns outright and pays no royalty on.

Research date: 2026-07-22.
zh-TW / ja sources are primary.
Quotes are paraphrased and flagged unless marked curl-verified; verbatim quoting was minimized because Facebook and Instagram block anonymous fetch.

IMPORTANT OWNERSHIP CORRECTION (verified against the registry and Wikipedia):
The routing note described MOS Taiwan as "part of the Uni-President 統一 group."
That is incorrect.
Taiwan MOS is operated by 安心食品服務股份有限公司, a joint venture whose majority shareholder is 東元集團 (TECO group, historically about 73% of the equity), not 統一 / Uni-President [S1, S2, S3].
The rest of this brief is built on the corrected TECO ownership.

- Taiwan operating entity: 安心食品服務股份有限公司 (An-Shin Food Services Co., Ltd.), the exclusive Taiwan operator of the Japanese MOS BURGER brand [S1, S2].

## 2. AUDIENCE

- Positioning-implied core: urban, quality-and-health-conscious QSR customers who accept a price premium over 麥當勞 / 肯德基; Wikipedia's brand summary states MOS's cleaner taste and quality "使其在都會區客群受到不少青睞" (wins favor among urban customer segments) while its higher prices and slower made-to-order service are recurring complaints (stated, tertiary source) [S2].
- Families and children are a deliberately targeted segment: the MOS Friends 雞塊兄弟 picture-book giveaways, kids' meals ("同樂會獨享餐"), and 小小達人 kids experience camps are all explicitly parent-child ("親子") programs, and the brand frames family dining as building "愛的記憶點" (a memory point of love) [S20, S21, S15].
- Skew inference (estimate, not stated): the combination of health messaging, higher price point, iced-tea fandom, and heavy family/kids programming points to family units and 25-45 adults (often parents), plus a tea-and-value younger cohort reached through coupons; treat as directional inference from campaign choices, not a disclosed demographic.
- Sentiment context: a 2025 wave of PTT / social criticism ("漸漸不想去", 4 complaints about quality and speed) shows an engaged but partly disaffected base that the company is actively trying to win back through store renovation and new formats (stated by company, see section 3) [S13, S14].

## 4. COLLAB / IP HISTORY

MOS Taiwan's character playbook has two distinct tracks:

A. In-house owned IP - MOS Friends 雞塊兄弟 (the dominant, recurring format):
- Concept: a cute IP "以熱門餐點『摩斯雞塊』為靈感" (inspired by the popular MOS Chicken Nuggets), made up of five differently-styled/expressioned nugget characters [S20, S24].
- Format: primarily picture-book giveaways with set-meal purchases (a soft, all-ages, parent-child "繪本" storytelling program), extended into plush charms, fans, waterproof stickers, and kids' experience camps.
  - 《雞塊兄弟與神秘森林》 - 2025-04 (Children's Day) - free picture book with purchase [S20].
  - 《雞塊兄弟與魔法小屋》 - 2025 summer - free picture book + fan/sticker premiums [S23].
  - MOS Friends plush-charm 5-pack - 2026-05-15 - NT$499 add-on [S24].
  - 《雞塊兄弟夏日出遊記》 - 2026-07 (MOS Friends 1st anniversary) - free picture book with set meals [S15, S26].
- Public read on results: no disclosed sell-through metric was found; the strongest observable evidence is behavioral - MOS has repeated the MOS Friends picture-book format at least four times across roughly 15 months (Apr 2025 to Jul 2026) and expanded it from books into paid plush charms and camps, which it would not do if the format underperformed (inference, not a stated metric).

B. External licensed IP:
- Sanrio 三麗鷗 (Hello Kitty, 蛋黃哥 Gudetama) - 2022-11-04 - 3 collab burgers plus 10 "MOS Party" add-on premiums (tableware/mug/bottle/plate/box/bag) [S27]. This is the clearest verified external character collab for MOS Taiwan and confirms the chain will run a burger-plus-branded-premium format with a third-party character IP.
- No other confirmed Taiwan external-character food collab (2023-2026) was found under queries "摩斯漢堡 台灣 聯名 卡通 IP 2024 2025 加價購" and "摩斯漢堡 三麗鷗 迪士尼 聯名 歷年"; the Japan Melody/Kuromi 2025 meal-toy campaign is Japan-market and is not counted here [S28].
- Adjacent licensing surface: MOS CARD quarterly card-face designs incorporate 動漫 IP artwork [S30]; a MOS x national-park "微旅行護照" cross-promotion also appears in brand social history (non-character, civic tie-in) [S9].

This "existing collab [mixed]" reality matches the map-v2 routing note: MOS has genuine character-meal capability and a warm all-ages posture, but its default character is one it owns (MOS Friends), and its one clear external IP was a top-tier Sanrio name.

## 5. DECISION CONTEXT

- Marketing / campaign decisions sit at An-Shin HQ, not with individual stores: MOS Friends drops, kids' meals, renovation strategy and menu launches all roll out chain-wide and simultaneously, and are announced through the official mos.com.tw news channel and the corporate/IR calendar (shareholders' meeting), indicating centralized HQ marketing control (inference from campaign structure) [S15, S8].
- Because Taiwan MOS is a TECO-group JV that also relies on Japan MOS Food Services for the brand and some products, any large character program plausibly touches both An-Shin's Taiwan marketing team and Japan brand oversight; the exact split is not publicly documented (flagged as unknown; do NOT assert Japan sign-off is required).
- Official cooperation surface (use this, not scraped contacts):
  - 合作提案 page: https://shop.mos.com.tw/page/view/Cooperation - lists 商品合作 (food / food-related) and 異業合作 (廣告交換 / 贈品交換 / 部落客試吃), with intake via LINE@ id @gka6150t or the shop's on-site message ("站內信"); note this page currently states it mainly accepts "官方商城網路相關廣告交換", so it skews to product and ad-exchange rather than large licensing, and a character-meal pitch may need to reach marketing directly [S19].
  - Official 聯絡我們 page: https://www.mos.com.tw/stakeholder/contactUs.html - main line (02)2567-5001; business/supplier channel email order@mos.com.tw; consumer service service@mos.com.tw; departmental contacts are published by surname + extension only (e.g. customer service 張先生 #502; investor relations 李小姐 #308; CSR 李小姐 #605) [S31].
- No individual marketing manager name is published on the official site (checked contactUs.html and the cooperation page, 2026-07); no personal emails or phone numbers were scraped. The correct first surfaces for a licensing pitch are the 合作提案 LINE@/站內信 channel and the departmental order@mos.com.tw business address, escalating to An-Shin HQ marketing.
- Named public figures for context only (NOT the pitch path): 黃茂雄 (負責人 / TECO chairman) [S1, S3]; An-Shin president/總經理 quoted on the FY2024 loss and pricing review in trade press (title referenced, name not confirmed on an official page - flagged) [S13].
- Timing read: with FY2024 the first-ever operating loss, active social criticism, and an "安心2.0" reinvention underway, MOS marketing has a stated 2025-2026 mandate to refresh brand perception and defend the family/kids franchise - a receptive backdrop for an all-ages character proposal (inference) [S8, S13, S14].

## 6. SUGGESTED ANGLE (suggestion, not a finding)

- Suggestion: lead with Cheesy Duck 確幸鴨 as an all-ages, meme-friendly sticker duck that slots into MOS's already-proven character-meal machinery - the exact picture-book-plus-premium format MOS runs for its own MOS Friends nuggets and ran with Sanrio - so the ask is "add an external duck to a playbook you already operate," not "invent a new format."
- Tie to evidence: MOS repeats the MOS Friends 繪本 giveaway roughly quarterly and has extended it into paid plush charms and kids camps, and Cheesy Duck's own picture-book and F&B angles map onto that book-and-charm template directly; a duck also reads as fresh next to nuggets while staying on MOS's soft, family-safe brand voice.
- Campaign goal fit: primarily brand rejuvenation / identity (MOS is publicly fighting an aging-perception and quality-criticism narrative and reinventing under "安心2.0") and family foot-traffic / seasonal (Children's Day and summer are its established character windows), with awareness as a secondary lift.
- Honest caveat for the pitch: MOS's default character is one it owns and pays no royalty on (MOS Friends), and its single clear external precedent was a blockbuster (Sanrio), so Cheesy Duck should target a window MOS is NOT filling with nuggets (or a co-star role alongside them) and lead with cost, flexibility, and a distinct duck personality rather than trying to displace the house IP.
