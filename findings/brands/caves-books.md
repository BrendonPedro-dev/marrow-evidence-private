# 敦煌書局 Caves Books - Partner-Brand Brief

敦煌書局 / Caves Books is a ~15-branch Taiwan bookstore chain specialised in English / foreign-language and language-learning (ELT/ESL) materials, operated by 敦煌書局股份有限公司 (統一編號 14004626, capital NT$150M, rep 陳文良) inside the wider 敦煌教育集團 Caves Education group - so its audience skews hard to students, teachers and language-learners rather than general lifestyle/gift buyers.
The single most load-bearing correction: the map's Jolly Gee "existing collab and repeated outreach" premise did NOT verify against any Taiwan source - no Jolly Gee (or any named character-IP) collaboration with Caves Books surfaced under the queries run, so that premise should be treated as a routing-note error and confirmed only against PBC's own CRM before any pitch cites shared history.
Category alignment (bookstore + stationery + sticker/craft goods for young learners) is real and documented, but the fit is education-first, which reshapes the angle.

Research date: 2026-07-22.
Sources are primarily zh-TW (company registry via g0v, official 敦煌教育集團 / E-CAVES sites, Chinese Wikipedia, official social pages); follower counts are labelled estimates from 2026-07 search metadata where on-page fetch was blocked.

## 2. AUDIENCE

- No first-party demographic disclosure was found; the below is inferred from catalogue, channel and platform signals and labelled estimate.
- Estimate (high confidence): the core buyer/follower base is language-learners and their gatekeepers - (a) English/foreign-language students from young-learner through adult, (b) parents buying graded readers and picture books for kids, and (c) English teachers/教師, who are served by a dedicated arm (Caves Connect, 師德) [S2][S7][S6].
- Stated positioning: the brand frames itself around language learning ("玩語言.來敦煌書局"; 語言學習的護國神山) and self-study/exam materials, confirming an education-first, not lifestyle-first, identity [S2][S10].
- Estimate: the university-campus bookshops and Campus Club pages skew a slice of the audience specifically to college students; the young-learner picture-book/sticker/craft segment is the sub-audience most relevant to character-IP goods [S6][S10].
- Implication for IP fit: an IP lands best here if it can carry an educational or "warm companion for learning" framing, or a gift/kawaii stationery angle for the young-learner shelf - pure adult lifestyle-collectible framing is a weaker match to this base.

## 5. DECISION CONTEXT

- Governance: this is a founder/HQ-led group (敦煌教育集團) with a centralised corporate structure - one legal operator (敦煌書局股份有限公司, rep 陳文良) over ~15 stores and multiple education arms - unlike a decentralised imprint federation; a national retail/文創 or licensing decision would most plausibly run through group HQ marketing/merchandising in 內湖, not individual store managers [S3][S5][S6].
- Caveat: day-to-day store social/LINE and local events are decentralised to each branch, but a chain-wide IP program is an HQ call [S10].
- Named marketing contacts: none published on official pages under the queries run - no individual marketing owner is surfaced first-party. Per PBC rules no scraped personal contact is reproduced.
- Official contact surfaces (public): group site https://www.caves.com.tw (集團/服務據點/聯絡); online bookstore customer service via https://www.cavesbooks.com.tw/zh ; Caves Connect editorial platform https://cavesconnect.cavesbooks.com.tw ; per-store Facebook + LINE 官方帳號 for local enquiries [S5][S2][S10].
- Practical approach note: the most credible first door is the group HQ / 敦煌出版 or e-commerce (E-CAVES) merchandising function, framed as an education-relevant goods program, rather than a single store.

## 6. SUGGESTED ANGLE (suggestion, not a finding)

- Suggestion: lead with Jolly Gee Studio (the ranked #5 lead IP) as a design-led stationery + sticker/craft line for the young-learner and student shelves, and/or an E-CAVES seasonal gift-with-purchase - leaning on the fact that Caves already merchandises stationery and sticker/貼紙 goods for kids, so a Korean retro-kitsch stationery capsule is a native shelf extension rather than a stretch [S12][S2].
- Because the audience is education-first, frame the IP around a "fun companion for language learning" or back-to-school/exam-season gift moment; Bichi Mao 彼奇貓 (ranked #30) is a secondary fit for a warmer, kid-friendly picture-book-plus-merch angle but is a weaker category lead than Jolly Gee here.
- Campaign-goal mapping: primarily FOOT-TRAFFIC / SEASONAL (back-to-school and exam-season stationery/gift capsules across ~15 stores + E-CAVES DTC), with a secondary AWARENESS goal (introducing a fresh design IP to a text/education-first retailer's shelf) [S2][S6].
