# Save Dogs Taiwan 台灣動物緊急救援小組 (ARTT) - Partner Brief for Bichi Mao 彼奇貓

"Save Dogs Taiwan" resolves to 台灣動物緊急救援小組 / Animal Rescue Team Taiwan (ARTT), registered as 社團法人台灣動物緊急救援推廣協會 - Taiwan's first government-approved legal animal-rescue fundraising NGO, founder-led (倪兆成) and now run by 執行長 倪京台.
It is a large-reach, founder-driven donor machine (Facebook ~414K vs Instagram only ~7.6K) whose entire collab track record is donation-based: charity photo calendars with 點點印 and, notably, a January 2026 charity keychain tie-in with the black-cat mobile game 《黑貓維茲》.
No illustrator or character-licensing precedent exists, and the map-v2 "rabbit/bear motifs" flag could not be substantiated under any query run here; the one real IP collab found is cat-themed, which is directionally on-point for Bichi Mao.

## 1. IDENTITY

- Official name: 社團法人台灣動物緊急救援推廣協會 (registered legal-entity name) / operating brand 台灣動物緊急救援小組, English "Animal Rescue Team Taiwan" (ARTT) [S1, S2, S3].
- Also self-labels the "台灣貓狗聯合勸募平台" (Taiwan Cats & Dogs United Fundraising Platform), a parallel task unit established 2009 for feed-donation to weak private shelters [S1].
- What they do: emergency rescue, medical care, sterilization (節育), socialization, and adoption of injured/abused/stray cats and dogs; feed-donation support to private shelters; and life-education (生命教育) lectures in schools and military units; cross-border (US/Canada) adoption placement for low-adoptability animals [S1].
- The official about page states, curl-verified: 「教育」才是解決台灣流浪動物問題的根本方法 (education is the fundamental method for solving Taiwan's stray-animal problem) [S1].
- Founded 民國84年 (1995) in Kaohsiung by 倪兆成 (Ni Zhao-cheng), a retired teacher; the about page states it was 全國第一個仿效歐美專為傷病受虐無主的流浪動物提供緊急救援與醫療服務的民間組織 (curl-verified) [S1].
- Incorporated (立案) 2007 with the Ministry of the Interior as 社團法人台灣動物緊急救援推廣協會; the about page states it is 全國第一個經政府核准，允許對外合法勸募的動物救援組織 (the first government-approved legal-fundraising animal-rescue org, curl-verified) [S1].
- Registered entity / 統一編號: 45430504; 立案字號 內政部台內社字第0960037625號; e-invoice charity code (發票愛心碼) 0958 [S2, S4]. Note: a direct query to the GCIS mirror company.g0v.ronny.tw returned no record under 45430504 (mirror gap, not a disproof); the 統一編號 is corroborated by the official site and the self-regulation-alliance profile [S2, S4].
- Governance: 社團法人 (incorporated association); member of 台灣公益團體自律聯盟 (Taiwan NPO Self-Regulation Alliance), member number A0295; annual financial and work reports are published on the official site (年度財務報告) [S4, S1].
- Scale (stated, curl-verified on about page): 拯救超過5000隻傷病流浪貓狗 (over 5,000 injured strays rescued in ~30 years); the feed platform 目前每月平均捐出逾新台幣600萬元的飼料，溫飽台灣本島及離島超過3萬隻弱勢生命 (~NT$6M/month in feed, feeding 30,000+ animals); shelter-improvement works have invested 超過新台幣2000萬元 (NT$20M+) [S1].
- Offices/contact surface: Taichung, No. 11-3, Lane 6, South Ziqiang St., East District (40151); PO Box 44, Taichung (40199); tel (04) 2360-0176 (weekdays 8:30-17:30); fax (04) 2360-9747; email volunteer_center@savedogs.org [S1, S3].
- Official site: https://www.savedogs.org/ ; donation portal https://donation.savedogs.org/ ; joint-fundraising site https://donation-networks.savedogs.org/ [S1, S5].
- Facebook (facebook.com/ARTT.saveDOGS): approx 413,956 likes / ~413K followers - estimate, from a Google-indexed search snippet of the page, as of 2026-07; not directly verifiable by fetch (single source) [S6].
- Instagram (@artt.savedogs): 7,607 followers, 97 posts - stated in page metadata, curl-verified, as of 2026-07 [S7].
- YouTube (channel UCcAEFaWp4kZJ5yn8C3t4boQ, "台灣動物緊急救援小組 - 台灣貓狗聯合勸募平台 - ARTT"): 2,630位訂閱者 (2,630 subscribers), 665 videos - stated, curl-verified, as of 2026-07 [S8].

## 2. AUDIENCE

- No first-party demographic breakdown of followers or donors is published; everything below is labelled accordingly.
- Stated: the LINE case study frames the target audience as 認同協會理念的民眾 (people who align with the org's mission), i.e. recurring donors and animal-welfare advocates reached via website campaigns, QR-coded receipts/envelopes, and partnerships (paraphrase) [S9].
- Stated: the org relies heavily on 定期定額 recurring donation and feed-認購 (feed-subscription) drives, plus e-invoice charity-code (0958) micro-donations and a wide payment stack (LINE Pay, 街口, 全支付, 悠遊付, 統一ibon, 超商條碼, etc.) - indicating a mass-retail everyday-donor funnel [S1].
- Estimate (inference from platform mix): the Facebook base (~414K) dwarfs Instagram (~7.6K) and YouTube (2.6K), pointing to a core supporter base skewing 35+ Taiwanese adults with a very small younger IG audience; this is a materially older/broader skew than peer NGO TSPCA's mix [S6, S7, S8].
- Estimate (inference from partner selection): corporate feed-認購 partners (統一超商/7-ELEVEN, Yahoo購物中心, 快電商, 寵物公園) point to a general-consumer, pet-sympathetic donor base reached at retail and e-commerce checkout rather than a boutique gift-buying niche [S11, S12, S13].
- Engaged core: 100+ nationwide volunteers, foster/adopter families, veterinary partners, and students/soldiers reached through life-education lectures [S14, S1].

## 5. DECISION CONTEXT

- Executive lead: 倪京台 (Ni Jing-tai) is publicly identified as 執行長 (Executive Director) and is named as the person driving the LINE official-account / digital-fundraising strategy; also appears as 執行長 in partner video coverage (GOMO寵物食品) [S9, S17].
- Structure: 社團法人 incorporated association operating through a nationwide volunteer network and veterinary partners; decision-making reads as founder/executive-director-led rather than committee-diffuse, based on public sources [S1, S9].
- Partnership posture: the org actively runs recurring corporate feed-認購 and e-commerce charity drives (7-ELEVEN, Yahoo, 快電商) and a game-IP charity sale, indicating an open, proposal-friendly, donation-first partnership stance; charity sales are routed through its own donation/e-commerce sites [S11, S15, S5].

## 6. SUGGESTED ANGLE (suggestion, not a finding)

This section is a suggestion, not a research finding.
ARTT has already run a character-IP charity tie-in that worked mechanically - the January 2026 《黑貓維茲》 black-cat game keychain - so a Bichi Mao charity capsule (warm healing-cat art on a keychain, tote, or feed-認購 gift item, proceeds to rescue) fits an existing, proven donation-based format rather than asking the NGO to adopt an unfamiliar licensing model.
Given ARTT's mass-retail donor funnel (7-ELEVEN, Yahoo, e-invoice code 0958) and its very large but older Facebook base, the natural structure is percent-of-sales or feed-認購 co-branding tied to stray-cat/adoption messaging, with a seasonal or anniversary trigger.
Campaign-goal mapping: primarily awareness plus donor-reach identity for PBC (introducing Bichi Mao to a large animal-welfare audience), with a secondary audience play - ARTT's weak Instagram (~7.6K) means the IP's younger social reach is a genuine value-add ARTT lacks, which strengthens the trade.
