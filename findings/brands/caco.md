# CACO (加州椰子國際股份有限公司) - Partner-Brand Brief

Taiwan's self-described largest licensed-collab apparel chain (roughly 64-70 stores plus CACO cafe) already runs Korean-IP cafe takeovers (JOGUMAN, 2026-07) and Thai healing-IP tours (Butterbear, 2026-01), making it a near-ideal fit for Jolly Gee Studio.

## 1. IDENTITY

- Brand name: CACO (no separate Chinese brand name in use; the site brands itself "CACO 最大聯名服飾品牌", earlier self-description "最大美式授權服飾品牌").
- Slogan: "Always Fun with CACO" (stated on official company page, curl-verified 2026-07-22) [1].
- Operating company: 加州椰子國際股份有限公司 (English trade name "California Coconut", per the brand-history note that CACO is the abbreviation of California Coconut), unified business number 24492069, published in the official site footer [1][2].
- Government registry (GCIS open data, fetched 2026-07-22): status 核准設立, authorized capital NTD 200,000,000, paid-in capital NTD 120,000,000, responsible person 闕怡瑩, registered address 臺北市大安區新生南路3段2號14樓, incorporated 2009-12-29 (民國98年) [3].
- What they sell: licensed-character T-shirts and casual apparel (men/women/kids, couple and parent-child designs), plus character merch (周邊), via directly-operated stores, department-store counters, and its own e-commerce site; it also distributes international apparel brands (Champion, Kangol, Ben Davis, United Athle, OUTDOOR, Carhartt, Dickies - each described on the official dealer page as distributed in Taiwan by 加州椰子 since 2020) [4].
- Sub-brands (official brand-list and company pages): CACO (main), NAVY (street/anime line with its own IG), CC.DAILY (women's line, officially described as "平價、韓系聯名" for 小資女), WAVE SHINE (women's), AR and ST (older American-retro/street lines named in third-party search summaries only - single source, unverified) [1][5].
- Food-and-beverage format: CACO cafe, with five locations named in press as of 2026-07 (台北東區, 台中漢神洲際, 台南小西門, 台南南紡, 高雄夢時代) [12].
- Scale: official company page states "2012年成立，台灣最大『美式授權服飾品牌』，擁有全台70間直營門市及自建官網" (curl-verified) [1]; my own count of the official store-list page on 2026-07-22 found 64 store entries in Taiwan (estimate, own count) [6].
- A Malaysia direct store is claimed only on job-board company profiles (104/小雞上工 recruiting text), not on the official site - single-source, treat as unconfirmed [7].
- Official site: https://www.caco.com.tw [1].
- Instagram @caco_tw: 136K followers, 6,900 posts (stated in Instagram page metadata, curl-verified as of 2026-07-22); bio per search snippet calls CACO "你的IP魔法師" (snippet-sourced, unverified wording) [8].
- Instagram @navy_co (NAVY sub-brand): 61K followers (stated in page metadata, fetched 2026-07-22) [9].

## 2. AUDIENCE

- CACO's own catalog structure targets men, women, and kids, with couple outfits (情侶裝) and parent-child sets (親子款) named first in the official site's meta keywords - evidence of a family/couples mass-market positioning (stated on official site source code, curl-verified) [5].
- The CC.DAILY line is officially described as "品牌專攻女性時裝市場，主打「平價、韓系聯名」小資女好入手單品" - i.e. budget-conscious young women who like Korean-style collabs (stated, curl-verified) [5].
- The NAVY line targets anime/street fans (current drops: Naruto, Dandadan, Hunter x Hunter, Crayon Shin-chan) (observed on official homepage, 2026-07-22) [17].
- Fan-community traffic: CACO pop-ups are covered by fan communities such as the Harry Potter fan site hpfl.net and discussed on Dcard's 女孩 board - consistent with a young, character-fandom, heavily female-skewing shopper base (estimate inferred from coverage; no official demographic data published) [14][20].
- No official age/gender statistics were found; all demographic reads above beyond the stated brand-line descriptions are estimates.

## 5. DECISION CONTEXT

- CACO is its own licensing operator: the official 授權品牌 page describes 加州椰子 itself as the Taiwan apparel-category agent for its major Western IPs, and recruiting profiles state it maintains a long-term in-house Taiwan design team ("長期培養台灣設計師團隊", curl-verified on the 小雞上工 company profile) [7][21].
- This implies IP-partnership decisions sit inside 加州椰子國際 itself (in-house licensing + design), not a separate parent - estimate based on the above, no org chart published.
- Official contact surface: the site's 優質廠商合作專區 (vendor-cooperation page) states "誠摯歡迎各界廠商跨領域合作" and publishes two official emails - 經銷信箱 caco.sale@gmail.com and general contact service@caco.com.tw - plus office phone (02)2363-8588, Mon-Fri 09:00-12:30 / 13:30-18:00 (all published by CACO on its official site, curl-verified) [24].
- The cooperation page lists welcomed categories including 宣傳品、贈品異業合作 and media/marketing partnerships, and pitches exposure across the CACO website, marketplaces, Facebook fan page, and nationwide stores [24].
- Named individuals: the only publicly named person is registry responsible person 闕怡瑩 (government company registry) [3]; no licensing-manager names appear on official pages, so outreach should go through the published cooperation emails above.

## 6. SUGGESTED ANGLE (suggestion, not a finding)

Suggestion: lead with Jolly Gee Studio (#1) - CACO has just proven it will build a full seasonal campaign around a Korean character IP (JOGUMAN apparel line plus a five-store CACO cafe takeover, Jul-Sep 2026), it explicitly positions its CC.DAILY women's line as 韓系聯名, and Jolly Gee's cafe-collab track record maps directly onto the CACO cafe + pop-up format.
This maps primarily to a foot traffic/seasonal goal (themed cafe takeover + gift-with-purchase waves are CACO's established playbook) with awareness as the secondary goal given CACO's 136K-follower IG and weekly-drop cadence.
As a second card, Bichi Mao 彼奇貓 or Cheesy Duck 確幸鴨 fits CACO's demonstrated appetite for Asian healing-style characters (Thai Butterbear's "城市療癒計畫" in 2026-01 and the LuLu x Capoo 軟萌 line in 2026-06), which maps to an audience/identity goal for the softer, warm-styling segment of CACO's female shoppers.
