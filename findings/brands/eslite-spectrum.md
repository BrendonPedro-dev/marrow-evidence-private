# 誠品生活 Eslite Spectrum - Partner-Brand Brief

Eslite Spectrum is a VENUE / RETAIL-CHANNEL and pop-up host, not a merch licensee - it is the TPEx-listed (上櫃, 股票代號 2926) mall/retail arm of the Eslite group, operating department-store-style lifestyle malls, ~35-37 Taiwan stores plus Hong Kong / Tokyo / Suzhou / Kuala Lumpur, its own 誠品文具 stationery halls, and a heavy calendar of character-IP 期間限定店 (pop-ups) and exhibitions.
The map's Jolly Gee #6 "collab-history" premise could NOT be verified (no Jolly Gee tie found), but the underlying category logic is genuinely strong: Eslite already retails and hosts Korean cute-character stationery IP (e.g. Romane's BRUNCH BROTHER in its 500-坪 文具影城, Korean webtoons in its 動漫祭) - so the right framing for both IPs is channel/pop-up partner, not licensee.
Research date: 2026-07-22.
Sources are primarily zh-TW (company registry proxies, official 迷誠品/EXPO/eslite.com sites, Taiwan trade and lifestyle press); Hong Kong (hket) and Japan-store references are labelled non-Taiwan.

## 5. DECISION CONTEXT

- Eslite is a large corporate retailer with functional buying/merchandising and exhibition/pop-up teams, plus a dedicated cultural-creative onboarding platform - so a character-IP proposal routes through commercial/merchandising and 展覽/期間限定 channels, not a single franchise owner.
- Primary public vendor channel: EXPO 誠品生活文創平台 (eslitexpo.com) - an online application form "合作洽談 / 頭家徵件 報名表單" with three tracks: 行銷合作 (marketing partnership), 品牌進駐 (brand residency), 肖年頭家徵件 (young-creator program); target categories explicitly include 文具/雜貨 (stationery/sundries) - the natural front door for a stationery/cute-character IP [S7].
- Secondary channel: 迷誠品 (meet.eslite.com) publishes a 合作聯繫 page routing cooperation proposals by type (brand/counter/pop-up/exhibition/media) to Eslite mailboxes with a stated ~3-working-day handling time (page returned 403 on direct fetch this session; content summarised from search metadata - flag: not verbatim-verified) [S30].
- Leadership: 董事長 吳旻潔 (Mercy Wu) - re-elected 2025-06; long-tenured, brand-defining executive who frames Eslite around cultural/lifestyle values ("誠品消失、除非我消失") [S9][S10].

## 6. SUGGESTED ANGLE (suggestion, not a finding)

- Suggestion: lead with Jolly Gee as a 期間限定店 / stationery-shop-in-shop, NOT a wholesale licensing pitch. The strongest evidence-backed hook is that Eslite already retails Korean retro-cute character stationery (Romane BRUNCH BROTHER in the 新店 文具影城) and already hosts Korean IP in-store (全知讀者視角 in 動漫祭) - so JOLLY/LYGEE/LYLEE slots into a proven, live merchandising lane rather than asking Eslite to do something new [S16][S26].
- Best fit / goal mapping: primarily FOOT-TRAFFIC / SEASONAL (a pop-up or holiday stationery capsule at a high-footfall flagship such as 新店/松菸/信義-successor), with a secondary AWARENESS angle (introducing a fresh Korean design IP to Eslite's ~301K-IG / large membership audience). SHUYA fits the same channel logic as a lower-intensity 快閃櫃位 / exhibition-adjacent pastry-or-lifestyle capsule (the SHUYA #28 "lifestyle-retail pop-up venue" premise is well-supported) [S7][S16][S18].
- Entry point: apply via EXPO 合作洽談 for a stationery/文創 residency, and/or pitch the 期間限定/展覽 team for a dated pop-up window tied to a seasonal peak (春節 / 動漫祭-adjacent) [S7][S26][S30].
