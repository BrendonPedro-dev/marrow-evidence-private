# 85度C (85°C Bakery Cafe) - partner-brand brief for SHUYA 舒雅

Taiwan's biggest bakery-cafe chain runs a proven quarterly character-cake collab machine (Pokemon, Sanrio, Shin-chan, Chiikawa, Conan, a bunny IP) and publicly frames IP cakes as its youth-rejuvenation lever - a near-perfect format fit for SHUYA, though its partner list skews to top-tier IPs.

Research date: 2026-07-22.
All zh-TW quotes below were curl-verified against the fetched page unless explicitly marked unverified.

## 1. IDENTITY

- Official brand name: 85度C / 85度C咖啡蛋糕烘焙專賣店 (English: 85°C Bakery Cafe, also 85 Cafe / 85 Degrees C) [S12].
- Listed parent: 開曼美食達人股份有限公司 (Gourmet Master Co., Ltd.), a Cayman-registered holding company, TWSE code 2723, listed on the Taiwan Stock Exchange on 2010-11-22 under the F-share regime (ticker names F-美食 / 美食-KY), also traded OTCQX as GUMCF [S12].
- This verifies the internal map's note: the group is TWSE-listed as 2723, but note the listed entity is the Cayman holding company, not the Taiwan operating company 美食達人股份有限公司 [S12].
- Founded 2004 by 吳政學; first store opened 2004-07-28 in 永和 (New Taipei); Taiwan headquarters is in 台中市南屯區 (Taichung Nantun industrial zone) [S12].
- What they sell: coffee, tea and fruit drinks, celebration cakes and slice cakes, Taiwanese-style bread, seasonal desserts; newer formats add in-store fresh baking (新型態店/二代店) and a standalone fresh-donut format 85度C生甜甜圈專賣店 launched from Aug 2025 [S1, S8, S10].
- Scale, Taiwan: roughly 430-440 stores (estimate; a third-party store-locator aggregation sums to about 429 stores, and 2025 press references about 440; no single official current Taiwan total was found) [S13, S14].
- Scale, China: 460 stores at the start of 2025, cut to under 400 by 2025-09-30, with a company-stated plan to cut to 270 or fewer by end-2025 - a stated closure of over 40% in one year ("全年關店幅度超過四成") [S9, S15].
- Scale, US: 88 stores as of early Nov 2025; 90 stores across 14 states as of Q1 2026 (84 direct, 6 franchised), with a stated target of at least 100 US stores by end-2026 [S10, S11].
- Group revenue: NT$13.77bn (137.7億元) for the first 9 months of 2025, with roughly NT$20bn (近200億元) annually; described as Taiwan's second-largest listed restaurant group after 王品 [S10].
- Official site: https://www.85cafe.com [S1].
- Facebook: https://www.facebook.com/85cafe - approx 249,000 page likes (estimate, as of 2026-07, taken from search-result metadata; direct page fetch was blocked) [S16].
- Instagram: https://www.instagram.com/85cafe/ - approx 56,000 followers (estimate, as of 2026-07, from search-result metadata; direct fetch blocked) [S16].
- Threads: https://www.threads.com/@85cafe - approx 21,600 followers (estimate, as of 2026-07, from search-result metadata; not directly verified) [S16].

## 2. AUDIENCE

- Mass-market, all-Taiwan neighborhood clientele across urban and rural areas; the brand self-describes as "深耕全台都會商圈與鄉間小鎮" (rooted in both city commercial districts and small towns) in its official site meta description (stated) [S14].
- Core buyers historically: office workers for coffee and families/young women for cakes; a dated academic marketing case put cake buyers at 15-40 (skewing single women) and coffee buyers at 23-50 office workers (estimate, undated student case study - treat as directional only) [S17].
- Aging-clientele perception is a live issue: a viral PTT post in May 2026 called stores an "阿伯群聚地" (uncle gathering spot), and 85度C responded on the record that it is renovating store formats and using trend products plus character cake collabs to stay fresh (stated by company) [S8].
- The company's own rejuvenation framing, curl-verified: "在產品上也跟上潮流，例如生甜甜圈、奶油烤年糕、杜拜巧克力Q餅、杜拜可可冰沙都被年輕人在社群媒體上廣為分享，還有多樣蛋糕聯名蠟筆小新、柯南、芙莉蓮、酷洛米等，藉此維持新鮮感" [S8].
- The 2025-2026 collab slate (Chiikawa, Sanrio, Shin-chan, Frieren) and K-pop tie-ins (張員瑛, NCT WISH) indicate deliberate targeting of young women, anime fans, and social-media-driven buyers (estimate/inference from campaign choices) [S3-S7, S18].

## 5. DECISION CONTEXT

- Taiwan stores are predominantly franchised (as of Feb 2014, 299 of 336 Taiwan stores - 88% - were 自願加盟; dated single-source figure, current ratio unknown), but campaigns, collab cakes, and pricing launch simultaneously chain-wide, so product/marketing decisions clearly sit with HQ, not franchisees (inference from campaign structure) [S22, S1].
- Marketing partnership proposals are routed through the HQ 行銷企劃部 (Marketing Planning Department); the official contact page states, curl-verified: "如果您的公司想與85度C合作，歡迎您準備相關資料mail至本公司行銷企劃部聯絡" and lists the departmental (non-personal) address 85mkt@85cafe.com [S23].
- That official contact page (https://www.85cafe.com/contactus.php) is the correct first surface for a licensing pitch; no individual marketing manager names are published on the official site (checked contactus.php and brand pages, 2026-07).
- Named public figures for context only: founder-chairman 吳政學 (official/Wikipedia), acting spokesperson 傅柏翔 (代理發言人, quoted in Nov 2025 investor-conference coverage) - IR-facing, not the marketing pitch path [S12, S10].
- Group HQ for Taiwan operations is in Taichung; corporate press for collabs flows through 工商時報 and lifestyle media, which the brand republishes on its own news page - the news page is a reliable public log of what HQ marketing greenlights [S1, S12].
- Timing note: with China being cut and the US carrying growth, Taiwan marketing's stated 2026 job is store-format rejuvenation and youth appeal - a receptive context for IP proposals (inference) [S8, S10, S11].

## 6. SUGGESTED ANGLE (suggestion, not a finding)

- Suggestion: SHUYA's dessert-prop, cream-and-chocolate bunny duo drops almost natively into 85度C's proven collab template - figurine-topped whole cake, NT$78 slices, DIY box, themed cups - and the Dec 2025 BUNNI KONBINY collab shows a cute bunny-plus-dessert aesthetic has already cleared their selection bar, while SHUYA's cream/chocolate palette maps directly onto actual cake SKUs rather than requiring adaptation.
- The pitch maps primarily to brand rejuvenation/identity (85度C publicly frames IP cakes as its answer to the aging-clientele narrative) and secondarily to seasonal foot traffic via the limited-quantity cake-wave format; the new 生甜甜圈 store format is a possible secondary canvas since donuts fit SHUYA's dessert-prop aesthetic.
- Honest caveat for the pitch: 85度C's verified partner list is top-tier licensed IP (Pokemon, Sanrio, Chiikawa, Conan, Shin-chan, Moomin, Frieren), so a lesser-known illustration IP should target an off-peak seasonal window or the donut sub-brand, and lead with cost/flexibility advantages over blockbuster licenses.
