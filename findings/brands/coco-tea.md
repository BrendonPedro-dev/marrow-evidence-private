# CoCo都可 CoCo Fresh Tea & Juice (Taiwan) - Partner-Brand Brief

CoCo都可 is Taiwan's largest hand-shaken (手搖) tea chain by store count (~600+ Taiwan stores, ~4,500 globally), operated by the Taipei-based Taiwan entity 億可國際飲食股份有限公司 (OCOCO International Co., Ltd., 統編 12915964) - so the operator is unambiguously Taiwanese and directly reachable, not a foreign franchisor.
The map-v2 "collab-history / [mixed]" premise VERIFIES cleanly for the TAIWAN market: CoCo has run a recent, repeatable character-IP-collab playbook - Sanrio 大耳狗喜拿 (Cinnamoroll) Dec 2025 and 名偵探柯南 (Detective Conan) July 2026, both全台 (nationwide) with cup art, themed pop-up stores, and add-on merch - so PBC would be pitching a brand that already believes character IP drives foot traffic and buys cute/kawaii licenses.
Structural caveat: CoCo's huge overseas franchise networks (China, US, SE Asia, etc.) run their own local collabs; those are NOT Taiwan activations and were excluded here - the collabs below are Taiwan-market only.

Research date: 2026-07-22.
Sources note: zh-TW sources are primary for operator, campaigns, and collabs; en/zh-CN used only for global scale. Social counts are search-metadata estimates (FB/IG not directly fetchable) and labelled as such. Registry verified via GCIS mirror.

## 1. IDENTITY

Official name: CoCo都可 / CoCo Fresh Tea & Juice (originally 都可茶飲; global brand site uses "CoCo Fresh Tea & Juice").
What they sell: hand-shaken (手搖) bubble/milk tea and fruit teas - signature lines include 珍珠奶茶, QQ鮮奶茶 (tapioca + pudding milk tea), 三兄弟 (three-topping series), 百香雙響砲, and seasonal fruit teas (葡萄柚果粒茶, 星空葡萄). Notably operates 全台統一品項與定價 (uniform nationwide menu and pricing), unusual for a franchise-heavy Taiwan tea chain.
Brand tone (first-party): mass-casual, playful, youthful; heavy use of cute/kawaii collab styling and QQ/pudding "療癒" (healing) framing in 2025-26 campaigns.

Ownership / parent + 統編 (registry-verified):

- 統一編號 (business ID): 12915964.
- Established: 2001-10-22; last registry update 2025-03-10; status active (核准設立).
- Capital: NT$90,000,000 (paid-in NT$90,000,000; 90,000 shares @ NT$1,000 par).
- Representative (代表人): 陳美英 (Chen Mei-ying). Board of four directors includes 洪肇水 (Hong Zhao-shui) and 郭靜芝 (Guo Jing-zhi).

## 2. AUDIENCE

Buyers/followers (estimate, inferred from product/campaign framing): mainstream, mass-casual young-adult and teen hand-shaken-tea drinkers nationwide - a very broad, high-volume, price-accessible base (600+ stores, uniform低價 menu), not a boutique/premium niche.
Collab-merch buyers skew toward kawaii/character collectors and younger fans: the 2025-26 collabs (Sanrio Cinnamoroll, Detective Conan) target 萌 (cute) merch collectors and anime/manga fandom; the李多慧 (baseball cheerleader) one-day-manager events target the large Taiwan baseball-fandom / cheerleader-fan audience.

## 5. DECISION CONTEXT

Structure: CoCo's global HQ is in Taiwan (Taipei/Shilin), and Taiwan is a company-operated + franchise market run by the local entity 億可國際飲食 - so Taiwan-market collabs (Cinnamoroll, Conan) are decided by the Taiwan operation/brand-marketing function, not by any overseas master-franchisee. The nationwide-uniform menu-and-pricing model implies a centralized brand/marketing team controlling activations 全台.
Ownership layer: closely held, family-founded (洪肇水 founder/chairman publicly; 陳美英 GCIS 代表人); both on the four-person board. No external corporate parent identified (contrast with Milksha/Jollibee).
No named individual marketing/brand-manager contact was found on official/public pages (no scraped personal contacts used).
Official contact surface (public inbound channels): 顧客服務專線 0800-890-998; 加盟諮詢專線 (02)2882-1826 #1590 (Mon-Fri 09:00-18:00); Facebook @cocofreshtea.tw; Instagram @cocotea.tw; LINE official account "CoCo都可" (page.line.me/554cchde); and the registered entity 億可國際飲食股份有限公司 (統編 12915964) via GCIS. PBC should route via these official channels; no rights/territory allocation is assumed beyond these stated facts.

## 6. SUGGESTED ANGLE (suggestion, not finding)

Suggestion: SHUYA 舒雅 (illustration IP) is the best-fit lead, consistent with its #8 ranking. CoCo has a proven, twice-yearly national mechanic - co-branded cup art + add-on collectible merch (bottles, bags, stickers) + themed pop-up stores - that is exactly the surface a warm illustrated-character/lifestyle IP fills; the December Cinnamoroll "療癒/萌" slot in particular maps onto SHUYA's illustrated dessert-prop visual style, and CoCo's audience is mass-casual and kawaii-merch-buying rather than premium.
Cheesy Duck 確幸鴨 is a credible secondary given CoCo's sticker-native add-on merch and mass casual audience (its collabs literally give away stickers over NT$100), and Bichi Mao 彼奇貓 could fit the same cute-character slot; but SHUYA leads on illustration/lifestyle fit.
Most credible campaign goal: seasonal foot-traffic + collectible-merch buzz (their add-on cup/bottle/sticker + themed-store template), with a secondary brand-warmth/identity lift - i.e. slot the IP into the same 12-month collab calendar the Cinnamoroll and Conan activations occupied. Open work: confirm the Taiwan brand-marketing contact and that a 2027 collab slot is uncommitted.
