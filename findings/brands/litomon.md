# 怪獸部落 LitoMon (怪獸製造有限公司) - Partner-Brand Brief

A founder-led Taiwan "全鮮肉寵食專家" (fresh-meat pet-food specialist) whose whole brand world is already built around friendly "小怪獸" (little monsters) and a cat-forward premium audience, making it a natural tonal host for a warm healing-cat character - but the map-v2 "existing collab history" premise did NOT verify: no character or licensed-IP collaboration was found under the queries run, so the routing note should be treated as false-until-proven.
Research date: 2026-07-22.
Sources are zh-TW primary (official site, GCIS registry mirrors, LINE), with zh-HK distributor pages as secondary; all verbatim Chinese quotes below were curl-verified against the fetched page unless explicitly flagged otherwise.

## 2. AUDIENCE

- Primary buyers are Taiwanese cat and dog owners who pay up for premium fresh-meat nutrition, addressed in brand copy as "毛拔麻/怪獸家長" and told the brand is a "全鮮肉寵食專家" (stated positioning) [S1, S6].
- The product mix and imagery skew cat-forward (主食罐/湯罐/貓砂/凍乾 lead the range, and the IG handle and posts foreground cats), so the follower base is likely cat-owner-heavy (estimate/inference from product mix, not a disclosed demographic) [S1, S8].
- Value segment: buyers who prioritize ingredient transparency and high meat content over lowest price, evidenced by the "全鮮肉"/"市售最高肉含量" and AAFCO/NRC framing (stated brand positioning, not audited) [S1, S5].
- Novelty-seeking sub-segment: the brand deliberately courts curiosity/word-of-mouth buyers with oddity SKUs (青蛙罐, 蟲蟲/野味 cans), implying an audience receptive to playful, collectible, limited-run product ideas (inference from product line) [S4].
- Geographic: Taiwan primary, with an active Hong Kong/Macau consumer base served through named authorized distributors [S7].
- No official first-party age/gender/income breakdown was published; all demographic shape above is inference or brand self-description, not audited data (labelled estimate).

## 5. DECISION CONTEXT

- Structure: small, founder/owner-led private limited company (怪獸製造有限公司, sole 代表人 劉亭君, capital NT$1,000,000); there is no franchise HQ layer and no evidence of a group/parent decision tier - so the decision surface is almost certainly the owner plus a lean in-house marketing/e-commerce team [S2].
- Marketing motion appears in-house and DTC/performance-led: heavy owned-channel content (blog SEO, LINE, IG/FB/YouTube), an affiliate program, and self-run online expos, rather than agency-fronted PR campaigns (inference from the channel footprint) [S11, S12, S13].
- No named marketing manager or brand-lead contact is published on official/public pages; the only named individual is the registry 代表人 劉亭君 (a legal-representative name, not a stated marketing contact) [S2].
- Official contact surface (use these, do not scrape personal contacts): general service email service@litomon.com, LINE official account @LitoMon (https://page.line.me/boe9799z), customer line 02-25630933 (週一至週五 09:30-18:00), all curl-verified from the site footer; these are corporate/customer-service surfaces, and no dedicated licensing-cooperation email was found on the site as of 2026-07 [S1, S6].

## 6. SUGGESTED ANGLE (suggestion, not a finding)

- Suggestion: lead with Bichi Mao 彼奇貓 as a warm, healing cat character that can live natively inside LitoMon's existing "小怪獸" brand world and its cat-forward premium audience, positioned as a limited-edition gift/seasonal collectible (co-branded 凍乾/主食罐 trial box or gift set) that gives the DTC store a fresh reason-to-buy and shareable identity, rather than as a rented mascot.
- Tie to evidence: the brand already leans on playful, collectible, word-of-mouth product framing (青蛙罐/蟲蟲 novelty SKUs) and runs its own online expos with new-customer hooks, so a limited character collab maps cleanly onto an existing merchandising and acquisition playbook [S4, S11].
- Goal mapping: primarily brand identity + awareness (a distinctive, ownable character to differentiate a mid-size DTC pet-food brand), with a secondary seasonal/acquisition play timed to an online-expo or gifting window; note that the map's "existing collab history" rationale is NOT the reason to pitch - the real fit is tonal/audience, and the collab would likely be this brand's first character-IP tie-up, which is a positioning opportunity but also means no internal precedent to point to.
