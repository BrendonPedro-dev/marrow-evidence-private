# snapshot (Snapshot 妞拍貼 - Korean-style photo-booth chain) - Partner-Brand Brief

Snapshot 妞拍貼 is a Taiwan-wide Korean-style photo-sticker chain whose entire business model already runs on rotating IP/character/illustrator photo frames - it publishes a public tiered price list (from NT$1,800/month), a self-service application form, and an archive of roughly 260 past outreach cases, making it one of the most collab-native, lowest-friction partner surfaces in this list for a sticker-native character IP.

Research date: 2026-07-22.
All zh-TW facts below were curl-verified against the fetched page or the GCIS/g0v registry unless explicitly marked as a search-metadata estimate.

## 1. IDENTITY

Disambiguation note (read first).
The task flags that several Korean-style photo-booth chains operate in Taiwan (Photoism, Photomatic, 인생네컷 Life4Cuts, Snapio, SerendiPIC, fotome, and others).
The brand literally named "snapshot" is confirmed as Snapshot 妞拍貼 / 韓式拍貼機, whose official Instagram handle is @snapshot.tw and whose operator brand and website is Snapfoto (snapfoto.co) [S1, S3].
This is unambiguous.
The self-service photo-booth product carries the "Snapshot" wordmark, the store fascia reads "Snapshot 韓式拍貼機, 妞拍貼", and the operator site self-describes as "Snapshot妞拍貼指定運營機構" [S1, S5].
The map-v2 routing note ("photobooth with extensive outreach events") matches this brand's public 合作案例 archive exactly, so I proceed with high confidence [S9].

## 2. AUDIENCE

- Core users are young, social-media-active consumers who take group/couple/fan photos and share them; the product's whole appeal is the printed keepsake plus the collectible collab frame (inference from product design and the collab slate) [S1, S7].
- Skew is very likely young and female-leaning, consistent with the Korean 인생네컷-style photo-booth category, but I found no sourced demographic breakdown for this brand specifically, so treat the "young female skew" as a category estimate, not a stated figure (estimate) [S1].
- A large, explicitly courted segment is K-pop / idol / drama fandom running 應援 (fan-support) campaigns: the brand publishes a dedicated 應援合作計畫 letting fans design a custom idol frame deployed fleet-wide, which signals a heavy fandom audience (stated program, audience inferred) [S12].
- Named fandom instances confirm this: a G.E.M.-adjacent K-pop artist frame (李雅英 Lee Ya-ying fan-support), M.O.N.T (Korean group) fan frames, and a singer 周深 debut-anniversary support frame (see section 3) [S10, S11, S13].
- A second courted segment is students / campus events (畢業展, 藝術季 sponsorships) and the local illustrator/繪師 creator community, indicating an audience that also values indie/cute original-character art, not only licensed blockbusters (stated via sponsorship cases) [S9, S10, S8].

## 5. DECISION CONTEXT

- Collab decisions are run centrally and productized by the operator 享印生活股份有限公司 (Snapfoto), not by individual franchise stores; the company publishes standardized collab tiers, an application workflow, and a self-service form, which indicates an in-house partnerships/marketing function rather than ad-hoc owner deals [S1, S7, S8, S12].
- Public, published cooperation tiers for illustrator/IP frame collabs (from the 老喬報 × Snapshot creator-frame plan, curl-verified) [S8]:
  - Plan A (basic): NT$1,800, 1 month, whole fleet, no revenue share, NT$500 discount-voucher support.
  - Plan B (revenue-share): NT$3,000, 3 months, whole fleet, includes sales revenue share, NT$3,000 vouchers.
  - Plan C (invitation-only / bespoke): fee by project, flexible duration, whole fleet or designated stores, sales revenue share, high-spec promo resources.
- Stated ideal partner profile for those tiers: illustrators / 繪師 / original-character creators, especially 二次元 (ACG), Q版 (chibi), and 動物擬人化 (animal-personification) styles - a profile that fits cute character-sticker IP directly [S8].
- Fan-support (應援) collabs follow a documented 6-step workflow (application form -> LINE contact -> template files -> design return and colour test -> trial print within 5 business days -> go-live plus optional NT$100-voucher giveaway), and they recommend starting about 4 weeks before an event [S12].
- Official cooperation / business-inquiry surfaces (no scraped personal contacts):
  - Business email: service@snapfoto.co (on the operator site and collab pages) [S1, S7, S12].
  - Business phone: 02-3765-5060 (operator site footer) [S1].
  - Instagram DM: @snapshot.tw for 合作 inquiries [S3, S12].
  - Self-service application form (Ragic): the creator-frame plan links a live application form at ap12.ragic.com/Snapshottw (public form URL, not a personal contact) [S8].
- Named person from public registry only (context, not a pitch contact): 陳彥廷 (董事長/代表人 of 享印生活股份有限公司); other directors 林伯彥, 謝旻軒, 江俊翰 and supervisor 邱奕慈 appear in the GCIS filing [S2].
- No individual marketing-manager name is published on the official brand/collab pages (checked snapfoto.co collab and 品牌合作 pages, 2026-07); the correct first touch is service@snapfoto.co or the IG DM plus the Ragic form.

## 6. SUGGESTED ANGLE (suggestion, not a finding)

Suggestion: lead with Cheesy Duck 確幸鴨.
Snapshot's own published ideal-partner profile is "二次元 / Q版 / 動物擬人化" original-character art on a printable photo frame [S8], which maps almost one-to-one onto a sticker-native, upbeat, all-ages animal character like Cheesy Duck - the frame is exactly the sticker-adjacent surface the character already lives on, and the low-friction NT$1,800-3,000 published tiers make a whole-fleet Cheesy Duck frame drop cheap to test.
Jolly Gee Studio is a very strong second (Korean retro-kitsch reads as photo-frame décor and suits the K-pop/응원 fan-photo aesthetic Snapshot already courts), and SHUYA is the natural pick if the goal is a physical photo-zone / co-brand limited store (snapshot.twip 聯名限定店 shows they run themed storefronts) rather than a frame-only drop [S7, S8].
Flag the campaign goal as primarily awareness plus audience acquisition into a young, fandom-driven, female-leaning base, with a secondary seasonal foot-traffic play if paired with a store-front pop-up; the whole-fleet 1-3 month frame is a fast, measurable pilot before committing to a themed-booth escalation.
