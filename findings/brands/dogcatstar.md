# 汪喵星球 DogCatStar (自力耕生股份有限公司 / 牧羊人集團) - Partner-Brand Brief

Taiwan's self-styled cat-can "罐罐王" already runs licensed-IP collabs (Disney) and welfare-linked drama tie-ins, and its whole brand promise ("始終以毛孩為本", a team of self-described "貓奴狗僕") is a near-native fit for a warm healing-cat character like Bichi Mao - but it is a founder-led group that outsources campaign PR, and it carries a live wastewater-pollution ruling worth pre-clearing.

Research date: 2026-07-22.
All zh-TW quotes below were curl-verified against the fetched page unless explicitly marked otherwise.

## 1. IDENTITY

- Official brand name: 汪喵星球 (English: DogCatStar; also written Dog Cat Star / DOG CAT STAR).
- Brand tagline used across channels: "始終以毛孩為本" ("always putting fur-kids first"), seen in the Instagram account display name (stated) [S8].
- Legal / operating entity: 自力耕生股份有限公司 (English registered name PLANDDO CO., LTD.) [S12, S13].
- Taiwan GCIS / 商工登記 record: 統一編號 54652703; 資本總額 NT$70,000,000; 代表人 孫宗德; 公司狀況 營業中; 核准設立(成立)日期 2013-11-28; 營業項目 including 動物飼品製造業 and 其他商品批發經紀業; registered address 臺北市松山區南京東路4段133巷5弄7號1樓; listed contact phone 03-5724831 (a Hsinchu 03 area code, consistent with northern operations) [S12, S13].
- Note a founding-year discrepancy to hold loosely: the company registry shows incorporation 2013-11-28, a jobs profile states "founded 2014", and brand-facing copy elsewhere says 2015; treat 2013-11-28 as the authoritative legal incorporation date and the later years as brand-narrative "launch" framing [S12, S13, S5].
- Parent / group: 牧羊人集團 (Shepherd Group), which company coverage says was formed as a group in 2021 to hold multiple pet brands, with 汪喵星球 as its flagship and founding brand [S1].
- Sister brands inside the group: 怪獸部落 (Monster Tribe), HeroMama, 超凝小姐, described by 商周 as part of a multi-brand ("6大分眾品牌") portfolio [S1, S5].
- Founders / leadership: co-founders 孫宗德 (also the group 執行長/CEO and the registered 代表人) and 鄭文閔, described as 清華大學 engineering graduates who started with roughly NT$2 million in capital [S2, S5].
- What they sell: cat and dog wet food / cans (罐頭), freeze-dried raw food (凍乾主食, using liquid-nitrogen freeze-drying and HPP ultra-high-pressure processing), treats (零食), supplements (保健品), and tofu cat litter (豆腐貓砂); the about page frames the core promise as "打造頂級、營養又平易近人的濕食" (curl-verified) [S3, S1, S4].
- Scale (production): the group operates a food factory in 桃園新屋 (Taoyuan, Xinwu) that 商周 says produces "每年產出上千萬個罐頭" (tens of millions of cans a year) with液態氮冷凍乾燥 and vacuum rapid-cooling equipment (stated by third-party profile) [S1].
- Scale (people): roughly 400+ employees, up from a 3-person startup, per a 2025 SME-agency profile and a jobs profile [S4, S2].
- Scale (revenue): 2022 group revenue exceeded NT$1 billion (2022年營收突破10億元), with a stated five-year average growth rate over 50% ("過去5年平均成長率逾50%") [S1].
- Market-position claim: the brand is repeatedly called the cat-can category leader ("罐罐王") and is stated to hold #1 share in pet cans, tofu cat litter, and freeze-dried in Taiwan (stated in group/press framing, not an audited figure) [S1, S5].
- Geographic reach: Taiwan primary, plus Hong Kong/Macau (described as a leading position), South Korea, Singapore/Southeast Asia, and the US via Amazon [S1, S4, S5].
- Official site: https://www.dogcatstar.com/ [S3].

## 2. AUDIENCE

- Primary buyers are Taiwanese cat and dog owners ("毛拔麻" / fur-parents) who self-identify as devoted pet caregivers; the brand explicitly mirrors this identity, stating over 85% of its own team owns pets and calling itself "一群貓奴狗僕" (curl-verified) [S3].
- Category weighting skews cat-forward given the brand's flagship "罐罐王" cat-can identity and cat-litter leadership, so the follower base is likely cat-owner-heavy (estimate/inference from product mix, not a disclosed demographic) [S1].
- Value-positioning signal: the brand targets owners who want premium nutrition without luxury pricing, per its curl-verified promise "健康的寵物食品不是奢侈品，要能讓每隻毛小孩長期享用" - i.e. mid-to-upmarket but mass-accessible, not ultra-premium-only (stated brand positioning) [S3].
- Cause-aligned segment: the audience overlaps with animal-welfare / rescue supporters, evidenced by the brand's welfare tie-ins (search-and-rescue dog fundraising, the 黑喵知情 50%-profit donation) and its stated use of rescue volunteers on the team (see sections 3-4) [S3, S9, S10].
- No official first-party age/gender/income breakdown was published; all demographic shape above is inference or brand self-description, not audited data (labelled estimate).

## 3. RECENT MOMENTUM (Jan 2025 - Jul 2026)

- 2025-05-07: independent animal-media outlet 窩窩 reported that 環境部 (Ministry of Environment) rejected 自力耕生's administrative appeal on 2025-04-30 and upheld a wastewater-pollution fine; the underlying incident was a 2024-10-04 inspection at the 桃園新屋 area where white foamy wastewater from tank-cleaning reached 新屋溪, fined NT$67,500 by 桃園市環保局 under 水污染防治法 (this is a reputational/ESG risk item, not a campaign) [S11].
- 2025-06-20: SME/agency and trade coverage highlighted overseas momentum, stating the brand achieved 7x revenue growth on Amazon US within one year ("只花了一年時間，就在亞馬遜美國站創下營收7倍成長") and framing a region-by-region product strategy (e.g. tofu cat litter for the US) [S4, S6].
- 2025 (issue-dated profile, 商周 第1923期): a feature positioned 汪喵星球 as the毛孩 "罐罐王" that "一招集團化攻進歐美日韓", detailing the multi-brand group strategy, the 桃園新屋 factory, and >NT$1bn revenue [S1, S5].
- 2025-12-05 to 2025-12-08: 汪喵星球 exhibited at the 台北寵物展 (Taipei Pet Show) at 台北南港展覽館二館 (Nangang Exhibition Center Hall 2), promoted on its own blog as "汪喵 2025 最後一場台北寵物展" with free-ticket giveaways (recurring brand presence at the major Taiwan pet expo) [S7].
- Employer-brand momentum: 2025 recruiting/culture coverage described the group actively hiring across a 400+ person workforce and referenced a high-view "動漫尾牙" (anime-themed year-end party) as a culture talking point; the specific view figure could not be independently fetched (the source returned HTTP 403), so treat the "破千萬觀看" claim as unverified [S17].

## 4. COLLAB / IP HISTORY

- Disney 迪士尼 - 2023 (published 2023-04) - official Disney IP license timed to Disney's 100th anniversary; a limited-edition licensed product line including a Toy Story cat-scratch house (Paw-Tex patented anti-scratch material, made in Taiwan) plus a Tokyo Disneyland ticket giveaway.
  The campaign PR page states, curl-verified: "今年適逢迪士尼100周年，汪喵星球成功取得迪士尼正版IP授權，並特別推出一系列限定商品，邀請毛小孩與家長一同歡度迪士尼百年慶典" [S18].
  Public read on results (from the executing PR agency 布爾喬亞/VocalMiddle): 30+ media placements and calculated PR value over NT$6 million (agency-reported, single source, not independently audited) [S18].
  Note a separate search-surfaced claim that 汪喵 was "台灣首個被迪士尼邀請戰略合作的寵物企業" (first pet company Disney invited into strategic collaboration) appeared only in a search summary and was not verified verbatim on a fetched page - treat as unverified [S6].
- 黑喵知情 (Taiwanese TV drama) - 2020-08 - began as product sponsorship and expanded into a joint public-welfare plan; the brand states the collaboration donates 50% of profits to animal-welfare groups, and coverage notes 100+ animals (including rescue/中途 animals) appeared in filming (older than the momentum window but a genuine IP/drama tie-in) [S9].
- 台灣偵搜犬協會 (Taiwan Search & Rescue Dog Association) - ongoing - a standing 公益 fundraising/donation program tied to product purchases, referenced on the official site and LINE content (welfare partnership rather than a character IP) [S10, S15].
- A "白貓Project" (Shironeko Project) fandom-wiki page titled "汪喵星球合作贈禮關卡" surfaced in search; this appears likely to be coincidental in-game stage naming rather than a confirmed brand collaboration, so it is excluded pending verification (flagged, not counted).

## 5. DECISION CONTEXT

- Structure: founder-led private group. Co-founder 孫宗德 is both group 執行長/CEO and the registered 代表人 of 自力耕生股份有限公司; co-founder 鄭文閔 is the other principal; there is no franchise HQ layer (this is a manufacturer/brand, not a store franchise) [S2, S5, S12].
- Marketing appears to run as an in-house team plus outsourced PR: dbc/SME coverage describes a social-video-heavy in-house marketing motion (pet-care education, product testimonials, culture content), while the Disney campaign was executed by external agency 布爾喬亞公關顧問 (VocalMiddle), which publicly credits itself with the campaign's media/PR outcomes [S4, S18].
- Implication for a pitch: the real decision surface is likely group marketing/brand leadership under the founders, and a licensed-IP collaboration precedent already exists internally (Disney), so the concept of paying for and executing a character license is not new to them (inference) [S18, S1].
- Named public figures for context only: 孫宗德 (執行長/co-founder, named in 商周 and SME coverage and the company registry) and 鄭文閔 (co-founder); these are leadership/PR-facing names, not a published marketing-manager contact [S2, S5, S12].
- Official contact surface: the correct first surfaces are the official site https://www.dogcatstar.com/ (which hosts brand, store, and cooperation-oriented pages), the LINE official account (315k friends, an active 2-way channel), and the Facebook/Instagram business pages; no individual marketing personal email or phone should be scraped, and no dedicated licensing-cooperation email was verified on the official site as of 2026-07 (the listed registry phone 03-5724831 is a corporate line, not a marketing contact) [S3, S15, S12].

## 6. SUGGESTED ANGLE (suggestion, not a finding)

- Suggestion: lead with Bichi Mao 彼奇貓, whose warm healing-cat style maps almost one-to-one onto DogCatStar's own "始終以毛孩為本" / "貓奴狗僕" identity and its cat-forward "罐罐王" audience, so the character reads as an extension of the brand's voice rather than a bolt-on license.
- Tie to evidence: the brand has already licensed and shipped a big-ticket character IP (Disney, 2023) and has a demonstrated welfare-collaboration reflex (黑喵知情 50%-profit donation, 偵搜犬 fundraising), which lines up with Bichi Mao's animal-welfare-friendly positioning - a natural hook for a limited-edition can/treat/凍乾 pack or seasonal gift set with a co-branded donation element.
- Goal mapping: this maps primarily to brand identity/audience (deepening emotional fit with existing cat-owner followers) and awareness (a fresh, ownable character versus Disney's rented halo), with a secondary seasonal foot-traffic/gifting play around the 台北寵物展 December window; pre-clear the 2024-2025 wastewater ruling in messaging so a welfare-flavored collab does not invite scrutiny.
