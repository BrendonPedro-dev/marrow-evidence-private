# 讀書共和國 Book Republic Publishing Group (遠足文化) - Partner Brief

Taiwan's largest independent trade-publishing federation - one legal entity (遠足文化事業) umbrella-branding 40 to 80+ editor-led imprints, 1,000+ new titles a year - whose company registry literally lists 書籍、文具零售業 and 印刷業 as business scope, giving the map's "publisher / stationery / print-goods fit" logic a genuine documentary basis; but the map's claimed "prior collab" with Jolly Gee is unverifiable and appears to be an error.

Research date: 2026-07-22.
Sources are primarily zh-TW (company registry, official group site, Taiwan trade press); one French-language publisher directory (hupinching.tw) and English YouTube channel metadata used for cross-checks.

## Disambiguation (read first)

- The map's "Book Republic" is almost certainly 讀書共和國出版集團 / Book Republic Publishing Group, the trade name of 遠足文化事業股份有限公司 (Walkers Cultural Enterprise Ltd.) [S1][S2][S6].
- Confidence: high. The group's own English branding is literally "Book Republic Publishing Group" (YouTube channel title) and "Bookrep" (site copyright), and its two official domains are bookrep.com.tw (bookstore) and bookrepublic.com.tw (corporate) [S2][S3][S7].
- Both domains resolve to the same group; bookrepublic.com.tw is the corporate/企業理念 face and bookrep.com.tw is the e-commerce bookstore [S1][S7].
- No separate English-named stationery-only "Book Republic" brand was found in Taiwan under the queries run; the publisher group is the only defensible match, and the map's own routing note ("publisher category") points at it [S3][S4].
- Caveat on the routing note: the "plus prior collab" premise could not be verified (see Section 4) and should be treated as likely erroneous until PBC's own CRM confirms it.

## 1. IDENTITY

- Legal entity: 遠足文化事業股份有限公司 / WALKERS CULTURAL ENTERPRISE LTD.; 統一編號 70446624 [S6][S8].
- Group trade name: 讀書共和國出版集團 (English "Book Republic Publishing Group" / "Bookrep") [S2][S3][S7].
- Registry facts: 代表人 郭重興; 資本額 NT$88,000,000; 核准設立 1999-10-22; registered address 新北市新店區民權路108之2號9樓 [S6][S8].
- Business scope (registry, verbatim): "書籍、文具零售業。運動器材零售業。玩具、娛樂用品零售業。包裝材料零售業。國際貿易業。一般廣告服務業。雜誌業。圖書出版業。有聲出版業。印刷業。印刷品裝訂及加工業" - i.e. stationery retail, toys/entertainment-goods retail, printing and print-finishing are all inside their licensed scope, not just book publishing [S6].

- Structure/scale: a decentralised federation of imprints; the group site's 出版社專區 lists roughly 70-80 brands, while trade-press profiles cite "about 40" core imprints; ~160 total staff, ~100 new books per month, and 1,000+ new titles per year [S3][S4][S5].
- Major imprints include 木馬文化, 野人文化, 八旗文化, 大家出版, 左岸文化, 衛城出版, 遠足文化, 幸福文化, 方舟文化, 大牌出版, and children's houses 小熊出版, 步步出版, 字畝文化, 小樹文化 [S3][S5].

## 2. AUDIENCE

- No first-party demographic disclosure was found; the following is inferred from catalogue and channel signals and is labelled estimate.
- Estimate: the buyer base is broad adult general-trade readers across humanities, current affairs, business and lifestyle - the group's identity is "multi-voice / diverse viewpoints," so audience is segmented per imprint rather than a single persona [S1][S4].
- Estimate: a distinct parent-and-child segment is served by the dedicated children's imprints (小熊, 步步, 字畝, 小樹), which is the segment most relevant to character-IP goods [S3][S5].
- Stated: the group frames itself around serious/knowledge-depth reading ("具備知識深度、容納多元觀點"), skewing its core toward committed adult readers rather than impulse gift buyers [S1].
- The 閱讀護照 loyalty scheme signals a repeat-purchase, membership-driven direct-to-reader base alongside channel sales (博客來, momo, 誠品) [S9][S13].

## 5. DECISION CONTEXT

- Governance model is the single most important fact for approach: the group runs as a decentralised "變形蟲" (amoeba) federation where each imprint's 總編輯 acts as a 準經營者 (quasi-owner) with autonomy over editorial and marketing, while the central platform supplies only finance, sales, distribution and admin [S4].
- Practical implication: there is usually no single group-HQ gatekeeper for a creative/IP project; the real decision-maker is the specific imprint's editor-in-chief whose list fits the IP (for Jolly Gee, most likely a children's or lifestyle/illustration imprint such as 小熊出版, 字畝文化, or a design-forward adult imprint) [S4][S5].
- Founder 郭重興 (社長) established and still represents the entity in the registry; leadership is founder-defined but operationally distributed to imprints [S4][S6].
- A group-level 版權 / foreign-rights function exists (a named rights contact for the group appears on a public French-language publisher directory, hupinching.tw); per PBC sourcing rules the individual's personal email is not reproduced here - use the official surfaces below instead [S14].
- Official contact surfaces (public): customer service (02)2218-1417 and service email service@bookrepclub.com.tw; per-imprint editorial contacts are published on each imprint's page within bookrep.com.tw; corporate enquiries via bookrepublic.com.tw [S7][S9][S3].

## 6. SUGGESTED ANGLE (suggestion, not a finding)

- Suggestion: pitch Jolly Gee not as "another author's book" but as a design-led illustrated/lifestyle title plus in-book premium (stickers, notecards) landing inside a specific imprint's list - leaning on the fact that the group's own registry scope already covers stationery, toys and printing, so a character-goods extension is native to their licensed business rather than a stretch [S6].
- Best entry point is imprint-level, not HQ: target the editor-in-chief of a children's house (小熊/字畝) for a Korean retro-kitsch picture-book-plus-merch format, or a design/lifestyle imprint (大牌/野人) for an adult gift-book format, matching the JOLLY/LYGEE/LYLEE cafe-and-stationery world [S4][S5].
- Campaign-goal mapping: primarily AWARENESS/IDENTITY (bringing a fresh Korean design IP to a text-first publisher's shelf) with a secondary SEASONAL/FOOT-TRAFFIC angle via the group's continuous joint book fairs and its DTC 閱讀護照 base [S9][S13].
