# heme (喜蜜) - Partner-Brand Brief

heme is a 100%-MIT open-shelf ("drugstore") color-cosmetics brand run by 喜蜜國際企業股份有限公司, a related enterprise of listed label 華研國際音樂 (HIM, TWSE 8446) - which is why S.H.E were its original spokespeople.
Its collab DNA is real but is **designer/illustrator packaging**, not character-IP licensing: no verified Taiwan character-IP makeup collab was found (the "cute cartoon eyeshadow" hits trace to retailer site-nav categories, not heme products), so the map's "[mixed] prior collab activity" premise does NOT verify as character-IP work.
Also disambiguate hard: "HEMEKO" (a Korean K-beauty platform) and @hemecosmetics_jp/Japan pop-ups are separate - do not attribute them to heme 喜蜜.

Research date: 2026-07-22.
Sources note: registry-first (GCIS via g0v mirror); brand statements before third-party coverage; all follower counts are labelled search-metadata estimates because IG/FB counts were not directly fetchable; single-source and inferred claims flagged inline.

## 1. IDENTITY

- Brand name: heme (喜蜜). Brand-name gloss "he & me" = cosmetics anyone can use regardless of gender/skin type (stated across brand story and multiple third-party write-ups) [1][6][9].
- Operating company: 喜蜜國際企業股份有限公司, English trade name "HEME INTERNATIONAL INC." (twincn company record) [2].
- Government registry (GCIS open data via g0v mirror, fetched 2026-07-22): 統一編號 80176493, status 核准設立, capital / paid-in capital both NTD 180,000,000, responsible person 呂世玉, registered address 臺北市松山區民權東路3段144號15樓, incorporated 2003-04-02 (民國92年); primary industry class "其他化粧品零售", last amendment 2026-03-17 [3].
- Ownership / group: 喜蜜國際 is a related enterprise of 華研國際音樂 (HIM International Music, TWSE 8446); 呂燕清 (華研 founder/chairman) and 呂世玉 sit on the board of both companies (likely relatives), and 華研 group has publicly discussed steering affiliates including heme toward a public listing (Chinese-language business blog citing 華研 plans - single-source, flagged) [4][5]. This ownership link is the direct explanation for S.H.E (a 華研 act) serving as heme's launch spokesgroup.
- What they sell: affordable open-shelf (開架) color cosmetics and light skincare - eyeshadow/blush/contour palettes, lipsticks and lip tints, concealers, foundation, brow products, setting powder, plus daily/monthly soft contact lenses; positioned "100% MIT", cruelty-free, Taiwan-native ingredients [1][6].
- Founding cadence: company incorporated 2003-03/04; the heme brand formally launched Feb 2005 after a ~2-year prep period [1][9].
- Channels / scale: sold through open-shelf retail (Watsons 屈臣氏, Cosmed 康是美, Poya 寶雅) and marketplaces (momo, Yahoo, Shopee 官方旗艦店 @iloveheme, Pinkoi); no heme-owned retail-store or department-counter network was found - it is a distribution/open-shelf brand, not a store operator [7][8][10]. Headcount not published on official pages.
- Overseas presence (brand-stated, older): extended to Singapore, Malaysia, Hong Kong, Macao, Canada and China; Japan retail via Loft/PLAZA from ~2021 (context, not recent momentum) [6][11].
- Official site: https://www.hemecosmetics.com.tw (legacy domain www.heme.com.tw still referenced in email address); customer line 0800-721-788, service-heme@heme.com.tw [7].
- Instagram @hemecosmetics: ~53K followers, ~922 posts (search-metadata estimate; a third-party influencer index shows 48.3K - both estimates, not curl-verified) [12][13].
- Facebook facebook.com/hemecosmetics: ~40K followers (search-metadata estimate, not directly fetchable) [12][14].
- Separate Japan-market IG @hemecosmetics_jp ~6,099 followers (search-metadata estimate) - same brand, Japan account [12].

## 2. AUDIENCE

- Brand-stated target: students and 小資 (budget-conscious) young office workers wanting high-CP-value products; lines are framed as "聰明保養"/"彩妝零失手" (smart skincare / foolproof makeup) - stated positioning [1][9].
- Gender framing: the "he & me" concept is officially unisex, but marketing, spokespeople (S.H.E) and product mix (lip/eye/base makeup) skew heavily female - inference from product line and campaigns; no official gender split published (estimate) [6][9].
- Price band: most products under ~NTD 500, reinforcing a young / entry-level mass-market shopper (third-party makeup coverage; estimate) [9].
- No official age/gender statistics were found; all demographic reads above the stated positioning are estimates.

## 3. RECENT MOMENTUM (approx. Jan 2025 - Jul 2026)

- Momentum is product-launch-driven, not campaign/collab-driven; no major PBC-relevant character activation surfaced in the last 18 months.
- 2026-01 (approx.): launched a 三色修容盤 (tri-color contour palette), two shades 00 白皙 / 01 自然 (search summary of Baidu/brand entry; month stated, treat as approximate) [11].
- 2025-2026: "New In" cadence on the official site includes 輕透持妝蜜粉/蜜粉餅 (setting powder/compact), 舒芙蕾唇頰霜 (souffle lip-and-cheek pot), 水光腮紅液, 校色遮瑕盤 and refreshed 八色眼影盤 "升級版明星商品" - a steady bestseller-refresh rhythm rather than event marketing (official collections page, fetched 2026-07-22) [8][15].
- Ongoing Japan/overseas retail distribution continues (Loft/PLAZA, cross-border marketplaces), but no 2025-2026 Taiwan press campaign was found [11].
- Note (false-positive excluded): 2025 Japan "VT × HEMEKO" pop-ups and Korea "HEMEKO" store openings belong to the Korean K-beauty brand HEMEKO, NOT heme 喜蜜 - excluded from momentum [16].

## 4. COLLAB/IP HISTORY

heme's real collaboration history is with **illustrators/designers on packaging and with a music-label spokes-relationship**, not character-IP licensing:

- Logo/branding: brand mark drawn by international fashion illustrator Jeffery Fuvimari (known for Madonna's "The English Roses" children's book) - brand-story claim [1][6].
- Local designer/illustrator packaging collabs: heme is described (brand story / third-party summaries) as an early Taiwanese brand to bring local creative designers - names cited include 聶永真 (Nieh Yung-chen) and "Baw" - onto packaging for BB-cream and "City" makeup series (secondary-source summaries of the brand story; specific designer names are single-source, flagged) [6][9].
- Spokesgroup: S.H.E were heme's first spokesgroup at 2005 launch and reportedly endorsed twice ("聰明保養小教主"), explained by the shared 華研/HIM ownership; talent tie, not a character-IP collab [5][9][17].
- Character / cartoon IP collab (Sanrio, Disney, Line Friends, etc.): **None found.** The "heme Hello Kitty / Disney / 汪汪隊 / 哆啦A夢 / 蛋黃哥 / 蠟筆小新 eyeshadow" appearance in search results traces to retailer 小三美日's store-wide character navigation categories, not to heme products; a direct check of heme's own catalog (e.g. 八色眼影盤 style options are colourway names 淡紫/杏粉/柔櫻, and the full official product list) shows no co-branded/licensed character SKUs [18][19][8].
  - Queries run (returned no genuine heme character-IP collab): "heme 喜蜜 聯名 卡通 角色 彩妝"; "heme 喜蜜 三麗鷗 Sanrio 迪士尼 卡娜赫拉 限量"; "heme 喜蜜 Hello Kitty / 汪汪隊 聯名 限定"; "heme 喜蜜 聯名 卡通 授權 IP 眼影盤 2023 2024"; "heme 喜蜜 快閃 期間限定 聯名 2025 2026"; plus a direct crawl of the official collections/all page and the s3 八色眼影盤 product page (2026-07-22).

## 5. DECISION CONTEXT

- Marketing likely sits inside 喜蜜國際 itself with group-level influence from 華研國際 (HIM) - the brand shares directors (呂燕清, 呂世玉) with the listed music/entertainment parent, and its signature marketing lever historically was label-artist endorsement (S.H.E), suggesting talent/brand decisions can run through the group (inference from shared directorship + endorsement history; no published org chart) [3][4][5].
- The brand recruits its own heme 彩妝 marketing/design staff (104/1111/518 job boards under 喜蜜國際企業), consistent with an in-house brand-marketing team rather than an external agency - inference from recruiting presence [20].
- Official contact surface (published by heme): customer-service line 0800-721-788 and email service-heme@heme.com.tw on the official site footer; Shopee 官方旗艦店 (@iloveheme) and IG/FB DMs are the public inbound channels [7][10]. No business-development or licensing-manager email was published on official pages.
- Named individuals: only publicly named people are registry directors 呂世玉 (responsible person) and 呂燕清 (group chairman) - both from company registry, not marketing contacts [3][4]. No marketing-manager name appears on official pages; outreach should start via the published service channel / official social DMs, and can reference the 華研 group relationship.

## 6. SUGGESTED ANGLE (suggestion, not a finding)

Suggestion: heme is a plausible-but-unproven fit - lead with **Jolly Gee Studio** on tone (playful, retro-kitsch matches heme's youthful open-shelf, illustration-forward packaging heritage), but pitch it as heme's **first character-IP makeup collab** rather than "more of the same," because no prior character-IP collab was verified - the precedent is designer-packaging, so a Jolly Gee limited-edition palette/lip set with collectible packaging is the natural bridge from what heme already does.
Frame the campaign goal as **awareness / brand-identity refresh** (a shareable collectible drop to re-energize a 20-year-old brand and lift social reach), with a secondary **seasonal-sales** hook via open-shelf retail (Watsons/Cosmed/Poya) and the Shopee flagship - not foot-traffic, since heme runs no owned stores.
As a second card, Cheesy Duck 確幸鴨 or SHUYA 舒雅 could suit a softer/cute-cat limited-edition packaging play; treat all of this as hypothesis - the honest read is heme has no character-IP collab track record, so expect a first-timer sell and confirm decision authority against the 華研 group.
