# PENROTE 筆樂 - Partner Brief

A ~30-year Taiwanese pen/stationery manufacturer-distributor (operating entity 丞儀企業有限公司) that has quietly become a serial character-IP licensee: its own catalog already runs full multi-SKU stationery ranges under at least eight licensed character brands - Taiwanese healing/LINE-sticker IP (柴語錄 Shiba Says, 醜白兔, 露咖貓 Looka Cat) and Japanese IP (胡子碰碰 OHIGE no PON).
That existing behavior - not scale or social reach - is the reason this is an unusually warm licensing target, and it directly validates both routing hypotheses.
PBC-internal note says outreach proposing a collab has already been sent; this brief builds around that thread rather than re-verifying it publicly.

Research date: 2026-07-22.
Sources note: zh-TW primary; official site (penrote.com.tw) and GCIS-mirror registry ranked above retailer and third-party pages; every claim carries a [S#]; single-source and estimate items flagged inline. Facebook/Instagram counts are search-metadata estimates (pages not directly fetchable); Threads count is fetch-verified.

## 2. AUDIENCE

- No first-party audience/demographic disclosure was found from PENROTE itself (stated absence); the read below is inferred from product mix and the licensor-stated audiences of the character lines PENROTE carries. Labelled estimate.
- Two buyer tracks are evident from the catalog: (a) a functional/office-and-school wholesale base (50-pen boxes, rulers, staplers, correction tools) and (b) a design-led young-adult "healing"/character-goods base concentrated in the licensed theme lines [S2][S3].
- Estimate - the character-goods base skews young female: the licensor of 柴語錄 Shiba Says (PENROTE's largest theme line at 89 SKUs) states its core audience is women 18-34, with LINE stickers sent ~21M times/month [S9]. That is licensor-stated for the IP, not PENROTE-measured - treat as a proxy for who buys PENROTE's 柴語錄 stationery.
- Estimate - the brand's own IG/Threads voice ("在地資深文具品牌," 手帳/journaling and pastel "療癒" framing in recent posts) targets journaling and stationery hobbyists, a predominantly young-adult, design-conscious segment [S6][S7].

## 4. COLLAB / IP HISTORY

PENROTE is an established character-IP licensee - this is its strongest strategic signal for PBC. Its catalog runs dedicated, multi-SKU stationery ranges under these licensed character brands (SKU counts from the official site theme index, 2026-07) [S3]:

- 柴語錄 Shiba Says - 89 SKUs (largest line). Taiwanese original IP by creator "Jing," rights held/managed by 雷科斯國際有限公司; licensor states 18-34 female core audience and ~21M LINE-sticker sends/month; retailer listings label PENROTE's items 【正版"獨家"授權】 (licensed) [S3][S9][S15].
- 胡子碰碰 OHIGE no PON - 11 SKUs (pens, staplers, ID holders, sticky notes, rulers). Japanese IP by illustrator Kanazawa Shin, licensed into Taiwan via 有你共創數位/UNII Creative from 2023 (same IP also collaborated with 五桐號 and iPASS一卡通) [S3][S5][S12].
- 醜白兔 (Ugly Bunny) - 15 SKUs. Taiwanese original IP (from 2017 LINE stickers; 40+ collabs incl. Uniqlo/Wacoal per press; went to Japan Licensing Expo and Vietnam in 2025) [S3][S11].
- 露咖貓 Looka Cat - 15 SKUs. Taiwanese illustration brand (emoji-face cat, "healing" positioning), licensed via 洛克露露數位科技有限公司 [S3][S10].
- 萌萌派對 (5), 守護精靈 (5), 布吉熊 (2), MEGU (1) - additional theme lines on the official site; individual IP owners not separately verified here [S3].
- Public read on results: no sales/results figures were published for any PENROTE character line (stated absence); the read is that the licensing program is sustained and expanding (multiple lines, ongoing new SKUs, 正版授權 labelling), not one-off [S3][S15].

## 6. SUGGESTED ANGLE (suggestion, not a finding)

- Lead with Bichi Mao (Jolly Gee #7 / Bichi Mao #13): the tightest evidence-based fit. PENROTE's flagship licensed line (柴語錄, healing Shiba, 18-34 female) and 露咖貓 (healing emoji-cat) show the exact tone and audience a Malaysian healing-cat character targets, and they already convert such IP into 80+ SKU stationery ranges - a Bichi Mao range would slot into a proven template [S3][S9][S10]. Maps primarily to audience-building/identity (a healing character that deepens the young-female stationery base), with a seasonal foot-traffic beat available via the July stationery fair [S13].
- Position Jolly Gee as the complementary card: its retro-kitsch character-family, stationery/lifestyle format matches PENROTE's multi-character theme-line strategy, and would diversify a roster that is currently Taiwan-sticker-heavy plus one Japanese IP (no Korean-sourced line yet) - an awareness play into design-led/手帳 buyers [S3][S6]. Suggestion only.
