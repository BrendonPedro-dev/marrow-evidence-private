# 三采文化 Sun Color Culture - Partner Brief

Taiwan's top-selling trade publisher (eslite No.1 publisher three straight years, ~60% of revenue from children's books) with a 20-year track record of licensing Korean and Japanese character IP into learning comics and gift-with-book merch - and an active PBC picture-book proposal already in their inbox.

## 1. IDENTITY

- Official name: 三采文化股份有限公司 / SUN COLOR CULTURE CO., LTD. (統一編號 23628454) [S1][S8].
- Founded 1990 by chairman 張輝明 and his wife from their living room in Tianmu, Taipei; now headquartered at 台北市內湖區瑞光路513巷33號8樓 (遠東貝塔大樓), 內湖科技園區 [S1].
- What they publish: children's knowledge comics and picture books, box sets (套書), literary fiction, healing essays (心靈勵志), psychology, business, Korean webnovel-derived light novels, plus 文創 merchandise and video content [S1][S2].
- Ownership: independent, founder-led company; registry lists 張輝明 as representative with registered capital NT$25,000,000 (registry data, single source) [S8].
- Scale: 2021 annual revenue approx. NT$360 million and 76 employees per 天下雜誌 profile (2022, single source); children's books contribute about 60% of revenue per editor-in-chief 曾雅青 in the same article [S7].
- Awards/standing: first publisher ever to win the Ministry of Economic Affairs 國家磐石獎 (28th edition, 2019); No.1 publisher on eslite's publisher sales ranking for three consecutive years from 2019 [S1][S7].
- Official site: https://www.suncolor.com.tw (own e-commerce store with membership program) [S2].
- Socials, all counts as of 2026-07:
  - Facebook 三采文化Suncolor: approx. 67,325 page likes (estimate, from search-result page data; not directly verifiable by fetch) [S9].
  - Facebook 三采童書粉絲團 (dedicated children's-books page): approx. 29,657 page likes (estimate, from search-result page data) [S10].
  - Instagram @readsuncolor: approx. 26,000 followers (estimate, from search-result page data) [S11].
  - YouTube suncolor三采文化 (@suncolor2008): 9.42萬 (94,200) subscribers and 19,556,386 total views (stated on channel, fetch-verified 2026-07-22; channel joined 2008-03-11) [S12].
  - LINE official account (line@生活圈) listed in site footer, follower count not public [S2].

## 2. AUDIENCE

- Core buyer base 1 - parents and school-age kids: 天下雜誌 reported that roughly half of Taiwan's elementary and junior-high students have read a Sun Color learning comic, and that parents buy full sets at once (2022, single source, paraphrased) [S7].
- Retail listings for their kids series carry 7-10歲 and 11-15歲 適讀 age bands with 注音 (fetch-verified on Kingstone) [S5].
- Children's marketing runs through parenting KOLs and group-buy (團購) communities such as 米蘭老師 and 林姓主婦 rather than traditional media (stated in 天下 profile) [S7].
- Core buyer base 2 - young-adult healing/self-help readers: house author 黃山料 was eslite's No.1 bestselling Chinese-language author for the fifth consecutive year in 2025, with top-10 chart placings in Hong Kong and Kuala Lumpur (fetch-verified) [S6][S13].
- Growing base 3 - Gen Z graphic-content readers: eslite's 2025 annual reading report (published 2025-12-09) says nearly 50% of new eslite members are Gen Z (21-35) and light-novel/comic revenue grew over 60% around its August 2025 anime festival, with under-29 buyers up 118% (fetch-verified; market-level data, not Sun Color-specific) [S6].
- Key lines relevant to character IP: 童書 (knowledge comics, picture books - including Japanese picture-book star 吉竹伸介, cited by an eslite buyer as having strong parent-side brand recognition), 心靈勵志/圖文 gift books (e.g. 銀髮川柳 series with illustrated premiums), and Korean webnovel light novels with collectible character cards [S5][S7][S14].

## 4. COLLAB/IP HISTORY

Sun Color has one of the deepest licensed-character publishing track records among Taiwan trade publishers.

- 2020: cross-industry collaboration with 台北故宮博物院 and Sanrio (三麗鷗), publishing 《凱蒂的故宮尋寶》, 《凱蒂╳故宮 手繪時光：炫彩刮畫著色組》 and 《凱蒂遊清明上河圖》 - a Hello Kitty museum-license book program (official About timeline, fetch-verified) [S1].
- 2003-present, Korean studio IP: introduced Korean knowledge comics 《科漫》《尋寶記》《科學實驗王》; 《尋寶記》 (by 姜境孝 and a Korean scripting team) has sold 21M copies globally, holds Taiwan's top library-borrowing record, and cumulative Taiwan sales of the Korean learning-comic lines exceed one million copies (天下, fetch-verified) [S7].
- Korean game IP: 《楓之谷大冒險》 and 《楓之谷數學神偷》 (Nexon MapleStory adaptation, 60+ volumes) and 《跑跑薑餅人》 Cookie Run series credited to Devsisters, including 城市大逃亡 vols 21 首爾 and 22 河內 and 腦力激盪遊戲 activity books (fetch-verified on Kingstone) [S4][S5].
- Japanese IP: 《名偵探柯南科學推理教室》 (青山剛昌 x Galileo工房, 10 volumes, fetch-verified on Kingstone); the long-running 《小妖怪》 series is a top search keyword on their own store; picture books by 吉竹伸介 [S5][S2][S7].
- 2016: exclusive Traditional Chinese license from Encyclopaedia Britannica for the 65-volume 《漫畫大英百科》 produced by Korea's MARIN (official About timeline, fetch-verified) [S1].
- 2015: launched 文創商品 and the 《空白童話書》 series with twelve well-known Taiwanese illustrators (official About timeline, fetch-verified; individual illustrator names not listed on the page) [S1].
- 2024: 《國家尋寶記》 20th-anniversary limited box set plus a 高雄市立圖書館 event bringing 姜境孝 to Taiwan for his first creator talk (official About timeline, fetch-verified) [S1].
- Format habit relevant to PBC: they routinely bundle character premiums - stickers, illustrated bookmarks, character info cards, battle cards - into books as purchase drivers [S5][S14][S15].
- Taiwanese LINE-sticker-style illustrator IP books (爽爽貓, Duncan, 白爛貓, 啾啾妹, 馬來貘 or similar): None found under ["三采文化 出版 爽爽貓 OR Duncan OR 白爛貓 OR 貓貓蟲 角色 圖文書", "爽爽貓 OR 啾啾妹 OR 馬來貘 出版社 三采", ""三采" 出版 LINE貼圖 插畫家 圖文書 貼圖 角色 新書 2024 OR 2025"].

## 5. DECISION CONTEXT

- Founder-chairman 張輝明 (b. 1958, 師大美術系, ex-design teacher) still drives strategy and is described by 天下 as trend-led and design-minded (title seen in 天下 profile and company registry) [S7][S8].
- 曾雅青 is 總編輯 (editor-in-chief), quoted in the 2022 天下 profile ("套書比單本書更有力量") and in a 2020 eslite interview; title as of those articles [S7][S20].
- The official submission page states that manuscripts (author intro, proposal outline, content) go to the editorial department (編輯部門) for review, with a reply within one month only if there is publishing interest; the official public submission channel is edit_admin@suncolor.com.tw with subject format 投稿_作品名稱(作者姓名) (fetch-verified via archived official page) [S21].
- General corporate contact surface: Tel 02-8797-1234 #218, fax 02-8797-1688, listed in the site footer; there is also a 客服信箱 form and a 團購服務 (group-purchase) channel [S2][S21].
- No dedicated public rights/licensing-team page was found; children's marketing appears to run as its own track (separate 三采童書粉絲團 Facebook page, KOL and group-buy programs) [S10][S7].
- PBC-internal context: an active picture-book publishing proposal is already with Sun Color; they acknowledged receipt and forwarded it internally, so the next touch should build on that thread rather than open a cold channel.

## 6. SUGGESTED ANGLE (suggestion, not a finding)

- Suggestion: lead with Cheesy Duck 確幸鴨 (#1) as the picture-book candidate, because it rides the live proposal thread and lands squarely in the segment that generates about 60% of their revenue, their strongest institutional muscle (children's/parent-child, book-fair presence, premium-bundling formats) [S7][S18].
- Jolly Gee Studio (#2) is the natural second card: Sun Color's licensing pipeline is heavily Korean (尋寶記, MapleStory, Cookie Run, Korean webnovels), so a Korean retro-kitsch character family with stationery/lifestyle goods fits their proven Korea-sourcing behavior and their gift-with-book premium format; Bichi Mao's healing style could later attach to their 療癒/gift-book adult line [S1][S4][S5].
- Campaign-goal mapping: Cheesy Duck maps to audience-building with parents/kids plus seasonal foot traffic via their summer children's fair and 童書展 events; Jolly Gee maps to awareness among Gen Z graphic-content buyers that eslite's 2025 report shows surging [S6][S18].
