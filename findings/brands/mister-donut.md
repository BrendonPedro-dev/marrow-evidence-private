# Mister Donut 統一多拿滋 (Taiwan) - Partner-Brand Brief

The map-v2 premise that Mister Donut Taiwan sits inside the Uni-President / President Chain (統一超商) group is CONFIRMED - it is operated by 統一多拿滋股份有限公司 (統編 27359304), a 50/50 joint venture set up in 2004 between 統一集團 (Uni-President Group) and Japan's Duskin/樂清, reaching 100 stores by Oct 2024.
More importantly for PBC, the map's "very frequent character-IP collaborator" read is strongly supported by Taiwan-market evidence: Mister Donut Taiwan runs a near-continuous cadence of character/illustration collabs (Pokemon x4, Sanrio, Snoopy/Peanuts, LINE Friends, Kanahei, and - critically - the homegrown Taiwan illustration character 貓貓蟲咖波 Bugcat Capoo, twice).
That makes this an unusually strong structural fit for an illustration IP like SHUYA, but also a top-tier-IP-heavy partner list that raises the bar.

Research date: 2026-07-22.
Sources note: zh-TW sources are primary for operator, stores, and Taiwan collabs; ja/en used only to separate Japan/overseas activations from Taiwan-market ones. FB/IG follower counts are search-metadata estimates (not directly fetchable) and are labelled as such.

## 1. IDENTITY

Official name: Mister Donut; Chinese 統一多拿滋 (operating entity 統一多拿滋股份有限公司).
What they sell: made-in-store doughnuts - signature 波堤 (Pon de Ring) chewy rings, yeast-raised and cake doughnuts, seasonal/limited flavors, plus made-to-order drinks (tea, coffee, matcha lines) and gift/party boxes. Indulgent-but-"quality-ingredient" positioning (matcha-house and chocolate-house tie-ups).

Ownership / parent (registry-verified): 統一多拿滋股份有限公司.
- 統一編號 (business ID): 27359304.
- Status: 核准設立 (active/approved).
- Capital: NT$300,000,000 registered / NT$150,001,000 paid-in.
- Representative (代表人): 陸永偉.
- Established: 2004-09-07.
- Registered address: 臺北市松山區東興路8號8樓 (registry); official contact page lists 台北市110信義區東興路61號5樓 (both on 東興路).
- Business scope: 烘焙烘蒸食品製造業 (baked/steamed food mfg), food & daily-goods retail, restaurants/beverage, international trade, other comprehensive retail.
- Source: company.g0v.ronny.tw GCIS mirror (統編 27359304); corroborated by twincn.com and Taipei okwork.gov.taipei company profile.
Ownership structure: 50/50 JV between 統一集團 (Uni-President Group) and Japan's Duskin 株式會社 (zh-Wikipedia romanizes as 樂清/Rakusei), which owns the Mister Donut brand in Asia. zh-Wikipedia additionally frames 統一超商 (President Chain Store, PCSC) as the operating group parent. So the map's "Uni-President link" is confirmed; the precise structure is a Uni-President group entity under a Duskin brand license, NOT a wholly-owned Uni-President brand.

Scale: 100 stores as of October 2024 (zh-Wikipedia; consistent with 20th-anniversary framing in Oct-2024 press). Footprint is a mix of standalone stores and department-store/mall counters (新光三越 etc.), with some 7-ELEVEN in-store 專區 pilots (same group channel).

## 2. AUDIENCE

Buyers/followers (estimate, inferred from campaign framing and coverage): mainstream urban/suburban families, students, and young adults nationwide who treat doughnuts as an everyday affordable treat (single doughnuts NT$49-79; buy-5-get-1 / buy-6-get-2 mechanics dominate), with a heavy skew toward cute-character collectors on the collab lines.
The collab merchandise (plush keychains, ceramic bowls/plates, tote bags, "只送不賣" gift-with-purchase items) is clearly pitched at social-active, female-skewing character fans and completists (estimate, from campaign copy across Snoopy, 咖波, Sanrio, Pokemon lines).
Broad, value-driven reach differentiates it from premium chains like Krispy Kreme; the brand's own copy leans on "萌" (cuteness) and collectibility.
No published first-party demographic breakdown found (stated: none; the above is estimate).

## 5. DECISION CONTEXT

Two-layer structure: the Mister Donut brand/format is licensed from Duskin (Japan) into a 統一集團 JV, but Taiwan-market execution - store network, local flavors, and the collab calendar - is run by the local operator 統一多拿滋股份有限公司 (統編 27359304, verified above), inside the Uni-President / PCSC group.
The volume and localization of Taiwan collabs (homegrown 咖波, 黃阿瑪, 一沐日; Taiwan-first Pokemon items) shows the local entity has real latitude over IP partnerships - so the Taiwan operator's brand/marketing team is the relevant decision surface for PBC, not Duskin Japan.
Named marketing individuals: none published on official/public pages (no scraped personal contacts included).
Official contact surface (from misterdonut.com.tw/Home/Contact): customer-service line 0800-527-000, email mdtcc@ptc-nec.com.tw, compliance/ethics email Advise@misterdonut.com.tw, plus a 夥伴招募 (partnership/franchise recruitment) section; inbound brand channels are the FB 官方粉絲團, IG @misterdonut_taiwan, and the LINE Official Account. Corporate counterpart is the registered entity 統一多拿滋股份有限公司 (統編 27359304), reachable via GCIS; as a group brand, senior marketing decisions likely coordinate with Uni-President group marketing (inferred, not documented).

## 6. SUGGESTED ANGLE (suggestion, not finding)

Suggestion: lead with SHUYA 舒雅 (illustration IP) into Mister Donut's proven, repeatable "cute illustrated-character doughnut + collectible merch (plush/ceramic/tote) + drink combo" template - the exact format already run with Kanahei, Sanrio, Snoopy, LINE Friends, and (most tellingly) the Taiwan homegrown illustration character 貓貓蟲咖波 twice. The dessert-prop, pastry-friendly SHUYA style maps directly onto波堤/doughnut faces and gift boxes, matching the map's category-fit note.
Caveat / bar-raiser: the partner list is top-tier-IP-heavy (Pokemon, Sanrio, Peanuts, LINE Friends, GODIVA), so SHUYA would be pitched against premium global names - but the repeated homegrown 咖波 / 黃阿瑪 collabs prove they will back a local illustration character, which is the credible wedge.
Likely campaign goal: seasonal foot traffic / limited-drop buzz (their core buy-5-get-1 mechanic) plus merch-driven collectibility, with a secondary awareness/identity lift for SHUYA at department-store counters. Label: suggestion only; no rights/territory assumptions made.
