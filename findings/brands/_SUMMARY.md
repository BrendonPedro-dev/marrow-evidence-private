# Cross-brand summary - map-v2 top-8-per-IP target research

Scope: the 25 unique brands that make up the top 8 ranked targets for each of PBC's four IPs (Bichi Mao 彼奇貓, Cheesy Duck 確幸鴨, Jolly Gee Studio, SHUYA 舒雅), deduplicated across IPs.
Every brand has an approach-ready brief in this folder and a row in `_INDEX.md`.
Research window: iterations 3-10, all briefs dated 2026-07-22 unless noted.

This file synthesizes what repeats across the 25 briefs: the angles that recur, the strongest target per IP, the systemic errors in the source map, the recurring risk patterns, and the contact-surface gaps.
It is a planning layer on top of the briefs, not a substitute for them - every claim here is sourced in the underlying brand file.

---

## 1. Headline patterns

**Every briefed brand already has a collab or campaign "slot" a PBC IP can drop into.**
No brand required inventing a format from scratch.
The recurring vehicles are: quarterly character-cake / character-doughnut drops (85度C, Krispy Kreme, Mister Donut, MOS Burger, McDonald's), co-branded cup + add-on merch + themed pop-up (CoCo都可, Milksha, Yifang), annual illustrator charity-merch (Taiwan SPCA, Save Dogs / ARTT), licensed-packaging stationery ranges (PENROTE, Sun Color, Book Republic), retail-channel / pop-up hosting (Eslite Spectrum, CACO, Caves Books, 展昭寵物展), and productized IP photo-frame licensing (snapshot).
The entry argument for almost every brand is "you already run this format with a comparable-tier Asian IP; here is a fresh one that fits your audience better."

**The map's routing-note "why" lines carry a high, quantifiable error rate - and it runs in both directions.**
Across the 25 briefs, a clear majority of the specific "[collab-history]" / "prior collab" premises did NOT verify for the Taiwan operator.
Confirmed-false or mis-specified collab premises: LitoMon, Book Republic, Caves Books, Eslite Spectrum, Save Dogs / ARTT ("rabbit/bear motifs"), 旅禾, McDonald's (real precedent is Baby Shark, not the Japan/China Sanrio/Pokemon runs), KFC (China / 海外限定, not Taiwan), Yifang (US-franchisee-run BART/Neopets, not Taiwan), heme (retailer site-nav false positives; HEMEKO is a separate Korean brand), LADY M (zero character-IP history anywhere).
Status/ownership premises that were also wrong: Krispy Kreme never "exited ~2015" (continuous since 2013), MOS Burger's parent is TECO 東元 not Uni-President, 旅禾 is Taichung not Kaohsiung, LADY M's "US Chapter 11 2023" is unverifiable.
Premises that DID verify: CACO (JOGUMAN/Butterbear), Milksha (KINGJUN, MIND.A.DAY), CoCo都可 (Cinnamoroll/Conan/FMA), Mister Donut (Pokemon x4 + homegrown 咖波/黃阿瑪), 85度C (quarterly cakes), Krispy Kreme (Sanrio/DINOTAENG), PENROTE (8+ licensed character ranges), DogCatStar (Disney 2023), Sun Color (20-year Korean/Japanese licensing habit).

**The single most common map defect is overseas / franchisee activation mis-attributed to the Taiwan operator.**
KFC (China toys), Yifang (California franchisee), heme (Korean HEMEKO), and the Japan/China halves of Mister Donut, McDonald's, and MOS Burger's search footprints all show the same trap: a foreign-market or foreign-franchisee character collab surfaces in search and reads as if the Taiwan operator did it.
Every brief filtered collab history to Taiwan-market, operator-run activity specifically, and this filter should remain the standing rule for any future target.

## 2. Strongest target per IP (suggestion, from the evidence in each brief)

Rankings below are a read on fit x actionability, not the map's raw score.

### Bichi Mao 彼奇貓 (cat / animal-welfare / warm-healing)
1. **Taiwan SPCA (#1)** - proven repeatable annual illustrator charity-merch engine, mission-aligned, donation-based; clearest on-tone flagship, with a Feb-to-July campaign calendar that sets approach timing.
2. **迷客夏 Milksha (#6)** - the sharpest fit-and-momentum combination: launched the Korean cat IP MIND.A.DAY (2026-05) built explicitly for the cat-parent "萌寵經濟" audience, a near-exact Bichi Mao template with a national beverage footprint.
3. **汪喵星球 DogCatStar (#2)** - self-styled cat-can "罐罐王" with real licensed-IP history (Disney) and a "毛孩為本" promise that is near-native for a healing-cat character; white-space for an original-illustration character.
- **White-space native-tone fits:** LitoMon (#3, in-house 小怪獸 world) and PENROTE (#13, serial healing-character stationery licensee) both have no cat/healing-cat license yet, so a Bichi Mao tie is net-new.
- **Event/venue plays:** 展昭寵物展 (#7, booth / mascot meet-and-greet with a direct pet-owner audience) and Save Dogs / ARTT (#4, donation-based, FB-native older donor audience).

### Cheesy Duck 確幸鴨 (picture-book + warm F&B)
1. **三采文化 Sun Color (#1)** - a live PBC picture-book proposal already in their inbox, plus a 20-year character-IP-into-books-and-premiums habit; the most advanced and most on-format target of the whole map.
2. **snapshot photobooth (#6)** - a fully productized, low-friction collab surface (published price list, application form, stated ideal partner 二次元/Q版/動物擬人化) that maps cleanly onto Cheesy Duck.
3. **麥當勞台灣 McDonald's (#3)** - the real Taiwan precedent (Baby Shark/Pinkfong picture-book Happy Meal, 2024) is directly on-angle, though the blue-chip bar and house IP 小麥麥 make a local seasonal reading activation the realistic entry.
- **House-IP caveat cluster:** MOS Burger (#2, MOS Friends) and McDonald's (#3, 小麥麥) both run royalty-free in-house picture-book characters an external duck must out-perform or co-star with.
- **Deprioritize:** Yifang (#8, collapsed Taiwan footprint + 2019 political-controversy overhang) and TFC 德州小騎士 (#4, ~8-store owner-led revival, no IP precedent, own 小騎士 mascot).

### Jolly Gee Studio (Korean retro-kitsch; stationery / lifestyle / cafe)
1. **CACO (#1)** - the cleanest proven precedent on the whole map: already runs Korean-IP cafe takeovers (JOGUMAN) and Asian healing-IP tours (Butterbear), a productized 廠商合作 surface, and self-describes as Taiwan's largest licensed-collab apparel chain.
2. **snapshot photobooth (#3)** and **Eslite Spectrum (#6)** - two channel/pop-up routes: snapshot for a productized photo-frame drop, Eslite as a venue that already retails and hosts Korean cute-character stationery IP (Romane BRUNCH BROTHER, webtoon 動漫祭).
3. **Sun Color (#2)** - strong secondary given its Korea-sourcing pattern, behind the live Cheesy Duck proposal.
- **Category-fit-but-net-new:** PENROTE (#7, serial character-stationery licensee, would be a first Korean line), heme (#8, first character-IP collab as collectible-packaging drop), Book Republic (#4, imprint-level entry via a 總編輯, not group HQ), Caves Books (#5, education-first back-to-school stationery, not lifestyle-collectible).

### SHUYA 舒雅 (illustration IP; lifestyle / pastry / bookstore)
1. **Krispy Kreme (#3)** - the cleanest high-fit target: continuously operating (contrary to the map), NO house character to compete with, and an established third-party illustration-IP doughnut-box + collectible-card template (Sanrio, DINOTAENG) SHUYA slots straight into, decided by the local operator 荃鴻.
2. **85度C (#1)** - a proven quarterly character-cake machine that publicly frames IP cakes as its youth-rejuvenation lever; format-perfect, though its partner list skews to top-tier IPs.
3. **CoCo都可 (#8)** - Taiwan's largest hand-shaken chain with a verified twice-yearly national character-IP program (Cinnamoroll, Detective Conan); a mass-audience cup+merch+pop-up mechanic with a December "療癒/萌" slot that fits SHUYA.
- **Strong-format-but-top-IP-heavy:** Mister Donut (#6) - rich Taiwan collab record, and its homegrown 咖波/黃阿瑪 precedents are the credible wedge for a non-blue-chip IP.
- **Net-new / lower-actionability:** LADY M (#5, zero character-IP history anywhere, ultra-premium, would be a first) and 旅禾 (#2, weak collab premise, fragmented corporate structure, 2024 mold episode).

**Cross-IP anchor brands (rank in multiple IPs' top 8):** CACO (all four IPs), Sun Color (three), snapshot (three).
These three are the highest-leverage single relationships because one contact can carry multiple IPs; CACO and snapshot are also the two most productized, lowest-friction collab surfaces on the map.

## 3. Recurring risk patterns (gate outreach on these)

**House-IP competition.**
Five briefed brands run their own royalty-free character an external PBC IP must out-perform or co-star with: MOS Burger (MOS Friends 雞塊兄弟), McDonald's (小麥麥 + 麥當勞叔叔), LitoMon (小怪獸), TFC (小騎士), and 旅禾 (its puff-helmeted Moai).
Pitch a window the house IP is not filling, or an explicit co-star role.

**Corporate-status / ownership churn.**
KFC Taiwan's operator 富利餐飲 is inside Jardine's active ~NT$13bn 2026 sale of the Taiwan/HK/Vietnam KFC+Pizza Hut business (buyer unresolved), LADY M's Asia model is shifting franchise-to-direct (China closure 2022, Singapore licensee ended 2025-12), and Yifang's Taiwan operation has structurally collapsed to ~6-13 stores.
These three are lower-actionability than their category scores imply and should be timing-gated.

**Reputational overhangs.**
DogCatStar carries a 2024-2025 wastewater-pollution ruling (upheld by 環境部), 旅禾 a 2024 food-safety (mold) episode, and Yifang the unresolved 2019 "一國兩制" political controversy.
Each is worth pre-clearing before a welfare- or quality-flavored collab.

**Legal-counterparty ambiguity.**
Several brands sit in fragmented or multi-entity structures where the licensing counterparty is not the obvious brand name: 旅禾 (original namesake 旅禾泡芙之家有限公司 dissolved 2026-01-02), Book Republic (decentralised amoeba federation, decision at imprint 總編輯 level), Mister Donut (50/50 Uni-President x Duskin JV on a Duskin brand license), MOS Burger (TECO group, not Uni-President), and heme (related enterprise of listed 華研國際音樂 HIM, decisions may run through the group).
Pin the legal counterparty before any proposal.

**Registry rep vs public founder/decision-maker mismatch.**
GCIS 代表人 repeatedly differs from the public founder/chairman (e.g. CoCo都可 rep 陳美英 vs founder 洪肇水; heme rep 呂世玉 alongside HIM chairman 呂燕清; Krispy Kreme's romanization varies).
Surface both; do not assume the registry 代表人 is the marketing decision-maker.

## 4. Contact-surface gap (the single biggest operational finding)

**Not one of the 25 brands publishes a named licensing or marketing manager.**
Every contact surface is departmental, a cooperation form, or a general channel:
- Cooperation-form / productized: CACO (廠商合作), snapshot (Ragic form + service@snapfoto.co), Eslite Spectrum (EXPO 文創平台 合作洽談), 85度C (行銷企劃部 via contact page), Mister Donut (0800 / mdtcc@ptc-nec.com.tw), LADY M (order@ladym.com.tw / 02-7730-8660).
- Departmental / HQ-led with no named contact: McDonald's (和德昌 marketing), MOS Burger (An-Shin HQ / 合作提案 LINE@), Krispy Kreme (荃鴻 via brand channels), KFC (富利餐飲), Milksha (Taiwan entity under Jollibee board), Caves Books (HQ merchandising), 展昭寵物展 (exhibition/marketing team), Sun Color (editorial dept, ~1-month review window).
- Founder/owner-led with outsourced PR: DogCatStar, LitoMon, PENROTE, TFC, Taiwan SPCA / Save Dogs (executive leadership, donation-based).
- Imprint-level: Book Republic (each imprint's 總編輯).

Implication: PBC's outreach playbook cannot rely on warm named contacts from public sources.
The realistic paths are (a) the productized cooperation forms for the lowest-friction brands (CACO, snapshot, Eslite), (b) departmental HQ marketing inboxes for the chains, and (c) founder/executive direct approach for the small owner-led and NGO targets.
Where the map cites internal "outreach already sent" (PENROTE, Sun Color), that PBC-CRM relationship is the warmest available lead and should be reused rather than re-established cold.

## 5. Audience notes worth carrying into positioning

- **Cat-parent / 萌寵經濟 cluster:** Milksha (MIND.A.DAY), DogCatStar, LitoMon, 展昭 (世界貓咪博覽會) - the tightest demographic match for Bichi Mao.
- **Channel-split signal:** Save Dogs / ARTT skews older / Facebook-native (~414K FB vs ~7.6K IG), unlike the younger IG/LINE-native F&B and lifestyle brands - a reminder that "animal-welfare NGO" does not imply a young audience.
- **Female 18-34 collectible-driven:** PENROTE (licensor-stated for 柴語錄), Krispy Kreme / 85度C collab lines, CoCo都可 / Mister Donut character drops, heme cosmetics - the core collectible-merch demographic across pastry, beverage, stationery, and cosmetics.
- **Student / teacher / parent:** Caves Books and (partly) Sun Color and Book Republic - an education-season, back-to-school foot-traffic audience rather than a lifestyle-collectible one.
