# 麥當勞台灣 McDonald's Taiwan (和德昌股份有限公司) - Partner-Brand Brief

McDonald's Taiwan is the island's largest QSR (~421 stores) and, since the 2017 handover, is run by a locally-owned developmental licensee (和德昌 / 德昱, 李昌霖) that has deliberately localized the brand - which is exactly what makes its picture-book Happy Meal precedent (Baby Shark 2024) a real but very-high-bar opening for Cheesy Duck.
The map-v2 "prior collab [mixed]" routing note checks out in direction but needs correction on specifics: McDonald's Taiwan's recent verified character tie-ins are Baby Shark (Pinkfong, 2024) and the Minecraft movie (2025), NOT the Sanrio / Pokemon / Line Friends the note implies (those recent Happy Meal runs were Japan/China, not Taiwan).

Research date: 2026-07-22.
zh-TW sources are primary; en/ja used only for cross-checks.
Quotes are paraphrased and flagged unless marked verified; Facebook and Instagram block anonymous fetch, so follower counts are search-metadata estimates.

The rest of this brief is built on the corrected 和德昌 entity.

## 1. IDENTITY

- Brand name: 麥當勞 (English: McDonald's); the global QSR (burgers, fries, McNuggets, McCafe, Happy Meal 快樂兒童餐).
- Taiwan operating entity: 和德昌股份有限公司 (Prospect Hospitality Co., Ltd.), the local developmental licensee holding McDonald's franchise/operating rights for Taiwan [S2, S3].
- Registry facts (統一編號 12411160), verified via the GCIS 商工登記 g0v mirror [S1]:
  - 公司狀況: 核准設立 (active/approved).
  - 資本總額 (authorized capital): NT$4,000,000,000.
  - 實收資本額 (paid-in capital): NT$2,756,095,600.
  - 代表人 / 董事長 (chairman): 李昌霖 (Li Chang-lin) [S1, S4].
  - 公司所在地: 臺北市中正區林森南路1號3樓 (Zhongzheng, Taipei) [S1].
  - Former name: 台灣麥當勞餐廳股份有限公司, renamed 和德昌 on 2017-06-05; the 統一編號 (12411160) is unchanged, confirming this is the same legal entity, not a new company [S2, S3].
- Ownership / handover structure: In 2015 McDonald's HQ announced it would move Taiwan to a "發展式特許經營" (developmental licensee) model; in 2017 李昌霖 (third-generation 仰德集團 / 士林開發 / 國賓大飯店 family) and 信義房屋 chairman 周俊吉 formed the holding company 德昱股份有限公司 - 李昌霖 holding ~70% - which acquired the Taiwan operating rights for about NT$5.09bn and took over operations from 2017-06-01 [S3, S5, S6].
- This is a genuine local-licensee model, not a directly HQ-run subsidiary: post-handover coverage repeatedly frames 李昌霖's team as having localized the brand (renovated "網美" store designs, local sourcing, 台味 campaigns) - relevant because local marketing autonomy is the whole point of the model [S6].
- Scale: Taiwan's largest QSR by store count; trade press reported a plan to reach about 421 stores by end-2024, described as a 40-year high, with continued expansion and 得來速 (drive-thru) dual-ordering rollout [S7, S8]. First Taiwan store opened 1984 [S9]. (Wikipedia separately cites a "600th location center" milestone; treat the 421 store figure as the better-sourced count and the 600 figure as unverified here - flagged.)
- Official site: https://www.mcdonalds.com/tw/zh-tw/ [S10].
- Instagram: https://www.instagram.com/mcdonaldstw/ - about 266,000 followers across ~1,226 posts (estimate, 2026-07, from search-result metadata; direct fetch blocked) [S11].
- Facebook: https://www.facebook.com/mcdonalds.tw/ - an active official page; the like-count returned in search metadata was implausible (appeared to conflate the global page), so NO reliable Taiwan figure is stated here (as of 2026-07) [S12].
- LINE: official account id @mcd.tw ("麥當勞"), the hub for 點點卡 payment/points and offers; friend count not found under queries run 2026-07 [S13].
- App: 麥當勞報報 / McDonald's App is the primary owned CRM surface (點點卡, 甜心卡, 隨買店取) [S13].

## 2. AUDIENCE

- Mass-market, all-ages reach: as the largest QSR chain in Taiwan, McDonald's serves the broadest QSR customer base on the island; no single niche (stated by scale, not a disclosed demographic) [S7].
- Families and children are a deliberately programmed core segment: the Happy Meal 快樂兒童餐, the 麥麥童樂會 parent-child activity site, and the 小麥麥體驗營 / family summer camp are explicit 親子 programs [S14, S15].
- Young adults / value seekers are reached through the app, 甜心卡, 點點卡 points and LINE offers - a heavy digital-CRM cohort (stated by the digital-first campaign structure) [S13].
- Skew inference (estimate, not stated): the Happy Meal + kids-camp programming targets parents of young children, while the app/coupon machinery skews to a younger 18-34 value cohort; treat as directional inference from campaign design, not a published demographic split.

## 4. COLLAB / IP HISTORY

McDonald's Taiwan runs two tracks, and the map's "prior collab [mixed]" note is best read against both:

A. Own / house characters (no external royalty):
- 麥當勞叔叔 (Ronald McDonald) and the classic 麥當勞 friends (漢堡神偷, 奶昔大哥, 大鳥姊姊) - long-standing brand mascots; the 麥當勞叔叔 & friends returned on the 2024-03 甜心卡 card designs after years of reduced visibility [S21, S22].
- 小麥 / 小麥麥 - a localized McDonald's Taiwan kids-facing IP; a 2022-06-04 official brand story announced a new IP "小麥" as part of the post-handover 和德昌 localization/cultural push, and 小麥麥 now anchors the 小麥麥體驗營 / 麥麥童樂會 kids program [S23, S14]. (The "小麥" launch is single-source to the official brand-news page, which returned 403 on direct fetch - flagged; the 小麥麥 program is independently visible on the live official activity site [S14].)

B. External licensed IP (verified Taiwan runs):
- Baby Shark / Pinkfong - 2024-08 - Happy Meal restyle + 6-book environmental picture-book giveaway [S16, S17]. This is the strongest verified external-character precedent AND it is a picture-book format, directly adjacent to Cheesy Duck's picture-book angle.
- Minecraft (movie) - 2025-03 - blind-box figures + skin cards + theme book [S18].
- Historical global Happy Meal IPs (Pokemon, Snoopy, Hello Kitty, Minions, etc.) have appeared in Taiwan over the decades, but the brand's own milestone/newsroom pages did not yield a clean dated Taiwan list under the queries run, so specific past titles are not asserted here beyond Baby Shark and Minecraft [S9].

Routing-note correction (important):
- The map-v2 "why" cited Sanrio / Pokemon / Line Friends / Kumamon as prior collabs.
- The recent high-profile Happy Meal runs for Crayon Shin-chan (蠟筆小新), Pokemon, and Sanrio Melody/Kuromi that surface in search were JAPAN and CHINA McDonald's campaigns, NOT verified Taiwan activations (each fetched source states 日本麥當勞 or 中國大陸麥當勞) [S24, S25].
- So the "prior collab" is real but its true Taiwan content is Baby Shark + Minecraft, not the blue-chip Sanrio/Pokemon names the note implies - treat the note as directionally right, specifically wrong.

## 5. DECISION CONTEXT

- Marketing decisions sit with 和德昌 (Taiwan) HQ marketing, not individual stores and not (day-to-day) global HQ: the whole rationale of the 2017 "發展式特許經營" handover was to give the local licensee operating and marketing autonomy to localize the brand, and post-handover campaigns (Team Taiwan, local sweet-potato sourcing, 網美 store redesigns) are visibly Taiwan-originated [S5, S6].
- Global brand guardrails still apply: McDonald's global brand standards and some worldwide Happy Meal IP tie-ins (e.g. Minecraft-movie) flow from HQ/regional deals, so a globally-run Happy Meal slot is a different, far higher bar than a locally-run seasonal activation; the exact split of authority is not publicly documented (flagged - do NOT assert where a given decision is made).
- Official contact / cooperation surface (use this, not scraped contacts): the corporate site https://www.mcdonalds.com/tw/zh-tw/ (newsroom, 關於我們, 聯絡我們) is the front door; no public 合作提案 / licensing intake form was located under the queries run 2026-07, so an approach likely routes through corporate/marketing contact channels on the official site [S10].
- No individual marketing manager name is published on the official Taiwan site (checked 2026-07); no personal emails/phones were scraped.
- Named public figure for context only (NOT a pitch path): 李昌霖 (和德昌 董事長 / chairman), the licensee principal [S1, S4].

## 6. SUGGESTED ANGLE (suggestion, not a finding)

- Suggestion: lead with Cheesy Duck 確幸鴨 as a warm, picture-book-native character that slots into the exact Happy Meal + free-picture-book format McDonald's Taiwan already ran with Baby Shark in 2024 - i.e. "a duck version of a reading/environmental book program you have already operated," pairing Cheesy Duck's picture-book IP with the 麥麥盒 kids-meal and the 麥麥童樂會 / 小麥麥體驗營 parent-child machinery.
- Campaign-goal fit: primarily family foot-traffic / seasonal (the Baby Shark and summer-camp windows are its established kids windows) with a secondary awareness / brand-warmth lift; identity is weaker here because McDonald's already has its own 小麥麥 house character.
- Honest high-bar caveat: McDonald's globally works with blue-chip IPs (Minecraft, Pokemon, Sanrio) and has a house kids IP (小麥麥) it pays no royalty on, so a global Happy Meal slot is unrealistic as a first ask; the realistic entry is a LOCAL, seasonal, Taiwan-originated activation (a reading/environmental picture-book giveaway or a regional kids-meal window) pitched to 和德昌's local marketing team - leaning on Cheesy Duck's book-native format, lower cost/flexibility, and a distinct duck personality rather than trying to win a worldwide toy programme.
