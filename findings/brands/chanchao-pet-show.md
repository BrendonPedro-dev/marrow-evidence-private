# 展昭寵物用品展 (Chan Chao Pet Show) - Partner-Brand Brief for Bichi Mao 彼奇貓

展昭寵物用品展 is the pet-category exhibition franchise of 展昭國際企業股份有限公司 (Chan Chao International Enterprise Co., Ltd.), Taiwan's oldest large private exhibition organizer (統編 23115308), and it runs a year-round national circuit of pet shows (台中 / 台北 / 高雄) plus a dedicated 世界貓咪博覽會 cat expo, with the 台北經典場 self-billed as Taiwan's largest (2,200+ brands, 2,100+ booths) and a 120,000+ fan Facebook page.
This is an EVENT/VENUE partner, not a consumer brand - the fit for Bichi Mao is a licensed-character booth, mascot meet-and-greet, or official IP partner presence at a pet expo, matched to a direct pet-owner audience.
No confirmed prior licensed-character / mascot IP partner at 展昭's pet shows was found under the queries listed in Section 4; the marketing/exhibition team is the correct first-contact surface per the PBC routing note.

Research date: 2026-07-22.
All zh-TW facts below were curl-verified against the fetched page or the GCIS/g0v registry unless explicitly marked as a search-metadata estimate.

## 2. AUDIENCE

- No first-party demographic breakdown of visitors is published on the pages checked; everything below is labelled accordingly.
- Stated (product mix): visitors are pet owners shopping across accessories (推車, 衣服, 玩具, 背袋, 項圈), consumables/用品 (逗貓棒, 貓抓板, 貓砂, 尿布), pet food (飼料, 凍乾, 肉泥, 罐罐, 零食, 潔牙), and pet health supplements - i.e. an audience of active dog and cat owners buying for their animals [S12].
- Stated (format): the Taipei show is a walk-in consumer expo with free electronic admission tickets (免費門票全面電子化), so the audience is broad general-public pet owners rather than trade-only buyers [S11, S12].
- Estimate (inference from cat expo): the standalone 世界貓咪博覽會, which restricts entry to cats and cat lovers (禁止其他寵物入場), signals a large, self-selecting cat-owner sub-audience distinct from the general dog-and-cat pet show - a direct match for a cat character [S9].
- Estimate (inference from scale + circuit): a national multi-city circuit with a 120,000+ fan page and 2,100+ booths implies tens of thousands of walk-in pet owners per edition; no audited attendance figure was found, so exact visitor numbers are not stated here [S6, S8, S12].
- Estimate (inference from partner mix): animal-welfare NGOs exhibit at the shows (Taiwan SPCA announced participation in the 2026 展昭台北寵物展), indicating an audience receptive to welfare/adoption messaging - aligned with Bichi Mao's animal-welfare fit [S17].

## 6. SUGGESTED ANGLE (suggestion, not a finding)

This section is a suggestion, not a research finding.
展昭's pet shows put Bichi Mao 彼奇貓 in front of exactly its strongest audience - active dog and cat owners - at national scale, and the 世界貓咪博覽會 cat expo is an even tighter match for a warm healing-style cat character; the natural formats are an official IP-partner / feature installation (a Bichi Mao 療癒 photo-zone or character booth) or a mascot meet-and-greet tied to one edition, rather than product merch, since the organizer sells the floor and already programs on-site experiential activities.
Because no prior licensed-character show partner was found, PBC would be proposing a first-of-kind character presence, which is a differentiation angle for the organizer (a novel visitor draw) but means the deal has to be built from scratch with the marketing/exhibition team rather than dropped into an existing template.
Flag the campaign goal as primarily foot-traffic / seasonal (a character meet-and-greet that pulls and dwells crowds at a specific edition) plus awareness (introducing Bichi Mao to a pet-owner base), with a secondary audience-acquisition play into the cat-owner segment at Meow Expo; the free-admission, high-cadence circuit makes a single-edition pilot low-risk before scaling across cities.
