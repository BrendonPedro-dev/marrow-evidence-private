# LADY M (Taiwan) - partner-brand brief for SHUYA 舒雅

LADY M is confirmed still operating in Taiwan as of 2026-07: 6 permanent boutiques plus a live pop-up cadence, run by Taiwan master-licensee 亞茜股份有限公司 (統編 42868475, foreign-owned/僑外資, last registry change 2025-12-30) - so the "Taiwan may have exited" worry does not apply here.
The map's dessert-prop category note holds, but the brief's own "US Chapter 11 in 2023" premise could NOT be verified under my queries; the real Asia disruption is licensing/franchise-model changes (China stores closed 2022; Singapore licensee ended 2025-12-11 as Lady M moves to run markets directly), not a US bankruptcy - treat the Chapter 11 claim as unconfirmed.
Critically for SHUYA: LADY M is an ultra-premium, gifting-led patissier with NO character/IP collab history found in Taiwan or globally - its collaborations are food/luxury (caviar, Broadway, seasonal fruit), so an illustration-IP tie-in would be a first for the brand.

Research date: 2026-07-22.
zh-TW sources are primary for Taiwan status/operator/campaigns; en sources for global ownership/history. All follower counts are labelled estimates; registry figures are from the g0v/GCIS open API.

## 1. IDENTITY

- Brand: LADY M (styled "Lady M"; informal Chinese rendering 這位M夫人). The official Taiwan site uses "Lady M" throughout and does not use a formal Chinese trade name [S1, S2].
- What they sell: premium French mille-crepe cakes (千層蛋糕), self-described as "被列為「美國十大蛋糕」的頂級甜點品牌," each cake built from 20+ layers of hand-made French crepes; sold as whole 9-inch cakes and slices, plus seasonal/market-exclusive flavors [S1].
- Global parent: Lady M Confections Co., Ltd., New York City, founded 2001 (originally a wholesale business) by Emi Wada; Ken Romaniszyn became CEO (joined 2008). Grew to ~50+ boutiques worldwide [S3].
- Global footprint (as of April 2026, per Wikipedia): United States 22 boutiques, Hong Kong 10, Macau 2, Taiwan 6, Mainland China 4; Singapore closed in Jan 2026 [S3].
- Taiwan operator (GCIS registry, curl-verified via g0v API): 亞茜股份有限公司, 統一編號 42868475; 資本總額 & 實收資本額 NT$46,400,000; 代表人/董事長 田子禾 (監察人 黃耀民); 核准設立 2016-08-03; 登記地址 台北市大安區光復南路240巷26號 (same as the Taipei flagship); 股權狀況 僑外資 (foreign-owned, consistent with a US parent); 最後核准變更 2025-12-30 [S4].
- Registry business scope: F203010 食品什貨飲料零售, F401010 國際貿易, C104020 烘焙炊蒸食品製造, F501030 飲料店, F501060 餐館, F203020 菸酒零售, ZZ99999 [S4].
- Taiwan market entry: January 2017 [S1].
- The Taipei flagship about page rendered an English operator name "Letoile Luxe Hospitality Co., Ltd." next to 亞茜股份有限公司; this English name is single-source and not in the registry, so treat it as unverified [S1 - flag].
- Taiwan store list (official pickup/boutique locations, 2026): Taipei flagship (國父紀念館站, 光復南路240巷26號), Taipei 晶華 Regent boutique, 遠百信義 (Far Eastern Xinyi), 遠百板橋, 台中誠品480, 台中 LaLaport - 6 locations [S1, S5].
- Official site: https://www.ladym.com.tw [S1]. Order line 02-7730-8660; email order@ladym.com.tw [S1].
- Instagram: https://www.instagram.com/ladymtaiwan/ - approx 25,000 followers, ~1,180 posts (estimate, as of 2026-07, from search-result metadata; direct fetch blocked) [S6].
- Facebook: https://www.facebook.com/Ladymtaiwan/ - approx 92,000 page likes (estimate, as of 2026-07, from search-result metadata; direct fetch blocked) [S6].

## 2. AUDIENCE

- Positioning-implied audience: affluent urban dessert buyers, gifting occasions (birthday/seasonal), and department-store shoppers; the brand sells at premium price points (9-inch cake NT$2,800; slice NT$280 in the 2026 Tainan pop-up) that self-select a higher-income buyer (stated pricing; audience inference) [S8, S9].
- Skews female and gift/experience-driven: Taiwanese lifestyle bloggers and Threads/IG posts frame Lady M as a "must-try" premium treat and an emotionally attached "愛店," with organic nostalgia when a boutique relocated - indicating a loyal, self-motivated fan base rather than a mass footfall crowd (estimate from social/press tone) [S6, S10].
- Tourist/destination pull: press repeatedly frames Lady M as "遊紐約必嚐" and "來自紐約," positioning it as an aspirational imported brand; Taiwan expansion into 台南/高雄 was covered as "南部人久等了" (southern Taiwan finally gets it), implying pent-up demand outside Taipei (stated in coverage; demographic detail is estimate) [S3, S11, S12].
- No first-party audience demographic breakdown was published; all above is labelled estimate/inference.

## 3. RECENT MOMENTUM (Jan 2025 - Jul 2026)

Taiwan:
- 2025 (Apr-Jun): first Kaohsiung presence - a pop-up at 義享時尚廣場 (E Sky Land) 1F with a pop-up-exclusive flavor 草莓抹茶千層蛋糕; covered as Lady M's first physical foothold in southern Taiwan [S11, S12].
- Late 2025: Taichung boutique relocated from 新光三越 (after the SKM store's reopening/renovation) to 誠品480; organic Threads posts lamented the move, and the current official list shows both 台中誠品480 and 台中 LaLaport (single-source social + official list) [S1, S10].
- 2026-05-21 to 2026-06-30: first Tainan pop-up at 台南新光新天地 (11:30-22:00, 售完即止), with a Tainan-exclusive new flavor 檸香奶綠千層蛋糕 (lemon custard + green-tea cream, citrus mirror glaze); 9-inch NT$2,800, slice NT$280; opening gift-with-purchase and a "保冷加持" 4-slice + cooler-bag bundle at NT$1,499 (published 2026-05-18) [S8, S9, S13].
- Ongoing: seasonal/父親節 flavors (e.g. 竹炭咖啡千層) and hidden birthday offers marketed via aggregators and LINE Gift [S7, S14].

Global (context for Taiwan operator model):
- 2022: Lady M announced closure of all Mainland China stores (franchise-partner exit); a Yicai report noted Lady M "may run own shops in China" after the partner shut outlets - it now shows 4 China boutiques again (as of Apr 2026), i.e. a model change, not a permanent exit [S3, S15].
- 2025-12-11 / Jan 2026: Lady M's Singapore licensing arrangement with local distributor Caerus Holdings ended; all Singapore outlets closed, and CEO Ken Romaniszyn said the brand will take a "more direct role" in that market as it enters its "next phase of growth" (a franchise-to-direct shift, widely covered by Straits Times/Mothership) [S16, S17].
- Net read: LADY M's Asia strategy is shifting from local licensees toward more direct/HQ control market-by-market; Taiwan's 亞茜股份有限公司 is still the standing operator and its most recent registry change is Dec 2025, but the global pattern means the Taiwan operating arrangement is worth re-confirming before any long-horizon deal (inference; flag) [S3, S4, S16].

## 4. COLLAB/IP HISTORY

- None found under queries: "Lady M 台灣 聯名 IP 卡通 合作 快閃", "Lady M Taiwan character collaboration", "Lady M 聯名 卡通". No character/illustration-IP collaboration was found in the Taiwan market [S18].
- Globally, LADY M's collaborations are food-/luxury-/culture-oriented, not character IP: e.g. Oishii Omakase Berry mille-crepes, Petrossian caviar mille-crepes, and a Broadway "Some Like It Hot" tie-in flavor (all US, 2023) - reinforcing that the brand has no character-IP precedent to point to [S19].
- Taiwan "collaborations" are effectively flavor + retail-partner activations with department stores (新光三越/新光新天地, 遠東百貨, 義享), i.e. market-exclusive flavors and pop-up gift bundles, not licensed characters [S8, S11, S12].
- Public read: a character-IP tie-up would be net-new territory for LADY M at both HQ and Taiwan-operator level - higher novelty, but no proven internal playbook or precedent to de-risk it (inference).

## 5. DECISION CONTEXT

- Taiwan marketing/commercial decisions sit with the master-licensee/operator 亞茜股份有限公司, an owner-led company (負責人/董事長 田子禾; single director + single supervisor structure), foreign-owned and aligned to the US brand - so pitches route to the Taiwan operator, likely with HQ brand-approval on any IP use (inference from registry structure) [S4].
- Campaigns are co-run with department-store partners: pop-ups and exclusive flavors launch through 新光三越/新光新天地 (SKM) and 遠東百貨 channels, meaning the malls' F&B/marketing teams are practical co-decision surfaces for a seasonal activation [S8, S11].
- Official public contact surface: order@ladym.com.tw and 02-7730-8660 (order/customer line, weekday hours), plus the official site contact page; no individual marketing-manager name is published on the official site or about page (checked ladym.com.tw/about and contact, 2026-07) [S1].
- 亞茜股份有限公司 also actively recruits via 518/104 job boards under the "Lady M" employer name, confirming an active local corporate entity with in-house staff (not a dormant shell) [S20].
- No scraped personal contacts included by design; the order email + department-store partner channel are the legitimate first surfaces.

## 6. SUGGESTED ANGLE (suggestion, not a finding)

- Suggestion: SHUYA's cream-and-chocolate dessert-prop illustration world maps visually onto LADY M's crepe-cake and glaze aesthetic, so the natural fit is a limited seasonal flavor + illustrated packaging/gift-set drop staged through a department-store pop-up (the exact format LADY M already runs for 台南/高雄) rather than a mass character-cake line.
- Campaign goal this best serves: awareness/brand-identity refresh and gifting/seasonal foot traffic at a specific mall pop-up - not mass volume (LADY M's price points and boutique count keep it niche/premium).
- Actionability caveat: LADY M has zero character-IP precedent and is a premium, HQ-approval brand mid-way through an Asia model reshuffle, so this is a lower-probability, longer-lead target than a mass bakery - best approached via the Taiwan operator 亞茜股份有限公司 and/or a partner mall, with a low-risk single-pop-up pilot proposal. Label: suggestion only.
