# Krispy Kreme (Taiwan) - Partner-Brand Brief

Krispy Kreme IS currently operating in Taiwan under a continuous local agent, so the map-v2 premise that it "exited around 2015 and re-entered" is not supported by any public source found - all evidence points to unbroken operation since December 2013 via 荃鴻股份有限公司 (Quanhong Co., Ltd.).
That makes this a live, actively-campaigning, heavy-IP-collaborating target rather than a dormant re-entry, and it is a frequent character-IP partner (Sanrio, DINOTAENG), which materially raises actionability versus the map's framing.

Research date: 2026-07-22.
Sources note: zh-TW sources are primary for Taiwan status, operator, and campaigns; en sources used for global ownership and history. Social counts are search-metadata estimates (FB/IG are not directly fetchable) and are labelled as such.

## 1. IDENTITY

Official name: Krispy Kreme; Chinese name 卡卡圈坊 (per zh.wikipedia; the map's "卡卡圈" is a shortened form).
What they sell: fresh-made glazed doughnuts (signature Original Glazed / 原味糖霜), seasonal/limited flavors, dozen gift boxes, coffee and drinks - an indulgent US dessert brand ("made fresh daily since 1937").

Global ownership/parent: Krispy Kreme, Inc. (NASDAQ: DNUT).
JAB Holding Company (Luxembourg, backed by Germany's Reimann family) is the controlling shareholder via JAB Indulgence / Agnaten SE, holding 74,885,435 shares = 44.15% of shares outstanding as of 2 Aug 2024 (SEC SC 13D/A).
Krispy Kreme was taken private by JAB in 2016 (~$1.35B) and re-IPO'd in July 2021.

Taiwan operator/franchisee (registry-verified): 荃鴻股份有限公司 (Quanhong Co., Ltd.).
- 統一編號 (business ID): 54055473.
- Status: 核准設立 (active); last approval update 2022-09-23.
- Capital: NT$93,500,000 (paid-in same).
- Representative (代表人): 徐鴻竑 / 徐鴻旋 (registry romanization varies; single-source, GCIS mirror).
- Approved/established: 2013-04-25 (i.e., set up months before the first store opened - consistent with continuous operation, not a re-entry).
- Business scope: candy/baked-goods manufacturing, food and beverage wholesale/retail, F&B consulting.
- Source: company.g0v.ronny.tw mirror of GCIS; corroborated by twincn.com listing no. 54055473.

Scale / history: First Taiwan store opened 2013-12-12 at 信義威秀 (Xinyi Vieshow), Taipei.
zh.wikipedia states 10 stores as of 2024; en.wikipedia (older revision) still says "five stores"; a late-2025 campaign ran across "全台8間門市."
Present footprint is a mix of standalone and department-store counters (新光三越, SOGO, 環球購物中心/Global Mall, 裕隆城, 長庚) across 北北基桃竹.
No public source found supporting the claimed ~2015 exit or a subsequent re-entry - flagged as a likely map error (see headline).

Official site: http://www.krispykreme.com.tw/ (Taiwan; note the site returned connection errors during research on 2026-07-22 - reachability should be re-checked).

## 2. AUDIENCE

Buyers/followers (estimate, inferred from campaign framing and press): urban young-adult and family dessert consumers in Greater Taipei/Taoyuan/Hsinchu who treat Krispy Kreme as an occasional indulgent/gifting treat and queue for limited drops.
Skews female and social-media-active on the collab lines - Sanrio and DINOTAENG collabs are pitched at "Kitty控" and cute-character collectors, and posts lean toward "萌" (cuteness) and collectible postcards (estimate, from campaign copy).
Gifting/seasonal behavior is evidenced by dozen gift boxes and Halloween/seasonal drops.
No published first-party demographic breakdown found (stated: none; the above is estimate).

Cadence read: Krispy Kreme Taiwan runs a steady stream of limited/seasonal and IP-collab drops - an active, campaign-driven brand, not dormant.

## 5. DECISION CONTEXT

Two-layer structure: global brand and product identity sit with Krispy Kreme, Inc. (JAB-controlled), but Taiwan market execution - store network, local/seasonal flavors, and collabs - is run by the local operator 荃鴻股份有限公司 (registry-verified above).
The Sanrio, DINOTAENG, pistachio, and 烤布蕾 campaigns are all Taiwan-market decisions, indicating the local operator has real latitude on IP collaborations - so 荃鴻 is the relevant decision surface for PBC, not global HQ.
Official contact surface (no scraped personal contacts): the Taiwan website (krispykreme.com.tw), the @krispykremetaiwan Facebook page, and the "Krispy Kreme TW" LINE official account are the public inbound channels; the registered entity 荃鴻股份有限公司 (統編 54055473) is the corporate counterpart identifiable via GCIS.
No named marketing individual was found on official/public pages - PBC would need to route via the official brand channels or the registered operator; do not assume rights allocation beyond these stated facts.

## 6. SUGGESTED ANGLE (suggestion, not finding)

Suggestion: SHUYA 舒雅 (illustration IP) fits Krispy Kreme's established, repeatable "cute illustrated-character doughnut box + collectible card + seasonal drink combo" format - the exact template already run with Sanrio (2021) and DINOTAENG (2025), where an illustration IP maps onto glazed doughnut faces and gift boxes.
The most credible campaign goal is seasonal foot traffic / limited-drop buzz (their queue-driving mechanic), with a secondary awareness/identity lift for SHUYA via co-branded boxes and postcards at department-store counters - a lifestyle/pastry angle that matches SHUYA's dessert-prop aesthetic.
Actionability is genuinely high here because the brand is operating and actively collaborating (contrary to the map's exit/re-entry premise); the main open work is confirming the collab-decision contact inside 荃鴻 and that SHUYA's illustration style reads at doughnut/box scale.
