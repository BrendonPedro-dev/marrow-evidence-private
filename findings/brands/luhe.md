# 旅禾 (旅禾泡芙之家 / 旅禾烘焙, Luho / Luff Puff) - Partner-Brand Brief

A viral Taichung tourist-gift cream-puff brand whose whole visual identity is already a self-made character (the puff-helmeted Moai statue and gorilla mascots), which drops naturally into a SHUYA dessert-prop packaging collab, but the brand sits inside a fragmented multi-company structure that just dissolved its original namesake company in Jan 2026, so the correct legal counterparty must be pinned before any pitch.

Research date: 2026-07-22.
All zh-TW quotes below were curl-verified or fetched-page-verified unless explicitly marked unverified or paraphrased.

## 1. IDENTITY

- Brand name(s): 旅禾泡芙之家 (Luho Puff House / "Luff Puff Choux House"), with the online storefront and 2025-2026 marketing now presented as 旅禾烘焙 (Luff Bakehouse / "Luffpuff"); both names are the same underlying brand and share the LINE account @luhopuff [S4, S6].
- Brand-name meaning, from the official about page (paraphrased, fetched-page-verified): "旅禾" comes from the idea of using travel (旅) to seek out ears of wheat/grain (禾), with 禾 symbolizing harvest and beauty; baking is the medium for "collecting" good flavors encountered while travelling [S4].
- What they sell: cream puffs (現灌泡芙 / 冰淇淋泡芙) are the signature product, made with a stated "100% 純鮮乳" cream and "70% 瑞士蓮 (Lindt) 巧克力"; the range also covers bread, cakes, scones, croissants, cinnamon rolls, cookies, and boxed gift assortments (泡芙禮盒, 餅乾禮盒, 鳳梨酥禮盒, 法式豆塔禮盒, 彌月禮盒) aimed at the伴手禮 (souvenir-gift) market [S4, S12, S13, S14].
- CONFIRMED as 旅禾泡芙之家 (the Taichung cream-puff / gift-pastry brand), NOT a Kaohsiung operation as the routing note guessed; the brand is Taichung-headquartered and Taichung-founded [S13, S14].

## 2. AUDIENCE

- Souvenir-gift and tourist buyers: the brand self-positions in the 伴手禮 (gift-souvenir) category with boxed pineapple cakes, cookie boxes, and puff gift sets, and its flagship sits inside 審計新村, a Taichung tourist/creative-district landmark, so a large share of demand is visitor / gifting rather than daily local (STATED positioning + inference) [S13, S14].
- Young, photo-driven, female-skewing collector base (ESTIMATE): the brand's biggest earned-media hits are Instagrammable "打卡" props - the giant puff-helmeted Moai statue and gorilla figures at 審計新村, and the viral 摩艾麵包 (Moai-shaped bread) - which repeatedly "洗版 IG" per lifestyle bloggers; this pattern indicates a young, social-media, cute-aesthetic audience, but no first-party demographic breakdown was found (label as estimate) [S13].
- Value / affordability angle: coverage repeatedly stresses "平價" (cheap) puffs (small puffs cited at NT$16) and "便宜好吃", so the buyer is value-conscious and impulse/gift-driven rather than premium (STATED in third-party coverage) [S13, S14].
- No sourced age/gender split exists on the public record; all demographic reads above are estimates inferred from product, location, and earned-media style.

- 2026 seasonal gift-box slate is live on the official storefront as of 2026-07: 「十圓十美｜2026中秋禮盒」 (Mid-Autumn 2026 gift boxes), 「忙著甜蜜｜2026芒果季」 (2026 mango season), 「爸爸的美好時光｜2026父親節」 (2026 Father's Day), plus a 「企業試吃申請」 (corporate/bulk sampling application) - showing an active, calendar-driven gift-box and B2B-gifting motion [S14].
- Ovaltine (阿華田) co-branded products are on shelf: 【阿華田聯名】麥芽巧克力生乳泡芙禮盒 (6-piece, NT$228) and 【阿華田聯名】脆酷力冰心維也納麵包 (3-piece); this is the clearest external partner collab on the public record, though it is a food-brand collab, not a character-IP collab, and no launch date was found (flag: date not found under queries as of 2026-07) [S12].
- Signature-product / earned-media momentum: the 摩艾麵包 (Moai-shaped bread topped with the brand's puff) went viral as an IG "打卡夯品" ("晚來搶不到"), and 摩艾咬一口 items were re-released; this self-generated "character" merchandising is the brand's main momentum engine rather than paid campaigns (dated by blog coverage 2023-2025, several undated) [S13].
- Corporate restructuring milestone: the original 旅禾泡芙之家有限公司 (42887607) was formally dissolved on 2026-01-02, while 旅禾國際有限公司 (NT$10m, incorporated 2024-05-03) stands as the active brand-front entity - a completed consolidation inside the Jan 2025-Jul 2026 window [S1, S3].
- Reputational overhang (just before the window): on 2024-07 the brand hit a food-safety incident - "爆內餡發霉" (mold found in puff filling) - and the Taichung 食安處 cited deficiencies with a limited-time改善 order; the brand posted a public 15-year-anniversary apology/commitment statement dated 2024-07-19 under 旅禾泡芙之家 / 旅禾國際有限公司 [S9, S10].

## 5. DECISION CONTEXT

- Marketing/product decisions read as owner/founder-led inside a small multi-company group, not a corporate marketing department: the founder-baker 白國文 heads 可麗莎有限公司, and the brand-front entity 旅禾國際有限公司 (responsible person 凃偉鵬) carries the website and e-commerce - so a licensing pitch has to first identify which legal entity signs, given the 2026 dissolution of the original namesake company [S1, S2, S3].
- Official cooperation / business-inquiry surface (no scraped personal contacts): the storefront and LINE list a customer-service line 04-2280-3030 (分機113 for 企業試吃/business, service hours Mon-Fri 09:00-17:30), a 「企業試吃申請」 corporate-inquiry form on the official site, the LINE OA @luhopuff, and the official sites luffpuff.com / luhopuff.com - these are the correct first surfaces for an approach [S6, S14, S12].
- No individual marketing-manager name is published on the official site (checked storefront and LINE as of 2026-07-22); the only named individuals are the GCIS-registered responsible persons (白國文 / 凃偉鵬 / 王瀅綺), which are corporate-registration roles, not published marketing contacts [S1, S2, S3].
- Context flag for the pitch: the recent corporate consolidation (dissolution of 42887607, rise of 旅禾國際) plus the 2024 food-safety episode mean the brand is in a reputation-rebuild / restructuring posture, which can cut both ways - potentially receptive to a fresh, cute, positive collab that resets the brand image, but also potentially cautious and bandwidth-constrained (inference) [S1, S9, S10].

## 6. SUGGESTED ANGLE (suggestion, not a finding)

- Suggestion: SHUYA is the lead card, and the fit is unusually native because 旅禾's entire earned-media identity is already a cute character built on top of a cream puff (the puff-helmeted Moai), so SHUYA's dessert-prop, cream-and-chocolate visual world can wrap the brand's puff/pineapple-cake gift boxes and the viral 摩艾/打卡 merchandising with a licensed, ready-made illustration character instead of a home-made one.
- Tie to evidence: the brand already ships seasonal gift boxes on a calendar (2026 中秋/父親節/芒果季), already does one external-brand collab (Ovaltine), and lives on Instagrammable打卡 props - all three are the exact mechanics a SHUYA packaging-and-photo-prop collab would ride.
- Campaign-goal flag: this pitch maps primarily to brand identity/rejuvenation and foot-traffic/seasonal (a limited gift-box + 審計新村 photo-prop drop), and secondarily to a reputation reset after the 2024 food-safety episode; it is a smaller, regional-Taichung, tourist-gift play, not a national mass-market one.
- Optional second card: Cheesy Duck 確幸鴨 (Thai duck, F&B/picture-book) could suit the same puff/gift-box canvas as a warmer, storybook-flavoured alternative, but the SHUYA dessert-prop world is the tighter match to a cream-puff brand and should lead.
- Hard caveat: before any approach, PBC must confirm the signing entity (可麗莎有限公司 vs 旅禾國際有限公司) given the Jan-2026 dissolution, and should size expectations to a ~32k-LINE / ~36k-FB regional audience, not a chain the size of 85度C.
