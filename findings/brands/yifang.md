# 一芳水果茶 Yifang Taiwan Fruit Tea - Partner-Brand Brief

The single most load-bearing finding is a routing-note correction: map-v2's "has both a collab and outreach event" premise is a false positive for the Taiwan market.
The only Yifang character-IP collabs that verify (BART anime mascots, Oct 2024; Neopets, Dec 2024-Jan 2025) were run by Yifang's US regional franchisees in California, not by the Taiwan operator - exactly the overseas false-positive pattern the task flagged.
Meanwhile the Taiwan operation itself has collapsed since the 2019 "一國兩制" political controversy from a 200+-store peak to roughly a dozen stores today, and its parent 墨力國際's public output is now dominated by overseas expansion and franchise-termination notices, making this a weak Taiwan-market foot-traffic partner despite a clean beverage-category fit on paper.

Research date: 2026-07-22.
Sources note: zh-TW sources (registry, Wikipedia, LTN/UDN/SETN press) are primary for the Taiwan operator, store count, and controversy; the collabs surfaced only in en/overseas sources and are labelled non-Taiwan. Social counts are search-metadata estimates (FB/IG not directly fetchable) and are labelled as such.

## 1. IDENTITY

Official name: 一芳台灣水果茶 / Yifang Taiwan Fruit Tea (also written 一芳水果茶).
What they sell: fruit tea (招牌水果茶 made with a secret hand-cooked fruit jam), 手炒黑糖 (hand-cooked brown-sugar) drinks, pineapple/apple green tea, and hand-shaken (手搖) teas; positioned on "natural / 天然手工" ingredients and a Taiwanese-heritage story.
Founded: ~March 2016, originating in 豐原, Taichung (per zh.wikipedia); the brand grew explosively via franchising in Taiwan and then overseas.

Ownership / parent + 統編 (registry-verified):
- Operating entity / franchise HQ: 墨力國際股份有限公司 (Inkism International Co., Ltd.; also rendered "Mooli International").
- 統一編號 (business ID): 54591495.
- Established: 2013-09-05; last registry change 2026-03-26; status 核准設立 (active).
- Capital (資本總額): NT$150,000,000; paid-in (實收資本額): NT$15,814,160.
- Representative / chairman (代表人 / 董事長): 柯櫻凱.
- Board (5 directors): 柯櫻凱 (董事長), 王保晸, YANG DONG SHAO, 李琬婕, 謝祖鶯 - all representing "Inkism International Holding Co., Ltd." (each subscribing NT$1,581,416).
- Registered address: 臺中市西屯區市政北二路282號9樓之7; registrar 臺中市政府.
- Business scope: 飲料批發/零售, 茶葉批發, 食品什貨, 飲料店, 餐館業, 智慧財產權業 (IP), 國際貿易, 無店面零售, etc.

These are different characters/romanizations - possibly a related individual or a subsequent representative change. Flagged as an unresolved discrepancy; do not treat "柯梓凱 = current legal rep" as confirmed.

Scale (Taiwan stores - the key structural fact):
- Taiwan today: single-digit-to-low-double-digit. The official global locations page lists only a handful of Taiwan stores (花蓮中華, 太平, 新營復興, 文心秀泰, 三井Outlet, 宜蘭, plus a 梧棲 address ~6-8); the Fonfood aggregator (2024 update) lists 13 Taiwan branches. Either way, low double digits at most.
- History: peaked at 200+ Taiwan stores (2016-2018); LTN reported "全台僅剩110家" with Kaohsiung down to 3 stores in the post-2019 contraction (2019-2020). The trend since is continued Taiwan attrition.
- Overseas: large master-franchise footprint (US, Canada, Japan, Hong Kong, Singapore, Philippines, Vietnam, Thailand, Malaysia, Australia, New Zealand, etc.) - this is where the brand's current growth and activity sit, NOT Taiwan.

## 2. AUDIENCE

Buyers/followers (estimate, inferred from product/positioning): mainstream fruit-tea and hand-shaken-tea drinkers drawn to the "天然手工水果茶 / 手炒黑糖" story; historically broad but now a thin Taiwan base given the ~dozen-store footprint.
The ~5.3K IG following on the main account is very small for a chain of Yifang's former scale, consistent with a Taiwan operation that has contracted sharply and shifted attention overseas (estimate).
No published first-party Taiwan demographic breakdown found (stated: none). Overseas franchise activations (US) skew toward younger anime/pop-culture and diaspora audiences (see Section 4), but that is not the Taiwan audience.

Cadence read: the HQ feed is dominated by international store openings and legal/franchise-termination notices; NO Taiwan seasonal campaign, new-product launch, or collaboration appears on the official news surface in this window. This is a brand managing a global master-franchise, not actively marketing to Taiwanese consumers.

## 4. COLLAB / IP HISTORY

Taiwan market: None found under the queries below. No character/IP collab, pop-up, or licensed-goods activation by the Taiwan operator (墨力國際) surfaced.

Overseas (verified, but explicitly NON-Taiwan - these are the routing-note false positive):
- BART (Bay Area Rapid Transit) x Yifang - Oct 16-31, 2024. Run by Yifang Taiwan Fruit Tea Northern California (US franchisee). BART's anime mascots featured on menu items and limited-edition collector cup-sleeves at four Bay Area locations. Source: bart.gov anime events page. (US, not Taiwan.)
- Neopets x Yi Fang Fruit Tea - Dec 2, 2024 - Jan 14, 2025. Run by Yi Fang Fruit Tea USA; four Neopets-character drinks (Kacheek, Aisha, etc.) with themed cup-sleeves and in-store décor across 9 California locations (SF Bay Area + Sacramento). Tied to Neopets' 25th anniversary. Source: portal.neopets.com. (US, not Taiwan.)

Read: character-IP collabs DO happen inside the Yifang system, but only at the overseas regional-franchisee level (US), commissioned and executed locally - there is no evidence the Taiwan operator runs, or has the marketing bandwidth to run, this playbook. For a PBC Taiwan-market pitch, these are not usable precedents; they are the "overseas false-positive" the brief was warned to expect.

Queries run (collab search): "一芳水果茶 聯名 IP 合作 卡通"; "一芳水果茶 聯名 IP 角色 快閃 活動 2024 2025 台灣"; "Yifang Taiwan Fruit Tea collaboration limited edition 2024 2025 US Japan"; on-site scan of yifangteaglobal.com/news. No Taiwan-market character/IP collab was found under these queries.

2019 political controversy (assessed - materially relevant): On 2019-08-05, during Hong Kong's anti-extradition protests, Yifang's China operator posted a Weibo statement supporting "一國兩制" and opposing the strikes and moved to terminate a "stand with Hong Kong" franchisee. This triggered heavy backlash in Taiwan; the chairman said Taiwan sales fell ~30% within a week, losses of ~NT$50M, and ~30 Taiwan stores closed - the start of the Taiwan footprint's long decline. Relevance: this is a live reputational-liability factor for any Taiwan brand considering co-branding with Yifang, and it partly explains why the Taiwan operation is now small and quiet. Sources: zh.wikipedia 台灣手搖茶業者政治表態事件; LTN; UDN; SETN.

## 5. DECISION CONTEXT

Structure: Yifang is a franchise-HQ brand. Marketing/brand decisions for the Taiwan market and the global system run through 墨力國際股份有限公司 (Inkism International) in Taichung, an owner/board-led operation (chairman 柯櫻凱; founder historically 柯梓凱). Overseas activations (incl. the US IP collabs) are executed by regional master-franchisees, not by HQ, so HQ is not a demonstrated character-IP-collab buyer for the Taiwan market.
Given the contracted Taiwan footprint, there is no evidence of a dedicated Taiwan-market marketing function actively commissioning collabs; the HQ surface is oriented to franchise development and governance (see Section 3 notices).
No named individual marketing contact was found on official/public pages (no scraped personal contacts used).
Official contact surface (public inbound channels): the 聯絡我們 / contact link on https://www.yifangteaglobal.com/ (and yifangtea.com), the franchise-enquiry (加盟) sections, the Facebook page https://www.facebook.com/yifangtea/, and IG @yifangtea.official; the registered entity 墨力國際股份有限公司 (統編 54591495) is identifiable via GCIS. PBC should route via these official channels; no rights/territory/exclusivity is inferred.

## 6. SUGGESTED ANGLE (suggestion, not finding)

Suggestion: deprioritize Yifang for a Taiwan-market activation. The clean beverage-category fit and the existence of US IP collabs are what elevated it in map-v2, but the collab premise does not hold for Taiwan, and a ~dozen-store Taiwan footprint cannot deliver the foot-traffic / seasonal goal that a beverage collab is normally pitched for.
If PBC still wants to engage, the least-bad fit is Cheesy Duck 確幸鴨 on a straightforward fruit-tea F&B / picture-book angle (a warm food-friendly duck suits a "水果茶" seasonal drink), mapping to an awareness goal rather than foot traffic; SHUYA 舒雅 could fit a cup-sleeve/illustrated-goods format but faces the same distribution ceiling. Two hard caveats: (a) the small, quiet Taiwan operation may lack the marketing bandwidth to execute, and (b) the unresolved 2019 "一國兩制" reputational baggage is a real co-branding risk for a Taiwan character IP - flag both to PBC before any outreach.
