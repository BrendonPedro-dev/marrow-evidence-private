# Taiwan SPCA 台灣防止虐待動物協會 (TSPCA) - Partner Brief for Bichi Mao 彼奇貓

Taiwan's flagship anti-cruelty NGO runs a proven annual illustrator-collab charity-merch engine (PIKANG 2026, I'm D 2025 raising NT$220k) plus F&B and calendar tie-ins - a natural donation-based home for Bichi Mao's cat and stray-care themes.

## 1. IDENTITY

- Official name: 社團法人台灣防止虐待動物協會 / Taiwan Society for the Prevention of Cruelty to Animals (Taiwan SPCA, TSPCA) [S1, S2].
- What they do: nationwide investigation of animal-cruelty and improper-keeping cases, animal-welfare education, rehoming/adoption of abused animals, policy advocacy, and research [S2, S3].
- The official about page states the founding purpose as 「用一切合法方式保護動物，減少所有動物所受的苦痛」 (curl-verified) [S2].
- Founded 2009-2010 by three founders who came from Canada to Taiwan; incorporated (立案) in 2010 [S2, S13].
- Governance: 社團法人 (incorporated association) with a member-elected 理監事會 (board of directors and supervisors); the site footer shows the MOI registration number 「台內社字: 1000016341」 (curl-verified) [S1, S2].
- The current fundraising permit shown on the donate page is 「勸募字號：衛部救字第1151360057號」 (curl-verified) [S4].
- Member of 台灣公益團體自律聯盟 (Taiwan NPO Self-Regulation Alliance), member number A0250; financial and work reports are published for public review [S13, S14].
- The igiving profile states the organization receives no direct government subsidy and is funded by donations, membership fees, merchandise, and service income (paraphrase, single source) [S15].
- Scale: staff headcount and total budget are not published on the pages checked; the association publishes an annual work report (2024 report released 2025-08-23 via Scribd and Google Drive links on its site) [S5, S6].
- Offices: Taipei (Da-an District, tel 02-2738-2130 / 2131) and Kaohsiung (07-355-6616), per the official site footer (curl-verified) [S1].
- Official site: https://www.spca.org.tw/ [S1].
- Facebook (facebook.com/taiwanspca): approx 175,620 page likes - estimate, from a Google-indexed snapshot of the page, as of 2026-07; not directly verifiable by fetch (single source) [S7].
- Instagram @twspca: 17.8K followers - estimate (rounded figure shown in page metadata), as of 2026-07 [S8].

## 2. AUDIENCE

- No first-party demographic breakdown of followers or donors is published; everything below is labelled accordingly.
- Stated: the association emphasizes recurring small donors - the donate page says stable regular donors are the association's greatest backing and invites 定期定額 (monthly) giving (paraphrase of curl-verified page text) [S4].
- Stated: donor touchpoints include an e-invoice charity code 「協會愛心碼1772」 for everyday retail donations, store donation boxes recruited across Taiwan, and a newsletter subscription via neticrm [S4, S1].
- Estimate (inference from platform mix): the Facebook page (~175K likes) dwarfs Instagram (17.8K), suggesting a core supporter base skewing 30+ Taiwanese adults, with a smaller younger IG/Threads audience [S7, S8, S9].
- Estimate (inference from partner selection): repeated flagship collabs with womenswear e-tailer OB嚴選, photo-gift brand 點點印, and vegan bakery 綠帶純植烘焙 indicate a female-skewing, pet-owning, gift-buying audience [S17, S18, S19].
- Estimate (inference from activity mix): adopters, foster (中途) families, shelter volunteers, and teachers/students reached via school lectures and education programs form the engaged core; the association also draws pet-expo foot traffic (Taipei pet shows in Feb 2025 and June-July 2026) [S20, S21].

## 6. SUGGESTED ANGLE (suggestion, not a finding)

This section is a suggestion, not a research finding.
TSPCA already runs a repeatable illustrator-collab charity-merch engine (I'm D in 2025, PIKANG in 2026, 13 characters including 麻吉貓 in 2016), so a Bichi Mao charity capsule - warm healing cat art on merch or an F&B gift box like the 綠帶 Mid-Autumn box - slots into an existing, proven format rather than asking the NGO to invent a new one.
The natural structure is donation-based co-branding (percent-of-sales or full-proceeds) tied to TSPCA's cat-abuse-prevention and foster/adoption messaging, ideally timed to their annual July campaign window or a seasonal gift-box moment.
Campaign-goal mapping: primarily awareness (animal-welfare storytelling through a healing cat character) with a secondary seasonal/foot-traffic play at the Taipei pet expos and partner F&B outlets; for TSPCA the draw is donor reach into Bichi Mao's younger pet-loving audience, which also serves PBC's identity goal for the IP.
