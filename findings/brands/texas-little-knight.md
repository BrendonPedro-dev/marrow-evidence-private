# 德州小騎士 TFC (德州小騎士有限公司) - Partner-Brand Brief

德州小騎士 is a heritage-nostalgia fried-chicken brand ("Since 1986", once the self-styled 速食界南霸天 with 50+ stores) that collapsed around 1999-2002 and is now a SMALL owner-led revival chain of roughly 8 southern-Taiwan outlets run by the third-generation 鄭謙一 (統編 83077975, capital just NT$5.4m) - so the map-v2 "long-running Taiwanese fast-food brand" framing is true in HERITAGE but not in current scale.
The routing note's claimed "prior collab" could NOT be verified: no Taiwan character/IP collaboration was found under the queries run (see section 4), and the only character in play is the brand's OWN "小騎士" knight logo mascot, which already fronts a "小小騎士" kids-meal line - the recurring house-mascot caveat applies.

## 1. IDENTITY

- Brand name: 德州小騎士 (self-styled English/short form "TFC" on its own site and domain tfc1986.com.tw; tagline "Since 1986" / "40年炸雞經典好風味"); the routing label "Texas Little Knight" is a literal translation, not the brand's stated English name [S2, S3].
- What they sell: a Taiwanese-American fried-chicken QSR - fresh daily hand-fried chicken (現點現做/現炸), chicken burgers (炸雞堡), wavy fries (波浪薯條), snacks and combo meals; the brand leans hard on 六七年級生 (people born in the 1970s-80s) childhood nostalgia [S3, S4, S5].
- Taiwan operating entity: 德州小騎士有限公司 (a 有限公司 / limited company) [S1].
- Registry facts (統一編號 83077975), verified via the GCIS 商工登記 g0v mirror [S1]:
  - 公司狀況: 核准設立 (active/approved).
  - 資本總額 (capital): NT$5,400,000 (a micro-cap; contrast MOS's NT$1bn authorized / McDonald's NT$4bn).
  - 代表人 / 董事長 (chairman): 鄭謙一 (Cheng Chien-yi), 出資 NT$800,000.
  - Other directors: 陳鋐隆 (出資 NT$2,200,000) and 張馨尹 (出資 NT$2,200,000) - i.e. 鄭謙一 is the public face but not the majority capital holder on paper (flagged; ownership economics not fully public) [S1].
  - 公司所在地 (registered): 臺南市東區小東里小東路198巷40弄17號1樓.
  - 核准設立日期: 2020-09-10; 最後核准變更: 2023-11-17.

- Ownership / lineage (brand's own telling): the brand traces to the Basset family (巴斯家族) Texas fried-chicken business; introduced to Taiwan in 1986; the second-generation chairman 鄭蓋聖 acquired the trademark rights and continued the Kaohsiung 五福路 operation, and the current third-generation operator is 總經理/董事長 鄭謙一, who is driving a nationwide direct-store revival [S2, S6].
- Trademark provenance: per trade coverage, the southern operator's family (a nephew, "鄭先生") reached a 2007-09 settlement with the US originator to 一次買斷 the 小騎士 Asia-region trademark and secure material-supply authorization - i.e. the current brand OWNS the mark in Asia rather than running a live foreign franchise (relevant to how freely it can license) [S7].
- Scale (current): about 8 outlets, all in southern Taiwan (Kaohsiung, Tainan, Pingtung, Chiayi), per the official site store list [S3]. This is a small revival footprint - a correction to any impression of a large national chain.
  - Official store list (2026-07): 義享門市 (高雄鼓山), 新左營車站門市 (高雄左營), 屏東車站門市, 安南醫院門市 (台南), 遠百成功門市 (台南), 三井台南門市 (歸仁), 大全聯嘉義門市, 台鋁門市 (高雄前鎮) [S3].
- Historic peak (for heritage context, not current): at its 全盛期 the southern operation was dubbed the 速食界南霸天 with about 52-53 stores; it declined from 1999 (民國88年) amid an economic downturn and a US-originator licensing dispute, and had largely exited Taiwan by around 2002 [S4, S5, S8].

## 2. AUDIENCE

- Core is explicitly nostalgia-driven: coverage and the brand's own copy repeatedly frame it as the 六七年級生 (born ~1971-1992) childhood-memory chicken, "屬於五六年級生的青春記憶", now returning [S5, S6, S12].
- Skew inference (estimate, not stated): the primary buyer is a 30s-40s adult acting on nostalgia (often now a parent bringing their own kids), reached in mall food-courts and department-store / hospital / outlet locations in Kaohsiung-Tainan-Pingtung; the "小小騎士" kids-meal line signals a deliberate secondary parent-child segment [S3, S13].
- Geographic skew is real: the current base is southern Taiwan, not the whole island (stated by the store list) [S3].
- No disclosed demographic or membership data was found; the above is directional inference from location choices and campaign language, not a company statement.

## 3. RECENT MOMENTUM (approx. Jan 2025 - Jul 2026)

The dominant storyline is a physical-revival / store-expansion push, not campaigns or IP:
- 2023: revival press wave - "德州小騎士" returns to Tainan and Kaohsiung ("捲土重來"), driving nostalgia coverage in UDN and Yahoo [S6, S8, S12].
- 2025-03: opened its first in-hospital outlet, 台南安南醫院門市 (B1F), on a "輕量模式" (chicken and snacks only, no full burger combos) - a small-format expansion channel [S13].
- 2025-04: 台南 second street-retail store confirmed at 台南三井 OUTLET 3F 美食廣場 (歸仁), described as opening that month; 遠百成功門市 also in the lineup [S13, S3].
- 2025 (dated on-site post): 台南遠百成功門市 正式開幕 announcement on the official site [S14].
- 2026 (year-turn): a "德州小騎士炸雞年終回饋迎接2026" promotion posted on the official Instagram - a routine seasonal value push, not an IP event [S15].
- Ongoing: continued mall / department-store / outlet / transit-station location roll-out (新左營車站, 屏東車站, 台鋁 MLD, 大全聯嘉義, 義享) rather than free-standing flagship stores [S3].
- No IP/character collaboration, kids-meal toy tie-in, or licensed-premium campaign was found in this window (see section 4).

In-house mascot (the competitive caveat):
- The brand's own identity IS a character: the "小騎士" (little knight) figure fronts the logo and signage, and the brand name literally means "Texas Little Knight" [S3].
- It already runs a kids sub-line branded on that mascot: the "小小騎士" menu category (e.g. "Ａ-經典配餐" NT$79, "8號-主餐小小騎士餐" NT$119) - a kids-meal surface, though no toy/character premium is documented with it [S17].
- Read-through: like MOS's MOS Friends and McDonald's 小麥麥, 德州小騎士 owns a house mascot it pays no royalty on, and it already has a kids-meal shell branded on that knight - so any external duck must co-star with, or clearly out-earn, the incumbent knight rather than assume an empty character slot (see section 6). That said, unlike MOS/McDonald's, there is NO evidence the knight has been built into a picture-book / plush / toy program - it currently reads as a logo mascot, not an activated IP.

## 5. DECISION CONTEXT

- Decisions are owner-led and centralized in a very small company: this is a family-run micro-cap (統編 83077975, capital NT$5.4m) whose public face is 董事長/總經理 鄭謙一, repeatedly named in press as the operator personally driving the revival and expansion - there is no evidence of a separate marketing department or franchise HQ layer [S1, S6, S7].
- Practical implication: a licensing pitch here reaches a decision-maker fast (owner-led), but the counterparty is tiny, southern, and capital-light, so any deal must be low-cost / low-minimum-guarantee and operationally simple to fit ~8 stores.

## 6. SUGGESTED ANGLE (suggestion, not a finding)

- Suggestion: pitch Cheesy Duck 確幸鴨 as a low-cost, plug-in kids-meal / packaging character that gives 德州小騎士's existing "小小騎士" kids line a collectible hook (sticker sheet, cup/box art, or a small premium) - matching the chain's nostalgia-and-family positioning while adding the meme-friendly, all-ages sticker energy the knight logo does not currently generate.
- Tie to evidence: the brand already has a kids sub-line ("小小騎士", NT$79-119) but no evidence of any toy/premium or IP program on it, and its whole revival narrative is 30s-40s parents revisiting a childhood brand with their own children - a natural family-character window that is currently empty of licensed IP [S17, S6, S13].
- House-mascot caveat (important): the brand IS its own knight character, so lead with a CO-STAR framing ("確幸鴨 visits 小騎士" / duck-and-knight kids set) rather than a replacement, and lean on cost and simplicity given the ~8-store, capital-light footprint [S3, S1].
- Campaign-goal fit: primarily foot-traffic / seasonal and family-audience acquisition for a small expanding chain (kids-meal uplift, mall-location differentiation), with brand-freshness a secondary benefit; it is NOT a scale awareness play - reach is small and southern, so weigh this as a low-cost proof-of-concept target, not a flagship account.
