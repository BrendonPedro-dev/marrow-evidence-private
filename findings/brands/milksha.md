# 迷客夏 Milksha (Taiwan) - Partner-Brand Brief

Milksha runs a proven, repeatable character-IP-collab playbook in the Taiwan market, and its most recent one (韓國「MIND.A.DAY」, launched 2026-05-06) is a cat character paired with pet bandanas, explicitly pitched at the "萌寵經濟" / cat-parent audience - which is the single most load-bearing finding for a Bichi Mao fit.
That collab is the public activation that most plausibly sits behind map-v2's PBC-internal "decisive signal noted in May 2026," and it means PBC is pitching into a brand that already believes cat/pet IP drives its foot traffic, not one that needs convincing.
Ownership is the key structural caveat: since Nov 2021 Milksha's parent is Philippine giant Jollibee (Jollibee Worldwide Pte. Ltd. holds the board), so collab decisions run through the Taiwan operating entity 迷客夏國際股份有限公司 under a Jollibee-controlled board.

Research date: 2026-07-22.
Sources note: zh-TW sources are primary for operator, campaigns, and collabs; en sources used for global Jollibee ownership. Social counts are search-metadata estimates (FB/IG are not directly fetchable) and are labelled as such. The MIND.A.DAY campaign body was curl-verified verbatim on the official site.

## 1. IDENTITY

Official name: 迷客夏 / Milksha (the brand unified its overseas English name to "Milksha" from Sept 2022; earlier English/romanized forms included "Milkshop").
What they sell: hand-shaken (手搖) tea and fresh-milk drinks - positioned on using real fresh dairy milk instead of creamer (奶精), sourced from Taiwanese dairy farms ("綠光牧場" / Green Light Farm concept), plus fruit teas and seasonal lines.
Brand tone (first-party): wholesome, agricultural-heritage, "認真做好東西，總有一天會被看見" (do good things earnestly and one day it will be seen), with a recurring "小確幸" (small daily happiness) and social-welfare ("綠光計劃") framing - a gentle, family-friendly voice, consistent with map-v2's "gentle wholesome tone" note.

Ownership / parent + 統編 (registry-verified):
- Operating entity: 迷客夏國際股份有限公司 (Milksha International Co., Ltd.).
- 統一編號 (business ID): 28949151.
- Established: 2008-03-24; last registry update 2025-12-09; status active.
- Capital: NT$300,000,000 (paid-in NT$160,000,000).
- Representative (代表人) / chairman (董事長): TONY TAN CAKTIONG - the founder and chairman of Jollibee Foods Corporation.
- Board is Jollibee-controlled: 董事長 TONY TAN CAKTIONG, 董事 CARL BRIAN TANCAKTIONG and DON ALEXANDER LIM all represent JOLLIBEE WORLDWIDE PTE. LTD.; local directors 黃士瑝 and 郭佳玲 also sit on the board.
- Registered address: 臺南市北區西門路4段271號7樓之3 (Tainan).
- Parent: In Nov 2021 Jollibee Foods Corporation (Philippines) acquired 51% of Milksha International for US$12.8M (per zh.wikipedia and press); the board composition above confirms Jollibee control.
- Source: company.g0v.ronny.tw GCIS mirror (統編 28949151); corroborated by twincn.com listing no. 28949151.

Founder: 林建燁 (Lin Jian-Ye), who started the brand in Tainan.
Founding year has conflicting public dates: zh.wikipedia says 2007; the official About page frames the first Tainan store around 2004 with the "綠光" concept; the company was incorporated 2008-03-24 (registry). Flagged as a minor discrepancy - the brand is ~2004-2008 origin, Tainan.

## 2. AUDIENCE

Buyers/followers (estimate, inferred from product/campaign framing): mainstream young-adult and family hand-shaken-tea drinkers across Taiwan (very broad 300+-store national footprint, not a Taipei-only niche), skewing toward health-leaning / "natural, no-creamer" and family-with-kids positioning (product names like "小孩愛吃 蜂蜜蛋糕" lean family/child-friendly).
Notably, Milksha itself has publicly identified a pet-owner ("毛家長" / 貓奴爸媽) segment as a target: its 2026 summer campaign cites 內政部/農業部 data that registered pets now exceed the number of children under 15 in Taiwan, and built merchandise (pet bandanas) for that audience (stated by brand; see Section 4).
Collab merch buyers skew toward collectors / cute-character (萌) and eco-cup audiences (estimate, from campaign copy emphasizing "限量" collectibles and reusable cups).
No published first-party demographic breakdown found (stated: none; the above blends brand statements and estimate).

## 4. COLLAB / IP HISTORY

Format: five add-on-purchase items - 造型環保杯 (NT$399), 布偶飲料袋 (NT$159), 寵物圍兜兜 / pet bandana (NT$129), 雙杯保冷袋 (NT$29), plus a "迷之喵盲袋貼紙" gift-with-purchase. ## 5. DECISION CONTEXT

Structure: Milksha's global HQ is Taiwan (the Taiwan entity is the worldwide parent for the brand's own stores), but corporate control sits with Jollibee - the board of 迷客夏國際股份有限公司 is majority-composed of Jollibee Worldwide Pte. Ltd. representatives (chairman TONY TAN CAKTIONG), with local directors 黃士瑝 and 郭佳玲.
Marketing execution: campaigns, seasonal menus, and the IP collabs (KINGJUN, 文博會, MIND.A.DAY) are all Taiwan-market decisions run out of the Taiwan operation, so the local marketing function - not Manila - is the relevant collab-decision surface; the Jollibee-controlled board is the ownership layer above it.
No named individual marketing contact was found on official/public pages (no scraped personal contacts used).
Official contact surface (public inbound channels): the 聯絡我們 page (https://www.milksha.com/contact-2.php), the 加盟專區 (franchise) section, the @Milkshatw Facebook page, the "迷客夏 Milksha" LINE official account, and the registered entity 迷客夏國際股份有限公司 (統編 28949151) identifiable via GCIS. PBC should route via these official channels; do not assume any rights/territory allocation beyond these stated facts.

## 6. SUGGESTED ANGLE (suggestion, not finding)

Suggestion: lead with Bichi Mao 彼奇貓. Milksha has just (May 2026) run a cat-character collab explicitly built around the "萌寵經濟" and "貓奴爸媽" (cat-parent) audience, including pet bandanas - a warm, healing-style cat IP with pet/animal-welfare affinity is a near-exact match for the format and audience Milksha itself defined, and Bichi Mao's cozy tone lines up with Milksha's "小確幸 / 綠光" wholesome voice.
Most credible campaign goal: seasonal foot-traffic + collectible-merch buzz (their proven add-on-purchase eco-cup / drink-bag / pet-item mechanic), with a secondary identity/warmth lift - i.e. slot Bichi Mao into the same summer-collab template that MIND.A.DAY occupied, ideally with a pet-item SKU that leans into Bichi Mao's animal-welfare positioning.
Secondary fits (both ranked lower for Milksha): Cheesy Duck 確幸鴨 also works as a warm F&B-friendly character, and SHUYA 舒雅 fits Milksha's demonstrated illustrated-cup /文博會-style program; but the cat-plus-pet evidence makes Bichi Mao the strongest lead. Open work: confirm the Taiwan marketing contact inside the Milksha operation and that the annual collab slot for 2027 is uncommitted.
