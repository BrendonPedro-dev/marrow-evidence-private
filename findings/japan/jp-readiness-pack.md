CONFIRMED: rows 19 (Loft, 18 illustrators), 52 (Don Quijote, 3 illustrators), 77 (graniph, 12 illustrators), 140 (Pixio, 4 illustrators).
ASSUMED: a roster slot is a different ask from a solo campaign and the census shows Japanese brands assembling these rosters themselves.
