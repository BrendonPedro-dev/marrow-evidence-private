The headline. On one common instrument, independent IP is 8.5% to 34.9% of co-branding campaigns across ten Asian markets - a 4.1x range that is continuous, not two tiers. The largest gap inside the distribution is 6.5 points and it occurs twice: once at the proposed tier boundary and once in the middle of the proposed top tier.

What does divide the ten markets. Not geography, not machinery, not press attribution, not scene depth. The form of the independent layer: whether its rows are named character IP or one-off artwork commissions, and if character IP, whether it is captive, consolidated, or routing direct to consumers. Three of the five new verdicts found that distinction independently.

2. The two-tier question - tested, and rejected

The commissioning framing for this document proposed that the five new markets split the region: East Asia's machinery markets (TW/JP/KR, 27-35%) against thin-layer maritime SEA (SG/PH and possibly VN at single digits), with MY and TH as the gradient. That framing was tested against the ten verdicts on four grounds. It fails all four.

The largest gap is 6.5 points and it occurs twice - once between Korea and Taiwan, inside the proposed upper tier, and once between Thailand and Hong Kong, at the proposed boundary. A boundary no wider than the widest gap inside one of the groups it separates is not a boundary. On the numbers alone this is a continuous distribution with a wide range, not two clusters.

2.2 Two of the five low markets cannot be placed in a tier at all, by their own verdicts. Quoted at its brand-side floor (2.6%) Hong Kong falls below Vietnam; quoted at its artist-side tranche hit rate (68.8%) it tops the series; its verdict calls the indie picture "provisional" and the portfolio picture "adequate". Indonesia's verdict instructs a floor reading with a soft ceiling near 20%, which is Thailand's level, and its one open ownership question (the INFIA call, worth ten rows) resolves the other way to 23/102 = 22.5% - above Thailand and Malaysia both. Its own words: "The whole verdict hangs on this call."

2.3 Geography does not predict membership. Hong Kong is an East Asian market and sits sixth. Thailand is mainland SEA and sits fifth, above it. Malaysia and Thailand are the two SEA markets in the upper group and neither has the machinery the "East Asia machinery" label is built on. "East Asia" and "maritime SEA" are not the cut.

2.4 The ordering changes when you tier on addressability instead of the ratio

Tier membership depends on which number you tier on, which is the plainest evidence that the tiers are not a property of the markets. The addressable range is 1.2% to 17.8% - 14.8x, wider than the ratio's 4.1x - and its largest gap is 4.2 points, also continuous.

2.5 What survives: a division by the form of the independent layer

Three of the five new verdicts found this independently, in three different languages, without coordinating. It is not where the market is. It is what kind of thing the independent rows are.

So the headline 8.5% and the underlying independent-character share of 1 in 47 (2.1%) are different claims, and only the second one answers the question this market was opened to answer. 2.1% is the lowest independent-character share in the nine-market series, and no earlier market required this distinction to be drawn, because in the earlier markets the independent rows were overwhelmingly character IP already.

3. The measurement story

This section exists so an external reader can check the instrument before checking the result.

One definition, applied ten times

The identical ownership test (who owns the IP, not who runs the brand), the identical pinned edge cases, the identical REPRESENTABLE sub-tag on every independent row. The pinned cases travel by name across all ten campaigns.md headers, and the precedent chain is cited by row number in every market: Butterbear is portfolio in Thailand on the Malaysia row 8 call; LuLu the Piggy is independent/REP=no on the Taiwan-v2 row 5 call, carried into Thailand and then into Hong Kong across six rows in the label's home market unchanged; venue-hosted properties are rows and the licensor's own retail is not, on the Taiwan-v2 row 126 call, applied in all ten; authorship is not ownership, on the Molly Factory precedent, applied to MOLLY in Singapore, to Kasing Lung and Kenny Wong in Hong Kong, to MOLLY and MEGA SPACE MOLLY in Vietnam; estates are portfolio, which places the Philippines' National Artists on the portfolio side; artwork licences are in scope on the illustration-IP limb, extended by the adidas PH x Aral Cru precedent, which is what admits Vietnam's rows 15, 38 and 39.

Every row is curl-verified against a live, dated source

Not a claim about a campaign - a fetched page: HTTP 200, with the year read off machine-readable page markup or a stated campaign window, never inferred. Thailand re-verified all 97 cited URLs and went from a claimed 85 rows to a verified 83. Singapore re-verified all 95, "each with a machine-readable datePublished in the page's JSON-LD. No row rests on an inferred date." Hong Kong re-verified all 138 and cites no news.google.com URL anywhere: "Google News is the index and the publisher page is the evidence." The Philippines verified all 117 and found a verification trap worth carrying - BusinessMirror serves randomly rotating 403s, so "a single-pass verifier would produce a misleading dead-link count." Vietnam verified 94 of 97, the three failures all one blocked domain and no row resting on it alone. Indonesia verified all 174 and added the series' dating rule: on flat-sitemap hosts, never take the year from metadata at all.

3.7 The keyword is not the classifier

Artist-side keywords are a self-presentation detector, not an ownership detector, and they fail in both directions in every market where they have been run. Taiwan's dtto friends bills itself as 「原創角色品牌」 and is Dcard platform-owned; Japan's BLUE HAMHAM is framed as one creator's work but is © CHOCOLATE, and ナガノマーケット self-presents as the creator's own market but is portfolio under the pinned Chiikawa rule. Korea produced the mirror image: 틴틴팅클 self-presents as an indie instatoon and its IP business is run by Iconix, while 먼작귀 appears in LG's own release as a "글로벌 인기 캐릭터 IP" with no mention of Nagano, yet is creator-originated. Singapore added tokidoki (row 26). Hong Kong added three at once (rows 85, 53/63, 83). Indonesia added the one that decides ten rows: Tahilalats, whose creator Indonesian press consistently calls a komikus, classed portfolio on four independent pieces of evidence about INFIA Corp's holding. The pinned per-IP ownership test did the classification work in every case. Accepting self-description would have inflated Taiwan alone from 27.5% to 31.9%.

The rule, carried verbatim from RESWEEP_SUMMARY.md:

any concentration claim made from a brand-side census should be assumed to be a query artefact until it has been tested against an artist-side sweep.

That rule applies to this page too. Section 5 applies it by name to the concentration findings in the new five markets, including the one it kills.

2. The instrument is common, but not uniformly deep

The first caveat is that the instrument is common but not uniformly deep. Taiwan's leg added 9 rows and Korea's added 23, not because Taiwan has less indie activity but because Taiwan's brand-side pass had already caught most of it; the residual risk is that a market with a low correction was well-swept and a market with a high correction may still be under-swept.

The direction is right and the mechanism is evidenced; the magnitude is not a confidence interval.

RESWEEP_SUMMARY.md

The corrected-thin / measured-thin / instrument-bounded distinction in section 4.3 is the ten-market form of this caveat, and it must travel with any use of the addressability numbers. Only Singapore's 1 and Vietnam's 4 are measured on a full instrument. Korea's 17 and Malaysia's 14 are corrections of numbers that were query artefacts. Hong Kong's 6, the Philippines' 14 and Indonesia's 8 are floors set by named, quantified instrument limits, and all three verdicts say so.

Three markets' instruments differ in kind, not only in depth, and that is new with this document.

  • Hong Kong's instrument was rebuilt mid-run, twice: "It is the first market in the series where the method itself broke and had to be rebuilt mid-run, twice, and both breaks are load-bearing on how the numbers below should be read." 479 of 781 candidates remain unresolved.
  • The Philippines' instrument is the narrowest in the series among the markets that used the WP-REST method: 20 portals of which 14 contributed, with spot.ph absent entirely.
  • Vietnam's instrument is a different method altogether - a three-wave sitemap archive sweep of 2,607,744 URLs across 28 publishers, because both the WP-REST portal sweep and the Google News RSS fallback are dead there.

Malaysia remains the least settled of the original five (honest range 18.4% to 25.0%, plus a named backlog worth about four points). 3. These are sample censuses, not exhaustive counts
