# Malaysia co-branding census - campaign log

This is a growing, curl-verified table of identifiable brand-x-IP **co-branding** campaigns in the Malaysia market, 2024-01-01 to run date.
Scope is co-branding only - a brand pairing with a character/illustration/toy IP for products, drinks, food, events, pop-ups or stores.
F&B-led but not F&B-only: retail, convenience, telco, banking, and mall campaigns count when a character IP fronts a consumer campaign.
Excluded: plain licensed merchandise at retail, KOL/celebrity endorsements, and brand-x-brand tie-ins with no character IP.
**Status at 2026-07-29: 103 curl-verified rows (21x 2024, 49x 2025, 33x 2026YTD).** The local-creator sweep that the earlier draft flagged as owed has now run: it was worked through the Malaysian licensing-agency rosters (M&M Creations, Majikku IP, Milolo), the local-illustrator trade press, and the brand-run artist-collab product lines, and it lifted the indie count from 7 to 13. See `_VERDICT.md` for the resulting ratio and the coverage-confidence read.
This is the MALAYSIA run in a region-by-region series (Taiwan v2: 129 rows at `findings/census/taiwan-v2/campaigns.md`; Japan: 131 rows at `findings/census/japan/campaigns.md`; Korea: 135 rows at `findings/census/korea/campaigns.md`); identical definition and rules so all markets compare row-for-row.

**Method (sample census, not exhaustive):** sweep of EN/BM/zh Malaysian lifestyle and trade press (SAYS, WeirdKaya, Vulcan Post MY, Marketing-Interactive, MARKETECH APAC, WauPost, TheSmartLocal MY, Hype MY, Siakap Keli, mall socials), brand official announcements and campaign pages (Tealive, ZUS, Pizza Hut MY, Secret Recipe, FamilyMart MY, MyNews, Touch 'n Go), the Malaysian promo-aggregator vein (EverydayOnSales, msiapromos, malaysiafreebies, syioknya), and licensing trade press.
Every row below was confirmed against at least one fetched source URL; rows resting on a single roundup source are flagged in the Notes column.
Phantom (unverifiable) entries are the known failure class and are kept out.

**Counting rules applied to the Malaysian promo landscape (so the row count is not inflated):**
- **One campaign, one row.** Where the same program is listed under several retailer legs or weekly waves (VITAGEN x Sanrio at MYDIN/HeroMarket/Jaya Grocer, Garnier x Miffy at Watsons/AEON Wellness, llaollao's four Minions listings), it is counted once and the other legs are named in Notes.
- **Prize-draw promos are NOT rows.** "Spend and win a Labubu / POP MART blind box" contests (Marrybrown, Sosbanyaki, myNEWS, SnowFit, Qi Fen Tian, OGON ODEN, Dadi Cinema, Bomb Battle x Coca-Cola) buy the toy as a prize; there is no licensed brand-x-IP campaign, so they are excluded.
- **Unnamed generic characters are NOT rows.** Capybara-shaped collectibles with no named IP (ZUS Dec 2024 sticker sheet, Kenangan Nov 2025 blind box) are excluded - no identifiable IP means no ownership call.
- **Illustrator IP counts, on the prior markets' precedent.** A brand licensing a named Malaysian illustrator's artwork or characters for a co-branded product line is a row (scope line above reads "character/illustration/toy IP"), matching Taiwan-v2 rows 120/127 and Korea rows 78/79. A brand hiring an artist to execute brand-owned creative with no licensed property is not.
- **Plain licensed merchandise at retail is NOT a row** (Becon Stationery Sanrio/Pokemon shelves, Gamers Hideout TCG coin deals, Parkson Disney Princess clearance); a retailer-RUN collectible or redemption program (FamilyMart blind boxes, Jaya Grocer plushies) is.

**YEAR tag** is by campaign start/run date: `2024` = 2024-01-01..2024-12-31, `2025` = 2025-01-01..2025-12-31, `2026YTD` = 2026-01-01..run date.

**IP class** = PORTFOLIO (owned/controlled by a major licensor, studio, media/platform conglomerate, or estate that manages OTHER IP) / INDEPENDENT (living-creator- or small-studio-owned, no conglomerate parent; agency-managed but creator-owned still counts - the Capoo precedent) / UNCLEAR (state why).

**Pinned edge cases (identical to Taiwan-v2/Japan/Korea runs):** Miffy/Mercis BV = PORTFOLIO (estate); Snoopy/Peanuts = PORTFOLIO (Sony controlling stake); Sanrio / San-X / Pokemon-Nintendo / Disney / KAKAO FRIENDS / LINE FRIENDS-BT21 = PORTFOLIO (platform-owned); government/public-body-owned mascots = EXCLUDED entirely (the Kumamon rule); a brand's OWN mascot = NOT COUNTED; co-owned/JV = whoever holds control, genuine 50/50 = UNCLEAR.

**Malaysia-specific rules:**
- **Franchise-local activation:** a global chain's MY arm (Pizza Hut Malaysia, Tealive, ZUS, Secret Recipe, MyNews, FamilyMart MY, McDonald's Malaysia) running a character campaign counts as a Malaysia row even when the IP deal was struck regionally - the campaign ran in MY. Brand recorded as the MY operator, with "regional deal, MY activation" noted where evidenced. A regional campaign is NOT counted into MY unless MY activation is evidenced (MY store presence, MY-market announcement, BM/MY-social promotion).
- **Source-language honesty:** sources span EN, BM, and zh. Where a row rests on a BM or zh source and translation confidence is low, it is flagged in Notes rather than guessed.
- **Known-entity rule:** Bichi Mao's own MY collabs are recorded from PUBLIC sources only, exactly like any other row - no internal knowledge enters the census.

**REPRESENTABLE** (INDEPENDENT rows only) = yes / no / unclear: yes = creator-owned, no conglomerate stake, not locked to an exclusive agency/captive arrangement in a way that precludes representation (a plausible PBC client); no = indie by ownership but already exclusively tied up; unclear = can't tell from public sources. Non-indie rows carry '-'.
