## Method and verification notes for this market

- **Instagram under a crawler user-agent** (`Googlebot/2.1`) was the workhorse, as in every prior market. `og:description` returns the count.
- **The Instagram web_profile_info API was unavailable for this entire market** - it returned `{"message":"Please wait a few minutes before you try again.","require_login":true}` on the first and only call attempted. Consistent with the Malaysia finding that the API cannot be planned on. No band here depended on resolving a rounded figure, so nothing was lost.
- **TikTok was checked under `facebookexternalhit/1.1`** for the market's animation/comic IPs, which is where Malaysia found the channel choice can flip. It did not flip here: Capoo 1,154, 麻糬爸 50.2k, Kanahei 17 - all far below their Instagram accounts. **Taiwan's large IPs are Facebook-and-Instagram-native, not TikTok-native.**
- **YouTube mattered for exactly one IP.** 黃阿瑪的後宮生活 is YouTube-first and its channel (1.56M) exceeds its Instagram (1M); the subscriber count came from the channel-header accessibility label `156萬位訂閱者`, with the 萬 = 10,000 multiplier applied.
- **A grep of all 38 census source pages for follower vocabulary** (粉絲 / 追蹤 / 訂閱 / followers / 萬人 / 萬粉) returned two usable hits - rows 126 and 136 - against dozens of false positives from publisher furniture ("udn粉絲團", "訂閱電子報", "官方粉絲團"). **This is the first market where the census's own sources yielded a channel-specific figure**, and it yielded two.
- **The census sources link no creator accounts at all.** Every `instagram.com/` link across all 38 pages resolves to a publisher, a venue or a brand (`myudn`, `ettodaytw`, `talkacemedia`, `cavatw`, `marieclairetw`, `techbang3c`, `pier2art`, `milksha_tw`, `happyhome.tw`, `lavietw`, `oneone.toy`). Same pattern as Thailand. Handles had to come from elsewhere.
- **Working handle-discovery routes in this market, in the order they paid off:** (1) the artist's link-in-bio page - Portaly recovered `@nuomi0213`; (2) the `og:description` of dated third-party Instagram reels - recovered `@mindaday_studio`; (3) the body of a *non-census* trade write-up - keedan.com carried `@matsui9988` inline where the census source had nothing; (4) name-plus-medium search; (5) direct guessing, which failed for most rows.
- **Not verifiable in this market:** Facebook page counts (they matter for Capoo, LAIMO, 白爛貓 and Mr. STRIKE, all Facebook-heavy Taiwanese IPs), X/Twitter (it matters most for Kanahei), and the `plus82.com.tw` licensing agent's blog, which returns HTTP 403 to a crawler UA.
