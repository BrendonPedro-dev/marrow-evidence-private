# Taiwan co-branding census - verdict

As of 2026-07-22 (fifth extension sweep, same day).

This verdict reads the campaign table in `campaigns.md` (129 curl-verified brand-x-IP co-branding rows, window 2024-01-01 to run date).
It is a **sample census, not an exhaustive count** - see Section 4 for limits.
The brief is to attack the founder thesis ("indies are shut out") as hard as the data allows; where the data undercuts the thesis, that is called out plainly.

**Per year:**

**Trend read:** across the two complete years the indie share now reads **22% (2024) and 32% (2025)** - up from the 15%/28% the census showed at 101 rows.
This fifth sweep added 28 rows by opening the gaming/mobile-app format and a department-store/public-event/venue-popup format, plus more 2024/2026 drink collabs; 13 of those 28 new rows came back independent, which lifted the overall indie share from 21.8% to **27.1%** and raised every single year.
The lift is not noise: it is what happens when the sweep moves off mass-volume-licensed-merch formats (fast food, beauty, supermarket loyalty) and onto **experiential formats - pop-up stores, public events, and game-launch tie-ins - where Taiwan illustrator IPs win disproportionately** (Section 3).
2026YTD ticked up to 25.0% on four new indie rows (Monday Bruce x 台北101, KKOTKA x 新光三越, 露咖貓 x 駁二, 咖波 x 咻咻史萊姆), but the 2026 sample is a partial year and carries 4 of the 7 UNCLEAR rows; treat it as thin, not as a trend.

Honest statement for the deck: **in the complete years 2024-2025, independent creator IPs took roughly 1 in 4.6 to 1 in 3.2 of identifiable Taiwan co-branding campaigns (22% in 2024, 32% in 2025), with corporate portfolios taking ~67-73%; the indie share rose year-on-year and rises further whenever experiential/event formats are sampled.**
Cite the 22-32% complete-year range rather than a single point - the number is coverage-sensitive (Section 4), and it has moved up, not down, as the census has broadened.

**Do indies get big-brand collabs or only small ones?** The sample says clearly: they get big ones, institutional ones, landmark-venue ones, game-launch ones, and global-conglomerate-beauty ones.

- 咖波 alone (now 8 rows) has landed a top-tier drink chain (清心福全), **two Taiwan mobile-game LAUNCH collabs (出發吧麥芬, 咻咻史萊姆 - both by 傳奇網路-adjacent studios choosing an indie IP to headline a launch)**, a major restaurant chain (Sushiro, six themed stores), **the national postal service (中華郵政)**, **a 7,100-store 7-ELEVEN 全店集點 wave fronting the 故宮 100th anniversary**, **a listed financial holding company's shareholder gift (華南金控)**, and **CTBC's 12th annual public ice rink built entirely around the character**.
- **The strongest new format finding: experiential/venue campaigns skew indie.** The department-store/public-event/venue-popup sweep returned 5 of 6 rows independent - Monday Bruce winning the **Taipei 101 observatory** special exhibition, 露咖貓 winning the **駁二 Pier-2 arts district**, KKOTKA touring two department stores, and 咖波's CTBC ice rink. When the campaign is about a physical experience rather than moving licensed volume, brands reach for indie IPs.
- The premium 7-ELEVEN 集點 format is no longer a portfolio monopoly (Kanahei, 白爛貓+我不是胖虎, 咖波 x 故宮, noodoll, Miffy all inside 7-11 waves), and the gaming format is genuinely mixed: Taiwan mobile games pick 咖波 to headline launches, while imported/large games (風之國度, Garena AOV, 心動小鎮) buy Sanrio.
- Marquee conglomerate counter-examples still hold: **馬來貘 LAIMO x CeraVe/L'Oréal** and **ZERO PER ZERO x 雪花秀/AmorePacific** - indies fronting the biggest cosmetics conglomerates on Earth.
- 黃阿瑪 and KKOTKA both show **repeat business** (富采 two consecutive years; KKOTKA two Taiwan venues), evidence of renewal rather than one-off novelty.

**Breadth caveat:** the indie bench widened sharply this sweep (13 -> 21 distinct IPs), the largest single-sweep jump in the census, adding matsui, Dinotaeng, KKOTKA, Monday Bruce, 露咖貓, 俵谷哲典, unpis, and solo mofusand.
But concentration remains: 咖波 is 8 of 35 indie rows (23%), and most IPs are established, popular names that brands sought out - not unknowns given a first break.
The one structural first-timer channel is still a government culture fair (臺灣印事/腋毛人 via 文博會), and several of the new entrants (Monday Bruce, 露咖貓, KKOTKA) broke in through **experiential venues**, not through mass-retail brand outreach.
Supporting (not counted, IP-x-IP): LuLu the Piggy x 咖波 ran a joint product line from 2026-06 (cava.tw/265108; gnn.gamer.com.tw/detail.php?sn=304779).

## 3. The thesis read

**Verdict: the data does NOT support "indies are shut out" - and the fifth sweep weakens the thesis further, because the two newly-opened formats (gaming launches and experiential/venue pop-ups) are the most indie-friendly the census has found. The defensible residual is a format-tier claim: mass-volume-licensed-merch formats (fast food, beauty, supermarket loyalty, fast fashion) run ~70-100% portfolio, while experiential and premium formats are genuinely open to proven indies. Overall indie share is now ~27% and rising.**

- The blunt thesis ("indies can't get deals / can't reach big brands") is **contradicted** at every tier the sample covers: national retail (7-ELEVEN waves), major chains (Sushiro, 清心福全, 麥味登, 丸亀製麵), public institutions (中華郵政, 故宮, Makung festival, **Taipei 101 observatory**, **駁二 Pier-2**), transit infrastructure (悠遊卡), listed corporates (華南金, 富采, **CTBC public ice rink**), department-store pop-ups (Kanahei A11, KKOTKA x2), **mobile-game launches (出發吧麥芬, 咻咻史萊姆, 超蝦大作戰)**, apparel (UNIQLO x mofusand, Decathlon x 俵谷哲典), and global-conglomerate cosmetics (LAIMO x CeraVe/L'Oréal; ZERO PER ZERO x 雪花秀/AmorePacific).
- The defensible refined thesis has two parts.
  **(a) Format tiers, now sharper.** The mass-media-fanbase, high-volume-licensed-merch formats ran essentially portfolio: fast food, conveyor-sushi gacha, convenience-store gaming/coffee tie-ins (11 of 11), coffee chains (6 of 6 portfolio or artist-UNCLEAR), fast fashion (GU/UNIQLO/PAZZO/CACO apparel skews portfolio: 6 of 8), supermarket loyalty (全聯 - 2 of 2), VTuber-era sports events (味全龍 x hololive, both years), hotels (3 of 4 Sanrio), and cosmetics/beauty (14 rows: 11 portfolio, 1 UNCLEAR, 2 marquee indie).
  **But the experiential formats invert this:** the department-store/public-event/venue-popup sweep was **5 of 6 indie**, and Taiwan-operated mobile-game launches reached for indie 咖波 twice. The moat is not "big brands vs indies" - it is **"move national licensed volume -> buy portfolio; build an experience or headline a launch -> indie is competitive."**
  **(b) Bench.** Indie access still concentrates in a handful of marquee creator IPs (咖波 alone is 23% of indie rows); the door is open to proven indies, wider than a year ago, but not yet to unknowns.
- Strongest quotes for the counter-thesis: the 7-ELEVEN x 咖波 x 故宮100周年 全店集點 across 7,100+ stores (talkacemedia.com/article/42024), **Monday Bruce's Taipei 101 observatory exhibition framed by press as 推廣台灣原創IP (udn.com/story/7241/9419978)**, **咖波 headlining two mobile-game launches (gnn.gamer.com.tw/detail.php?sn=261446, sn=301523)**, 華南金's 2026 shareholder gift (money.udn.com/money/story/5613/9412556), and CeraVe x 馬來貘 (marieclaire.com.tw/beauty/news/84300).
- Sharpest fact **for** the portfolio-moat argument: the high-volume formats (fast food, beauty, supermarket, fast fashion, gacha) remain portfolio almost without exception, and the biggest indie wins are all pre-existing marquee IPs the brands sought out - the moat is format-shaped, not brand-size-shaped.

Net: pitch this as **"the door is open and widening - experiential and launch formats are already indie-competitive - but the bench is thin and the high-volume licensed-merch tiers stay portfolio-locked."**
A ~22-32% complete-year indie share (rising), carried by a proven few who now reach flagship 7-11 waves, national institutions, landmark venues, and game launches, while fast food, coffee, fashion, supermarkets, gacha, hotels, and cosmetics run portfolio almost without exception.
That framing is honest to the data and stronger for a licensing-infrastructure pitch than "indies are shut out": the opportunity is widening the bench and unlocking the volume formats, not breaking down a single wall.

## 4. Method limits (stated plainly)

- **Sample, not census.** 129 rows from a curated sweep of zh-TW press, official campaign archives, and roundups; absolute counts are illustrative, ratios are the usable signal.
- **The ratio is coverage-sensitive - and has trended UP as coverage broadened.** Complete-year indie share read ~33% at 17 rows, ~40% at 39 rows, ~33% at 58 rows, 18%/32% at 84 rows, 15%/28% at 101 rows, and **22%/32% at 129 rows**; each swing tracked which formats were added (indie-heavy premiums/postal/events/gaming vs portfolio-heavy chain, beauty, and fashion formats). The remaining undated backlog (HOYACASA x 黃阿瑪, DEVILCASE x 白爛貓, 合作金庫 x 卡娜赫拉 cards, 塔麻可吉 pop-up) is mostly indie/held for phantom-guard reasons and would nudge the share up if dated/fetchable.
- **December clustering.** The year-end collab season is over-covered by roundups; much of the 2024 mass is December.
- **Chain-archive, beauty-roundup, and now venue-sweep weight.** 17 of 129 rows are 7-ELEVEN waves, 14 are drugstore/beauty, and this sweep deliberately over-sampled experiential venues/games; big-chain archives and single-topic sweeps are easy to verify and therefore over-represented relative to one-off brand campaigns that never hit the press. The 5-of-6 indie result in the venue sweep is real but reflects that format, not the market as a whole.
- **Window.** 2024-01-01 to 2026-07-22; **2026 is a partial year**, its rows lean on roundup/venue clusters, and it holds 4 of the 7 UNCLEAR calls - do not cite 2026YTD standalone.
- **Single-source rows.** Rows resting on one roundup or one microsite are flagged in the table's Notes column; flagged rows are directional, not independently corroborated. GNN gamer.com.tw pages return 403 to WebFetch and to plain curl, and were verified via curl with a browser User-Agent header.
- **UNCLEAR is honest, not lazy.** Seven rows (OBJECT/SOSO ownership, 功夫 film-IP borderline, 乖乖 brand-mascot borderline, OSAMU GOODS estate ambiguity, Bunni Konbiny ownership, 草間彌生 artist-vs-character borderline, Brunch Brother ownership) are counted separately and never force-bucketed.
- **Classification sensitivity.** Miffy is INDEPENDENT on the ownership rule (Mercis bv) despite legacy-brand scale; KKOTKA (YOUNG FOREST studio) is INDEPENDENT but flagged as possibly UNCLEAR pending deeper ownership check; mofusand alone is INDEPENDENT (creator ぢゅの) even though row 19's Sanrio-paired mofusand was PORTFOLIO; 伊藤潤二 is PORTFOLIO as publisher/licensor-managed (木棉花) manga IP. Venue-hosted pop-ups (A11, 華山, 松菸, 駁二, 誠品, KKOTKA, Taipei 101) are kept per the row-20 precedent and flagged as borderline vs plain retail licensing.
