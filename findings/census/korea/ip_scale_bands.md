# Korea - IP scale bands

Per-row follower-scale bands for every INDEPENDENT row in `findings/census/korea/campaigns.md`.
Band vocabulary is exactly `lt_10k` / `10k_50k` / `50k_200k` / `200k_1m` / `gt_1m` / `unknown`, matching the /start intake's `follower_band` codes character-for-character.
Band reads the IP's PRIMARY single channel at campaign time - never a cross-platform total.

`campaigns.md` is not modified by this file.

**Scope:** 53 INDEPENDENT rows (28 REP=yes, 15 REP=unclear, 10 REP=no). No PORTFOLIO or UNCLEAR rows appear here.
**Retrieval date for every current count below: 2026-07-30.**

**Korea is an Instagram market, and this is the first market in the pass where that was tested rather than assumed.**
Every Korean IP whose Instagram account was located was also checked on X via the fxtwitter API and on TikTok, and **X lost every single time** (망곰이 737K IG vs 233,823 X; 최고심 306K vs 189,803; 다이노탱 393K vs 357; 무직타이거 203K vs 9,828; 웜그레이테일, 조구만, 에스더버니, 깜자, 마인드어데이 have no X account at all).
TikTok is effectively unused by Korean character IPs: 짤쓸사람 5 followers, 최고심 20, 다이노탱 8, 무직타이거 121, 유랑 no account.
This is the exact inverse of Japan, where 33 of 45 banded rows read from X, and it means the platform-choice risk that dominates Japan barely exists here.
The two exceptions are both non-Korean or non-illustration IPs: 치이카와 and 빤쮸토끼 are Japanese and X-native, and 빵빵이 / 김햄찌 are YouTube-native.

## Method and verification

**Channel routes used**, all curl-verified on 2026-07-30:
- Instagram, crawler UA against the plain profile URL, `og:description` (31 of the 49 banded rows).
- X/Twitter, `https://api.fxtwitter.com/<handle>` (7 rows: 가나디 x3, 치이카와 x3, 빤쮸토끼, 비야 - and as the anti-drift cross-check on every Instagram row where an X account exists).
- YouTube, `https://www.youtube.com/channel/<id>/about`, `subscriberCountText` (3 rows: 빵빵이, 다이노맨, 김햄찌). **This route worked here where it regressed in Japan**, because the `/channel/<id>/about` form on a single-IP channel yields the channel's own count rather than the featured-channels trap. The locale returned 萬 units and was converted.
- TikTok, `facebookexternalhit/1.1` UA. Run across the market's five largest IPs and it never changed an answer.

**Handle discovery, what worked in Korea.** The Japan finding does not carry over: a grep of all 53 fetched census source pages for social links returned **publisher and brand handles only** (bridge_economy, cmnkorea, ilovecharacter, lg_with_u, moneytodaynews, sportsseoul, shinsegaegroup, and so on), never a creator. An `@`-mention grep of the same 53 pages returned only newsroom email addresses. Two handles were already in `campaigns.md` itself (rows 88 and 154). Everything else came from Korean-language search plus the IP's own site (estherbunny.net confirmed `@estherbunny.kr`; noyisin.com confirmed `@noyisin`), and from the non-census trade write-up route (the ilovecharacter creator-interview archive supplied row 93's dated figure).

**Name-guessing failed almost completely again**, and the reason is romanisation rather than obscurity: 최고심 is `@gosimperson`, 곽철이 is `@qwakcheol_official` (not `kwakcheori`), 깜자 is `@jobeam_studio`, 하이다나 is `@haydonna_art`, 노이신 is `@noyisin`, 나무13 is `@__tree_13`, 가나디 is `@bdemgmr`. **Campaign-press follower figures: 2 usable hits in 53 sources, both channel-named.** A whole-corpus grep of all 53 fetched sources for 팔로워 / 팔로어 / 구독자 / 만 명 returned two real hits, and both set a band: heraldcorp on 빵빵이 ("유튜브 구독자 224만명", row 59) and ilovecharacter on 비야 ("X(옛 트위터)에서 100만 명 이상의 팔로어", row 145). Everything else the grep surfaced was sidebar noise (population figures, museum visitor counts, Disney+ subscriber stories). **A third and fourth at-campaign band came from bracketing rather than from a single dated figure** (rows 93 and 155): a dated figure before the campaign plus a live figure after it, with both endpoints inside the same band, cannot be moved by drift in either direction. This is stronger than the single-floor construction used in Thailand and Taiwan, and it is available whenever a growth-stage IP has any dated figure at all.
