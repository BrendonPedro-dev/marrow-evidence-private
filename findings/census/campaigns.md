# Taiwan co-branding census - campaign log

This is a growing, curl-verified table of identifiable brand-x-IP **co-branding** campaigns (聯名) in the Taiwan market, 2024-01-01 to run date.
Scope is co-branding only - a brand pairing with a character/illustration/toy IP for products, drinks, events, or stores.
Excluded: plain licensed merchandise at retail, KOL/celebrity endorsements, and brand-x-brand tie-ins with no character IP (e.g. a drink brand x a cosmetics brand, or a drink brand x a textile label).

**Method (sample census, not exhaustive):** sweep of zh-TW lifestyle/food press (聯合新聞網 udn, 自由時報 玩咖, WalkerLand, 造咖 cava.tw, 食尚玩家), campaign roundups, convenience-store event pages, and licensing coverage.
Extension sweep (2026-07-22) added the 7-ELEVEN official campaign archive (7-11.com.tw/event), finance press for corporate-premium collabs (ETtoday財經雲, 經濟日報), gaming press (巴哈姆特 GNN), airline/transit coverage (中央社, udn科技玩家), and brand campaign microsites.
Every row below was confirmed against at least one fetched source URL; rows resting on a single roundup source are flagged in the Notes column.
Phantom (unverifiable) entries are the known failure class and are kept out.

**YEAR tag** is by campaign start/run date: `2024` = 2024-01-01..2024-12-31, `2025` = 2025-01-01..2025-12-31, `2026YTD` = 2026-01-01..run date.

**IP class** = PORTFOLIO (owned/controlled by a major licensor, studio, or media/platform conglomerate) / INDEPENDENT (creator- or small-studio-owned, no conglomerate parent) / UNCLEAR (state why).
