# Thailand - IP scale bands (independent rows)

Enrichment pass over `thailand/campaigns.md`. One row here per INDEPENDENT census row.
The census table is the index and is not edited by this pass.

**Band vocabulary (exactly these codes):** `lt_10k` / `10k_50k` / `50k_200k` / `200k_1m` / `gt_1m` / `unknown`.
These match the /start intake's `follower_band` codes character-for-character.

**Band definition:** the IP's PRIMARY channel following at the time of the campaign - a single channel, not a cross-platform total.

**Evidence basis codes:** `at-campaign` (countable figure at or near the campaign window) / `current-proxy` (only a CURRENT count findable; retrieval date recorded, NOT at-campaign truth) / `unknown` (no countable public evidence; Notes record what was checked).

**Coverage:** 17 independent rows (11 REP=yes, 5 REP=unclear, 1 REP=no). 1 at-campaign, 14 current-proxy, 2 unknown.

**Thailand is the first market in this pass where the two unknowns are both foreign IPs activating in Thailand** (a Korean art-toy label and a Chinese studio's characters), not Thai creators.
Every Thai-creator row in this market resolved to a countable channel. That is a better indie-side result than Singapore or Hong Kong produced.

## Method notes for this market

- **Not one Thai trade-press source in this market states a following.** All sixteen census source URLs for the independent rows were fetched and grepped for `ผู้ติดตาม` / `ฟอลโลว` / `follower`; every one returned nothing. This is now the third market where campaign press carries zero scale data, and Thailand is the cleanest test of it because both trade titles were swept.
- **The one at-campaign figure came from outside the census's own sources** - an English-language animation-industry trade feature (animationXpress) covering an ASEAN content summit panel, not from the Thai campaign press. That is a route worth trying in the remaining markets: industry-conference coverage quotes creators on their own audience in a way brand PR never does.
- **Thai trade press links no artist social accounts.** Grepping nine census source pages for `instagram.com/` or `facebook.com/` handles returned only the publisher's own accounts and the Facebook tracking pixel. Handle discovery here was search-driven, not source-driven, which is the opposite of the Hong Kong pattern.
- **Handle discovery needed the Thai name, not the IP name.** Guessing off the character name failed for Bad Meaw, Dylie, SHEWSHEEP and Molly Factory; searching the creator's Thai legal name from the census's own class-evidence column found all four. The census's insistence on naming the creator in Thai is what made this pass possible.
- **Squatted and near-miss handles are common and dangerous here.** `@dylie`, `@dylieofficial`, `@gongkan`, `@lilitae`, `@lili_tae`, `@maleenaree`, `@crybunny`, `@sijuki`, `@dotoy` all exist, all look plausible, and none is the IP - most are dormant accounts with under 100 followers. Every band above records the rejected handle so the choice is checkable.
- **Two of three foreign IPs in this market could not be banded** (YIMU ART, DOTOY) while every Thai-creator row could. The pattern is that China- and Korea-based small labels activating in Thailand carry their audience on platforms this method cannot read, and their Thai activation press names no handle.
