# Indonesia - IP scale bands (independent rows)

Enrichment pass over `indonesia/campaigns.md`. One row here per INDEPENDENT census row, plus census row 2 on its Si Juki side (the MIXED row, included per the task's scope rule).
The census table is the index and is not edited by this pass.

**Band vocabulary (exactly these codes):** `lt_10k` / `10k_50k` / `50k_200k` / `200k_1m` / `gt_1m` / `unknown`.
These match the /start intake's `follower_band` codes character-for-character.

**Band definition:** the IP's PRIMARY channel following at the time of the campaign - a single channel, not a cross-platform total.

**Evidence basis codes:** `at-campaign` (countable figure at or near the campaign window) / `current-proxy` (only a CURRENT count findable; retrieval date recorded, NOT at-campaign truth) / `unknown` (no countable public evidence; Notes record what was checked).

**Multi-creator rows** (6 and 77) carry the **highest band present** in the row's Band cell, with per-creator bands in the `## Multi-creator detail` table below. That detail table is the one to count creator-level distributions from - the same convention the Philippines and Hong Kong files use.

## Method notes for this market

- **Not one Indonesian census source states a following.** All thirteen independent-row source URLs were fetched and grepped for `pengikut` / `follower`; every one returned nothing. Indonesia therefore joins Thailand, Singapore, Hong Kong and the Philippines - **five markets, zero at-campaign figures in the campaign press itself**.
- **The one dated figure came from an influencer-listicle vein, not from campaign press.** `ice.id`'s "5 Influencer Seni Indonesia" carries a machine-readable `datePublished` and states per-artist Instagram counts. This is a genuinely new evidence channel for the remaining markets and worth trying early - but it comes with a warning attached, below.
- **Listicle follower figures are dated at the article, not at the measurement, and that distinction decides bands.** The same ice.id page settles Darbotz (both drift readings land in one band) and fails to settle Muklay (the two readings land three tiers apart). The usable test is not "is the article dated near the campaign" but "do both drift directions from this figure land in the same band". Applying that test is what keeps row 7 honest and stops row 10 from being falsely precise.
- **Indonesian creators publish under working names, not legal or census names.** Hari Prast is @harimerdeka; Si Juki's channel is @jukihoki, not any `sijuki` variant; Muklay's is his own nickname. Two of the three would have been recorded `unknown` on direct-name guessing alone. Search on the working name and the IP name together, not on the creator name the census records.
- **The character-vs-creator split is at its most extreme in this market.** Si Juki: character 803K, creator 44K - three tiers. This is not a corner case to note in passing; it is the single largest source of potential band error in the Indonesian set, and it runs in the opposite direction to Hong Kong's BUCKET MAN (where the *creator* was the larger account).
- **Facebook remains the blind spot.** Several of these artists almost certainly carry larger Facebook followings than Instagram ones, and Facebook page counts are not curl-verifiable under any user-agent tried. Every band here is therefore "largest evidenced channel", which is the rule as written, but it is a floor rather than a proven maximum.
