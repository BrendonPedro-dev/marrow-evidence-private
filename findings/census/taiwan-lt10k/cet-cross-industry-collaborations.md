Build 4 artefact. Swept 2026-08-07 from both live editions.

```

```

**Why this is different from every other vein in this run.** Veins A, E and F are indexes of *people*: a partner page, a roster record, an exhibitor page. This is an index of *deals* - the 文博會 organiser (台灣設計研究院, under 文化部) writing up, in its own words, every cross-industry partnership it brokered that year, naming both parties, the product and the dates.
For a task whose unit of output is a collaboration, that is the difference between a lookup table and a source.

**It is also a mechanism finding, not just a source finding.** Every accepted row it produced traces to the same intermediary. For sub-10k Taiwanese IP, the route to a national brand runs through a state-backed trade show rather than through the brand's own inbound channel - the organiser is doing the matchmaking that the creator's follower count would not win on its own.

**Sweep mechanics.** ids 1-70 on both hosts. Both apps 302 unknown ids rather than 404ing, so a page counts only if the final URL's trailing id equals the requested id. Page chrome (nav, footer, related links) is stripped before reading; the body is prose and names its counterparties inline.

**The four 'no partner named' entries are the vein's real failure mode.** 新光三越 gives away a 「限量創意公仔」 and names no designer; 宇萌數位 links 75 IP brands and names none; GOODART says 「與全球藝術家聯名」 and names none. Each is a documented brand collaboration with a small IP that cannot be turned into a row, and there is no obvious second source for any of them.

## The lead that produced seven rows, and it was not in this section

The 2026 品牌商展 opening release - `https://creativexpo.tw/zh-TW/posts/38`, not a `collaboration_responses` page - contains the clause that mattered:

> 「ibon雲端列印再度攜手文博會，邀集16位新銳創作者推出超過140款原創授權商品」

The same release also names two further deals that this build did not work: **NeverHaveIEver x 10 組臺灣原創IP** into the New York Shoppe Object show, and the 2026 LaLaport stamp collaboration. And the phrase 「再度」 says there was a **2025 ibon cohort**, whose creator list was not found.

**Lesson for the next build: sweep `posts` as well as `collaboration_responses`.** The organiser's news feed carries deals that its own partnership section does not, and the 16-creator clause is one sentence inside a 3,000-character release.
