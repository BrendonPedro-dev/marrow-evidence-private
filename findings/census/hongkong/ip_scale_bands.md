# Hong Kong - IP scale bands (independent rows)

Enrichment pass over `hongkong/campaigns.md`. One row here per INDEPENDENT census row.
The census table is the index and is not edited by this pass.

**Band vocabulary (exactly these codes):** `lt_10k` / `10k_50k` / `50k_200k` / `200k_1m` / `gt_1m` / `unknown`.
These match the /start intake's `follower_band` codes character-for-character.

**Band definition:** the IP's PRIMARY channel following at the time of the campaign, single channel not cross-platform total.
**Hong Kong is the market where the anti-drift rule bites hardest.** Three rows here have a character channel *and* a distinguishable creator/label channel, both evidenced, at different bands. The character's channel governs in every case, and the alternative is recorded so the choice is auditable. Row 87 is the extreme case: the character reads `10k_50k`, the creator's personal account would read `200k_1m`.

**Evidence basis codes:** `at-campaign` (countable figure at or near the campaign window) / `current-proxy` (only a CURRENT count findable; retrieval date recorded, NOT at-campaign truth) / `unknown` (no countable public evidence; Notes record what was checked).

**Multi-creator convention.** Row 82 carries three creators. Its Band cell holds the highest band present; per-creator bands are in the *Multi-creator detail* table below, which is the one to count from for creator-level distributions.

## Method notes for this market

- **The @-mentions of a dated third-party Instagram post are a working handle-discovery route** when campaign press links nothing. The `og:description` of `https://www.instagram.com/reel/<id>/` fetched with a crawler UA returns the full caption *including* its date and its @-mentions, which is how two of row 82's three handles were recovered. Worth trying before concluding a creator has no channel - but check each tagged account, because venues and stockists get tagged too.
- **Rounded `og:description` values are a real limitation at two band edges.** "10K" straddles `lt_10k`/`10k_50k` and "50K" straddles `10k_50k`/`50k_200k`. Two rows here hit it (87 at "11K", 82's 豚豚 at "50K"). Where the campaign predates the count, the drift direction resolves the edge: a proxy read forward in time overstates, so the lower band is the safe call. Where the campaign is *concurrent* with the count that argument is unavailable - which is exactly row 73's problem.
- **Character-channel vs creator-channel is not a corner case in Hong Kong, it is the norm.** Rows 78/79/81/89/91/92 (character 266K vs label 75K vs regional 8K), row 87 (character 11K vs creator 224K) and row 90 (character 25K vs creator 21K) all present the split. Nine of thirteen rows. A pass that silently took "the biggest account with the right name on it" would have banded HK far higher than the rule permits.
- **This market has the most repeated IPs of any so far**: LuLu the Piggy appears in six of thirteen rows and Plastic Thing in two. Eight of thirteen rows resolve to two IPs. Any pooled band distribution over census rows will therefore be dominated by LuLu's `200k_1m` - the summary must say so rather than present HK's distribution as if it described thirteen independent IPs.
