# Hong Kong co-branding census - campaign log

This is a curl-verified table of identifiable brand-x-IP **co-branding** campaigns in the Hong Kong market, 2024-01-01 to run date (2026-07-29).
Scope is co-branding only - a brand pairing with a character/illustration/toy IP for products, drinks, food, events, pop-ups or stores.
F&B-led but not F&B-only: retail, convenience, telco, banking, mall, transit and loyalty campaigns count when a character IP fronts a consumer campaign.
Excluded: plain licensed merchandise at retail, KOL/celebrity endorsements, and brand-x-brand tie-ins with no character IP.
**Status at 2026-07-29: 77 curl-verified rows (35x 2024, 24x 2025, 18x 2026YTD). BUILD IN PROGRESS - this is the first tranche, not the finished census.**
Every source URL in the table below was re-fetched at build time: **115 URLs, all HTTP 200.**
The candidate pool this tranche was drawn from holds **781 filtered campaign candidates** of which 302 have been resolved to publisher URLs and 77 adjudicated into rows; the remaining pool is the next tranche's work and the target remains ~120-140.
This is the HONG KONG run - market seven in a region-by-region series (Taiwan v2: 129 rows at `findings/census/taiwan-v2/campaigns.md`; Japan: 131 rows at `findings/census/japan/campaigns.md`; Korea: 135 rows at `findings/census/korea/campaigns.md`; Malaysia: 103 rows at `findings/census/malaysia/campaigns.md`; Thailand: 83 rows at `findings/census/thailand/campaigns.md`; Singapore: 81 rows at `findings/census/singapore/campaigns.md`); identical definition and rules so all markets compare row-for-row.

**Method (sample census, not exhaustive).**
Hong Kong broke the method used for the six earlier markets, and the replacement is worth stating precisely because it changes what the coverage claim means.

The six earlier markets were swept by hitting a small set of named consumer portals through their own WordPress REST endpoints (`/wp-json/wp/v2/posts?search=...`).
**That vein does not exist in Hong Kong.**
Every major HK title was probed directly and failed: `hk01.com`, `am730.com.hk`, `holidaysmart.io`, `hk.ulifestyle.com.hk` (港生活), `girlstyle.com`, `urbanlifehk.com`, `mamidaily.com`, `timeout.com`, `tatlerasia.com`, `elle.com.hk`, `esquirehk.com`, `marketing-interactive.com` and `openrice.com` all return HTTP 404 on `/wp-json/wp/v2/posts` (headless or non-WordPress stacks); `weekendhk.com` (Weekend Weekly), `nmplus.hk` (新假期), `orangenews.hk` and `discoverhongkong.com` return 403 behind Cloudflare; `localiiz.com` returns 526; `bastillepost.com` returns 503; `sundaykiss.com` exposes the endpoint but throws a WordPress DB error on any `search=` parameter; `thehoneycombers.com` exposes the endpoint but returns an empty array for every query.
Only `sassyhongkong.com` answered a WP REST search cleanly.
A one-portal census is not a census.

**The replacement vein: Google News RSS, resolved to publisher URLs, then curl-verified at the publisher.**
`news.google.com/rss/search` accepts a query, a locale (`hl=zh-HK&gl=HK&ceid=HK:zh-Hant` and the `en-HK` equivalent) and Google's `after:`/`before:` date operators, and returns up to 100 items per query with the publisher's own `pubDate` and `source` attribution.
Its article links are Google-encoded, so each was resolved back to the publisher URL through Google's own `DotsSplashUi/data/batchexecute` endpoint using the per-article `data-n-a-ts` / `data-n-a-sg` signature pair carried on the article page.
Every retained row was then confirmed by a `curl` against the **resolved publisher URL**, returning HTTP 200 with the campaign detail in the page body.
No row cites a `news.google.com` URL; Google News is the index, the publisher page is the evidence.
The Date column is the evidenced campaign window where the body states one, and the publisher's own publication date otherwise (marked "announced").

**What this buys and what it costs.**
It buys breadth the WP-REST method never had: **135 queries returned 5,125 distinct articles across 60+ HK publishers**, led by 香港01 (411), U Food (310), UHK 港生活 (300), 香港經濟日報 HKET (226), U Lifestyle (213), 星島頭條 (187), Time Out Hong Kong (160), 新假期 (136), AM730 (131), Eastweek (97), Popbee (93), on.cc 東網 (48), Esquire HK (45), SCMP (39) and ezone.hk (30).
That is the Cantonese-register consumer press the brief asked for, and it is a far wider publisher net than any earlier market's five portals.
It costs an indexing dependency: coverage is now bounded by what Google News indexed for HK, not by what a named publisher published.
Titles absent from Google News are absent from this census regardless of their own output, and the 100-item-per-query cap means high-yield keywords were split by year to break the ceiling.
This limit is stated again in `_VERDICT.md` section 5 and is the honest ceiling on the coverage claim.

**Hong Kong-specific rules:**
- **The label-capital rule - this market's defining call.** Hong Kong is the home market of Toyzeroplus (LuLu the Piggy), How2work, Kennyswork and the wider designer-toy label economy, and the brief predicted this class would decide the market's indie number.
The rule, dug past every label to the creator: a character **owned by the label** is PORTFOLIO; a character **creator-owned but exclusively label-channelled** is INDEPENDENT with **REP=no**; a character **creator-owned and non-exclusively label-distributed** is INDEPENDENT with REP set per evidence.
**LuLu the Piggy / Toyzeroplus = INDEPENDENT, REP=no** carries in unchanged from Taiwan-v2 row 5 and Thailand row 12 - and note that in Hong Kong this is the *home-market* application of a precedent set abroad.
- **The cross-border trap (the GBA edition of the SG regional-HQ rule).** HK and Greater-Bay/mainland campaigns are routinely announced together and reported by HK media as one item.
A row requires **evidenced HK activation**: named HK stores/outlets, an HK-market launch date, an HK mall or transit venue, HK$ pricing, or .hk commerce.
Mainland-only or Macau-only activations announced via HK media are rejected and listed in "Rejected rows" below.
- **Mall and transit concentration, and the anti-inflation merge.** K11, Langham Place, Times Square, Harbour City, LCX, New Town Plaza, Pacific Place, Elements, Festival Walk, apm and MOKO run character campaigns at very high density, and MTR runs character tie-ins at network scale.
One campaign is one row even when it spans several venues of one operator: venues are noted in the Notes column, not split into rows.
- **Art-toy retail.** The licensor's own retail is NOT a row - POP MART store openings and a Sanrio/Toyzeroplus own-store opening are excluded.
A licensor property **hosted by a third-party venue** IS a row, on the Taiwan-v2 row 126 venue precedent.
- **State-adjacent brands.** The Kumamon rule excludes government-*owned mascots* - a test on the IP side.
A statutory body or state-linked operator (MTR Corporation, Ocean Park, the Hong Kong Tourism Board) appearing on the *brand* side, licensing a third-party character IP, is a normal row and is flagged in Notes.

**REPRESENTABLE** (INDEPENDENT rows only) = yes / no / unclear: yes = creator-owned, no conglomerate stake, not locked to an exclusive agency/captive arrangement in a way that precludes representation (a plausible PBC client); no = indie by ownership but already exclusively tied up; unclear = can't tell from public sources.
Non-indie rows carry '-'.
