## 1. ORIGIN

Kanahei is one named person, not a studio.
The public record establishes a pre-character phase, a platform phase, and only then a licensing phase.

**Pre-character phase (2002-2013): mobile wallpaper, then commercial illustration.**
Her own licensing-window press release and her official site both give the same one-line origin: "2003年に女子高生イラストレーターとしてプロデビュー" ("professional debut in 2003 as a high-school-girl illustrator"), and describe her as specialising in communication-oriented content, "ガラケーデコメやLINEスタンプなど" ("feature-phone decoration mail and LINE stickers").
Source: [at Press, 2025-04-01, 株式会社ミキサー](https://www.atpress.ne.jp/news/0705633); [カナヘイの小動物 official site, WORKS](https://kanaheis-small-animals.jp/works/).

The 2014 master-licence press release from TV Tokyo Holdings gives a fuller version of the same origin, and is the earliest first-party account found:
"高校のときから自身のイラストサイトに個性的なアニメ、漫画キャラクターのパロディで人気を博し、在学中の2003年に集英社の『Seventeen』誌で、プロ・デビューを果たしました。これをきっかけとしてパロディ画像を止め、独自の「カナヘイ画」で、イラストや3D・CG、PCや携帯電話の待受画像、Flashアニメを発表して人気を不動のものにしました。"
Translation: "From high school she became popular through distinctive parodies of anime and manga characters on her own illustration site, and while still enrolled made her professional debut in 2003 in Shueisha's Seventeen magazine. Taking that as the cue she stopped the parody images, and made her popularity permanent by releasing illustrations, 3D/CG, PC and mobile-phone standby images and Flash animation in her own 'Kanahei-ga' style."
Source: [PR TIMES, 2014-11-06, 株式会社テレビ東京ホールディングス](https://prtimes.jp/main/html/rd/p/000000304.000002734.html).

Note what that says about sequence.
The parody work was the audience-builder, and she dropped it at the point of turning professional.
Eleven years of commercial illustration and mobile content sit between the debut and the first licensing deal.

**Platform phase (2014): LINE Creators Stickers.**
LINE's own award page records `カナヘイのピスケ&うさぎ` (creator credited as カナヘイ) taking 準グランプリ (runner-up grand prize) at LINE Creators Stamp AWARD 2014.
The same page states the prize itself included "公式スタンプでの販売権" ("the right to be sold as an official sticker"), and that 20,000+ creator stickers had been sold since the service opened in May 2014.
Source: [LINE Creators Stamp AWARD 2014, creator.line.me](https://creator.line.me/ja/award/2014).

The TV Tokyo Holdings release of the same year states it differently and is worth keeping separate rather than merged:
"2万5千以上のタイトルが競う激戦の「LINEクリエイターズスタンプ」で、「カナヘイ」8タイトルの内、5タイトルがトップをとりました。"
Translation: "In the fiercely contested LINE Creators Stickers, where over 25,000 titles compete, 5 of Kanahei's 8 titles took top place."
Source: [PR TIMES, 2014-11-06](https://prtimes.jp/main/html/rd/p/000000304.000002734.html).
The two figures (20,000 vs 25,000 titles) come from two different publishers on different dates and are reported here as each source states them, not reconciled.

**Licensing phase (2014-11-06 onward).**
See Japan, mechanism, below.
The gap between the platform result and the master-licence signing is measured in months, not years.

**Character portfolio.**
`カナヘイの小動物` is an umbrella, not a single property.
The official site and the 2025 licensing release list, as named characters: ピスケ＆うさぎ (Piske & the pink rabbit), ねーねーねこ, 敬語うさぎ (keigo/polite-speech rabbit), ゾンビ猫 (Zombie Cat), むーとぎゅー (Mu and Gyu), 小うさぎ, 楽しそうなオバケ.
Sources: [official site CHARACTER](https://kanaheis-small-animals.jp/characters/); [at Press, 2025-04-01](https://www.atpress.ne.jp/news/0705633).
Piske & Usagi is the one both sources single out as the lead ("大人気キャラクターの「ピスケ＆うさぎ」").

## 2. JAPAN (home market)

### 2.1 ENTRY

Japan is the home market, so "entry" is the wrong frame.
The dateable event is the transition from self-published digital work to third-party licensed commerce.

That date is **2014-11-06**, when TV Tokyo Holdings announced that its subsidiary 株式会社テレビ東京コミュニケーションズ (TV Tokyo Communications, "TXCOM") had signed a master licensee exclusive contract with Kanahei covering merchandising, advertising and audiovisual adaptation.
The release states the licensing solicitation opened the same day: "本日から商品化、広告のライセンシーを募集いたします" ("from today we are recruiting merchandising and advertising licensees").
Source: [PR TIMES, 2014-11-06, 株式会社テレビ東京ホールディングス](https://prtimes.jp/main/html/rd/p/000000304.000002734.html).

The first dateable **physical retail** presence found is **2015-09-19**, when Kiddy Land opened an official shop, `カナヘイのおみせ` ("Kanahei's Shop"), on the first floor of its Osaka Umeda flagship, initially as a limited run to 2015-11-15.
Kiddy Land's own release names TXCOM as the licensor: "株式会社テレビ東京コミュニケーションズがライセンシングする「カナヘイの小動物」のオフィシャルショップ".
Source: [PR TIMES, 2015-09-10, 株式会社キデイランド](https://prtimes.jp/main/html/rd/p/000000091.000001117.html).

So the Japan sequence is: platform result (2014) → master licence (2014-11) → own-brand retail shop-in-shop (2015-09).
Ten months from signature to a dedicated retail footprint.

### 2.2 MECHANISM

Three distinct rights-holder arrangements are on the public record, in this order.

**(a) 2014-11-06 to (at least) 2025-02: TXCOM as master licensee.**
TXCOM is the TV Tokyo group's IP/licensing subsidiary.
The 2014 release describes the scope as "商品化、デジタル・コンテンツ、テレビ番組コンテンツやデジタル媒体の開発・運営及びクロスメディア広告事業" ("merchandising, digital content, TV programme content and digital media development/operation, and cross-media advertising").
Source: [PR TIMES, 2014-11-06](https://prtimes.jp/main/html/rd/p/000000304.000002734.html).
TXCOM did not only broker: it published seasonal retail campaigns under its own corporate name as late as 2024-12-10 (`冬のシーズンフェア「黄昏ブルーアワー」`) and 2025-02-18 (`春のシーズンフェア「よりみちパン巡り」`).
Sources: [PR TIMES, 2024-12-10, テレビ東京グループ](https://prtimes.jp/main/html/rd/p/000001919.000002734.html); [PR TIMES, 2025-02-18, テレビ東京グループ](https://prtimes.jp/main/html/rd/p/000001953.000002734.html).

**(b) 2025-04-01 onward: 株式会社ミキサー (Mixer Inc.) as worldwide licensing window.**
Mixer's own release states: "「カナヘイの小動物」の全世界におけるライセンス窓口が、2025年4月1日より株式会社ミキサーに決定いたしました" ("the licensing window for Kanahei's Small Animals worldwide has been decided, from 1 April 2025, as Mixer Inc.").
The same release announces a full relaunch of the official site.
Source: [at Press, 2025-04-01, 株式会社ミキサー](https://www.atpress.ne.jp/news/0705633).
Corroborating negative signal: TXCOM's current IP business page lists Peanuts/Snoopy, Miffy, Disney, Peter Rabbit, Thomas and the Korean IP WASABI BEAR as its IP work, and does not list Kanahei.
Source: [txcom.jp IP事業](https://txcom.jp/business/ip/), read 2026-08-05.

Note the wording carefully.
The public record says "ライセンス窓口" (licensing window/contact point) for Mixer, and "マスターライセンシーの独占契約" (master licensee exclusive contract) for TXCOM.
Those are not the same term, and this file does not assert they describe the same legal arrangement.
Territory, exclusivity and terms beyond the words quoted are not stated by either source and are not inferred here.

**(c) Throughout: the creator retains a direct channel.**
Two pieces of evidence.
First, the copyright notations printed in the 2015 Kiddy Land release differ by product type: merchandise carried `© kanahei / TXCOM`, while the LINE sticker released in the same campaign carried `©kanahei` alone.
Source: [PR TIMES, 2015-09-10](https://prtimes.jp/main/html/rd/p/000000091.000001117.html).
Second, the current official site carries its own corporate enquiry desk for "ライセンス使用に関するお問い合わせ" and "タイアップ・キャンペーンのご依頼", and the site footer reads `© kanahei` with no licensee mark.
Source: [kanaheis-small-animals.jp/contact](https://kanaheis-small-animals.jp/contact/), read 2026-08-05.

This is the PBC-relevant detail in the Japan mechanism.
The sticker line, which is the audience engine, stayed under her own copyright line at the moment the merchandise line went under a broadcaster subsidiary's mark.

### 2.3 GROWTH SHAPE

Japan is not a one-off-collab market.
Four distinct formats are documented, and they run concurrently rather than replacing each other.

**Own-brand permanent retail.**
The Kiddy Land Osaka Umeda shop opened 2015-09-19 as a limited run.
It was still trading ten years later: the official site announced a `カナヘイのおみせ ～10th Anniversary～` at the same Kiddy Land Osaka Umeda store on 2025-11-21, with a Piske & Usagi greeting event on 2025-11-23, and with stock also at Kiddy Land Harajuku and Kiddy Land Ikebukuro Sunshine City.
Sources: [PR TIMES, 2015-09-10](https://prtimes.jp/main/html/rd/p/000000091.000001117.html); [official site news, 2025-11-20](https://kanaheis-small-animals.jp/news/20251121/).
A decade of continuous single-store presence at one retailer is a stronger signal than any individual collab in this file.

**Department-store pop-up circuit.**
Takashimaya has run `カナヘイの小動物 ゆるっとストア` repeatedly: Osaka store announced 2024-10-25, Yokohama as one of three character stores inside a `キャラクターPOP UP STORE FES` running 2025-12-17 to 2025-12-22 (announced 2025-12-16, the other two being さくらももこ ゆめいろ商店 and KAMEN RIDER STORE), Osaka Takashimaya again announced 2026-04-01.
The Yokohama release lists eight `ゆるっとストア限定` (store-exclusive) Kanahei SKUs with prices, which is a licensed-product line rather than a display of existing stock.
Sources: [PR TIMES, 2024-10-25, 株式会社髙島屋](https://prtimes.jp/main/html/rd/p/000000863.000069859.html); [PR TIMES, 2025-12-16, 株式会社髙島屋](https://prtimes.jp/main/html/rd/p/000001297.000069859.html); [PR TIMES, 2026-04-01, 株式会社髙島屋](https://prtimes.jp/main/html/rd/p/000001378.000069859.html).

**Seasonal fairs run by the rights side itself.**
TV Tokyo group issued the winter 2024 and spring 2025 season fairs under its own name (see 2.2a).
These are campaign frameworks that licensees hang product on, not single brand deals.

**Corporate collaborations across unrelated categories.**
Verified individually below, and tabulated in section 8.
The categories span cloud software (Canva), auto parts retail (Autobacs), consumer electronics retail (Yamada Denki), children's apparel (Narumiya x Plarail), plush/character IP (Pickles the Frog), hotel rooms (New Otani), and platform services (LINE Chef).

**Cross-IP sticker collaborations.**
The official WORKS page lists collaboration LINE stickers with, among others, ONE PIECE, Snoopy, Demon Slayer (鬼滅の刃), Winnie the Pooh, Tiger & Bunny, Pokémon, MARVEL, Star Wars and Pixar.
Source: [official site WORKS](https://kanaheis-small-animals.jp/works/), read 2026-08-05.
These are listed by the rights side as her own work record.
This file does not name a counterparty year for each, because the WORKS page is undated per item; individual pairings should be dated from their own source before use.

**Scale of the Japan collab record.**
A PR TIMES keyword search for `カナヘイ` returns 369 releases.
Source: [PR TIMES keyword search, `カナヘイ`](https://prtimes.jp/main/action.php?run=html&page=searchkey&search_word=%E3%82%AB%E3%83%8A%E3%83%98%E3%82%A4), read 2026-08-05.
That is a count of releases mentioning her, not a count of deals; some releases are about the same campaign and some mention her only in passing.
It is cited here as evidence of press-release density, not as a deal count.

## 3. TAIWAN

### 3.1 ENTRY

Two different dates answer "entry", depending on whether the question is about rights or about consumers.
Both are recorded here rather than collapsed into one.

**Rights side, by 2016-05-07: a Taiwanese licensing agent already lists the property.**
三貝多股份有限公司 (San-Byte Creative Co., Ltd., Taipei) carried a brand page headed `KANAHEI 的小動物` under 業務範疇 / 授權業務 ("business scope / licensing business") in an Internet Archive capture dated 2016-05-07.
Source: [Wayback capture 2016-05-07 of sanbytecreative.com brand page `Part=AD000012`](https://web.archive.org/web/20160507113529/http://www.sanbytecreative.com/front/bin/ptdetail.phtml?Part=AD000012&Category=110912).
That is a latest-by date, not a start date.
No source read states when the appointment was made, by whom, or on what terms.

**Consumer side, by 2017-06-20: a national convenience chain runs a Kanahei points campaign.**
7-ELEVEN Taiwan (統一超商) ran a campaign site titled `7-ELEVEN 卡娜赫拉夏日出遊集點送` ("7-ELEVEN Kanahei summer outing points collection"), captured by the Internet Archive on 2017-06-20 with product pages for a two-way shoulder bag, a hooded towel, a large cooler tote and a ceramic bowl-and-chopstick set.
Source: [Wayback capture 2017-06-20 of 7-11.com.tw/event/17KanaheiSummer/index.html](https://web.archive.org/web/20170620035616/http://www.7-11.com.tw/event/17KanaheiSummer/index.html); [product-notice page](https://web.archive.org/web/20170619215121/http://www.7-11.com.tw/event/17KanaheiSummer/notice.html).
The archived pages carry no campaign date range, so the capture date is the evidence: the campaign was live on 7-ELEVEN Taiwan's own domain by 2017-06-20.

So the Taiwan order is: agent listing (by 2016-05) → mass-retail campaign (by 2017-06) → exhibition (2017-12).
Search effort for anything earlier: Wayback CDX sweep of the agent's whole domain back to its first capture (2013-07-21) and of `7-11.com.tw/event/*anahei*`, plus Chinese-language web search on 卡娜赫拉 with 2016 and 授權 terms.
Nothing earlier than 2016-05-07 surfaced.

### 3.2 MECHANISM

**A named Taiwanese licensing agent, not a Japanese licensor operating directly.**
三貝多 describes itself as founded in 2012 and as having converted from image-licensing management into a full brand-agency business: "三貝多成立於2012年 ... 將原本圖像授權管理核心，拓展到生活創意層面，轉型為全方位的品牌代理公司，業務範疇包括圖像授權、商品開發、影音代理、海外展覽引荐...等."
Translation: "San-Byte was founded in 2012 ... expanding its original core of image-licensing management into the lifestyle-creative field, transforming into a full-service brand agency company, with a business scope including image licensing, product development, film/video agency, and introduction of overseas exhibitions."
Source: [sanbytecreative.com 關於我們](https://www.sanbytecreative.com/front/bin/ptlist.phtml?Category=110907), read 2026-08-05.

`卡娜赫拉的小動物` appears in two places on that company's current site: its 授權業務 (licensing business) roster of sixteen brands, and the brand picker on its 授權合作申請 (licensing cooperation application) form, which is the intake route a would-be Taiwanese licensee uses.
Sources: [授權業務 list](https://www.sanbytecreative.com/front/bin/ptlist.phtml?Category=110912); [授權合作 application form](https://www.sanbytecreative.com/front/bin/ptlist.phtml?Category=110909), both read 2026-08-05.
The site does not use the words 總代理 (sole agent) or state a territory, term or exclusivity for this brand, and none is asserted here.

**The same agent also sells at retail itself.**
三貝多's 店鋪資訊 (store information) page lists exactly one sales channel: `newii 網路商城`.
Source: [店鋪資訊](https://www.sanbytecreative.com/front/bin/ptlist.phtml?Category=110910), read 2026-08-05.
newii.com.tw's footer identifies its operator as 三貝多股份有限公司, 統一編號 (Taiwan business ID) 53943211, at the same Taipei address.
Source: [newii.com.tw](https://www.newii.com.tw/pages/kh-info), read 2026-08-05.
Its Kanahei catalogue is labelled item by item as either 【日本進口】 ("Japan import") or 【台灣授權】 ("Taiwan licensed").
That split, printed on the agent's own storefront, is the clearest single artefact in this file of two parallel supply routes running under one agent: import the Japanese-made line, and separately manufacture a locally licensed line.

**Exhibition promoters are separate companies from the agent.**
The large Taiwanese exhibitions are run by exhibition-promotion firms, with the agent appearing sometimes as co-organiser and sometimes not at all (section 3.3).
寬宏藝術經紀股份有限公司 (Kham Arts) appears as 主辦單位 on three of them; 三貝多 appears as 協辦單位 on one and as joint 主辦單位 on another.
This is a different shape from Japan, where the rights holder itself issued the seasonal campaign releases.

**Not established.**
No source read connects the Taiwan agent to either 株式会社テレビ東京コミュニケーションズ (master licensee 2014) or 株式会社ミキサー (worldwide licensing window from 2025-04-01).
Whether the Taiwan appointment ran through the Japanese master licensee or directly from the creator is unknown on the public record read here.

### 3.3 GROWTH SHAPE

Four formats, running for roughly a decade, all documented from their own sources.

**Ticketed exhibitions (2017 onward), and Taipei was the first one anywhere.**

主辦單位: 飛躍文創股份有限公司、寬宏藝術經紀股份有限公司.
Source: [huashan1914.com exhibition listing](https://www.huashan1914.com/w/huashan1914/exhibition_18022119193672164).

## 9. CROSS-MARKET SYNTHESIS

PARTIAL.
Japan, Taiwan and Hong Kong are built.
Korea, Thailand and China are not, so nothing below is offered as the finished sequence.

Six observations are safe to record because each rests on a quoted primary source, and together they constrain what the synthesis can later say.

1. The licensor's stated overseas method at the outset was **trade-show exhibition into a Japan Pavilion**, not appointment of a named territory agent.
Source: [PR TIMES, 2014-11-06](https://prtimes.jp/main/html/rd/p/000000304.000002734.html).
Whether that plan executed, and what agents took over per territory, is what the remaining market sections must establish.

2. In Taiwan the mechanism that is actually visible is **a resident licensing agent**, 三貝多, listed with the property by 2016-05-07 and still listing it in 2026, with an application form that is the intake route for Taiwanese licensees.
That is a different mechanism from the Japanese one, where the rights holder itself issued campaign releases.
Sources in section 3.2.

3. The **first large-scale exhibition of this IP anywhere happened in Taipei**, not in Japan, according to the venue's own listing (2017-12-20).
Source: [huashan1914.com](https://www.huashan1914.com/w/huashan1914/exhibition_18022119193672164).
That single fact is enough to rule out a naive "home market saturates first, then exports" reading, and any synthesis written later must account for it.

4. **The earliest documented consumer-facing activity in Hong Kong (2016-03-01) precedes the earliest documented consumer-facing activity in Taiwan (2017-06-20).**
Sources: [UHK 港生活, 2016-03-10](https://hk.ulifestyle.com.hk/activity/detail/104212/); [Wayback 2017-06-20 of the 7-ELEVEN Taiwan campaign](https://web.archive.org/web/20170620035616/http://www.7-11.com.tw/event/17KanaheiSummer/index.html).
This does **not** establish that Hong Kong came before Taiwan.
The Taiwanese consumer date is an archive capture bound, and the Taiwanese rights-side date is earlier still (the agent's own brand page carried the property by 2016-05-07).
What it does establish is that the two markets opened within roughly the same fifteen-month window, which rules out any reading of Hong Kong as a late follower of a settled Taiwan operation.

5. **The one Hong Kong document that prints a rights line names the Taiwanese agent, not a Hong Kong one.**
"©️ kanahei / TXCOM | www.kanahei.com | Licensed by San-Byte Creative Co., Ltd. All Rights Reserved."
Source: [ksarun.hk via Wayback, 2021-12-14](https://web.archive.org/web/20211214084109/https://ksarun.hk/zh/).
On the evidence in hand the agent layer is **regional rather than per-territory**, at least across the two Chinese-language markets built so far.
That is a single-document finding and is graded accordingly in `_VERDICT.md`.
It is not a statement about the territory, exclusivity or term of any agreement, none of which any source read states.

6. **Both Chinese-language markets ran a public-transport-operator collaboration, and Hong Kong's came first.**
MTR Corporation commemorative ticket set, from late May 2018 ([UHK 港生活, 2018-05-07](https://hk.ulifestyle.com.hk/activity/detail/110559/)); Taiwan High Speed Rail merchandise and painted train, from November 2019 ([thsrc.com.tw, 2020-08-10](https://www.thsrc.com.tw/ArticleContent/dccf6923-ab8f-4810-843b-71f7e286f342)).
The formats then diverged sharply: THSR ran a multi-year programme across merchandise waves, food and drink lines and a station store, while the MTR appearance is a single commemorative-ticket drop placed by its own covering outlet in the same series as Sanrio, Kumamon and Doraemon tickets.
Same counterparty category, very different depth.
