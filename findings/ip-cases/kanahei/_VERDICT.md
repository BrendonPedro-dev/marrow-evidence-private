## What is safe to carry out of this build

1. The agency layer has three states on the Japanese public record, with dates: creator-direct before 2014-11-06; TXCOM as master licensee from 2014-11-06; Mixer Inc. as worldwide licensing window from 2025-04-01. Each is quoted from the party's own release.
2. The gap between platform result and master licence was months, not years. LINE Creators Stamp AWARD 2014 runner-up, then a broadcaster subsidiary's exclusive master licence in the same year.
3. The licensor's stated overseas method at the outset was trade-show exhibition into a Japan Pavilion, not appointment of a named territory agent. This is a quoted plan, not a verified outcome.
4. The sticker line and the merchandise line carried different copyright notations in 2015 (`©kanahei` vs `© kanahei / TXCOM`). The audience engine stayed under her own mark.
5. A decade of continuous shop-in-shop presence at one retailer (Kiddy Land Osaka Umeda, 2015-09 to at least 2025-11) is the strongest single Japan durability signal found.
6. Taiwan runs through a resident licensing agent, 三貝多 (San-Byte Creative, founded 2012, Taipei), carrying the property by 2016-05-07 and still carrying it in 2026. It is the mechanism Japan does not use for its own market.
7. That agent operates both channels at once: its own online mall labels each Kanahei item either 【日本進口】 (Japan import) or 【台灣授權】 (Taiwan licensed). Import and local manufacture run in parallel under one intermediary.
8. The first large-scale exhibition of this IP anywhere was in Taipei, from 2017-12-20, per the venue's own listing. Whatever the eventual synthesis says, it has to accommodate a marquee format debuting outside the home market.
9. The Taiwanese counterparties are infrastructure-scale, not character-retail-scale: the largest convenience chain (four campaigns, one with a 240,000-unit first batch on a single SKU line) and the national high-speed rail operator (2019-11 to 2025-07, two painted trains, three merchandise waves, a food line, a station pop-up).

## What must NOT be carried out of this build

- Any statement about territory, exclusivity or deal terms beyond the exact words quoted. Neither Japanese source defines the scope of Mixer's "ライセンス窓口", and no Taiwanese source states 三貝多's terms at all.
- Any claim that 三貝多 was appointed by TXCOM, by Mixer, or by the creator. Nothing read establishes the chain of title into Taiwan.
- Any claim that 三貝多(香港) or 上海玛齐品牌管理 represents this property. Their existence is sourced from a contact page; their brand scope is not stated anywhere read.
- Any current audience figure. The CocoPPa 2-million figure is from 2015 and is a platform-follower count on a third-party app. The Taiwanese Facebook figure is a like count, not a follower count, on a page whose operator is unstated.
- The 2016-05-07 and 2017-06-20 Taiwan dates as start dates. They are archive capture dates and therefore latest-by bounds only.
- Any cross-market pattern claim. There are two markets in this build and one of them is home.
- The 369-release PR TIMES count as a deal count. It is a press-release mention count.
