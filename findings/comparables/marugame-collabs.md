# Marugame Udon (丸亀製麺 / 丸龜製麵) character / IP collaboration history - comparable evidence for the Toriki pitch

As of 2026-07-24.

## HARD LIMIT (top)

This document is Marugame Udon's collaboration history with OTHER, unrelated IPs.
It is NOT, and must NEVER be presented as, a Bichi Mao partnership.
The only usable claim is: "a closely comparable casual-dining chain has successfully run character collaborations - including with independent, creator-owned characters."
Never "Bichi Mao worked with Marugame."
Every single row below is Marugame paired with some other, third-party character/IP.

## Why Marugame is the comparable

Marugame Udon is a mass-market Japanese casual-dining chain (hand-made udon, freely-mixed tempura, low ticket, high volume), operating in Japan and - via 台灣東利多 - in Taiwan.
It is closely comparable to Torikizoku in format and positioning: a Japanese-origin, affordable, everyday casual-dining chain.
Gary's framing: if a chain that looks a lot like Torikizoku has run character collaborations - and especially if it has run them with independent, non-portfolio characters - the case for approaching Torikizoku gets much stronger.

Scope: Japan AND Taiwan, window 2020-present (~5 years).
Every in-window row is verified against a real source page that confirms the pairing (curl / WebFetch confirmed).
IP class is assigned on the census ownership test used across this project (who OWNS the IP, not who runs the brand), with the REPRESENTABLE sub-tag where the census already scored the IP.

## 3. Portfolio-vs-indie split

**In-window (n=7), on the census ownership test:**

| Class | Count | Share | Which |
|---|---|---|---|
| Corporate portfolio | 4 | 57% | LINE Friends (熊大), Demon Slayer, Dragon Ball Z, Sumikko Gurashi (San-X) |
| Independent (creator-owned) | 3 | 43% | Kanahei's Small Animals, 奧樂雞 OLOGY, 爽爽貓 SongSongMeow |

**By market this is the important cut:**

- **Japan (n=2): 0 independent, 2 portfolio.**
  Both verified Japan collabs are blue-chip corporate anime/game IP (Dragon Ball Z, Sumikko Gurashi), and both are 2026.
  Every Japan collab on record across ALL years (including the pre-2020 context set - One Piece, Gundam×Hello Kitty, WOFF) is a major rights-holder portfolio property.
  Marugame's home-market playbook is big-portfolio only.
- **Taiwan (n=5): 3 independent, 2 portfolio.**
  Taiwan is where the indie evidence lives.
  Three of the five Taiwan collabs are creator-owned characters, and two of those - 奧樂雞 OLOGY (掰掰啾啾) and 爽爽貓 SongSongMeow (SECOND) - are genuinely small, single-creator Taiwanese indie IP, run in consecutive years (2024, then 2025).
  SongSongMeow is scored REPRESENTABLE=yes in this project's own Taiwan census; it is exactly the class of open indie IP PBC would represent.
  (Kanahei is creator-owned by the census test but is a large, exclusively-master-licensed mass property - REP=no - so it counts as independent-by-ownership but is not a "small open indie" like the other two.)

The honest one-line read: **Marugame does big-portfolio anime tie-ins in Japan, but in Taiwan it has repeatedly and recently built full merchandise campaigns around independent, creator-owned local characters - including at least one (SongSongMeow) in the exact open-indie class PBC targets.**

## 4. The Toriki-case read (1 paragraph)

Marugame Udon - a mass-market Japanese casual-dining chain closely comparable to Torikizoku in format and positioning - has demonstrably run full-scale character/IP collaborations (menu tie-ins, collectible 「うどん札」cards, 集點加價購 merchandise, giveaways, pop-up stores, whole-store decoration), proving a Toriki-like chain will invest in this playbook as a marquee marketing vehicle.
Crucially, the evidence is not limited to blue-chip anime: in its Taiwan arm Marugame has, in two consecutive years, built entire collectible-merch campaigns around INDEPENDENT, creator-owned Taiwanese characters - 奧樂雞 OLOGY (illustrator 掰掰啾啾, 2024) and 爽爽貓 SongSongMeow (SECOND, 2025) - the latter sitting in the exact "representable independent Taiwan IP" bucket PBC would pitch.
That is the strongest form of the comparable evidence and it is market-matched: a Torikizoku-comparable chain, in Taiwan, has already chosen to work with non-portfolio indie characters, not only with big licensors.
The one honest caveat is depth: the in-window record is 7 campaigns (5 Taiwan, 2 Japan), the two genuine small-indie cases are both Taiwan and both very recent (2024-2025), and Japan itself shows zero indie collabs, so the supportable claim is "a comparable chain runs character collabs and, in the Taiwan market specifically, will back independent creator IP" - not "comparable chains everywhere routinely back indies."
HARD LIMIT (restated): none of this is a Bichi Mao partnership; every collaboration above is Marugame with an unrelated third-party IP, and this evidence may only be used to argue that a Toriki-comparable chain runs character collabs - never to imply any Bichi Mao / Marugame relationship.

## 5. Method / coverage limits

- **Both markets covered** (Japan + Taiwan), 2020-present.
- **Well covered:** Taiwan 2021-2025 (a clear ~once-a-year cadence, every row datable to exact start/end and multi-source or official-source verified) and Japan 2026 (two campaigns fully datable, multi-source).
- **Genuine gaps (searched, not found):**
  - Japan 2020-2025 returned NO verifiable character collab despite targeted year-by-year and IP-by-IP searches (Chiikawa, Pokemon, Conan, Doraemon, Molcar, Miffy, Snoopy, Sanrio, Monster Strike, Puzzle & Dragons, Uma Musume, illustrator collabs). Three signals suggest this is a real gap, not a search gap: press frames the 2026 Dragon Ball Z campaign as Marugame's first use of collectible character 「うどん札」cards; Marugame's own Mochi-Mochi club 2019-2024 recap lists no external character collabs; the pricey.jp campaign roundup lists only the 2026 DBZ tie-in.
  - Taiwan 2020 and 2023-calendar mid-year: no additional verifiable character collab surfaced (the Kanahei campaign bridges late-2022 into early-2023).
  - Taiwan characters searched with NO Marugame pairing found: 蠟筆小新 Crayon Shin-chan, 吉伊卡哇 Chiikawa, 三麗鷗 Sanrio, 角落生物, 布丁狗, 拉拉熊, 蛋黃哥, 咖波/貓貓蟲 Capoo, 白爛貓, Disney, 寶可夢, 哆啦A夢, 櫻桃小丸子.
- **Dating confidence:** all 7 in-window rows have exact start/end dates from official or primary press.
- **Excluded (not character/IP collabs):** ordinary/seasonal menu launches; celebrity/talent endorsements; food/retail/payment co-branding with no character (Mister Donut, 7-11, Kagome 可果美, momo, 中信 ATM, QB HOUSE, 桃園市民卡).
- **A note the earlier internal speculation:** an earlier internal note speculated a specific Marugame × indie pairing without a source. That was NOT carried forward. The indie collabs recorded here (OLOGY, SongSongMeow, and the ownership-independent Kanahei) were each verified from scratch against primary or official sources.
