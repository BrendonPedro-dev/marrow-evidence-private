# Finding 1: BRAND ERASURE (反客為主) - public corroboration hunt

**Claim from our two brand interviews:** brands co-branding with more-famous IPs lose their own recall - the IP gets remembered, the brand doesn't.

**Compiled:** 2026-07-16 (iteration 3 of the corroboration run).
Every quote below was verified verbatim against the page source (curl of raw HTML), not just an AI summary of the page.

**Verdict so far: PARTIALLY CORROBORATED (medium confidence).**
The mechanism is well-documented publicly, but in a behavioral and financial form rather than as a recall claim: trade media state outright that collab budgets become "working for the IP" instead of building brand equity, and reported consumer behavior shows the brand's own product treated as the disposable part of its own collab (tea discarded or proxy-drunk, IP merch kept and resold at near-set price; the same IP running simultaneous collabs across interchangeable brands, with the merch - not the brand - deciding the purchase).
What was NOT found under the queries tried: any public first-person brand-side admission of the literal recall claim ("consumers remembered the IP but not us"), and no zh-TW instance beyond one weak columnist piece on dilution.
The exact idioms from our interviews (反客為主, 為人作嫁) do not appear in any collab post-mortem found - the mainland press uses 为IP打工 (working for the IP) and 谷子化 (merch-ification) for the same phenomenon.

## Instances

### 1. 雷报 via 36kr (Jul 2025): blind faith in collabs turns the marketing budget into a war of attrition "working for the IP" instead of building brand equity

- **URL:** https://36kr.com/p/3361190115624962 (36kr republication, 2025-07-02; original: 公众号 雷报, author 段轻; also on 界面 JMedia: https://www.jiemian.com/article/12979239.html, whose raw HTML does not embed the body - quote verified on the 36kr mirror). Title: 《72起IP联名，半年烧钱上亿，新茶饮营销为何"高投入低热度"？》.
- **Who:** 雷报, an IP/entertainment-industry trade media outlet - MEDIA/industry-report voice.
- **Quote:** 「联名的本质是"用IP势能撬动消费决策"，其成功不在于IP本身的热度，而在于能否将短期流量转化为销量、转化为品牌资产。因此，对品牌而言，盲目迷信联名如同饮鸩止渴，将营销预算变为"为IP打工"的消耗战」
  (Gloss: "the essence of a collab is using the IP's momentum to sway purchase decisions; success lies not in the IP's own heat but in whether short-term traffic converts into sales and into BRAND EQUITY. For brands, blind faith in collabs is drinking poison to quench thirst - it turns the marketing budget into a war of attrition of 'working for the IP'.")
- **Verdict line:** **corroborates** - the trade press's own summary of the sector is that collab spend by default accrues to the IP, not the brand, unless actively converted; "为IP打工" is the exact brand-erasure mechanism stated as an industry-level warning.
  Flag: media voice, editorial conclusion of a data piece, not a quoted brand executive. Same article is already cited for finding 2 (different passages); counts once per finding as a shared source.

### 2. 刺猬公社 via 数英 (Jul 2023): consumers keep the IP merch and discard the brand's product - separate packaging, near-set-price merch resale, and proxy-drinking

- **URL:** https://www.digitaling.com/articles/952461.html (数英 authorized post, 2023-07-17; authors 陈梅希、欧阳, source 公众号 刺猬公社 ID: ciweigongshe). Title: 《奶茶联名那么多，为什么只有茶百道出圈了？》, on the 茶百道 x 未定事件簿 collab.
- **Who:** 刺猬公社, a content-industry media outlet reporting observed consumer behavior - MEDIA voice, behavioral evidence.
- **Quote 1:** 「游戏玩家出于收藏周边的目标，会要求店家分开打包——奶茶装在塑料袋里，杯套、贴纸、明信片、票签等周边商品装在联名纸袋里。」
  (Gloss: "game fans, aiming to collect the merch, ask the shop to pack separately - the tea in a plastic bag, and the cup sleeve, stickers, postcards and ticket stubs in the collab paper bag.")
- **Quote 2:** 「这样一套完整"无损版"周边，在二手交易平台闲鱼上，一度卖到60元，几乎和原套餐等价。换言之，购买者如果高价将周边售出，可以不花钱免费喝奶茶。」
  (Gloss: "a complete 'mint' merch set at one point sold for 60 yuan on Xianyu - nearly the price of the original set. In other words, a buyer who sells the merch drinks the tea for free.")
- **Quote 3:** 「因为部分游戏玩家所在地没有茶百道门店，闲鱼和小红书的用户甚至还谋划起了代喝业务。需要周边的玩家负责奶茶套餐的费用，代喝者帮买后留下奶茶，把全部周边寄给玩家」
  (Gloss: "fans in cities with no ChaPanda store even organized a 'proxy-drinking' business: the fan who wants the merch pays for the tea set, the proxy buys it, keeps the tea, and mails all the merch to the fan.")
- **Verdict line:** **corroborates (behaviorally)** - in the brand's own hit collab, the entire consumer value sat in the IP-branded items; the brand's actual product was the throwaway. The market priced the merch at ~100% of the set and the tea at ~0.
  Flag: media observation of fan behavior, not a brand post-mortem; the article frames the collab as a SUCCESS for 茶百道's exposure, so erasure is our reading of the reported behavior, flagged as such.

### 3. 剁椒Spicy via 数英 (Feb 2025): the same IP runs simultaneous collabs across brands, and the merch - not the brand - becomes the purchase decider

- **URL:** https://www.digitaling.com/articles/1315572.html (数英 repost, 2025-02-10; author 豆子, source 公众号 剁椒Spicy, original title 《拆解超100个案例：搭载"吃谷热"，品牌IP联名迎来新增长？》; also on CBNData: https://www.cbndata.com/information/293537).
- **Who:** 剁椒Spicy, an entertainment-marketing media outlet - MEDIA voice, 100+ case analysis.
- **Quote:** 「随着越来越多品类加入这场营销战，同一个IP同期官宣多个联名合作的情况已非常普遍，粉丝圈层重叠的情况下，周边会成为粉丝消费决策的关键要素。去年10月，哈利·波特就集中释放了一批联名，与包括名创优品、番茄口袋、凌美、奈雪的茶在内多个品牌同期合作。」
  (Gloss: "as more categories join the marketing war, one IP announcing multiple simultaneous collabs is now very common; with fan circles overlapping, the MERCH becomes the key factor in fans' purchase decisions. Last October Harry Potter released a batch of collabs at once, working simultaneously with MINISO, Tomato Pocket, LAMY and Nayuki.")
- **Verdict line:** **corroborates** - when one IP fronts four brands at once and the merch decides the purchase, the brands are interchangeable vessels for the IP; brand identity drops out of the decision entirely.
  Flag: media analysis; the article's framing is opportunity (how to win the merch war), not warning - the interchangeability is documented, the erasure conclusion is ours.

### 4. 光明日报 via 新华网 (Jan 2025): brands that collab purely to ride traffic end up damaging their own brand value

- **URL:** http://www.news.cn/tech/20250115/ebbcd021a14e4dcaa80455ead63c78c8/c.html (新华网 repost of 光明日报, 2025-01-15, author 王丹). Title: 《IP联名并非万能钥匙》.
- **Who:** 光明日报 commentary - STATE-MEDIA editorial voice.
- **Quote:** 「比如，有的品牌完全为联名而联名，硬蹭流量，反而损害了品牌价值」
  (Gloss: "for instance, some brands collab purely for the sake of collabing, force-riding the IP's traffic, and end up damaging their own brand value instead.")
- **Verdict line:** **weak corroboration (adjacent)** - asserts collabs can subtract from the brand's own equity, but as value damage, not recall loss, and without a named case.
  Flag: editorial commentary, no sourcing.

### 5. 數位時代 (zh-TW, Mar 2021): each collab lowers brand scarcity and breeds a sense of cheapness; loyalists prefer the "pure-blood" original

- **URL:** https://www.bnext.com.tw/article/61665/co-branding-cheap-value-march (數位時代 BusinessNext, 2021-03-19, columnist 涼鹿 Annie). Title: 《品牌聯名好吸睛？小心越聯越「廉價」》.
- **Who:** marketing columnist on 數位時代 - COLUMNIST voice (not brand-side), the only zh-TW instance found.
- **Quote 1 (section heading + passage):** 「品牌稀缺性降低，廉價感油然而生……合適的品牌聯名自然製造佳話，但更多的聯名像一場賭注」
  (Gloss: "brand scarcity drops and a sense of cheapness arises... a well-matched collab makes a good story, but most collabs are a gamble.")
- **Quote 2:** 「部分品牌忠誠者對聯名款興趣不高，寧願回本店買血統純正的貨色。」 and the advice 「保持自己的身價，用「創意」做出聯名。」
  (Gloss: "some brand loyalists have little interest in the collab edition and would rather go back to the store for the pure-blood original" / "keep your own worth - do collabs with creativity.")
- **Verdict line:** **weak corroboration (adjacent)** - describes collab-driven dilution of the brand's own standing, the flip side of the IP keeping the value; does not state the recall-loss mechanism.
  Flag: personal-experience column by a blogger/columnist, 2021, consumer-goods general (not IP-licensing specific).

## Not found (honest negatives)

- **No first-person brand-side voice** saying "the IP got remembered, we didn't" (or 反客為主/喧賓奪主/為人作嫁 applied to their own collab) was found in zh-TW, zh-CN, ja, or en under the queries tried.
- **The interview idioms are absent from public post-mortems:** searches for 反客為主/喧賓奪主/為人作嫁 + 聯名 return idiom dictionaries, generic how-to listicles, and legal explainers - not case analyses. The mainland press expresses the same mechanism as 为IP打工 and 谷子化 instead.
- **zh-TW is thin:** one columnist piece (instance 5); Taiwanese licensing coverage found (udn, Openbook, 經貿透視) is uniformly success-framed.
- **Dropped as unusable:** a 知乎专栏 piece (zhuanlan.zhihu.com/p/1983901879730721808) claiming 超60%的联名产品仅在包装上印刷IP logo and 联名方好感度下降43% - vendor/agency content marketing with unsourced statistics; the 21经济网, thepaper.cn, CBNData 193333, and 深响/36kr 2571700074079622 candidates were fetched and contained no erasure passages.

**Queries tried (main ones):** 聯名 反客為主 IP 品牌 行銷 案例; 品牌聯名 喧宾夺主 IP 联名 消费者只记得IP 品牌被遗忘; 联名 为他人做嫁衣 IP 品牌 营销; 联名 记住了IP 品牌 忘了 消费者 茶饮; 喧賓奪主 聯名 品牌 IP 授權; 联名 给IP打工 品牌 茶饮; 奶茶 联名 周边 倒掉 吧唧 只要赠品; 反客为主 联名 IP 品牌 营销 风险; 聯名 品牌 失去 自己 定位 模糊 風險 行銷 台灣; "买谷弃奶" 联名 IP 品牌; "记住的是IP" OR "记住了IP" 联名 品牌 消费者; 為人作嫁 OR 為他人作嫁 聯名 品牌.

## Confidence line

**Medium.** Three mutually independent mainland sources (trade media conclusion, reported consumer behavior, 100+ case analysis) document the mechanism - collab value accruing to the IP while the brand supplies budget and product - and two adjacent voices (state-media editorial, zh-TW columnist) add the brand-value-damage flip side. But the specific RECALL claim from our interviews (consumers remember the IP, not the brand) has zero direct public instances: the strongest public form of finding 1 is "the brand becomes the IP's disposable channel," not "the brand is forgotten." Treat the recall phrasing as our interviewees' articulation, publicly supported only by inference from behavior.
