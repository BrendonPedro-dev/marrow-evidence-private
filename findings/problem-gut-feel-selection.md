# Finding 3: GUT-FEEL SELECTION - public corroboration hunt

**Claim from our two brand interviews:** IP selection is decided on relationships and instinct, with fame as the only proxy for expected performance.

**Compiled:** 2026-07-16 (iteration 1 of the corroboration run).
Every quote below was verified verbatim against the page source (curl), not just an AI summary of the page.

**Verdict so far: CORROBORATED** - six independent public instances (three new this pass, three reused from the incumbent-voice corpus), spanning zh-TW, zh-CN, ja, and ko sources.
Honest caveat: the strongest instances are incumbents and industry researchers describing brand behavior; a first-person brand-side self-admission ("we picked it because the boss liked it") has NOT yet been found and remains the open hunt item.

## New instances found this pass

### 1. udn 聯合數位文創 (agency manager 顏銘錫): fame is the licensees' first criterion, and a love-driven pick that flopped

- **URL:** https://www.ieatpe.org.tw/magazine/ebook347/storypage03.html
- **Publication:** 《貿易》雜誌 (Trade Magazine, 台北市進出口商業同業公會 IEAT), NO.347, May 2020. Article: 「玩轉IP授權 開拓新客群」, by 葉惟禎.
- **Who:** 顏銘錫, 聯合數位文創數位事業群IP授權暨商品發展部經紀中心經理 (manager at udn's IP licensing agency arm - AGENCY-side voice describing licensee behavior, not a brand speaking about itself).
- **Quote 1:** 「圖像IP的知名度確實是所有被授權方第一考慮的要素，但高知名度與高授權金是畫上等號的。」
  (Gloss: "IP fame is indeed the first consideration of every licensee - but high fame equals high licensing fees.")
- **Quote 2 (article narration, recounting an anonymous licensee):** 「過去某家廠商十分喜愛一個漫畫IP，決定將此IP印在紅包袋上，並做一些kuso文字。但推出後銷售熱度始終不高，即使在該漫畫粉絲團行銷，依然賣得［不好］。」
  (Gloss: a manufacturer that "deeply loved" a comic IP put it on red envelopes; sales stayed cold even when marketed to the IP's own fan community - selection by personal affection, and it failed.)
- **Quote 3:** 「若被授權方自行尋找創作者談合作會辛苦一些，畢竟背後無專業團隊協助資料分析整理與市場研究。」
  (Gloss: licensees going direct have no professional team behind them for data analysis and market research.)
- **Verdict line:** **corroborates** - fame named as the universal first proxy, an affection-driven pick documented as a failure, and brands described as lacking analytical support.
  Flag: agency-side voice (udn is itself an incumbent broker we profiled in `../research/taiwan-licensing-verdict.md`), single source for the anecdote.

### 2. 靠谱二次元 2026 industry report: most IP collab decisions still run on 眼缘 (eye-chemistry) and decision-maker preference

- **URL:** https://eu.36kr.com/zh/p/3827274204517256 (36kr republication; original: 靠谱二次元 公众号 kpACGN, 2026-05-27, 《2026年「国产IP联名」趋势报告》)
- **Who:** 靠谱二次元, an ACGN-industry research/media outlet - INDUSTRY-RESEARCH voice characterizing brand behavior across its 100-brand case library, not a single brand speaking.
- **Quote:** 「品牌方选择IP合作的主要影响因素有三个方面：首先是热度和口碑，大热IP都想追，流量就是销量；其次是决策者偏好，现阶段品牌方和IP方的行业信息差较大，对彼此产业的运作模式并没有那么了解，"喜欢=了解=好操作"成为常见心理，除了极少数头部，大多数IP的合作与否仍依赖眼缘；另外一个很重要的影响因素，是从众心理，品牌方自然会优先选择与有成功案例、在互联网上能检索到正向口碑与销售盛况的IP合作。」
  (Gloss: brands pick IPs on (1) heat and buzz - "traffic is sales", (2) decision-maker preference - "I like it = I understand it = it's easy to run" is the common mindset, and outside the few head IPs "whether a collab happens still depends on eye-chemistry (眼缘)", and (3) herd behavior - copying IPs with searchable success stories.)
- **Verdict line:** **corroborates** - the report's own taxonomy of selection criteria is fame, personal preference, and imitation; no quantitative fit input appears anywhere in it.
  Flag: scope is 联名 marketing collabs around ACGN IP (zh-CN), adjacent to but not identical with physical-product licensing programs; single report.

### 3. 深响 (media analysis, Feb 2025): collab selection is openly called 玄学 - the brands that bet biggest picked the wrong movie

- **URL:** https://www.cbndata.com/information/293610 (CBNData authorized repost of 深响, 2025-02; also on sina: https://finance.sina.com.cn/tech/csj/2025-02-07/doc-ineisaue7581702.shtml). Title: 《春节档联名启示：爆款难押，品牌玄学仍然待解》.
- **Who:** 深响 (DeepEcho), a marketing/business media outlet - MEDIA voice, but the evidence inside is behavioral: a named list of major brands that collectively bet on the wrong IP.
- **Quote:** 「一方面是爆款难求，联名成玄学。就像这次春节档，其实商务合作最多的影片是《封神2》——伊利、洋河梦之蓝、BOSS、老庙黄金、瑞幸、海底捞、高德地图、喜临门、雪花啤酒、高梵……都是大牌。但不得不承认的是，这波押宝确实输给了选择《哪吒2》的品牌。」
  (Gloss: "hits are hard to call - collabs have become an occult art (玄学). This Spring Festival season the film with the most brand deals was Creation of the Gods 2 - Yili, Yanghe, BOSS Zhipin, Laomiao Gold, Luckin, Haidilao, Amap, Sleemon, Snow Beer, Goelia... all big brands. But this round of bets undeniably lost to the brands that picked Ne Zha 2.")
- **Verdict line:** **corroborates (indirect)** - it does not quote a brand admitting instinct, but it documents at scale that a dozen sophisticated brands, choosing under the current toolkit, mispriced the pairing; the industry's own word for selection is 玄学 (occult art / guesswork).
  Flag: media analysis, not brand-side; the 押宝/betting frame is the journalist's.

## Reused instances from the incumbent-voice corpus (already cited in research/)

### 4. JD.com IP licensing head 高雷 (2022): licensing is a "相对感性" (relatively gut-feel) industry

- **URL:** https://www.licensing.org.cn/9410.html (Licensing International China column, 2022-07-21; full sourcing in `../research/china-platforms-verdict.md` §2)
- **Who:** 高雷, 京东零售 IP文娱业务负责人 - PLATFORM-INCUMBENT voice, the closest thing to brand-side authority in the corpus.
- **Quote:** 「未来大数据驱动版权合作才是真正能让这个行业更上一层楼。大数据给合作双方合作匹配提供支撑才能让授权从相对感性的行业有理论的数据支撑。」
  (Gloss: only future big-data matching support could give licensing - a relatively gut-feel industry - theoretical data support.)
- **Verdict line:** **corroborates** - the person running JD's licensing platform states the industry runs on feel today and data-supported matching is an aspiration.

### 5. WITZ co-CEO 김정민 (2023): IP-value measurement and licensee-tailored IP recommendation are still "awaiting launch"

- **URL:** https://ilovecharacter.com/news/newsview.php?ncode=1065574542202454&dt=m (I Love Character column, 2023-05-22; full sourcing in `../research/japan-korea-verdict.md` §3)
- **Who:** 김정민, co-CEO of WITZ (Korean licensing-platform VENDOR).
- **Quote (as documented in the verdict file):** IP 가치를 측정하는 서비스 and customized IP recommendation described as services that 「출시를 앞두고 있다」 (are awaiting launch).
- **Verdict line:** **corroborates (indirect)** - a licensing-platform vendor confirms no quantified selection aid existed to sell in 2023, so brands necessarily selected without one; three years later WITZ still does not ship it.

### 6. Sumitomo Corporation PR (2022): matching runs on trade shows and personal introductions

- **URL:** Sumitomo CHARATARO PR, sourced in `../research/japan-korea-verdict.md` §1 (beta site recovered via Wayback).
- **Who:** Sumitomo Corporation - TRADING-HOUSE INCUMBENT voice.
- **Quote:** licensing matching 「依然として展示会や紹介などアナログな方法に頼っている」
  (Gloss: still relies on analog methods - trade shows and introductions.)
- **Verdict line:** **corroborates** - "relationships as the selection mechanism" stated as the industry diagnosis by a major incumbent; their attempted fix was a directory, not a score.

## Confidence line

**Corroborated with MEDIUM-HIGH confidence.**
Six independent instances across four languages agree that fame, personal preference (眼缘/喜愛), relationships (紹介/展示会), and herd behavior are the operative selection criteria, and two incumbents state in their own words that data-supported selection does not exist yet.
The gap keeping this below HIGH: every voice is an incumbent, agency, or industry researcher describing brands; zero named brand managers describing their own process first-person have been found under the queries above.
