**Researched: 2026-07-24 | IP: 星期一的布魯斯 MONDAY BRUCE | Owner: 原石創意國際有限公司 STONE CREATIVE INT. CO., LTD. (creator/designer Phoebe Fu 菲比); toy manufacture & global blind-box distribution via TOYS ALLIANCE (Hong Kong)**

This IP is one of the census's proven Taiwan indies: `findings/census/taiwan-v2/campaigns.md` (row 115) records the 2026 台北101 89F 觀景台 special exhibition as the marquee "indie IP wins a flagship landmark venue" example, and `_VERDICT.md` names Monday Bruce in the ~5-creator domestic representable bench (REP=yes) and cites it as the lead case for the census's strongest new format finding: **experiential/venue campaigns skew indie**.

> **KEY CONTRAST FLAG (read before the sequence):** Monday Bruce is a fundamentally different growth case from Capoo and LAIMO.
> Capoo and LAIMO are **fame-first** IPs - a huge free social/sticker audience (built 2011-2016) pulled brands to them, and their climbs run through drinks / convenience / finance / beauty co-brands.
> Monday Bruce is **designer-toy-native and much younger**: the character was created **2021** and its FIRST product was a **blind box** (Dec 2022), so it grew through the **collectible-toy + pop-up-exhibition + licensing-expo** circuit, internationalised fast via pop-ups (HK/Japan, 2025), and its highest-tier "brand" moment to date is an **experiential landmark venue (Taipei 101 observatory)**, not a mass-retail volume co-brand. Different playbook, and a much shorter clock.

## 1. ORIGIN

- **Creator: Phoebe Fu (菲比)**, a Taiwanese designer who worked several years as an office worker (白領/social "corporate slave") and poured that experience into the character. Source (official creator page, curl-verified): https://www.mondaybruce.com/author-introduction?lang=en ("Designer Phoebe Fu ... became a white-collar worker ... expressing them through blues").
- **The character:** **Bruce**, a world-weary but cute **rabbit** office worker who dreads Monday but grits his teeth and goes to work anyway - a mirror of modern white-collar life. The rabbit identity is confirmed in press: 巴哈姆特/GNN describes him as "厭世卻可愛的兔子角色「布魯斯」" (https://gnn.gamer.com.tw/detail.php?sn=287508). (The census's "melancholy-rabbit IP" label is correct.)

MONDAY BRUCE 2023 All Rights Reserved." appears site-wide, and the licensing contact page names 原石創意國際有限公司 as the licensing counterparty (product-stone@hotmail.com). ## 3. THE ESCALATION READ

**Monday Bruce climbed FAST and along a toy/experiential track, not the fame-then-retail track of Capoo/LAIMO.**

- **The clock is the headline.** The character was created **2021**, shipped its **first product (a blind box) in Dec 2022**, and reached a **flagship institutional landmark - the Taipei 101 observatory - by April 2026**: roughly **3.3 years from first product to a national landmark venue**. That is the shortest climb of the three Taiwan indies mapped so far (Capoo ~a decade of sticker fame first; LAIMO ~2 years but off a huge pre-existing viral audience).
- **First "major/institutional" moment: the Taipei 101 89F observatory special exhibition (2026-04)** - and it arrived as an **experiential venue campaign**, explicitly framed by 台北101 董事長 賈永婕 as "推廣台灣原創IP" (promoting Taiwan original IP to foreign visitors). The DBS Bank blind box (undated) is the other institution-tier partner, but the dated, verified big moment is the venue.
- **The visible inflection is the 2025 internationalisation-by-pop-up wave.** Within a single quarter (Aug-Oct 2025) Bruce ran a **Hong Kong pop-up (AIRSIDE)**, a **Japan pop-up (Marui Namba)**, and appeared at the **Shanghai China Licensing Expo** - then **Tokyo Licensing Japan 2026**. The IP went cross-border via **pop-up stores and B2B licensing expos**, not via a domestic mass-retail wave - a licensing-led, not volume-led, escalation.
- **There is no fame-first flywheel here.** Capoo and LAIMO had 500k-millions of free followers before their first big brand came; Bruce's audience was built **through the product and the pop-up circuit itself** (blind-box collectors, exhibition foot-traffic). The escalation currency was **collectible desirability + experiential venues**, not social reach.
- **What is NOT yet in the record:** a convenience-store full-store wave, a drink-chain handshake, a beauty co-brand, or a mass premium - the channels on which Capoo and LAIMO broke into volume. Bruce's ceiling break (so far) is prestige-experiential (Taipei 101), not mass-retail.

**Shape of the climb:** designer blind-box debut (2022) → serialised toy cadence + domestic pop-up/exhibition circuit via 哈玩具 (2023-2025) → first consumer-brand co-brand (FANFANS cafe, 2025-06) → international pop-ups (HK AIRSIDE + Japan Marui, 2025-08) → B2B licensing expos (Shanghai CLE / TTF / Tokyo Licensing Japan, 2025-26) → **landmark institutional venue (Taipei 101 observatory, 2026-04)** + bank blind box (undated).

## 4. FORMAT PROGRESSION

Monday Bruce did NOT follow the "drink → merch → store-wave → institutional" retail ladder at all.
It ran a **toy-and-experiential** progression:

**Blind-box collectible (2022 debut, own product) → serialised toy line + domestic pop-up exhibitions (2023-2025, via 哈玩具 HOBBY TOY) → themed F&B space (2025-06 FANFANS cafe) → overseas pop-up stores (2025-08 HK AIRSIDE, Japan Marui) → B2B licensing/toy expos (2025-26 Shanghai CLE, TTF, Giftionery, Tokyo Licensing Japan) → landmark venue special exhibition (2026-04 Taipei 101 observatory) + co-branded bank blind box (DBS, undated).**

- **It broke into the big leagues on an EXPERIENTIAL VENUE** (Taipei 101 observatory), not on a product co-brand - the single clearest data point for the census finding that *when a campaign is about a physical experience rather than moving licensed volume, brands (and here, landmark venues) reach for indie IPs.*
- **The core format is the collectible blind box** - the IP's origin, its recurring revenue cadence (S1-S8), and the vehicle for its brand collabs (DBS and LUX are both *blind boxes*, not stickers or drinks). This is a **designer-toy IP**, distinct from Capoo's sticker-origin and LAIMO's illustration-origin.
- **Pop-up stores and licensing expos, not mass retail, carried the escalation** - the IP scaled reach through experiential venues (HK/Japan/Taiwan pop-ups) and B2B expos courting licensees, a licensing-first growth engine.
- **The mass-retail formats that made Capoo and LAIMO (convenience, drink, finance card, beauty) are absent** from the public record so far - the format Bruce has NOT yet entered.

## 5. GROWTH PATTERN SUMMARY (the observable playbook)

This is the **PUBLIC sequence, inferred from campaign announcements** - not the creator's stated strategy.
Monday Bruce's observable playbook was **designer-toy-first, experiential-and-licensing-led, and fast**: created in 2021 and launched as a **blind box in Dec 2022**, the IP built its audience through the collectible-toy product itself and a recurring **pop-up-exhibition circuit** (run domestically with 哈玩具 HOBBY TOY), rather than through a pre-built free social/sticker following the way Capoo and LAIMO did.
From 2025 it escalated by **going cross-border via pop-up stores (Hong Kong AIRSIDE, Japan Marui) and B2B licensing expos** (Shanghai CLE, Tokyo Licensing Japan) - a licensing-led expansion - and its highest-tier moment to date is an **experiential landmark venue, the Taipei 101 89F observatory special exhibition (2026-04)**, explicitly promoted by 101's chairman as showcasing Taiwan original IP, which is exactly the census's "experiential/venue campaigns skew indie" pattern.
For PBC's market-building story, Bruce is the **counter-model to the fame-first indies**: a young, company-owned (原石創意 STONE CREATIVE, self-licensed) collectible IP that reached a national landmark in ~3.3 years through toys, pop-ups and licensing-expo B2B - and that has **not yet touched the mass-retail convenience/drink/finance channels** where Capoo and LAIMO broke into volume, marking the obvious next-stage headroom.

## Engine determination

**Call: DESIGNER-TOY / GOODS-FIRST** (the engine the original timeline research defines against fame-first: the product and its circuit build the audience, rather than a pre-built free social audience pulling brands in).

Observable evidence for the call:

- **The IP was born as a product.** The first commercial artifact is the TOYS ALLIANCE blind box MBR-A01 (2022-12, curl-verified) - not a sticker set, webcomic, or viral post. The character predates it by only ~1 year.
- **No fame base existed at any observable point.** The single hard figure in the record - 24K IG followers *during the Taipei 101 exhibition itself* (2026-07) - is below even the `50k_200k` tier at the arc's institutional peak. A fame-first arc shows six-figure-plus reach *before* the first brand deal; this arc never shows it even after the biggest deal.
- **The escalation ran on the goods-first circuit's signature formats:** serialised blind-box cadence (S1-S8), designer-toy fairs (TTF), B2B licensing expos (Shanghai CLE, Giftionery, Tokyo Licensing Japan), operator-run mall pop-ups (哈玩具 HOBBY TOY), and internationalisation by pop-up store (HK AIRSIDE, Japan Marui) - the same vocabulary as the LuLu the Piggy arc, and the formats the Bichi Mao determination lists as absent from a fame-first record.
- **Its brand collabs are toys.** The DBS and LUX co-brands are co-branded *blind boxes* (MBR-DBS01, MBR-LM01) - the collab vehicle is the collectible itself, where a fame-first IP's collab vehicle is its image on someone else's product.

Confidence note: **high** - this is the pool's cleanest single-engine arc.
The ordering is unambiguous (product first, audience never large), the format vocabulary is entirely goods-side, and the one scale figure actively refutes a fame explanation.
The honesty limit: the 2023-2024 coverage gap means the *shape* of the early circuit is under-documented, and with no historical follower data the audience curve is unobservable everywhere except one point - the engine call rests on format evidence, which is abundant, not on scale evidence, which is a single number.
For comp use: **a goods-first / designer-toy prospect comps to this arc; a fame-first prospect does not.**
