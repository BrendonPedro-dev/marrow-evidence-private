# IP growth timelines - index

Reconstructed PUBLIC collaboration sequences for the census's proven indie IPs, to build PBC's market-building playbook ("here is how indie IPs that made it actually escalated").
Source of targets: `findings/census/taiwan-v2/_VERDICT.md` (and the JP/KR census runs).

> **Public sequence only - decision context is interview-sourced, not here.**
> Every timeline is the OBSERVABLE sequence reconstructed from public campaign announcements.
> It does NOT contain pitches-and-rejections, deal terms, fees, or the creator's reasoning - that is private and comes from the INTERVIEW PROGRAM, never from this run.

## Method note

Each dated deal is curl-verified to a real source; Wikipedia/fan wikis are used as LEADS only, never as the citation.
Pre-census-window (pre-2024) deals were re-researched and curl-verified for this run; 2024+ deals carry over from the already-curl-verified census tables.
Gaps in the record are stated as gaps, not filled. Single-source rows are flagged. "Earliest deal found under these queries" is stated honestly - the true first deal may predate coverage.
