# 罐頭豬 LuLu the Piggy - IP growth timeline

**Researched: 2026-07-24 | IP: 罐頭豬 LuLu the Piggy (小猪露露) | Owner: created by designer Cici; owned by her brand Cici's Story; produced / co-created and globally licensed-distributed by TOYZEROPLUS (Hong Kong) since 2019.**

> **Public sequence only - decision context is interview-sourced, not here.**
> This file is the OBSERVABLE collaboration sequence ONLY, reconstructed from public campaign announcements.
> It does NOT contain: who LuLu / Toyzeroplus pitched and got rejected by, deal terms, fees, the creator's strategy or reasoning, or why a given brand / venue / mall was chosen.
> That decision context is private and comes from the INTERVIEW PROGRAM, never from this run.

This IP is the census's **regional (non-domestic-Taiwan) art-toy heavyweight**: `findings/ip-timelines/_INDEX.md` lists it as a target with the note "麥味登; the IP-x-IP line with Capoo; census REP=no (ToyzeroPlus/52TOYS)".
Unlike Capoo / LAIMO / Monday Bruce (all Taiwan-origin indies), **LuLu is a Hong-Kong-licensed, mainland-China-designer art toy** whose Taiwan presence is an *import/expansion*, not a home market - which is exactly why it is useful to the playbook: it shows how an already-huge regional blind-box IP *enters* Taiwan (department-store pop-up → local IP-x-IP → local QSR / convenience), rather than how a Taiwan indie is born.

> **KEY CONTRAST FLAG (read before the sequence):** LuLu is **designer-toy-native like Monday Bruce, but the opposite of "young and small."**
> It was a blind-box phenomenon across HK / mainland China from 2019, and by the time it reaches Taiwan in the public record (Dec 2024) it is **already a major IP** running 3-4 metre inflatable mascots in flagship malls. So its *Taiwan* sequence is a **top-down market entry** (arrive big, via a department-store pop-up), not a bottom-up climb. The escalation to watch here is **geographic** (HK/China core → Thailand/Taiwan expansion) layered on the usual **format** escalation (blind box → mall/venue → convenience/QSR).

## 1. ORIGIN

- **Creator: Cici**, an independent handcraft artist who started with **felted-wool art in 2014** and is the founder of **Cici's Story**, the brand that owns LuLu the Piggy. Source (Wikipedia as LEAD, then confirmed on the official site): the official About page https://luluthepiggy.com/pages/about-lulu curl-verified HTTP 200 with "Cici", "2019", "toyzeroplus", "luncheon" and "Hong Kong" all present in-page.
- **The character:** **LuLu**, a pinky-pink **pig elf** that (in the fiction) climbed out of a **can of luncheon meat (罐頭)** out of curiosity to explore the world - hence the Chinese name 罐頭豬 ("canned pig"). Theme: "healing" / "companionship". Source: official About page (above) + Wikipedia lead.
- **When/where it started:** the character became a trend IP when **Toyzeroplus (HK) collaborated with Cici to create LuLu's first blind-box series in 2019**, debuting at the **2019 Shanghai Toy Show**. Source: official site (Toyzeroplus + 2019 confirmed in-page) + Wikipedia lead. **This is the origin anchor: 2019.** Pre-2019 activity (Cici's felted-wool / Cici's Story handcraft era from 2014) predates the blind-box IP and this coverage.
- **Ownership:**
  - **IP created by Cici, owned by Cici's Story** (product pages carry "Cici's Story x ToyZero Plus"; e.g. the Farm / Celebration blind-box series are credited "by Cici's Story x ToyZero Plus"). 
  - **TOYZEROPLUS (Hong Kong)** is the producer / co-creator and the **global licensing-distribution engine** (official global storefront https://luluthepiggy.com "SHIP WORLDWIDE 全球直送"; Taiwan distributor storefront https://www.toyzeroplus.com.tw/products curl-verified HTTP 200, carries 罐頭豬 LuLu 盲盒). This is a **company/agency-licensed** structure (designer + licensing house), NOT a lone self-licensed creator like Capoo. Matches the census REP=no classification (owned via ToyzeroPlus; 52TOYS is a mainland distribution partner).
  - **Confusion flag:** LuLu is frequently mislabelled online as a **POP MART / 泡泡瑪特** character. It is **not** - it is a Toyzeroplus / Cici's Story IP. No Pop Mart ownership or partnership was verified; treat any "LuLu is Pop Mart" claim as reseller error.

## 4. THE ESCALATION READ

**LuLu's public sequence is a GEOGRAPHIC escalation stacked on a format escalation - and by the time it hits Taiwan it is already a major IP arriving top-down.**

- **First "third-party brand/venue" moment: the T.O.P. mall Valentine's installation (HK, Feb 2020)** - ~1 year after the 2019 blind-box debut. Note it is a **shopping-mall venue**, not a product co-brand: LuLu broke into brand work through **experiential mall installations in its home market (Hong Kong)**, the same "venue-first" pattern seen with Monday Bruce, but at far bigger physical scale (3m inflatables from the very first outing).
- **The engine is Toyzeroplus's licensing-distribution reach, not a lone creator's social fame.** Where Capoo/LAIMO were pulled up by free sticker/FB audiences, LuLu was pushed out by a **HK licensing house** across malls and retail - a company-scaled expansion.
- **The first mass-retail-channel moment was early: a 7-Eleven Hong Kong精品 line in Aug 2022** - i.e. within ~3 years of the 2019 debut LuLu was already on a top-tier convenience chain *in its home market*, well before it ever reached Taiwan. Convenience-store access is therefore a **HK-core capability LuLu had years before its Taiwan entry**, not a Taiwan-localisation move.
- **2024 is the visible internationalisation inflection**, all clustered on the **5th anniversary**: HK mall event (MOSTown, Jun-Aug), **Thailand market entry** (Siam Discovery country-first pop-up, Jul-Aug), a **second HK 7-Eleven line** (Sep), then **Taiwan market entry** (Shin Kong Mitsukoshi Taipei, Dec). Multiple markets/channels in one anniversary year.
- **Taiwan specifically is entered TOP-DOWN:** the first public Taiwan deal (Dec 2024) is a **flagship department-store pop-up with a 3.4m mascot at 新光三越 台北南西** - i.e. LuLu arrives in Taiwan *already* at the tier a domestic indie spends years climbing toward. It then **localised** via (a) a **Taiwan-star IP-x-IP** (Bugcat Capoo, Nov 2025 - its first-ever Capoo collab, and notably it partnered the census's top domestic indie) and (b) a **domestic QSR** (麥味登, May 2026) and a **TW convenience campaign** (7-11 TW, ~May 2026).
- **Highest-tier partners overall:** a **global licensor (Hasbro MONOPOLY)** on the IP-x-IP axis, and **convenience-store chains (7-Eleven HK, and TW)** on the channel axis. **No bank/finance/transit partner** appears - the institutional-finance rung that Capoo reached is absent from LuLu's public record so far.

**Shape of the climb (public):** 2019 blind-box debut (Shanghai) → HK mall installations (T.O.P., 2020) → **HK 7-Eleven convenience line (Aug 2022)** → [huge HK/China blind-box growth, under-covered by en/zh-TW press here] → 2024 5th-anniversary internationalisation wave: HK mall event + Thailand entry + 2nd HK 7-Eleven → **Taiwan entry via flagship department store (Dec 2024)** → Taiwan localisation: **Capoo IP-x-IP (Nov 2025)** + **麥味登 QSR + 7-11 TW (May 2026)**, with **Hasbro MONOPOLY** as the marquee licensor tie-in (undated).

## 5. FORMAT PROGRESSION

LuLu ran a **blind-box → experiential-venue → retail-channel** progression, with **geography as the real escalation variable**:

**Blind-box collectible (2019 origin, the core product and revenue engine) → experiential mall installations / events (T.O.P. 2020, MOSTown 2024, dept-store pop-ups) → convenience-store merch lines (7-Eleven HK from Aug 2022, again 2024; 7-11 TW ~2026) → country-entry pop-up stores (Thailand Siam Discovery 2024, Taiwan 新光三越 2024) → local QSR co-brand (麥味登 2026) + licensed IP-x-IP (Capoo 2025, Hasbro MONOPOLY undated).**

- **It broke into new MARKETS on department-store / mall pop-ups** (Siam Discovery, Shin Kong Mitsukoshi) - the venue is the market-entry vehicle, then the channel deals (convenience, QSR) follow.
- **The core format never changes: the blind box.** Every brand tie-in orbits the collectible (the Capoo deal is a co-branded figure/plush; MONOPOLY is a licensed collectible board game). LuLu is a **designer-toy IP** through and through - like Monday Bruce, unlike sticker-origin Capoo/LAIMO.
- **The formats it broke into the "big leagues" on are the physical-experience venue (malls/dept stores) and the convenience-store channel** - not a drink-chain handshake (none verified) and not finance (none verified).
- **The Taiwan-specific format ladder is compressed:** department store (Dec 2024) → local IP-x-IP (Nov 2025) → QSR + convenience (May 2026) - roughly 18 months from Taiwan entry to a domestic QSR/convenience presence, because the IP was already major on arrival.

## 6. GROWTH PATTERN SUMMARY (the observable playbook)

This is the **PUBLIC sequence, inferred from campaign announcements** - not the creator's stated strategy.
LuLu the Piggy's observable playbook is **designer-toy-first and licensing-house-scaled, escalating primarily by GEOGRAPHY**: a Cici's Story character launched as a **Toyzeroplus blind box in 2019 (Shanghai Toy Show)**, it grew into a HK/mainland blind-box phenomenon and broke into brand work through **experiential mall installations** in Hong Kong (T.O.P., 2020) rather than through a social-media fame flywheel.
Its visible inflection is the **2024 5th-anniversary internationalisation wave** - HK mall event, a **Thailand country-first pop-up**, a **HK 7-Eleven line**, and then **entry into Taiwan via a flagship department-store pop-up (新光三越 台北南西, Dec 2024)** - after which it **localised in Taiwan** through a first-ever **IP-x-IP with the census's top domestic indie 貓貓蟲咖波 Capoo (Nov 2025)** and a domestic **QSR (麥味登) + convenience (7-11 TW) push (May 2026)**, with **Hasbro's MONOPOLY** as its marquee global-licensor tie-in.
For PBC's market-building story, LuLu is the **"big regional IP enters Taiwan top-down" case**: it does not climb the Taiwan indie ladder, it *parachutes onto the top of it* (department-store scale on day one) and then buys local relevance via a hometown-hero IP-x-IP (Capoo) and mass-retail channel deals - the mirror image of how a home-grown indie like Capoo or Monday Bruce built up. Its untouched rungs (verified) are **drink-chain, bank/finance, and transit** - the same institutional-finance ceiling Capoo cleared but LuLu, so far in the public record, has not.
