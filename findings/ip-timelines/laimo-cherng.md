## 1. ORIGIN

- **Creator: Cherng, 本名 楊承霖 (Yang Cheng-lin)**, born 1990-03-17 in Taipei; graduate of 銘傳大學 數位媒體設計學系 (Ming Chuan University, Digital Media Design). His mother is singer 黃美珍.
- **Started on Facebook: fan page opened 2011-12-03**; **first illustration posted 2012-01**; **formal debut 2012-06**. He drew black-and-white line illustrations of everyday life, using obscure animals (seahorse, green pepper, and the Malayan tapir 馬來貘) as characters; the tapir took off and became the flagship.
  Source (lead: Wikipedia https://zh.wikipedia.org/zh-tw/Cherng); corroborated by the 2015-05-19 ETtoday licensing profile https://finance.ettoday.net/news/505423.
- **First commercial product: 2013-12-25 LINE stickers 「Cherng's對話插畫集」** (launched the same day as 彎彎's set) - among **Taiwan's first wave of paid LINE stickers**. Source: ETtoday https://finance.ettoday.net/news/505423 (lead-verified against Wikipedia chronology).
- **English/brand name:** the tapir is marketed as **LAIMO 來貘/馬來貘**.
- **Ownership from day one to now:**
  - **IP created and owned by the individual 楊承霖** from 2011/2012 (self-created character, no publisher or committee).
  - **2013-2014 early management:** agency 默默文創 (MoMo Creative), which ended in a management-contract dispute and the agency's bankruptcy in Dec 2014.
  - **2015-01-28 to end-2023:** licensing/agency contract signed to **華研國際音樂 HIM International Music** (listed entertainment co.); its licensing dept ran the異業合作 (cross-industry collab) programme. Source: 華研 artist page https://www.him.com.tw/artistscon1.php?mid=46; signing date per Wikipedia chronology.
  - **2024 onward:** 華研 contract ended; licensing handled in-house by **承三工作室 (Cherng Studio)**, the entity on the current official site (cherngy.com footer: "承三工作室 cherng studio").
  - **Taiwan business-registry entry for 承三工作室:** a formal 統一編號/company record was **not** curl-confirmed this run (findbiz.nat.gov.tw is a JS/form search that did not return via curl). Flagged as unverified - the studio name is confirmed only from the official site.

## 3. THE ESCALATION READ

**LAIMO broke into a MAJOR brand almost immediately - it did not climb a ladder of small local shops.**

- **First "major brand" moment: 大同 Tatung (2014-02-25)**, a listed home-appliance corporate ("台灣家電龍頭"), giving the tapir a co-designed 《潮家電系列》 with a 1,000-unit rice cooker that sold out in one hour.
  That is **roughly 20 months after formal debut (2012-06)** and **~26 months after the FB page opened (2011-12)** - and it is the character's essentially FIRST verifiable brand deal, alongside a Taipei Zoo PSA (2014-01) and a 7-ELEVEN premium (late 2014).
- **Time from first verified deal to first big-brand moment: effectively zero** - within the same season (early 2014) LAIMO landed a municipal zoo, a listed appliance maker, and a top convenience chain.
  The currency was viral Facebook fame (500k+ followers by 2015) plus being in Taiwan's first paid-LINE-sticker wave; brands came to the character.
- **The visible inflection point is the 2015 professionalisation:** the 2015-01-28 signing to **華研國際音樂** put a listed entertainment company's licensing machine behind the IP, and within months came the **CTBC 酷玩卡 bank card (2015-06)** and the 爽啾貘 IP-x-IP exhibition.
  This is the moment casual viral collabs became a managed licensing programme - the platform that produced the dense 2021-2022 run (7-11, momo, MOS x2, GUCCI, PAZZO, 全家 full-store, 茶湯會, PONY).
- **The prestige ceiling breaks in 2025** with **CeraVe / L'Oréal** - a global beauty conglomerate fronting a Taiwan indie across MOMO/蝦皮/屈臣氏/康是美/全聯 - the highest brand tier in the record, and the census's marquee counter-example.
- **Contrast with Capoo:** Capoo climbed self-licensed in-house the whole way. **LAIMO's biggest-growth decade (2015-2023) ran through an agency (華研)**; it returned to self/studio control (承三工作室) only after 2023. Same "fame-first, major-brand-early" shape, but a very different ownership machine underneath.

**Shape of the climb:** viral-fame entry → **immediate major-brand access (2014 大同 / 7-11 / zoo)** → agency professionalisation + bank card (2015 華研, CTBC) → exhibitions + IP-x-IP (2015-2018) → dense chain/QSR/convenience/drink/apparel run (2021-2022) → international (Japan, 2023-24) → global-conglomerate beauty peak (2025 CeraVe) → return to studio self-licensing (2024+).

## 4. FORMAT PROGRESSION

LAIMO did NOT follow the textbook "drink → merch → store-wave → institutional."
It ran roughly:

**Digital stickers (2013 LINE) → institutional/PSA + home-appliance merch + convenience premium + cosmetics (2014 zoo / 大同 / 7-11 / HEME) → finance card + IP-x-IP exhibitions (2015 CTBC 酷玩卡, 爽啾貘) → retail/QSR/convenience/drink/apparel wave (2021-2022 7-11, momo, MOS, GUCCI, PAZZO, 全家 全店, 茶湯會, PONY) → international place-branding (2023-24 Japan 富士吉田) → global-conglomerate beauty (2025 CeraVe) + repeat convenience (2025 全家).**

- **It broke into the big leagues on HOME-APPLIANCE MERCH first** (2014 大同), not on drinks - unusual, and it landed a bank card (finance) as early as 2015.
- **Beauty is a through-line, not a late arrival:** HEME cosmetics (2014-15) long predates the marquee CeraVe deal (2025) - the beauty format was in the mix from the start and the 2025 CeraVe deal is the tier-up, not the debut.
- **Handshake drink chains came relatively late** (茶湯會, 2022) - after appliances, a bank card, 7-11, MOS, and GUCCI - the opposite order from a typical 手搖-first indie.
- **The convenience-store full-store wave** (全家 全店, 2022; repeated 2025) is the format it used to reach mass-retail scale.
- **The prestige break came on BEAUTY** (2025 CeraVe/L'Oréal), the single highest brand tier in the record.

## 5. GROWTH PATTERN SUMMARY (the observable playbook)

This is the **PUBLIC sequence, inferred from campaign announcements** - not the creator's stated strategy.
LAIMO's observable playbook was **fame-first, major-brand-immediately, then agency-scaled**: viral Facebook illustration (from 2011-12) and a first-wave paid-LINE-sticker launch (2013) built a huge free audience, and that reach - not a ladder of small local deals - pulled a listed appliance maker (大同), a top convenience chain (7-ELEVEN), a cosmetics brand (HEME) and a municipal zoo into its EARLIEST verifiable collaborations, all in 2014.
In 2015 the licensing turned professional under a listed entertainment agency (華研國際音樂), which held the contract through the IP's densest growth decade and produced the 2021-2022 wave of QSR, convenience full-store, drink-chain, e-commerce, luxury-sticker and apparel deals, before the tier-topping CeraVe/L'Oréal beauty collab in 2025 and a Japan place-branding tie-up.
The defining feature for PBC's market-building story, and the correction to the census: **unlike Capoo, LAIMO's climb was NOT self-licensed for most of its life** - it was agency-represented by 華研 from 2015 to end-2023 and only moved to in-house studio licensing (承三工作室) from 2024, so its "representable/open" status is a recent, post-agency condition, not an original one.
