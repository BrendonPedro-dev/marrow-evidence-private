## Cross-pool findings (what the retrofit established)

1. **Fame-first arcs plateau in one band while the deal ladder keeps climbing.**
LAIMO spent twelve years inside `200k_1m` through GUCCI and CeraVe; Bichi Mao ran its entire dated ladder inside `200k_1m`.
Capoo is the partial exception: its prestige tier followed a `gt_1m` crossing, but its institutional ENTRY also preceded any large measured follower base.
Consequence for the comp feature: **fame-first comps key on deal-ladder rung, not band transitions** - the band is a floor check, not a progress meter.

2. **Social bands systematically understate goods-first IP heft.**
Monday Bruce held a Taipei 101 landmark exhibition on a 24K account; LuLu held an institutional 7-Eleven HK line while its global IG was at-or-below ~100K.
Consequence: **goods-first comps key on the toy/venue/geography ladder**, and a goods-first prospect's small social band must never be read as small heft.

3. **Engine-matching is binary and the machine is the secondary axis.**
A goods-first prospect comps to Monday Bruce or LuLu only; a fame-first prospect comps to Capoo, LAIMO, or Bichi Mao only.
Within an engine, match the machine: self-licensed / creator-run prospects comp to Capoo or Monday Bruce; agency- or house-partnered prospects comp to LAIMO or LuLu; a fame-first prospect already monetising creator-run goods comps to Bichi Mao's variant.

4. **The pool's anchor evidence vocabulary, for downstream reuse:** drift-proof bracket (strongest), dated press floor, at-campaign read, short-gap current-proxy, weak current-proxy, and the two honest failure classes - bracket-straddle (ladder ends in different bands, cannot decide) and narrow straddle (unknown per vocabulary, but with comp-usable numeric bounds, e.g. Capoo's KRTC row pinned within ~8% of the 1M boundary).

5. **Scale-at-first-major-deal is mostly unanswerable in this pool.**
LAIMO's first major falls inside its explosion window (straddle), Capoo's first institutional deal is a straddle, Monday Bruce's early rows are unknowns, LuLu's early rows are unknowns, and Bichi Mao's true first deal is undated.
The pool answers ladder-shape and era questions well; it does not answer "how many followers buys a first deal", and the comp feature should not pretend otherwise.
