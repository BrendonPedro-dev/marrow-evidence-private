## 3. THE ESCALATION READ

**Bichi Mao is fame-first with a long quiet build: five years of audience-and-goods, then the entire brand ladder in ~25 months.**

- **The audience came first, and it came fast and international.** Digital drawing from June 2019; by April 2021 the Instagram was past 200K (archived press), with the profile piece noting most followers were outside Malaysia. Verified counts then climb 392,160 (2022-07, Wayback) → 409K (2023-07, press) → 573K (2024-03, press) → 995K (2026-07, live).
- **The first monetisation layer was creator-run goods, not brand deals:** a how-to-draw book by 2021-04, three books on Amazon by 2023-07, merch/Patreon/conventions per the 2023 profile, digital stickers via Stipop by 2023-12. No dated brand deal exists in this window.
- **The dated brand ladder then compresses into ~25 months:** apparel chain (SODA, 2024-06) → own solo exhibition (2024-08) → national cake chain (Secret Recipe, 2025-08) → **national rail operator with a train livery + transit card (KTM, 2025-11/12)** → QSR (Pizza Hut, 2026-06) → beauty/pharmacy (SimplyHeal via Watsons, 2026-07).
- **First institutional partner: KTM (2025-11)** - a government-owned rail operator wrapping trains in the IP and issuing a co-branded transit card for Visit Malaysia 2026. In the MY census's format map, transit/institutional formats are otherwise closed to independents, which makes this the record's standout rung.
- **The Taiwan signal is an expo, not a deal:** the 2026 Taiwan IP Expo pairing (Bichi Mao × 丑白兔, Sunway Pyramid, 2026-05) is a TAICCA-organised creator crossover - the observable first step toward the Taiwan market, with no fetched source reporting a converted brand campaign yet.
- **Every dated brand event sits inside one scale band.** From the first dated collab (2024-06) to the latest (2026-07), the primary channel stayed within `200k_1m` - the ladder from mid apparel to institutional rail ran without the IP crossing 1M on its primary channel (it sits 0.5% below that edge at 2026-07-30).

**Shape of the climb:** webcomic audience build + books/merch/Patreon (2019-2023, no dated brand deals) → small undated brand work (packaging, camera, amenities; named 2024) → apparel collection (SODA, 2024-06) → own solo exhibition (2024-08) → national chains (Secret Recipe 2025-08) → institutional transit (KTM train + KOMLINK card, 2025-11..2026-01) → cross-border expo signal (Taiwan IP Expo, 2026-05) → QSR + beauty (Pizza Hut 2026-06, SimplyHeal x Watsons 2026-07).

## 4. FORMAT PROGRESSION

**Books/self-goods (2021-2023) → digital stickers (by 2023) → apparel co-brand (2024) → own exhibition (2024) → cafe-chain campaign (2025) → transit livery + payments card (2025-26) → licensing-expo cross-border showcase (2026) → QSR packaging + beauty product (2026).**

- The progression is **reach-monetisation first** (books, merch, stickers - formats a webcomic audience buys directly), then **brand formats in rising tiers** - the classic fame-first ordering, compressed.
- The **institutional rung arrived via transit (KTM)**, not banking or convenience - and explicitly rode a national tourism push (VM2026), the same "public campaign wants local IP" mechanism the census saw in the KTM census row.
- **No blind-box / designer-toy circuit anywhere in the record** - plush exists only as keychain merch in the IP's own channels (TOKYOPOP store), not as a collectible-toy growth engine.
- The **not-yet formats**: convenience store, drink chain, bank card, telco/device - the volume formats the MY census found closed to independents remain absent here too, except transit.

## 5. GROWTH PATTERN SUMMARY (the observable playbook)

This is the **PUBLIC sequence, inferred from campaign announcements** - not the creators' stated strategy.
Bichi Mao's observable playbook was **fame-first, goods-monetised, late-laddering**: an Instagram webcomic audience built from 2019 that passed 200K within roughly two years, monetised for its first five years through creator-run goods (books on Amazon, merch, Patreon, Stipop stickers) rather than brand deals, and then ran the entire dated brand ladder - apparel, national cake chain, national rail with a transit card, QSR, beauty - in about 25 months from mid-2024, all while its primary channel stayed inside the `200k_1m` band.
Its institutional break came through a **public-sector tourism vehicle** (KTM x Visit Malaysia 2026) rather than finance or convenience, and its first observable move toward Taiwan is the **2026 Taiwan IP Expo creator pairing** at Sunway Pyramid, which no fetched source yet reports converting into a brand campaign.

## Engine determination

**Call: FAME-FIRST** (the Capoo/LAIMO engine, as the existing timeline findings define it: a large free social audience built first, which then pulls brands), **with a goods-monetised early phase noted as a variant trait, not a third engine.**

Observable evidence for the call:

- **Audience precedes every dated deal by years.** IG past 200K by 2021-04 (archived press); the first dated brand collab is 2024-06 (SODA). The reach existed before the brand ladder - the defining fame-first ordering.
- **No designer-toy circuit.** The record has no blind box, no toy-fair presence, no collectible-drop cadence, no pop-up-store-led internationalisation - the Monday Bruce / LuLu engine's signature formats are absent. Plush appears only as own-channel merch keychains.
- **Brands entered at mid tier and climbed to institutional within the fame window** (apparel 2024 → national chains 2025 → national rail 2025 → QSR/beauty 2026), matching the fame-first pattern of brands reaching for an existing audience.
- **The variant trait:** unlike Capoo/LAIMO (whose free sticker/social reach was the whole early story), Bichi Mao's pre-brand years show a visible creator-run goods business (books on Amazon by 2021, merch/Patreon/conventions per 2023 press). That is monetisation OF fame, not a goods-first growth engine: the goods sold to an audience the webcomic had already built, whereas Monday Bruce's blind box BUILT the audience. Recorded as a trait, not a hybrid call.

Confidence note: **moderate-to-high.**
High on the engine ordering itself (the dated record is unambiguous that reach preceded deals).
Moderated by two honesty limits: the undated 2022-2023 small collabs mean the first-brand-deal date is uncertain by up to ~2 years, and the pre-2021 audience curve is unverifiable (`unknown` band), so *how* the fame was built (organic virality vs. events vs. reposts) is not observable in this record.
For comp use: **a fame-first prospect comps to this arc; a goods-first prospect does not.**
