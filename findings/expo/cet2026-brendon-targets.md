## Market briefings

### Hong Kong - 33 rows, every one IP, no brand side at all

The census found **6 distinct addressable creators in the entire HK market**, with its
verdict stating the realistic ceiling is above 6 and naming the reason. Thirty-three HK
creators in one hall is therefore either a large census undercount or a list dominated by
label-attached properties - HK is the art-toy label capital and the mechanism identified
is crowding-out, where labels absorb the creators and captive means not representable.
Phase 0 of the research answers this name by name.

HK independent properties the census did find: LuLu the Piggy, Plastic Thing, the iSQUARE
trio, LeonLollipop, BUCKET MAN.

**Number prohibition.** HK indie share is bounded 2.6% to 14.0%, biased upward, and its
own verdict says 14.0% is not quotable on its own. It never appears in an email, a teaser,
or a booth conversation. Internal priority use only.

### Thailand - 14 rows, 7 IP, 4 Brand, 3 Org

9 distinct addressable creators, 20.5% independent share, 11 addressable rows. The figure
is publishable. The mechanism is **erasure**: Thai brand PR names the licensor and omits
the creator, a measured 2.2x effect between brand-side and artist-side queries. The
verdict's own line is that in Thailand the fix is to ask the creator because the press
will not tell you.

That is the strongest honest rapport line available: their own market does not record
their work, and PBC found it by searching for them rather than for the brands.

Small enough that all 14 get individual attention.

### Poland (2) and Macao (1) - outside the census entirely

The ten-market census covers every market on the attendee list except these two. There is
no market evidence for either, so no evidence-based line exists and none should be
improvised. All three are Org type with no published email. Treat as booth-floor
introductions only.
