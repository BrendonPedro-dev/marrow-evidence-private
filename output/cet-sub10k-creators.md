## The one line to have ready

7-ELEVEN's ibon x 文博會 特印企劃 signed **16 creators** for 140+ print SKUs across 7,300 stores.
**12 of the 15 whose follower count could be resolved are sub-10k - 80%.** Eight of those twelve are accepted rows in this file, and **all eight are on this list**.

That is a national brand's actual signed cohort, not a roster of who was available, and four in five of it sits under ten thousand followers.

Say it as **12 of 15 resolved**, not 12 of 16. The sixteenth creator, 木子島工作室, was never resolved to a handle or a count, so it is unresolved rather than out of band; counting it in the denominator would quietly assert it is above 10k.

## Caveats to carry, if the numbers come up

- The follower counts are current-proxy reads, not at-campaign figures.
  For the ibon rows the gap is about one month, the shortest in the file; for 茶包先生 it is about three months.
- **`representable` was tested on all of these, and only four of the ten came back `yes`.**
  A `yes` means the creator's own channel advertises licensing or solicits collaborations. It does **not** mean no exclusive licensor sits behind them.
  An `unclear` means the channel says nothing either way, which is the normal state of a small creator's bio. It is not a negative and it is not a reason to skip the booth.
  All ten publish a direct email on their exhibitor page in a directory the organiser calls a 圖像授權交易平臺, so all ten are reachable regardless.
- The 46.8% sub-10k figure for this show's floor is computed on the 402 exhibitors who publish an Instagram handle, out of 629 exhibitor pages. The 227 who publish none are unbanded, not zero.
