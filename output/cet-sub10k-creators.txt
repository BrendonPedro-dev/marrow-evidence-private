THE ONE LINE TO HAVE READY

7-ELEVEN's ibon x 文博會 特印企劃 signed 16 creators for 140+ print SKUs
across 7,300 stores. 12 of the 15 whose follower count could be resolved
are sub-10k - 80%. Eight of those twelve are accepted rows, and all eight
are on this list.

Say it as "12 of 15 resolved", not 12 of 16. The sixteenth creator,
木子島工作室, was never resolved to a handle or a count, so it is unresolved
rather than out of band.


IF THE NUMBERS COME UP

- Follower counts are current reads, not at-campaign figures. For the ibon
  creators the gap is about one month.
- REP was tested on all of these and only four of the ten came back YES.
  YES means the creator's own channel advertises licensing or solicits
  collaborations. It does NOT mean no exclusive licensor sits behind them.
  UNCLEAR means the channel says nothing either way, which is normal for a
  small creator's bio. It is not a negative and not a reason to skip the
  booth.
- All ten publish a direct email on their exhibitor page, in a directory
  the organiser calls a 圖像授權交易平臺. All ten are reachable regardless.
- The 46.8% sub-10k figure for this show's floor counts the 402 exhibitors
  who publish an Instagram handle, out of 629 exhibitor pages. The 227 who
  publish none are unbanded, not zero.


NOT ONE of these five licensees - RHINOSHIELD, DEVILCASE, 7-ELEVEN, the
hotel, the ferry operator - publishes a way to contact the creator it
licensed. Every route back to these people runs through their own channel
or through this show. Which is why you are walking the floor.
